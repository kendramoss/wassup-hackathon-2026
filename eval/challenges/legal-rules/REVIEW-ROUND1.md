# Review round 1 (after the D969 run)

The frozen first study is unchanged: candidate `20b97e0f`, datasets, labels and `results-*.jsonl` are byte-identical, and `go run ./cmd/legalchallenge check` still reproduces them with the amended engines. The items below are review-derived regressions, recorded as Go unit tests, not as a second reserved score.

1. Tariff watch: an action above 100 percent that reuses the heading of an embedded undated list (for example 9903.88.01) overwrote that list's 25 percent component with the action's rate and dates while the action was not in force. Cause: the first fix kept the duty package's 100 percent list bound and overrode the component rate by heading, whether or not the action was in force. Root fix: `duty.MaxListRate = 5` replaces the literal 100 percent list bound, matching the 500 percent bound of `Dataset.Validate` (a rate above 5 or at or below 0 is still refused); the stand-in and override in `tariffwatch` are removed; a component takes an action's dates only when that action is in force. Behaviour change: a list rate between 100 and 500 percent is now accepted where `NewAdder` used to refuse it; country-wide duty rates keep their 100 percent bound. Tests: `TestListRateBoundMatchesTheActionBound`, `TestFutureOrExpiredActionDoesNotOverwriteAnEmbeddedListComponent`, `TestActionAbove100PercentWithExclusionsAndPlan` (in force, not yet, expired, whole-subheading exclusion valid and lapsed, annual and plan effect).
2. Textile 6309-6310: (c)(1) precedes the specific rule, so a good wholly obtained in one country keeps that country under (c)(1) even when packed elsewhere (the first fix wrongly took that away). With a complete list that names no packing stage, the rule cannot name a country and the check now asks (`operation_not_stated`) instead of naming the manufacturing country. Test: `TestPackagingRuleKeepsWhollyObtainedPriorityAndAsksWithoutAPackingStage`. No frozen row changed.
3. Scoring semantics, disclosed: a USMCA match compares the status, exact RVC where labelled and only the alternatives and information needs named in the label (the engine may report more; needs are a subset check); it is not an exact full-output comparison. Textile matches are status, origin and paragraph only; reason classes are a separate metric and are not part of a match. Row status `match` for `textile-test-138` and `-139` therefore coexists with a wrong expected class.

# Review round 2: component identity

Semantics now implemented in `tariffwatch.Evaluator` (source judgment): an undated list entry is the baseline for its heading. A Section 301 action in force on the entry day with the same heading supersedes that entry only for the subheadings and the origin it covers; it is not added to the entry, so a line is never charged both the baseline and the action. Other lines and other origins of the same heading keep the baseline, and outside the action's window the baseline is back. Only the component that came from the action carries its dates. Whole-subheading exclusions and the annual and plan arithmetic act on the single resulting component. `Dataset.Validate` refuses two actions of one heading and kind that share a day, a line and an origin (successive windows, disjoint lines and disjoint origins are allowed, and a revised heading still replaces its action by heading), so no rate is invented or summed. Behaviour change: before, an in-force action on an embedded heading was charged on top of the list entry (0.25 plus 1.5 on 9903.88.01), and an uncovered line of that heading was stamped with the action's dates.

Tests: `TestInForceActionSupersedesTheListOnlyWhereItCovers` (covered line replaced once, uncovered line keeps the baseline and no dates, before and after the window, action naming another origin), `TestSupersedingActionWithExclusionsAndPlan` (whole exclusion valid and lapsed, annual and plan change equal to 150 percent minus the baseline, not added to it), `TestOverlappingActionsOnTheSameHeadingAreRefused`; the earlier future and expired tests still pass. The frozen first study files are unchanged and still reproduce.
