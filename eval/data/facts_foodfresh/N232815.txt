In your letter dated August 28, 2012, you requested a tariff classification, marking, and country of origin ruling.The products are freeze-dried miracle fruits (berrylike fruit of species Synsepalum dulcificum), also known as miracle berries or miraculous berries. The said fruits weighing 0.45 – 0.5 grams each, are products of Philippines, freeze dried in Japan, and packed in air-tight containers.
