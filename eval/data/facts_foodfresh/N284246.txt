In your letter dated March 7, 2017 you requested a country of origin ruling on Marinated Salmon. You have outlined a case in which salmon from the United States will be harvested and then headed, gutted and frozen in the United States. The resulting product is shipped to China where the salmon will be filleted, frozen and shipped back to the United States. After importation the frozen salmon fillets are thawed, cut into smaller pieces, and marinated. The product is packaged for sale in the United States.
