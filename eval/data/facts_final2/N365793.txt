In your letter dated October 6, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on windchimes for the purposes of current trade remedies. The product under consideration is a windchime identified as the metal butterfly windchime, SKU# 6107813. It measures approximately 31.5 inches in height and 9 inches in diameter. Each windchime consists of a disk-shaped metal top piece holding five sleek black metal tubes cut into staggered lengths, a clapper, and a daisy-shaped flower windcatcher, all suspended beneath. Three metal chains are connected to a metal butterfly, which serves as the main crown element at the top of the disk, and is connected by chains to the S-hook for hanging. In your ruling request submission, you state that the windchimes are assembled in Vietnam using components sourced from both China and Vietnam. The components sourced from China are the S-hook, iron chains, and nylon strings. The balance of components, including the top pieces, flowers, butterflies, clappers, and windcatchers, are produced in Vietnam. The assembly process in Vietnam includes making the metal components from raw metal sheets and tubes, and combining them with the Chinese-origin S-hook, iron chains, and strings to fabricate the finished windchimes. This manufacturing process involves metal stamping, cutting, spot welding, polishing, powder coating, paint spraying, and hand-painting (using coating and paint of Vietnamese origin). Finally, the finished products undergo testing and inspection before being packaged for export. The Bill of Materials (BOM) demonstrates that the manufacturing operations in Vietnam contribute approximately 86% of the total cost of the windchimes.
