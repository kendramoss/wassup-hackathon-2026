In your letter dated September 23, 2026, you requested a tariff classification and country of origin determination ruling. The merchandise under consideration is the Cash Wand, model number A-CSH-0020, which is a contactless payment device. This payment device consists of a plastic outer casing in the shape of a wand with a payment chip module embedded within it and attached to a clasp. It contains an integrated circuit (IC) that stores a code that enables consumers to make near-field communication (NFC) payments at point-of-sale terminals. Moreover, the secure microcontroller (payment chip) stores and transmits payment data when the device is read by an NFC-enabled payment terminal.
