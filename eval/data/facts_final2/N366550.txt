In your letter dated October 30, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling. The merchandise under consideration is identified by model number HB30200 and described as a USB C to SD+Micro SD Hub. The subject device is comprised of a printed circuit board assembly (PCBA) within an enclosure. On the face of the enclosure are two memory card slots, one to hold a standard SD card and the other to accept a Micro SD card. Attached to the housing is an insulated cable terminated with a USB C connector. The subject device is designed to be connected to a computer or other personal device, allowing for the device to read and access information on various memory cards. In your request, you state that the manufacturing process occurs primarily in the Philippines through various steps. Initially, the insulated USB cable is cut to length and the ends are prepared for the addition of the USB-C connector. The connector is then soldered onto one end of the cable. Next, a bare PCB is imported from China and the necessary electrical components are added via surface mount technology (SMT). This includes the SD card reading technology. The other end of the cable is then attached to the finished PCBA before the enclosure is assembled covering the PCBA. The finished device is then tested and packaged for shipment to the United States.
