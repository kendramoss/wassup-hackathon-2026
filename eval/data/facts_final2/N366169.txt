In your letter dated October 19, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on a glass shower door, model FXSD4870DW06BL1. The merchandise under consideration is a glass shower door consisting of tempered glass panels and multiple hardware components including aluminum square tubes, guide rails, rubber strips, expansion plastic anchors, screws, screw caps, washers, and handles. The float glass used for the panels is manufactured in either Indonesia or Vietnam, where the glass is produced by the float process, annealed, cooled, and cut into sheets. The finished shower door is packaged in China and exported to the United States.
