In your letter dated October 19, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on an air purifier. Descriptive literature was provided for our review. The item under consideration is described as the Mighty air purifier, which is an electronic home appliance designed to remove various airborne pollutants, including fine dust, harmful gases, and bacteria, from indoor air. The air purifier uses a 3-in-1 custom filter (pre-filter, deodorization filter, and HEPA filter) to create a cleaner indoor environment. The device measures 17 inches by 18 inches by 9.5 inches and weighs 12 pounds. It is designed for room sizes up to 361 square feet. The air purifier is made of components sourced from China, South Korea and Vietnam. The Chinese components include the harness assembly, ionizer, power cord assembly, particle sensor and magnet. The South Korean components include the magnetic bracket, motor guide cover, deodorization filter, EVA pad, filament tape, diffusion film, inlay film, gasket, fan insert, acrylonitrile butadiene styrene (LG ABS), nut, prefilter mesh, screw and washer. The Vietnamese components include the box, cushion, HEPA filter, label, manual, motor, main module printed board assembly (PBA), power module PBA, slip sheet, reed switch and vinyl. The manufacturing of the air purifier occurs in Vietnam and starts with the injection molding of the plastic components. Prior to assembly, the control/top cover sub-assembly, upper bracket assembly, motor and fan assembly, power PBA and ionizer assembly, and harness and particle sensor assembly are pre-assembled. These sub-assemblies are then combined to form the semi-finished air purifier. Following this, the top/rear and power cord assembly and the rear guide filter integration are carried out on the semi-finished air purifier, after which specification labeling is applied and the filter and front cover assembly is installed. Finally, the finished air purifier is tested, inspected, cleaned, and packaged for retail sale.
