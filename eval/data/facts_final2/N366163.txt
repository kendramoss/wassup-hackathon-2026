In your letter dated October 19, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on an air purifier. Descriptive literature and technical data were provided for our review. The item under consideration is described as the Champ air purifier, which is a cylindrical electronic home appliance designed to remove various airborne pollutants, including fine dust, harmful gases, and bacteria, from indoor air. The Champ uses a 3-in-1 custom filter (pre-filter, HEPA filter, and activated carbon) to create a cleaner indoor environment. The device measures 12 inches by12 inches by 23 inches and weighs 17 pounds. It is designed for room sizes up to 546 square feet. The air purifier is assembled in Vietnam with components sourced from Vietnam, South Korea, and China. The particle sensor, power cord assembly, particle sensor harness, SMPS harness, magnetic cover cistern, and bonding materials are sourced from China. Components such as the LG ABS resin, screws, inlay film, mesh, insert-fan, and motor guide cover are from South Korea. The remaining key components and parts are all sourced locally in Vietnam, including the filter assembly, motor, main key module printed board assembly (PBA), power module PBA, cartons, cushions, and various additional packaging and plastic components necessary to complete the finished article. The air purifier is manufactured through a final assembly process in Vietnam. Prior to assembly, the power PBA, top assembly cover, and particle cover are pre-assembled. The semi-finished good is assembled by installing the frame, power cord, mesh, upper housing, dust sensor, reed switch, motor, and fan. Following this, the motor and fan assembly and the power PBA assembly are installed, and the connectors, power PBA cover, guide flow, side cover, and top cover assembly are integrated into the main body. The specification sticker, front cover, rear cover, duct cover, particle cover, cylindrical filter, and filter cover then complete the installation. The units are then inspected, tested and packaged for shipment to the United States.
