In your letter dated September 16, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling and a marking ruling on alloy steel oil country tubular goods (“OCTGs”) and nonalloy steel couplings on behalf of your client, Deep Oilfield Pipe Manufacturing (Cambodia) Co., Ltd. Photographs of the proposed marking were included in your inquiry. The products under consideration are alloy steel OCTGs and nonalloy steel couplings for use in oil and gas drilling and production operations. According to your submission, the OCTGs and couplings will be imported into the United States (U.S.) in three different scenarios: 1) OCTGs, specifically electric resistance welded (“ERW”) steel casing and tubing, imported without couplings, 2) API 5CT couplings imported separately, and 3) OCTGs imported with preassembled couplings. In the first scenario, alloy steel coils of Chinese, South Korean, or Malaysian origin are sent to Cambodia where they are slit, straightened, leveled, and welded into tubes. These ERW tubes are then heat treated, sized, straightened, inspected, tested, etc., prior to importation into the U.S. The tubes are manufactured to API specifications. In the second scenario, API 5CT steel couplings are manufactured in Cambodia from unfinished nonalloy steel pipe (coupling stock) of Chinese origin. In Cambodia, long lengths of seamless steel pipe are cut to short lengths, internally and externally turned, beveled, threaded, phosphate coated, marked, painted, packaged, and then shipped to the U.S. You state that the coupling stock is manufactured into finished API 5CT couplings as a result of the extensive machining, inspection, testing, and finishing operations performed in Cambodia. The couplings are used to connect lengths of casing and tubing. In the third scenario, alloy steel coils of Chinese, South Korean, or Malaysian origin are exported to Cambodia where they are slit, straightened, leveled, and welded into tubes. These ERW tubes are then upset
and heat treated on both ends, machined and threaded. Next, an API 5CT coupling of Cambodian origin that is manufactured from Chinese origin coupling stock is installed on the threaded tube ends. Finally, the OCTGs and coupling are painted, marked, packed, and subsequently imported into the U.S.
