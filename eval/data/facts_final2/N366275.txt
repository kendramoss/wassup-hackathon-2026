In your letter dated October 21, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on an electric ceramic wax warmer for purposes of current trade remedies. The product under consideration is an electric wax warmer, identified as the "5in Ceramic Boots Wax Warmer, SKU#6109595." The wax warmer is in the form of a pair of classic black cowboy boots with iconic white western stitching patterns. Each wax warmer consists of a pair of ceramic boots, a ceramic vessel sitting atop the boots, a light bulb, a light socket, a power cord with an end plug, and wax cubes. The submitted literature indicates that all components are produced in Vietnam, except for the light bulb and the lamp sockets, which are imported from China. The assembly process in Vietnam includes making the ceramic boots and combining them with the imported light bulbs and sockets to fabricate the finished wax warmers. Overall, this manufacturing process in Vietnam involves molding, slip casting, trimming, drying, bisque firing and glazing, glaze firing, metal stamping, spot welding, plastic injection molding, and hand-painting. Finally, the finished products—the wax warmer and wax cubes—are packaged together for export. The Bill of Materials (BOM) demonstrates that the manufacturing operations in Vietnam contribute approximately 87% of the total cost of production and final value of the finished products.
