In your letter, dated October 27, 2026, you requested a binding tariff country of origin ruling for paper cups. Product information was submitted for our review. For the paper cups, you outline a scenario wherein jumbo rolls of polyethylene-coated paper are manufactured in China. The jumbo rolls are shipped to Malaysia where they will be printed, die-cut into cup components, formed into cups, sealed, formed with rims, and finished. For the round and rectangular paperboard containers, you outline a scenario wherein coated or uncoated paperboard from China is printed, die-cut, and molded into paperboard containers in Malaysia. For the container lids, you outline a scenario wherein coated or uncoated paperboard from China is printed, die-cut, and molded into lids in Malaysia.
