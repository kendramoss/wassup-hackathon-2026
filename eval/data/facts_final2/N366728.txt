In your letter, dated November 5, 2026, you requested a binding tariff classification, country of origin, and applicability of trade programs ruling on coated parchment paper. Product information and photos were submitted for our review. The product under consideration is coated parchment paper, both bleached and unbleached. You indicate that the product is food-grade baking paper coated with silicone on both sides. The silicone renders the paper greaseproof. You do not identify the process by which the paper pulp or the paper is made. It is not clear if the paper meets the definition of parchment paper as set forth in the Explanatory Notes to the Harmonized System (ENs). The paper is packaged in rolls measuring 12 to 15 inches in width and 24 to 164 feet in length. The paper weighs 39 grams per square meter. Regardless of whether the paper meets the definition of parchment paper, the ENs to heading 4806 (parchment paper) are clear that coated parchment is not classifiable in heading 4806 with other parchment paper: “The heading excludes papers which have been rendered greaseproof or waterproof by coating, impregnation or similar processes after manufacture of the paper (headings 48.09 or 48.11).”
