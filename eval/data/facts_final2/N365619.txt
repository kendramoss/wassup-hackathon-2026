In your letter dated October 1, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on pneumatic die grinder kits. Product information was submitted with your request. The items under consideration are two models of pneumatic die grinder kits for industrial polishing and grinding applications. During operation, compressed air enters the tool and is directed to a cylinder which houses a rotor. The highly compressed air forces the rotor to spin at high speed. The generated rotation is transferred to a connected spindle, collet or chuck, and finally the shank of an attached grinding tool. Item number 1151200007, described as a pneumatic angle die grinder with a red anodized aluminum housing, features a 90-degree head, a 6.3 millimeter (mm) to 3 mm reducing collet, and a speed rating of 20,000 rotations per minute. Item number 1151200008, described as a pneumatic straight die grinder with a black anodized aluminum housing and red end caps, features a 1/4-inch collet and a speed rating of 22,000 rotations per minute. Each die grinder will be packaged as a kit for retail sale with 10 mounted grinding points and two wrenches in a blow-molded plastic tool case. You confirmed that the wrenches are used to fasten the chucks around the shank of a grinding point attachment. As you described, the impact wrench is assembled in Malaysia from Malaysian and Chinese components. In Malaysia, metal stock and rough metal blanks from China undergo CNC machining such as turning, facing, milling, drilling, tapping, and deburring to obtain the form of the finished housing, rotor, cylinder, work spindle, and air inlet fitting. With regard to the housing, aluminum block sourced from Malaysia is die-cast to shape prior to CNC machining. These processes deliver the precise shapes, dimensions, splining, drilled holes, threading, slots, and surfaces which the components rely on for functioning. The rotor also undergoes heat treatment to achieve specified hardness and mechanical properties.
Additionally, several Chinese-origin components are imported to Malaysia ready for assembly or consolidation into the kits without further processing. These components include, but are not limited to, the trigger levers, valve stems, rotor blades, plates, springs, mounted grinding points, pins, bushings, and bearings. Assembly of the angled die grinder (Item number 1151200007) begins with the installation of the muffle and air inlet into the aluminum housing. Following that, the valve stem is installed into the bushing and that subassembly is installed into the housing. A spring, the air regulator, the locking plug, a bearing, and the end plate are then installed to the housing. At that point, the rotor blades, front plate, and angle gear are installed to the rotor. The clamp nut is installed to the housing for installation of the clamp plant and angle head. In the following steps, a bearing, the clamp plate, the work spindle, the chuck, and the collet nut are sequentially installed to the angle head. The trigger lever is then installed to the housing to finish the assembly process. Finally, the angle die grinder is tested and packed with the loose accessories for export. Assembly of the straight die grinder (Item number 1151200008) begins with the installation of the plastic base and air inlet into the aluminum housing. After that, the spring is installed into the valve stem, and the valve stem and spring are installed into the air regulator. In the next steps, the valve stem, spring, and air regulator subassembly is installed into the housing, and a locking plug is installed. The rotor blades and the end plate with a bearing is installed to the rotor. At that point, the rotor is installed into the cylinder, and a rotor busing, front plate, and bearing are installed. The rotor and work spindle are then installed into the aluminum housing. The chuck is installed to the work spindle, and the clamp plate and cap are installed to the housing. In addition, the collet nut is installed into the work spindle, the trigger lever is installed to the housing with a pin, and a bushing is installed into the pin. Finally, the straight die grinder is tested and packed with the loose accessories for export.
