In your letter, dated September 18, 2026, you requested a country of origin determination. You have presented a scenario in which Atlantic salmon (Salmo salar) is farm-raised and harvested in Norway. Following harvest, the salmon is beheaded, gutted, and chilled in Norway before being shipped to Poland for further processing. In Poland, the backbone, rib bones, dorsal fins, shoulder belt tissue, belly fat, white membrane, and pin bones are removed. The salmon is subsequently processed into fillets and portioned into specified weight ranges and customer required configurations. The finished portions undergo final inspection and are packaged and labeled in Poland in preparation for export to the United States. You seek a determination as to the country of origin of the above-described product.
