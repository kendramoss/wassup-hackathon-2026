In your letter, dated October 2, 2026, you requested a ruling on the country of origin of a screen system. A sample of the fabric and photographs of the item were provided with your request and will be retained for training purposes. Item #S7-D, described as a “S7 Insect Screen Double,” is a retractable double screen system designed to be permanently attached to a framed opening, such as a window or door opening and intended to protect the home against insects while allowing ventilation. The retractable screen system consists of an aluminum frame (head and sill components) and two screen assemblies. Each screen assembly consists of a mesh fabric attached to a roll tube fitted with cones and cables on one end and a handle pull bar attached to the other end. You state the fabric is composed of 72 percent polyvinyl chloride (PVC) and 28 percent polyester woven mesh fabric. Prior to weaving, the 100 percent polyester multifilament yarn is coated with PVC and treated for Ultraviolet (UV). Each screen assembly is attached to the aluminum frame on the left or right side and fully retracts while not in use for an unobstructed view. Each screen opens horizontally by gently pulling the pull bar to the desired position. To retract the screen, one pushes the pull bar to draw the mesh fabric back into the screen assembly. The insect screen double maximum frame height is 126 inches, frame width is 157 inches, and frame thickness is 2 inches. You also state that the screen system is imported to the United States in knock down condition and that it is a completely finished unit, packed with all components, ready to install in a window or door opening. You state the manufacturing operations for the screen system are as follows: Australia: • The plastic cones are produced and shipped to Poland. China:
• The frame blocks, fixing screws, and stainless adjusting cone plate are produced and shipped to Poland. • The 100 percent polyester multifilament yarn is produced and shipped to the United States on spools. Germany: • The Centrack seam tape is produced and shipped to Poland. Spain: • The bearings are produced and shipped to Poland. Thailand: • The stainless-steel wire is produced and shipped to Poland. The United States: • The 100 percent polyester multifilament yarn is coated with PVC and UV. • The 72 percent PVC and 28 percent polyester mesh fabric is woven and shipped to Poland in rolls. • The springs and zinc coated copper crimp are manufactured and shipped to Poland.
