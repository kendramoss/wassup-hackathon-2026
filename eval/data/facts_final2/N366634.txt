In your letter, dated November 2, 2026, you requested a binding tariff classification, country of origin, and applicability of trade programs ruling on wooden cleats. Product information and photos were submitted for our review. The product under consideration is wooden cleats. The cleats are used as a structural component in the manufacture of wooden crates. The cleats will be imported separately from the crates. The cleats are constructed of Gmelina wood (a tropical wood), and measure 11/16 inch high by 11/16 wide inch by 11 inches long. Photos show that the cleats are shaped diagonally on the ends (like a bevel) and have two 3/4 inch grooves across the width of the wood 3 1/2 inches from each cleat end. The cleats are not finished or coated with any material. In your letter, you suggest classification in subheadings 4415.10.9000 and 4407.29.0296, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS), which provide for wooden crates and wood sawn lengthwise, respectively. We disagree with both classifications.
