In your letter dated September 14, 2026, on behalf of your client Horizon Hobby, LLC, you requested a country of origin ruling. The merchandise under consideration is a radio control transmitter, model iXSR+, item number SPMR6425A, which is a handheld radio remote controller used to control radio-controlled toy cars and boats. This radio controller operates on a 2.4 GHz radio frequency. The subject radio control transmitter is comprised of approximately 100 components that are manufactured in various countries. The two main printed circuit board assemblies (PCBAs) are the Plano RF transmitter module and the MBA main computer PCBA. The Plano RF transmitter module PCBA is used for communication with the receiver of the toy car or boat. The MBA main computer PCBA is the main computer board that converts analog inputs into digital information to be transmitted by the Plano RF transmitter module. The Plano RF transmitter module and the MBA main computer PCBAs are produced in the United States using surface mount technology (SMT) and dual in-line package (DIP)/wave solder processes. These processes involve populating a printed circuit board (PCB) with various electrical and electronic components, including resistors, capacitors, fuses, and radio frequency (RF) chips. The PCBAs are then run through test fixtures to load U.S.-developed firmware, verify functionality, and calibrate RF performance. Model number iXSR+ also includes an Android System-on-Module (SOM) produced in China, which includes an Android touch screen for the user interface and a Wi-Fi/Bluetooth module. According to the information provided, the Android SOM provides the user interface to adjust parameters on the MBA and is not used in the transmission or reception of signals.
All non-Chinese components, including the Plano RF transmitter module PCBA and the MBA main computer PCBA, are shipped to China for final assembly. In China, the following assembly operations will occur to create a complete radio control transmitter: the plastic housing will be screwed and snapped together; the Chinese LCD screen will be screwed into the plastic housing and attached to cables; triggers and wheels will be screwed, pressed, and soldered with wire harnesses; cables and switches will be soldered and press-fit into the housing; the Plano RF transmitter module PCBA and the MBA main computer PCBA will be installed into the plastic housing; and additional software and firmware will be installed. In addition, the Chinese PCBAs for non-RF transmission/reception user control, i.e., throttle, touch panel user interface, and steering potentiometer mechanism, will be assembled into the complete product. Then, the battery and decals will be added, testing will occur, and the complete radio remote controller will be packaged and shipped to the United States.
