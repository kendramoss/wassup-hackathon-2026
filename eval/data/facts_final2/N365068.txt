SKU #11230 is a mattress pad designed to be placed on top of a mattress to provide additional comfort. It is comprised of an outer shell of 100 percent cotton woven fabric filled with nonwoven batting composed of 98 percent cotton and 2 percent other fibers. The shell fabric is bleached and dyed white. The top and bottom panels that make up the shell and the batting material are quilted together. The quilted mattress pad is designed to cover the top of a mattress and is secured by a fully elasticized skirt of 100 percent cotton jersey knit fabric, available with either an 11-inch or 18-inch depth. The product is packaged for retail sale under “The Company Store” brand in a reusable bag consisting of nonwoven fabric on the front, top, bottom, and sides of .5 mil polyvinyl chloride (PVC) on the back. The top of the bag features a zipper closure. The bag measures 14 x 16 x 5 ½ inches.
