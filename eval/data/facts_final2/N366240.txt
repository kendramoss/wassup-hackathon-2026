In your letter dated October 21, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on a ceramic wax warmer for purposes of current trade remedies. The product under consideration is described as a “Red Shed 7.5-inch Ceramic Rooster Wax Warmer” (hereinafter "the wax warmer"). The ceramic wax warmer is in the shape of a rooster. The item consists of a removable shallow ceramic bowl that sits on the ceramic rooster’s back to melt wax, along with a power cord. Wax cubes will be packaged with the warmer. The products will be imported into the United States in retail packaging for commercial sale. The submitted literature indicates that all components are produced in Vietnam, except for the heating elements, which are imported from China. The assembly process in Vietnam includes making the ceramic rooster and combining it with the imported heating elements to fabricate the finished wax warmers. Overall, this manufacturing process in Vietnam involves molding, slip casting, trimming, drying, bisque firing and glazing, glaze firing, metal stamping, spot welding, and powder coating. Finally, the finished products—the wax warmer and wax cubes—are packaged together for export. The Bill of Materials (BOM) demonstrates that the manufacturing operations in Vietnam contribute approximately 92% of the total cost of production and final value of the finished products.
