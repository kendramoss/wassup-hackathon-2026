In your letter dated October 14, 2026, you requested a ruling for classification and country of origin. There are two electrical aircraft power controllers under consideration with this request, identified as the PowerCube VR and the Engine Manager Pro. The PowerCube VR is an all-in-one system for electrical power conversion, distribution and control. In use, it receives electrical power from batteries or other direct current power sources, converts and distributes the power to a number of connected systems such as flight-control electronics, sensors, radio-control equipment, various peripheral devices and servo loads. The PowerBox Engine Manager Pro is an all-in-one device for electrical power conversion, switching, control and monitoring system for engine installations. In use, it receives, converts, distributes and controls electrical power for external engine ignition systems and other engine related equipment while also providing engine control, monitoring and communication functions. The manufacturing process for the PowerCube VR begins in Germany, where a printed circuit board assembly (PCBA) is produced. Globally sourced electronic components—such as diodes, microcontrollers, amplifiers, power inductors, and board connectors—are assembled onto a bare printed circuit board using surface-mount technology (SMT) and through-hole technology (THT). The PCBA is then programmed with firmware and integrated with additional components sourced from China or Germany into an aluminum housing to create the complete PowerCube VR module. Finally, the finished module is inspected, tested, and packaged for export to the United States. The manufacturing process for the Engine Manager Pro begins in China with the production of a printed circuit board assembly (PCBA). Globally sourced electronic components—such as capacitors, diodes, amplifiers, voltage regulators, inductors, connectors, and a microcontroller—are assembled onto a bare printed circuit board via SMT and THT. The PCBA is subsequently exported to Germany, where it is
programmed with firmware and combined with additional components sourced from China or Germany within an aluminum housing to complete the Engine Manager Pro module. The finished module is then inspected, tested, and packaged for export to the United States. Classification In your request, you suggest the correct heading for both units should be 8537.10.9170, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS). We agree.
