In your letter dated September 25, 2026, you requested a tariff classification ruling. Additional information was provided via email. The imported product is a high-pressure syringe set, model REF 200111.The complete syringe set is housed in a blister tray, which consists of a barrel, a plunger assembly, a coiled tube, a fill tube, a long spike and dust caps. The high-pressure syringe is intended to be used with a contrast media injector to deliver contrast media and/or saline within the area of Computed Tomography (CT), Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) and Digital Subtraction Angiography (DSA). As you state, the high-pressure syringe is for single use only. Additionally, it is intended for use by trained healthcare professionals including physicians, radiologists, nurses, and other qualified medical personnel, in hospitals, diagnostic imaging centers and other healthcare facilities.
