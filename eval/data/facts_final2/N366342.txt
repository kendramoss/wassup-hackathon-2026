In your letter dated October 23, 2026, submitted on behalf of GVA Lighting, Inc., you requested a country of origin ruling on LED lighting fixtures for the purpose of applying current trade remedies. The merchandise at issue consists of four models of LED lighting fixtures, identified as the STR9® SERIES, STR10™ SERIES, HIGHLIGHTER® SERIES, and FL™ SERIES. Manufacturing begins with the production of the LED PCBAs (Light-Emitting Diode Printed Circuit Board Assemblies) in the United States. The U.S. facility receives bare PCBs, discrete LEDs, and other electronic components from various countries; applies solder paste; places the LEDs and other surface-mount components using automated SMT (Surface Mount Technology) equipment; passes the assemblies through a reflow oven to form solder joints; inspects and electrically/functionally tests the completed PCBAs; and performs rework if required. Programming for all relevant PCBAs may be completed in the United States as well. The completed LED PCBAs are then shipped to Canada to be used in making the LED lighting fixtures. The U.S.-origin PCBAs are sent to Canada and combined with other components, such as housings, power supplies, and wires, to fabricate the finished products, which are subsequently imported into the United States. Depending on the model, the assembly operations in Canada typically include string assembly, wiring, optical installation, potting/sealing, LED-board installation, lens closure, labeling, and packing.
