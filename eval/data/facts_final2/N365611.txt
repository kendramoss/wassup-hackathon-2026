In your letter dated October 1, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on an air cleaning gun. Descriptive literature was provided for our review. The item under consideration is described as a Cyclone Air Cleaning gun (item number 1111200215), which is a handheld, pneumatically operated mechanical appliance designed for automotive detailing, interior deep cleaning, and debris removal. The device features an aluminum pistol-grip gun body equipped with a standard quick-connect air inlet plug that attaches to an external pressurized air compressor. The cyclone also features a specialized, hard-wearing plastic cone-shaped nozzle housing an internal, ultra-flexible high-velocity oscillating tube. When compressed air is released via the integrated manual trigger mechanism, the internal tube rotates at exceptionally high RPMs (creating a “cyclone” or “tornado” vortex), which dynamically pulses a powerful, spiraling jet stream of air. The air cleaning gun is made from components sourced from China and Malaysia. The Chinese components include the rubber extended blow tube, plastic pellets used to make the horn-shaped nozzle, valve core assembly, spring, metal trigger, trigger fixing pin, and male quick connect fitting. The Malaysian components include the aluminum ingots to make the gun body and the brass bar stock to make the tube connector. All of the components are then sent to Malaysia where the aluminum ingots are melted and diecast into semi-finished components. The components are then machined, including drilling and threading to form the finished gun body. Additionally, the tube connector and male quick connect fitting are CNC machined, drilled, grooved, deburred and inspected to form the finished components. Finally, the horn-shaped nozzle is made by injection molding the plastic pellets into the finished component. Once the metal and plastic components are made, they are then assembled with the remaining Chinese components to form the complete Cyclone Air Cleaning gun.
