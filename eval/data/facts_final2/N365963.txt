In your letter dated October 12, 2026, on behalf of VYV, Inc., you requested a country of origin ruling on LED lighting fixtures for purposes of applying current trade remedies. The merchandise at issue consists of three models of LED lighting fixtures identified as Commercial Models Made Primarily of Metal, Life Sciences Models, and Commercial Models Made Primarily of Plastic. You indicate that the LED PCBAs (Printed Circuit Board Assemblies)—or the light sources—each consist of a metal-core printed circuit board that has the circuitry required to power an array of LEDs. The LED array is designed and positioned on the PCBA based on Vyv’s proprietary methods and know-how for delivering the required antimicrobial dosage to the space being protected. LED PCBAs and associated control components are manufactured by and sourced from qualified suppliers using the antimicrobial LEDs supplied by Vyv. All LED PCBA manufacturing is done in the United States via Surface Mount Technology (SMT). You explain further that the LED PCBAs, which include the circuitry and LED diodes (light sources), are manufactured in the United States using SMT operations. The LED drivers are produced in Mexico, the United States, or China, and will be programmed in Canada or the United States. In the presented manufacturing scenario, the US-origin PCBAs will be sent to Canada, combined with the components made in Canada to fabricate the finished products, and then imported into the United States. The LED lighting fixtures assembly operations in Canada, depending on the specific models, primarily include the following: 1. Lighting Fixture Housing Manufacture Sheet metal (cold-rolled steel, stainless steel, or aluminum of various gauges) is fabricated into fixture housings, reflectors, brackets, door frames, and associated structural components.
2. Lens Manufacture Raw lens material, consisting primarily of acrylic or polycarbonate sheets of US origin, is fabricated through cutting and forming operations. 3. Powder Coating and Finishing Housings and associated metal components are cleaned, pre-treated, powder-coated, cured, and visually inspected. Material is of Canadian origin. 4. Housing Sub-Assembly Reflectors, mounting plates, hardware, brackets, and retainers manufactured in step 1, along with other mechanical components, are assembled to create the complete fixture housing assembly. 5. Driver Installation LED drivers obtained from qualified suppliers are mounted within the fixture housing. In certain configurations, driver output parameters may be configured in Canada prior to installation. Such configuration does not alter the circuitry, manufacture, or fundamental function of the driver. 6. LED PCBAs Installation The imported LED PCBAs are secured to the fixture housing using screws, rivets, clips, or other fastening methods. The LED PCBAs remain fully identifiable as the light-generating assemblies before and after installation into the fixture. No component population, SMT assembly, solder reflow, electronic manufacturing, or modification of the LED PCBA occurs in Canada. 7. Controls Integration Depending upon customer requirements, additional devices may be installed and configured, including dimming controls, occupancy sensors, daylight harvesting sensors, wireless communication modules, emergency battery packs, and control interfaces. 8. Wiring and Interconnection Wiring harnesses and electrical interconnections are completed between the LED driver, LED PCBAs, controls, and sensors using wiring of suitable gauge, terminal blocks, or suitable connectors, as required by the fixture design. 9. Optical Assembly Lenses, diffusers, reflectors, louvers, shields, and other optical components are installed and secured. 10. Final Assembly and Testing
