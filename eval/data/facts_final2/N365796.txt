In your letter dated October 6, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on insulated gate bi-polar transistors (IGBT). The merchandise under consideration is identified as a 1200 V/75 A FieldStop Trench Power IGBT, part number: AMBQ40T120RRATH, which is a discrete semiconductor device. This item provides high-speed switching, low power loss, high input impedance, short-circuit withstand capability, and PTC (Positive Temperature Coefficient) Heater application in electric vehicles and hybrid vehicles. In your submission, you state that the front-end manufacturing process takes place in South Korea which includes the following processes. Coating Exposure Develop Dry Etch Wet Etch Photoresist Strip Oxidation / Diffusion LPCVD Ion Implant Chemical Vapor Desposition Physical Vapor Desposition Wafer Basckside Process Probe Test
You state that the front-end processing forms the structure and establishes the electrical characteristics, including the switching functionality of the Power IGBT. All wafer fabrication processes are performed in South Korea to form the entire electrical circuit that defines the function of the IGBT dies. The back-end manufacturing process that takes place in China includes: Wafer Saw Die Attach Wire Bond Mold Laser Mark Package Singulation Packing and Shipping The back-end assembly process in China is limited to cutting the fabricated wafer into individual dies and packaging them using several steps, including die attach, wire bonding, molding or encapsulation, marking, and final inspection. You state that the back-end process does not redesign the semiconductor structure or alter the core electrical switching function which was already established through the front-end fabrication.
