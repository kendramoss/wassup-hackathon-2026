In your letter dated November 8, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on metal furniture. In lieu of samples, technical and illustrative literature, a product description, and a manufacturing synopsis were provided for review. The “3-Piece Bistro Set (SKU# 6124187)” is a pair of powder coated iron metal folding chairs, and a powder coated iron metal folding table. Interlacing polyethylene (PE) imitation rattan wicker is present throughout the metal frame of the chairs and the table and forms the seat base, the seat backrest, and the tabletop surface. The chairs are not upholstered, and their dimensions are approximately 22" wide, 18" deep, and 33" tall. The table dimensions are approximately 23" long, 23" wide, and 28" tall. The ruling request outlines an operation wherein PE rattan and plastic boards will be sourced in China. The material components will be shipped and exported to Cambodia. In Cambodia, iron components (left, right, front, and rear seat rails, vertical and folding table frame rails, square rails, brackets, angles, cross members, rivets, washers, snap clips, hex sockets, screws, lock nuts), plastic components (washers, sleeves, foot glides, tube plugs), foam sheets, and cardboard will be locally sourced. The manufacturing process undertaken in Cambodia involves (1) the metal rail components are cut to length using a metal laser tube cutting machine and bundled together according to length, (2) the metal rail components are then bent and welded together using a bending machine and a robotic welding machine to form the seat legs, seat foundation rails, backrest rails, square table rails, and the table legs, (3) the welded metal components are then trimmed, cut, and deburred of rough edges using handheld rotary griding and polishing tools, (4) the polished table and chair frame rails are painted and powder-coated using electrophoresis, spray machines, and are then hung to dry, (5) workers hand weave and cut the polyethylene rattan wicker strands onto the metal frame seat foundation surface, the seat backrest, and the tabletop surface using hand tools, (6) a mechanical riveting machine is used to press rivets at specific intervals into the metal frame rails and joints of the seat and the metal frame rails and joints of the table, (7) workers assemble the seat legs to the seat foundation and backrest using
handheld tools and assembly hardware, (8) workers assemble the folding table legs to the tabletop surface using handheld tools and the assembly hardware, and (9) workers install washers, rivets and endcaps to the seat and the table legs.
