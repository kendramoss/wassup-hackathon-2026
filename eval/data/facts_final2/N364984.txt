This is in response to your letter dated September 10, 2026. You requested a ruling on whether the proposed marking, "DESIGNED IN THE NETHERLANDS / CRAFTED IN CHINA" on the outside carton, is an acceptable country of origin marking for the imported goods. A photograph of the marked sample was submitted with your letter for review. The product is the Extensa E25 decorative cast-iron outdoor lamp kit from China. You inquire whether the individual product inside the carton may remain unmarked when the retail carton bears the proposed label. The proposal describes a 120 × 60 mm matte polyester label with permanent pressure-sensitive adhesive. It is printed with "DESIGNED IN THE NETHERLANDS" on one line and "CRAFTED IN CHINA" directly underneath. The two lines use the same font style and size. The label will be positioned clear of straps, edges, tape, and carrier labels, and will be affixed directly to the flat side panel of the carton. No separate country of origin marking is proposed for the E25 lamp kit inside the carton. The marked carton, which is tightly bound and reinforced with heavy-duty strapping bands running vertically and horizontally around the exterior, will remain intact until delivery to the ultimate U.S. purchaser.
