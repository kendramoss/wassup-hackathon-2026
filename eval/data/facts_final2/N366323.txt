In your letter dated October 23, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on metal wall décor. Photographs and descriptions of the merchandise and manufacturing process were included in your inquiry. The article under consideration is described as the RS 48-Inch Metal Star Décor, SKU# 6107806. It is a decorative metal ornament in the shape of a five-pointed star. The star is painted a cream color with floral leaf patterns painted in navy blue. It includes a built-in hanging loop on the top so the consumer can hang it on a door, wall, window, fireplace mantel, or the like. The star measures 48 inches wide, 5.1 inches deep, and 46.5 inches tall. You state this item is intended for indoor or covered outdoor use in the home. You describe a production process in which the components of the hanging décor are stamped out of iron, punched, welded together, powder coated, and painted in Vietnam. Those components, along with an iron S-hook and chain and a steel screw from China, are then assembled into the finished hanging metal star décor and then packed for shipping in Vietnam.
