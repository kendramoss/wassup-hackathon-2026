In your letter dated October 14, 2026, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of your client, Eagle Carports. The merchandise under consideration is described as a solar corrugated metal roof which is identified by model number AP-PVROOF-724. The solar roof panels consist of crystalline silicon solar cells laminated directly to the corrugated metal sheet with a junction box affixed with interconnection cables, designed to serve as a standalone roof or to be installed on top of an existing roof structure. The solar metal roof measures 86.61 x 20.40 inches with maximum power output of 175 Watts. We note that the subject solar roof panels are not imported with inverters, optimizers or any similar power managing devices.
