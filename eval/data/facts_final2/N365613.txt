In your letter dated October 1, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on a pencil tire gauge. Descriptive literature was provided for our review. The item under consideration is described as a pencil tire gauge (item number 1121200011), which is a mechanical instrument designed to measure and indicate the internal air pressure of pneumatic tires. The item is imported as a complete, fully assembled unit and has a chrome-plated stainless steel outer sleeve tube, a PA66 (polyamide 66) air chuck head, a PA66 plastic indicator scale rod, and a stainless-steel pocket clip. Internally, the gauge houses a rubber O-ring, a rubber piston/stopper, a stainless-steel compression spring, and a PA66 piston guide sleeve. In operation, the PA66 air chuck head is pressed against a tire valve stem, allowing pressurized air to enter the tube and force the rubber piston against the stainless-steel compression spring. This mechanical action expels the graduated scale rod outward from the base of the sleeve tube, providing a visible, calibrated pressure reading. The device measures 16.8mm wide by 140mm long. The pencil tire gauge is made from components sourced from China. The components include the O-ring, rubber piston, scale rod, scale guide sleeve, pocket clip, PA66 plastic pellets (used to make the air chuck head) and stainless steel (used to make the spring and outer sleeve). All of the Chinese components are then sent to Malaysia where the compression spring and outer sleeve are CNC machined. Additionally, the PA66 plastic is injection molded, chrome plated, and inspected to form the finished air chuck head. Once the metal and plastic components are made, they are then assembled with the remaining Chinese components to form the complete pencil tire gauge.
