In your letter dated October 2, 2026, you requested a tariff classification ruling on notebooks and sketchbooks. Product information, photographs, and samples were submitted for our review. The first item under consideration is item code 2409059, “Angela Harding: Shetland Otter and Windsong Notebook (FTPA43).” The softbound notebook features decorative embossed polyurethane covers and contains 176 lined pages. It is equipped with two ribbon page markers. The pages have rounded edges and measure approximately 148 mm x 210 mm (5.83” x 8.27”) at their longest and widest points. You indicate this notebook is manufactured in China. The second item under consideration is item code 2309052, “Claude Monet: Bridge Over a Pond of Water Lilies Quarto Journal (FTQNB).” The notebook features decorative hardbound paperboard covers and contains 128 lined pages with two ribbon page markers and a magnetic flap closure. The pages measure approximately 180 mm x 245 mm (7.09” x 9.65”). You indicate this notebook is manufactured in China. The third item under consideration is item code 2210213, “Vincent Van Gogh: Starry Night Sketch Book (FTSB070).” The sketchbook features decorative hardbound paperboard covers and contains 144 blank pages with two ribbon page markers. The pages measure approximately 215 mm x 277 mm (8.46” x 10.91”). The binding is section-sewn. You indicate this sketchbook is manufactured in China. The fourth item under consideration is item code 2501109, “Butterfly Sketch Book.” The sketchbook features decorative softbound paperboard covers and contains 176 blank pages with one ribbon page marker. The pages have rounded edges and measure approximately 210 mm x 265 mm (8.27” x 10.43”) at their longest and widest points. The binding is section-sewn. You indicate this sketchbook is manufactured in Malaysia.
For item code 2409059, you proposed classification in subheading 4820.10.2060, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS). For item code 2309052, you proposed classification in subheading 4820.10.2040, HTSUS. We agree.
