In your letter dated September 23, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on helical coil springs on behalf of your client, Silgan Dispensing Systems Corporation. Informational material was submitted with your request. The products under consideration are helical coil springs. The springs are made from steel wire supplied by various vendors located in China, the European Union, India, South Korea, and Vietnam. Upon importation, the wire is shipped to a manufacturing facility in the United States where the wire is converted into springs. At this facility, the imported wire is fed through a machine which winds the wire around a mandrel forming springs and then cuts them at their required length. The newly cut springs are then sent to an oven to undergo heat treatment, improving durability and reducing internal strain from the coiling process. The springs are then rinsed and cleaned through a series of chemical and hot water baths. Lastly, the springs are allowed to cool and dry resulting in the final product, to be packed and shipped.
