In your letter dated September 1, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on tris(1,3-dichloro-2-propyl)phosphate (TDCP), CAS No. 13674-87-8. The subject product, tris(1,3-dichloro-2-propyl)phosphate (TDCP), CAS No. 13674-87-8, is also known as TDCPP. You indicate that it is primarily used as an additive flame retardant in flexible and rigid polyurethane foams. You provided documentation describing the manufacturing process in India. The TDCP is produced through a controlled reaction of phosphoryl oxychloride with epichlorohydrin, both sourced from India, using a proprietary catalyst. After the reaction is complete, the crude product is purified through a series of washing and separation steps.
