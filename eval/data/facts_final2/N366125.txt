In your letter dated October 15, 2026, you requested a tariff classification, country of origin, and USMCA applicability determination ruling. Descriptive literature was provided for our review. The item under consideration is identified as the KRAKENmax LoRaWAN Ultrasonic Clamp-On Flow Meter, which is an electronic instrument designed to measure, monitor, and record the volumetric flow rate of liquids within a pipe without disrupting the fluid system. The device utilizes transit-time ultrasonic technology, employing paired external transducers that clamp directly onto the exterior of a pipe (copper, PVC, stainless steel, carbon steel) to transmit and receive acoustic signals through the liquid medium. The differential travel time of these ultrasonic waves is processed internally to calculate flow velocity and volume. The unit features an integrated LoRaWAN (Long Range Wide Area Network) wireless communication module, enabling long-range, low-power telemetry of flow data to a centralized gateway or cloud network. It is enclosed in a weather-resistant housing suitable for industrial, municipal, and agricultural water management applications, and is powered by a 12 V DC adapter. The KRAKENmax LoRaWAN Ultrasonic Clamp-On Flow Meter is made from components predominately sourced from China. The first component is the ultrasonic flow meter (model D116, RS485 version), of Chinese origin, which measures and displays the flow on its integrated LCD. It can output the data by wire (RS485/Modbus, analog/pulse) but has no wireless capability. The second component is also sourced from China and is identified as the Milesight UC100 LoRaWAN controller, which is a generic RS485-to-LoRaWAN converter. The 12 V DC power adapter, cables, connectors and labels complete the component list and are sourced from various countries. Components are shipped to Canada, where the assembly process takes place. Initially, client pipe specifications (including material, outside diameter, wall thickness, lining, and fluid type) are obtained and programmed into the flow meter, along with the transducer mounting method and spacing, ensuring the unit
is prepared for clamp-on installation at the client’s site. Next, the meter is inspected and bench-tested prior to hardware integration. This integration involves incorporating the power supply, wiring the meter’s RS485 output to the UC100, and mounting the UC100 inside the meter enclosure. Following the completion of core hardware assembly, the UC100 is configured with SenseCore’s device profile (comprising the Modbus register map, polling interval, scaling, units, and payload format). Additionally, personnel provision the LoRaWAN credentials and the US915 frequency plan, load SenseCore’s payload decoder, and register the device on the corresponding network and platform. Finally, the finished units undergo final testing, labeling, and packaging for importation into the United States. CLASSIFICATION
