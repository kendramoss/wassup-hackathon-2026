In your letter, dated September 24, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling for your client, Ningbo Feihong Stationery Limited Corporation. The request was for wrapping paper. Product information was submitted for our review. The product under consideration is wrapping paper. You outline a scenario wherein the base paper is fully manufactured in Germany and the paper is printed and embossed in China. Pulp is processed, pulp is manufactured into sheet paper, the paper is coated, surface sized, and calendered, and the paper is finished in Germany. The master rolls are then transported to China, where they are printed with designs, embossed, cut to size, and re-wound and packaged.
