In your letter dated September 27, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling. The item under consideration in this request is an automotive headlight switch assembly, model number KD-2002, which is a multi-function switch assembly used to control a vehicle’s headlights and instrument illumination intensity level. The automotive headlight switch assembly is installed in close proximity to the driver’s seating position in a vehicle for the purpose of adjusting the vehicle’s instrument panel illumination intensity level and selecting the exterior lighting such as headlights OFF, headlights ON, headlights AUTO ON/OFF, and parking lamps only ON. In Thailand, electronic components such as resistors, diodes, and LEDs are assembled onto a bare printed circuit board via surface mount technology (SMT). In Thailand, with components sourced from China and Thailand, the PCBA is further assembled into a plastic housing with other switch components including electrical contacts, springs, and mechanical components such as a Rotary Knob and Rotary Wiper Contacts to make a complete automotive headlight switch assembly. The finished assembly is inspected, tested, and packaged for export to the United States.
