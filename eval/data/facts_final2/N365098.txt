In your letter dated September 16, 2026, you requested a country of origin and a marking ruling on nonalloy steel API 5CT oil country tubular goods (“OCTGs”) and nonalloy steel OCTGs imported with nonalloy steel couplings on behalf of your client, Deep Oilfield Pipe Manufacturing (Cambodia) Co., Ltd. Photographs of the proposed marking were included in your inquiry. The products under consideration are nonalloy steel API5CT OCTGs and nonalloy steel OCTGs imported with nonalloy steel couplings for use in oil and gas drilling and production operations. According to your submission, the OCTGs and couplings will be imported into the United States (U.S.) in two different scenarios: 1) OCTGs, specifically electric resistance welded (“ERW”) steel casing and tubing, imported without couplings, and 2) OCTGs imported with Cambodian manufactured nonalloy steel couplings preassembled. In the first scenario, nonalloy steel coils of Chinese, South Korean, or Malaysian origin are sent to Cambodia where they are slit, straightened, leveled, formed, and electric-resistance welded into tubes. These ERW tubes are then heat treated, sized, straightened, inspected, tested, etc., prior to importation into the U.S. The tubes are manufactured to API specifications. In the second scenario, nonalloy steel coils of Chinese, South Korean, or Malaysian origin are exported to Cambodia where they are slit, straightened, leveled, and welded into tubes. These ERW tubes are then upset and heat treated on both ends, machined, and threaded. Next, an API 5CT coupling of Cambodian origin that is manufactured from unfinished Chinese origin coupling stock that is cut to short lengths, internally and externally turned, beveled, and threaded, is installed on the threaded tube ends. Finally, the OCTGs and coupling are painted, marked, packed, and subsequently imported into the U.S.
