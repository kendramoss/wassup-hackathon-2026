In your letter dated September 30, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on a decorative iron garden stake. Photographs and descriptions of the merchandise and manufacturing process were included in your inquiry. The products under consideration are described as RS 21.5-Inch Solar Tulip Stakes, SKU # 6112176, 6109770, and 6109771. Each style is a decorative garden stake made of iron with a powder coated finish, and a solar powered light. Each style is shaped like a flower stem with two tulips and two leaves. The different styles represent different color combinations of flowers. Each garden stake measures approximately 7 inches long, 3 inches wide, and 20.5 inches high. You state these garden stakes are intended for indoor or outdoor use. You describe a production process in which the components of the garden stake are stamped out of iron, welded together, polished, and powder coated in Cambodia. Those components, along with iron wire, iron tube, and solar lights from China, are then assembled into the finished garden stake and then packed for shipping in Cambodia.
