In your letter dated September 18, 2026, you requested a ruling regarding tariff classification, country of origin, and eligibility for preferential treatment under the USMCA. Images were provided in lieu of a sample. The product under consideration is described as a large wreath board. It is a purpose-designed, reusable wreath-making and craft device. It is circular, approximately 15.5 inches in diameter, and is made wholly of polypropylene plastic. This wreath board is used in the construction of decorative wreaths, floral designs, and craft arrangements. It contains multiple concentric molded working rows with strategically positioned attachment holes. The rows are molded with numbers and guides to assist the user in selecting attachment locations and consistently positioning decorative materials. Zip ties or other fastening materials are inserted through the predetermined holes to attach decorative mesh, ribbon, fabric, floral materials, and other wreathmaking components. As the large wreath board would be considered an article of plastic, and as it is not more specifically provided for elsewhere, the applicable subheading will be 3926.90.9989, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS), which provides for “[o]ther articles of plastics and articles of other materials of headings 3901 to 3914: [o]ther: [o]ther: [o]ther.” The column one, general rate of duty is 5.3 percent ad valorem. The duties cited above are current as of this ruling’s issuance. Duty rates are provided for your convenience and are subject to change. The text of the most recent HTSUS and the accompanying duty rates are provided at https://hts.usitc.gov/.
Your submission states this large wreath board is injection molded and finished in Canada. The polypropylene plastic resin used in the manufacturing process will be sourced from the United States.
