In your letter, dated October 2, 2026, you requested a binding classification, country of origin, and application of trade programs ruling on paper artwork. Information and photos were submitted for our review. You have submitted two pieces of artwork for our review. The first is artwork constructed of folded pieces of uncoated paper presented in a three-dimensional format. The folded pieces of paper are cut, folded, and glued to a rigid plastic backing. The second is artwork constructed of multiple varieties of paper strips that are interwoven into sheets, cut to shape, and combined by stacking and adhering. The interwoven strips are not mounted on any kind of backing.
