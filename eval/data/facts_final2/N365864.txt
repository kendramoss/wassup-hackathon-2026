In your letter dated October 7, 2026, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of your client, California Costume, Inc. You submitted a sample of a child size costume identified as Hello Kitty Bubble Dress, item numbers 3027-035 and WMT3027-035, which consist of a hooded long-sleeved dress made of man-made knitted textile fabric. The permanently attached hood and the back of the dress has a deep, unfinished back opening with raw, unfinished edges. The back opening is closed with a single small tab of hook and loop fastener. The top short sleeve and the bottom long sleeve both have large, visible, loose overlock stitching. The flimsy half elastic waist consists of a strip of exposed elastic stitched directly to the back of the dress with one row of long, loose straight stitching. Although the dress has a two-ply hem with a bubble bottom and large, visible, loose overlock stitching on top, the dress is not well-made. The dress is stitched together with a single line of long, loose straight stitching.
