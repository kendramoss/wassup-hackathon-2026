In your letter dated October 9, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling. The merchandise under consideration is identified as the Geniuso V501 Interactive Talking Pen. The subject device is comprised of an optical sensor, a printed circuit board assembly (PCBA), a speaker, and a rechargeable battery within an enclosure in the shape of a handheld pen. The device is designed to interact with specially encoded books, cards, and other educational materials. When the user passes the optical sensor over the material, the information is scanned and triggers audio, music, games, and other interactive educational tools. We note that the printed material is not at issue with this request. In your request, you state that the manufacturing process begins in China where the PCBA is manufactured along with the optical sensor, speaker, microphone, and other electrical components. These components are then installed within the housing which constitutes the final physical assembly. The assembled pen is then sent to Slovakia where the product specific software is developed and installed. This includes the addition of the interactive educational content.
