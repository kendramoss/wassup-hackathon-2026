In your letter, dated November 5, 2026, you requested a binding tariff classification ruling on origami artwork. Product information and photos were submitted for our review. The product under consideration is origami artwork. Based on a photo provided by the customer, an origami image is made from cut and folded paper. The example submitted is an origami rendition of a bride and groom at a wedding. The composition is framed and there is a printed background in the frame. You outline a scenario wherein Chinese-origin paper is cut, folded, designed, and assembled in Canada. In your letter, you suggest classification of the origami artwork in subheading 9701.99.0000 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS), which provides for “Paintings, drawings and pastels, executed entirely by hand, other than drawings of heading 4906 and other than hand-painted or hand-decorated manufactured articles; collages, mosaics and similar decorative plaques: Other: Other.” We disagree. A Chapter 97 “work of art” must be a work of the free fine arts, rather than the decorative or industrial arts. (See Headquarters Rulings HQ 087577, HQ 089094, and HQ 088792). The origami piece is more specifically provided for elsewhere in the tariff schedule.
