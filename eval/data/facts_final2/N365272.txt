In your letter dated September 21, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on Tetracaine Hydrochloride Ophthalmic Solution, USP 0.5% in dosage form. Tetracaine Hydrochloride Ophthalmic Solution is a medicinal preparation containing Tetracaine Hydrochloride, an ester-type local anesthetic, as the active ingredient. It is indicated for procedures requiring a rapid and short-acting topical ophthalmic anesthetic. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Tetracaine Hydrochloride, is manufactured in Switzerland and that the finished solution in dosage form is made in India. In India, Tetracaine Hydrochloride, will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Tetracaine Hydrochloride Ophthalmic Solution in dosage form.
