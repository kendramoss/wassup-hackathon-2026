In your letter dated September 23, 2026, on behalf of your client, Jiangmen Lingfeng Electric Appliances Manufacturing Co., Ltd., you requested a country of origin ruling on electric stove heaters for purposes of applying current trade remedies. The merchandise under consideration is referred to as Electric Stove Heater Model H3D-B15 (the “Stove Heater”). Model H3D-B15 is an electric stove heater designed to provide heated airflow while presenting the appearance of a traditional stove/fireplace through a decorative illuminated log assembly. The heating function is produced through the coordinated operation of a resistance heating element, electric motor, and wind-turbine/fan assembly. The heating element generates heat, while the motor drives the wind-turbine assembly to move air through the heating system and distribute the heated airflow. The Thailand-manufactured PCBA provides the appliance's electronic control functions, including temperature and heating control, fan interlock, user-interface functions, safety monitoring and protection, flame-effect control, and power management. These functional components are incorporated into a fabricated metal housing together with the front door, decorative illuminated log assembly, wiring, safety components, and related hardware. The Stove Heater is manufactured in Thailand from components and materials originating in Thailand, Vietnam, and China. The resistance heating element is manufactured in Vietnam. In Thailand, however, significant motor manufacturing and assembly occur; the heating element is incorporated into the heating system and integrated with the motor and wind-turbine assembly; the metal housing is fabricated; and the various electrical, structural, heating, and decorative components are assembled into the finished appliance. The completed Stove Heater is also tested and inspected in Thailand. You describe the manufacturing process in Thailand as follows:
A. Motor Manufacturing The motor is not imported into Thailand as a completed foreign-origin motor. In Thailand, lacquered wire and related inputs are used to manufacture the coil assembly through coil winding and wiring, lead-wire preparation and soldering, insulation, and performance testing. The completed coil assembly is then inserted into a China-origin stator. A China-origin rotor with shaft and gear housing are subsequently assembled with the stator/coil assembly, and the motor components are fastened together. The completed motor undergoes electrical and performance testing in Thailand, including hi-pot, noise, current, leakage-current, and low-voltage startup testing. Thus, although the stator and rotor with shaft originate in China, significant motor manufacturing and assembly operations occur in Thailand before the motor is incorporated into the Stove Heater. B. Heating and Wind-Turbine Assembly The resistance heating element itself is manufactured in Vietnam and shipped to Thailand. The completed heating assembly is not manufactured in Vietnam. In Thailand, the imported heating element is installed into the heating box to form the heating assembly. That assembly is then installed into the wind-turbine assembly, and the motor is incorporated into the combined heating and airflow system. The resulting assembly undergoes performance testing and inspection in Thailand. Thus, although the resistance heating element originates in Vietnam, the complete forced-air heating system is created through the integration of the heating element, motor, and wind-turbine assembly in Thailand. C. Metal Housing and Final Assembly The metal housing is fabricated in Thailand from coated sheet through multiple punching, trimming, deburring, hemming, bending, and forming operations. The functional and structural systems are then integrated into the finished Stove Heater in Thailand. The Thai operations include assembly of the housing, switches and circuit board, installation of the heating/motor/wind-turbine system, assembly of the front door and rear board, and final product integration. The finished unit undergoes aging, performance testing, appearance inspection, packaging, and storage in Thailand. The remaining identified China-origin components include the power cord, switch, lead wires, flame-effect lighting assembly, screws, and nuts.
