In your letter dated October 23, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on a decorative iron windmill. Photographs and descriptions of the merchandise and manufacturing process were included in your inquiry. The article under consideration is described as an RS 5ft Goose Windmill, SKU# 6111018. It is a freestanding decorative iron windmill intended for use on the porch or in the garden. It consists of spinning windmill blades and a large tail on top of a tall tower. Two large decorative metal geese, two small flowers, and some tufts of grass are positioned between the blades and the tail. The windmill measures 31.89 inches x 18.11 inches x 60.24 inches, and weighs 14.7 lb. It can be secured into the ground with included ground stakes. You describe a production process in which several components including the decorative geese, flowers, and tufts of grass are stamped out of iron in China. Those components are shipped to Vietnam where they are incorporated with the remaining components that are made in Vietnam including the windmill frame, blades, and tail. In Vietnam, the pieces are welded together, polished, powder coated, spray painted, hand painted and then assembled into the finished article. It is then packed for shipping.
