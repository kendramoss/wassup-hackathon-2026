In your letter dated October 19, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on an air purifier. Descriptive literature was provided for our review. The item under consideration is described as the Champ air purifier, which is a cylindrical electronic home appliance designed to remove various airborne pollutants, including fine dust, harmful gases, and bacteria, from indoor air. The Champ uses a 3-in-1 custom filter (pre-filter, HEPA filter, and activated carbon) to create a cleaner indoor environment. The device measures 12 inches by12 inches by 23 inches and weighs 17 pounds. It is designed for room sizes up to 546 square feet. The air purifier is made of components sourced from China, South Korea and Vietnam. The Chinese components include the bonding, particle sensor wire harness, SMPS wire harness, magnetic cover cistern, motor, power cord assembly and particle sensor. The South Korean components include the motor guide cover, filament tape, film, insert for the fan, label, acrylonitrile butadiene styrene (LG ABS), mesh, nut, polypropylene (PP), screw and washer. The Vietnamese components include the box, lower and upper cushions, stickers, foam for the particle sensor, manual, main key module printed board assembly (PBA), power module PBA, slip sheet, switch, filter assembly, and vinyl. The manufacturing of the air purifier occurs in Vietnam and starts with the injection molding of the plastic components. Prior to assembly, the power PBA, top cover assembly, and particle cover are pre-assembled. The semi-finished good is assembled by installing the frame, power cord, mesh, upper housing, dust sensor, reed switch, motor, and fan. Following this, the motor and fan assembly and the power PBA assembly are installed, and the connectors, power PBA cover, guide flow, side cover, and cover top assembly are integrated into the main body. The specification sticker, front cover, rear cover, duct cover, particle cover, cylindrical filter, and filter cover are then installed. Finally, the finished air purifier is tested, inspected, cleaned, and packaged for retail sale.
