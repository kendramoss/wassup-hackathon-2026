In your letter dated October 1, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on air tool filters. Descriptive literature was provided for our review. The first item under consideration is described as a 1/4-inch mini air tool filter (item number 1111200196), which is a compact, point-of-use inline air filtration device specifically designed to attach directly to pneumatic tools (such as impact wrenches, grinders, and spray guns) or air lines via standard 1/4-inch NPT (National Pipe Thread) inlet and outlet ports. Constructed with a lightweight, impact-resistant clear polycarbonate housing, an aluminum alloy cap, and a porous brass element, the unit is designed to trap airborne solid contaminants, such as pipe scale, rust, and dust, and separate moisture, oil, or condensed water from compressed air before it enters the air tool. As compressed air passes through the filter element, particulates are retained, and separated liquid collects at the bottom of the transparent bowl ready to be drained by the valve. The second item under consideration is described as a compact 3/8-inch air filter (item number 1111200194), which is a compact, point-of-use inline air-filtering apparatus designed for installation within pneumatic lines and compressed air systems. It features a 3/8-inch female NPT inlet and outlet porting arrangement to channel air streams directly through a high-efficiency filtration matrix, utilizing a bronze filter element. The filter housing is constructed of an aluminum alloy valve body paired with a transparent polycarbonate bowl and integrated guard, allowing immediate visibility of captured moisture and debris. Operating under pressures ranging between 0 and 150 PSI, the device functions mechanically to intercept, coalesce, and isolate moisture, oil vapor, dust, and rust particles from the compressed air flow before it reaches downstream air tools or pneumatic machinery. The unit is equipped with a standard drain valve at the base of the reservoir cup to evacuate gathered contaminants periodically.
The 1/4-inch mini air tool filter is made from components sourced from China and Malaysia. The Chinese components include the aluminum machining blank (to make the valve body), O-ring, locking collar, filter element, plastic base, screws, springs, retaining ring, filter bowl, and mounting bracket. The Malaysian component is the brass round bar used to make the drain valve core and drain valve seat. The 3/8-inch air filter is made from components sourced from China and Malaysia. The Chinese components include the aluminum machining blank (to make the valve body), O-ring, locking collar, filter element, plastic base, filter bowl, metal protective cover, springs, retaining rings, screws, mounting bracket and locking nut. The Malaysian component includes the brass round bar used to make the drain valve seat, drain valve core and hex lock nut. For both filters, the Chinese components are then sent to Malaysia. In Malaysia, the metal components are constructed using a CNC machining process that starts with precision drilling and deburring and finishes with a final inspection. Once the metal components are made, they are then assembled with the remaining Chinese components to form the complete 1/4-inch mini air tool filter and 3/8-inch air filter.
