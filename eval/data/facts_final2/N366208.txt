In your letter dated October 19, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on metal wall décor. Photographs and descriptions of the merchandise and manufacturing process were included in your inquiry. The article under consideration is described as the RS 24.5-Inch Bow Décor, SKU# 6107797. It is decorative metal ornament in the shape of a bow and painted in a red, white, and blue stars and stripes pattern. It includes a built-in hanging loop on the top so the consumer can it on a door, wall, window, or the like. The bow measures 17.1 inches wide, 2.95 inches deep, and 24 inches long. You state this item is intended for indoor or covered outdoor use in the home as a seasonal decoration. You describe a production process in which the components of the hanging décor are stamped out of iron, welded together, powder coated, and painted in Vietnam. Those components, along with a triangle hook and screw from China, are then assembled into the finished hanging bow décor and then packed for shipping in Vietnam.
