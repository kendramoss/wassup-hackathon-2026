In your letter dated October 1, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on an air pressure regulator. The product at issue is referred to as a 3/8-IN Compressed Air Pressure Regulator, item number 1111200200. The regulator features an adjusting knob and a pressure gauge positioned in front of a valve. The valve consists of an aluminum valve body that incorporates a valve core assembly, springs, an O-ring, a diaphragm, a valve seat and a bolt. Once connected to a compressed air system, the regulator functions as a valve that stabilizes incoming air pressure to an adjustable constant output pressure for downstream pneumatic tools and equipment. With respect to origin, the final assembly of the regulator occurs in Malaysia using components from China and Malaysia. Prior to the final assembly, the valve body and the adjusting knob are manufactured in China. The valve body is exported to Malaysia to undergo a machining process to add threading. Less than three percent of material is removed during the machining process. The final assembly process begins by inserting a mounting bracket and a Chinese lock nut on a valve body. Afterwards, a Chinese spring and diaphragm are installed onto a Chinese valve seat that is subsequently inserted into the valve body. The process continues by placing a knob over the valve seat. A pressure regulating spring is then installed onto the valve core assembly, which is followed by the placement of an O-ring. Finally, an adjusting knob and a pressure gauge are then screwed onto the valve body. Once complete, the air regulator is tested, inspected and packaged ready for shipment.
