In your letter dated September 30, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on a decorative garden stake. Photographs and descriptions of the merchandise and manufacturing process were included in your inquiry. The item under consideration is described as a 17-inch Duck with Bandana, SKU# 6109233. It is a decorative garden stake consisting of a duck made of stamped iron, a two-pronged rod that stakes into the ground, and solar powered lights. The duck's body is painted white and has a cutwork feather-like pattern, its bill and feet are painted orange, and it is wearing a yellow bandana with painted white flowers and dark stems on it. The garden stake measures 10.25 inches long x 5.25 inches wide x 17 inches tall. You state this item is intended for indoor or outdoor use in the home as a seasonal decoration. You describe a production process in which the components of the garden stake are stamped out of iron, welded together, polished, powder coated, and painted in Cambodia. Those components, along with iron wire, iron tube, and solar lights from China, are then assembled into the finished garden stake and then packed for shipping in Cambodia.
