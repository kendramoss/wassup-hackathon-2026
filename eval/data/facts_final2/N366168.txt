In your letter dated October 19, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on an air purifier. Descriptive literature was provided for our review. The item under consideration is described as the Waffle Cone air purifier, which is a cylindrical electronic home appliance designed to remove various airborne pollutants, including fine dust, harmful gases, and bacteria, from indoor air. The air purifier uses a 3-in-1 custom filter (pre-filter, HEPA filter, and deodorization filter) to create a cleaner indoor environment. The device measures 8.5 inches by 8.5 inches by 15 inches and weighs 6.5 pounds. It is designed for room sizes up to 167 square feet. The air purifier is made of components sourced from Indonesia and China. The Indonesian-origin components include the base cover, motor bracket, fan, air duct cover, air inlet, nuts, washers, screws, control plate, cable clamp, outlet grille, motor cover, sensor box, main body, air inlet filter frame, base fixing ring, sensor cover, power box cover, power box, and packaging materials. The Chinese-origin components include the dust sensor wiring assembly, cable grommet, foam pad, sensor cover foam pad, female connector, power cable, power board assembly, converter board assembly, dust sensor, protective film, rubber feet, silicone rings, motor wiring, motor, LED light strip, control PCB assembly, and filters. The manufacturing of the air purifier occurs in Indonesia and starts with the injection molding of plastic components. Prior to assembly, all components undergo a thorough inspection process. The semi-finished good is assembled by integrating the fan, motor, and sensors into the main body, which is made of injection-molded plastic. Following this, the control board assembly is installed, and the first functional test is conducted to verify the dust detection function and power supply operation. After completing the first test, the filter, bottom cover assembly, and sensor cover assembly are installed. The product then undergoes cleaning and visual inspection before proceeding to the final functional test. Once all inspections and tests are completed, the product is packaged for shipment.
