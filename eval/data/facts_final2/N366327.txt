In your letter dated October 23, 2026, you requested a ruling regarding tariff classification, country of origin, and eligibility for preferential treatment under the UAFTA. Images were provided in lieu of samples. The products under consideration are described as Eppiflex English paper piecing (EPP) templates. They consist of uncoated polyethylene terephthalate (PET) film that is laser-cut into geometric shapes including hexagons, diamonds, triangles, clamshells, and other specialized patchwork shapes. These templates are used to support fabric during hand sewing for patchwork and quilt making projects. They are subsequently removed and reused. The templates are sold as basic shape packs, assorted template packs, and project-specific kits. Basic packs generally contain templates and a printed cover sheet. Other packs may include layout diagrams, printed patterns, or multi-page project instructions.
