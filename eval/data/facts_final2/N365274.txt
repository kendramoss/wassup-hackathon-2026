In your letter dated September 22, 2026, you requested a tariff classification ruling on KP-Topcoat. In your submission you state that KP-Topcoat, also identified as Kalklitir Top Coat, is a mineral-based protective coating supplied in dry powder form. The product is intended for use as a finishing and protective coating over decorative wall finishes. It is mixed with water prior to application and applied in a thin layer to the finished wall surface. The product is supplied as a white, odorless solid powder and is soluble in water. Once mixed with water, it forms a water-based coating intended to provide additional surface protection while maintaining a matte appearance. The product is classified by the manufacturer as a paint/coating and is suitable for consumer, professional, and industrial use. The Safety Data Sheet identifies the product as a mixture and states that it does not contain substances that are required to be individually declared under the criteria of Section 3.2 of REACH Annex II. KP Topcoat is non-flammable and is not classified as hazardous under the applicable European CLP classification. The finished product is manufactured and packaged in Belgium by Kalklitir BV and will be exported from Belgium to the United States.
