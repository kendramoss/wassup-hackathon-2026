In your letter dated September 15, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on an automotive carrier assembly on behalf of Seohan Innobility Co., Ltd. The article under consideration is an automotive carrier assembly identified as the MV/ME Carrier Assembly, part number 52710-DO000. The merchandise is a component of the rear suspension system of a motor vehicle. The carrier provides mounting points for the lower arm, upper arm, assist arm, trailing arm, constant velocity joint, wheel speed sensor, brake caliper, and wheel. The carrier assembly supports the rear wheel load and facilitates the functions of power transmission, wheel-speed detection, vibration and shock absorption, braking, and steering. The finished carrier assembly consists primarily of the MV/ME Machined Carrier (part number 52715-DO000), the LFa Pillow Ball Assembly (part number 55130-B1000N), and the MV/ME Bush (part number 55278-GI000). According to the information provided, the manufacturing process begins in South Korea with the production of the MV/ME Cast Carrier (part number M52715-DO000). Aluminum ingot, aluminum scrap, aluminum-strontium, and magnesium of Chinese origin are imported into South Korea. The submitted photographs show the aluminum received in ingot and scrap form, the aluminum-strontium received in round-bar form, and the magnesium received in small ingot or block form. These raw materials undergo melting, tapping, degassing, casting, trimming, cutting, deburring, heat treatment, X-ray inspection, and packaging, creating a specifically shaped casting. The casting is then inspected and machined to establish the reference surface and inner diameter. Additional operations are performed on the caliper, arm-link mounting points, and other surfaces and openings. The carrier undergoes grinding, finishing, and inspection. Following machining, the LFa Pillow Ball Assembly, and MV/ME Bush, which are sourced from Korean suppliers, are incorporated into the carrier. The bush
components are press-fitted into the appropriate mounting locations, and the pillow ball assembly is installed. The completed article is then inspected and supplied as the MV/ME Carrier Assembly, part number 52710-DO000.
