In your letter, dated September 30, 2026, you requested a binding classification, country of origin, and marking ruling on a beaded valance. Information and photos were submitted for our review. The product under consideration is item BV26-01, a beaded valance. The item consists of an aluminum profile that is drilled and strung with several strands of wooden beads. The beads measure 12mm in diameter and are strung in a specific color order to form a picture of a branch with leaves and apples. The strands are specific lengths so that the bottom edge of the valance follows the shape of the apples and leaves. The valance is designed to be used in a doorway, much like a beaded curtain, but does not block the full doorway. The valance styles measure up to one meter wide and are up to 600mm in length.
