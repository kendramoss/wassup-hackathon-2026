In your letter, dated October 12, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling for plywood. Product information was submitted for our review. The product under consideration is plywood. You outline a scenario wherein plywood cores consisting of three to seven plies (no ply exceeding 6mm in thickness and grains of plies oriented at 90-degree angles) are manufactured from tropical wood in Malaysia. The cores are then exported to Indonesia, where they are laminated with outer plies of hardwoods including maple, poplar, oak, and birch woods from the United States, Canada, Latvia, and China. The finished panels will be exported from Indonesia to the United States.
