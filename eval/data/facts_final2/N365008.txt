The processes performed in Mexico are as follows: The sinks undergo nickel electro plating to apply a decorative nickel finish. The plated sinks are exported from Mexico and re-imported into the United States. Your request also concerns the eligibility of the five copper bathroom sinks under subheading 9802.00.5060, HTSUS. Subheading 9802.00.5060, HTSUS, provides a partial or complete duty exemption for articles returned to the U.S. after having been exported to be advanced in value or improved in condition by any process of manufacture or other means, provided that the documentary requirements of Section 10, Customs Regulations (19 CFR 10.8) are satisfied.
