In your letter dated October 15, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling for leather hides. Descriptions of the manufacturing processes and photographs were provided for review. The merchandise under consideration is crust leather imported into United States from China. The production process begins with raw American bovine hides that are soaked, dehaired and chromium-tanned to a wet-blue state within the United States. The wet-blue hides are subsequently exported to China, where they undergo processing to be transformed into crust leather. Following importation into the United States, the crust leather will undergo finishing operations in accordance with specific customer requirements. Below are the stated processes performed in each country. Processes performed in the U.S., from raw hide to wet blue hides: a) Soaking: Hides are treated with enzymes, detergents and soda ash. b) Unhairing: Hair is removed using sodium hydrosulfide, soda ash and lime. c) Liming: Hides are swelled and opened by adding lime. d) Deliming: Hides are de-swelled and residue lime is removed using ammonium salt. e) Bating: Hides are treated with proteolytic enzymes to enhance the surface appearance by removing the residual grease and unwanted proteins. f) Pickling: Hides are prepared for tanning using salt and sulfuric acid. g) Tanning: Hides are chemically stabilized with a trivalent chromium compound and a neutralizing agent, and transformed into "wet blue." A fungicide is added to prevent mold growth during storage. h) Packaging: Hides are graded, folded and palletized before loaded into a container for shipping to China. Processes performed in China, from wet blue hides to crust leather: a) Washing: Wet blue hides are soaked in water within a wooden drum. b) Sammying: Excess water is wrung out in a sammying machine to adjust the water content.
c) Splitting: Wet blue is split into two layers by a splitting machine. The top grain leather is used for the production of crust leather. d) Siding: Wet blue hides are manually cut in half, from roughly 50 square feet into two sides of 25 square feet each. e) Shaving: The thickness of the wet blue is finally adjusted in the shaving machine by removing material from their backside. f) Retanning/dyeing: Hides are loaded into wooden drums with chemicals to achieve the desired leather colors and characteristics. Fatliquors, resins, fillers are added sequentially during the process. g) Setting out: Hides are wrung and stretched in a setting-out machine to remove excess water and create a smooth surface. h) Vacuum drying: Hides are vacuum-dried to maximize water removal at a relatively low temperature in a short time. i) Staking: Rigid hides are softened in a staking machine through mechanical action. j) Inspection: The formed crust leather undergoes quality control checks for color, softness and dye penetration throughout their thickness. k) Packaging: Crust leather is packed for export to the U.S. finishing plant.
