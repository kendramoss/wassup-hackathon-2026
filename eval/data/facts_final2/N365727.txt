In your letter dated October 4, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling. Product information was submitted with your request. The item under consideration is a tool set packaged in a plastic carrying case for retail sale. In addition to the blow-molded storage case, the set consists of: 80 - Assorted Screwdriver Bits 16 - Nut Driver Bits 1 - Bit Extension 1 - Standard Driver Handle 1 - Adjustable Torque Driver Handle You state that the assorted steel screwdriver and nut driver bits are designed to interface precisely with corresponding fasteners. During use, the bits transfer torque from an attached handle (or bit extension) to tighten or loosen fasteners without stripping. The standard driver handle is described as a manual driver handle with an integrated bit holder shaft. The adjustable torque driver handle is a mechanical driver handle which enables the user to apply torque to a desired amount. It features an internal spring, calibrated torque mechanism, scale display, and an adjustable knob. When the selected torque limit is reached, resistance from the fastener disengages a clutch mechanism and creates an audible clicking noise to alert the user. This action allows the handle to spin freely and prevent over-tightening or stripping. In correspondence, you clarified that all included bits can be used with both driver handles interchangeably. The screwdriver bits, nut driver bits, and bit extension are forged in Thailand to their final physical shape. After forging, they are shipped to China for heat treatment to impart hardness and mechanical properties. In
regard to the standard driver handle, the steel shaft is forged and machined in Thailand to its final, functional shape. In China, the steel shaft from Thailand is heat treated to increase hardness and durability. Also in China, the plastic handle is injection molded around the steel shaft from Thailand and locked in place. According to the submitted information, the adjustable torque driver handle is manufactured completely in China. First, steel is forged and machined to its final shape and heat treated. Following that, all mechanical subcomponents are prepared, assembled into the handle’s outer shell, and calibrated. You assert that all of the tool set’s constituent items are packed together in China into a blow-molded plastic case ready for retail sale. Regarding country of origin, you propose Thailand on the basis that the bits manufactured in Thailand impart the essential character of the entire tool set. We disagree.
