In your letter dated October 13, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling for marking purposes on behalf of your client, Faro International, Inc. Product images were submitted in lieu of samples. The product under consideration is a retractable gel pen. In your submission, you state the ink cartridge is manufactured in Malaysia using German ink, a Swiss ball, and a Malaysian tube and tip. In Malaysia the ink is injected into the tube and sealed with the tip, transforming it into a complete ink cartridge. The spring is also manufactured in Malaysia. The completed ink cartridge and spring are shipped to the US. In the US, the Malaysian components are assembled with external components sourced from Japan, including the barrel (body), nose cone, rotator, cam, clip holder, clip, and plunger, to form the finished pen.
