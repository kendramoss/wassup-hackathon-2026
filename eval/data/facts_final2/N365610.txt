In your letter dated October 1, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on a pneumatic blow gun. Descriptive literature was provided for our review. The item under consideration is described as a high-performance blow gun (item number 1111200214), which is an aluminum pneumatic hand tool featuring a 1/4" NPT female threaded inlet designed to connect to standard compressed air line couplers or hoses. The device utilizes a pistol-grip style, incorporating an ergonomically configured squeeze-trigger lever to manage compressed air flow. The blow gun functions mechanically by directing incoming compressed air into an inner valve mechanism controlled by the operator’s grip. Upon engagement, the air is accelerated toward the uniquely integrated “Ultimate Flow Tip”. This specialized, vented nozzle incorporates side intake ports utilizing the Venturi effect, which draws ambient room air into the primary stream, dramatically multiplying the total volume of compressed air output, achieving roughly 92 SCFM. The device is designed to clear dust, metal shavings, and debris from machinery, engines, workbenches, and industrial surfaces. The blow gun is made from components sourced from China and Malaysia. The Chinese components include the aluminum bar stock used to make the extended blow tube, plastic pellets used to make the soft grip, O-ring, springs, trigger, trigger fixing pin, rubber tip nozzle, metal pointed nozzle and thread adapter. The Malaysian components include the aluminum ingots to make the gun body and the brass bar stock to make the valve seat and valve core. All of the components are then sent to Malaysia where the aluminum ingots are melted and die-cast into semi-finished components. The components are then machined, including drilling and threading to form the finished gun body. Additionally, the valve seat, valve core, and extended blow tubes are CNC machined, drilled, grooved, deburred and inspected to form the finished components. Finally, the soft grips are made by
injection molding the plastic pellets into the finished component. Once the metal and plastic components are made, they are then assembled with the remaining Chinese components to form the complete high-performance blow gun.
