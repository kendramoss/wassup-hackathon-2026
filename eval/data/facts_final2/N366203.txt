In your letter dated October 19, 2026, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of your client Medisca, Inc. Additional information was received by email on November 3, and November 5 2026. The product under consideration is VersaPro Cream Base, a topical cream base intended for use in multiple applications and dosage forms. According to the product information provided, VersaPro Cream Base is a white oil-in-water formulation which demonstrates strong drug compatibility and permeation characteristics The principal ingredients consist of an inorganic compound; a polyhydric alcohol and acid ester, wax, a siloxane liquid, a preservative blend, and a sugar alcohol. The product is described as non-comedogenic and hypoallergenic, making it suitable for pharmaceutical and cosmetic applications. You suggest classification of the VersaPro Cream Base under subheading 3304.99.5000, HTSUS, which provides, in relevant part, for “Beauty or make-up preparations and preparations for the care of the skin (other than medicaments): Other: Other: Other.” We disagree.
