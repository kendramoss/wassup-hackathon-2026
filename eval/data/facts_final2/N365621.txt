In your letter dated October 1, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on a pneumatic ratchet wrench. Product information was submitted with your request. The item under consideration is a pneumatic ratchet wrench (Item number 1151200011) to tighten and loosen nuts and bolts for automotive and industrial assembly work. The ratchet wrench features a 1/2-inch square drive anvil, single ratchet pawl head, reversible direction, and quick connect plug. You state that the ratchet wrench is capable of delivering 150 rotations per minute (free speed) and a max torque of 61 Newton-meters (45 foot-pounds). During operation, the user applies pressure to the lever which allows compressed air to enter the tool. The compressed air is precisely channeled by the cylinder housing to push against rotor vanes. This action provides the primary rotational power which is transferred to the wrench’s anvil. The single-pawl ratchet mechanism allows for rotation in one direction while preventing motion in the reverse direction. As you described, the ratchet wrench is assembled in Malaysia from Malaysian and Chinese components. In Malaysia, rough metal blanks and bar stock from China undergoes CNC machining such as drilling, threading, turning, boring, grinding, and deburring to obtain the form of the finished cylinder housing, ratchet housing, and quick-connect plug. With regard to the wrench housing, aluminum ingots sourced from Malaysia are die-cast to shape prior to CNC machining. These processes deliver the precise shapes, dimensions, drilled holes, threading, and surfaces which the components rely on for functioning. All Malaysian-manufactured parts undergo testing for performance and inspection for quality.
Additionally, several Chinese-origin components are imported to Malaysia ready for assembly without further processing. These components include, but are not limited to, the rotor, the rotor vanes, the ratchet anvil assembly, the ratchet yoke, the crank shaft, the valve stem, valve plugs, end plates, bearings, and gears. Final assembly of the ratchet wrench begins with the installation of the valve seat fitting and valve plug onto the wrench housing. After that, the rear bearing is installed to the rear end plate. The rear end plate, rotor vanes, cylinder housing, front end plate, and front bearing are installed into and onto the rotor. Following that, the cylinder housing containing the rotor and other components is installed into the housing. A ring gear is then installed onto the housing, and an idler gear is installed onto the idler gear shaft. At that point, the idler gear shaft, clamp nut, and ratchet housing are installed onto the wrench housing. The drive bushing is installed onto the crank shaft which is installed into the rachet housing. In the next steps, the ratchet yoke and ratchet anvil assembly are assembled onto the ratchet housing. Assembly finishes with the installation of the lever, pin, and air inlet with quick-connect plug onto the wrench housing. Finally, the completed ratchet wrench is tested for function and accuracy and packed in a box for export to the United States.
