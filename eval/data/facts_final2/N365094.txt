In your letter dated September 16, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on a metal lantern wax warmer for purposes of current trade remedies. The product under consideration is a metal wax warmer in the form of a cylindrical lantern. Each wax warmer consists of a finial (iron ring and hanger), a wax melt dish atop, a metal lantern cap, a light bulb, a socket, a protective cage (cage bars and guard ring), a glass cylinder, faux wood accent rings (one circling just under the top cap and the other framing the bottom base), a pedestal base, a power cord, and wax. The submitted literature indicates that all components are produced in Vietnam, except for the lamp sockets, which are imported from China. The assembly process in Vietnam includes making the metal components, the glass cylinders, faux wood accent rings, light bulbs, power cords, and wax, then combining these Vietnam-originating components with the imported lamp sockets to fabricate the finished wax warmers. Overall, this manufacturing process in Vietnam involves metal cutting, stamping, spot welding, polishing, powder coating, plastic injection molding, paint spraying, and hand-painting (with Vietnamese-origin coating and paint). Finally, the finished products undergo testing and inspection before being packaged for export. The Bill of Materials (BOM) demonstrates that the manufacturing operations in Vietnam contribute approximately 97% of the total cost of production and final value of the finished products.
