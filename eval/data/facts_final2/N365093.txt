In your letter dated September 16, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on a windchime for purposes of current trade remedies. The product under consideration is a windchime identified as the “RS 31.5in metal chicken windchime,” which measures approximately 31.5 inches in height and 8 inches in diameter. Each windchime consists of a dome-shaped metal top piece with metal tubes, flowers, roosters, a clapper, and a flower windcatcher suspended beneath it. Three metal chains are connected to a hook (referred to as the “S-hook and iron chains”) at the top of the dome for hanging. In your ruling request submission, you state that the windchimes are assembled in Vietnam using components sourced from both China and Vietnam. The components sourced from China are the S-hook, iron chains, and nylon strings. The balance of components, including the top pieces, flowers, rooster bodies, clappers, and windcatchers, are produced in Vietnam. The assembly process in Vietnam includes making the metal components from raw metal sheets and tubes, and combining them with the Chinese-origin S-hook, iron chains and stings to fabricate the finished windchimes. This manufacturing process involves metal stamping, cutting, spot welding, polishing, powder coating, paint spraying, and hand-painting (using coating and paint of Vietnamese origin). Finally, the finished products undergo testing and inspection before being packaged for export. The Bill of Materials (BOM) demonstrates that the manufacturing operations in Vietnam contribute approximately 90% of the total cost of final value of the windchimes.
