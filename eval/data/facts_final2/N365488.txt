In your letter dated September 28, 2026, you requested a classification and country of origin ruling. The merchandise under consideration is an Ozark Trail electric hand warmer device, which is described as a handheld heating device designed to electrically heat and warm the user’s hands. The device is comprised of a heating element and control printed circuit board assembly (PCBA) within a plastic enclosure. The warmer is further equipped with an LED battery indicator light and display to show power status, along with three adjustable heat settings. Though classification is not at issue with this request, we find it appropriate to comment as you note what we determined to be an incorrect classification for the device within your request. In your request, you note that the correct classification for this device is under subheading 8516.79, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS). We disagree. The subject hand warmers are intended to be used primarily outdoors, not for domestic purposes, and are unlike the exemplars listed in the Explanatory Notes (ENs) for this heading.
