In your letter dated September 1, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on an iron decorative windmill. Photographs and descriptions of the merchandise and manufacturing process were included in your inquiry. The article under consideration is described as a RS 5ft Duck and Bucket Windmill, SKU# 6104568. It is a freestanding decorative iron windmill intended for use on the porch or in the garden. It features spinning windmill blades and a large metal duck pumping water into a small bucket. A small metal duck and three tufts of grass complete the scene. The windmill measures 31.89 inches x 18.11 inches x 59.45inches, and weighs 14.7 lb. You describe a production process in which several components including the decorative large duck, water pump, hand lever, small bucket, and small duck are stamped out of iron in China. Those components are shipped to Vietnam where they are incorporated with the remaining components that are made in Vietnam including the windmill frame and windmill blades. In Vietnam, the pieces are welded together, polished, powder coated, spray painted, hand painted and then assembled into the finished article. It is then packed for shipping.
