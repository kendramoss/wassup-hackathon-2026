In your letter dated September 30, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Horizon Hobby, LLC. The merchandise under consideration consists of radio control transmitters, models iX14+ and iX14+SE, items SPMR14010A and SPMR14020A. These handheld radio controllers remotely control toy aircraft, using a 2.4 GHz radio frequency (RF). Models iX14+ and iX14+SE are each comprised of approximately 100 unique components manufactured in a variety of countries. According to the information provided, the three main printed circuit board assemblies (PCBAs), the Plano RF Transmitter Module, the MBA Main Computer, and the Waco Receiver are manufactured in the United States (U.S.) through surface-mount technology (SMT) and dual in-line package (DIP)/wave-soldering processes. These processes consist of populating a printed circuit board (PCB) with various electrical and electronic components, including resistors, capacitors, fuses, and radiofrequency (RF) chips. These printed circuit board assemblies (PCBAs) generate radiofrequency signals and perform transmission, control, and reception functions. All non-China origin components including the Plano RF Transmitter Module PCBA, MBA Main Computer PCBA, and Waco Receiver PCBA will be shipped to China for final assembly. The Android System-on-Module (SOM) touch-screen graphical user interface (GUI), the trim PCBA, the CBA PCBA, and the printed circuit boards containing magnetic encoders are all manufactured in China. These boards perform secondary functions. The subject radio remote-control transmitters also contain a Chinese-origin Android SOM that includes a touch-screen user interface and a Wi-Fi/Bluetooth module. According to the information provided, the Android SOM serves only as a user interface for adjusting parameters on the MBA and is not involved in RF transmission, control, or reception functions.
In China, the following final-assembly operations will be performed: the Chinese-origin plastic housing will be screwed and snapped together; gimbals or sticks will be screwed, press-fit, and soldered to wire harnesses; cables and switches will be soldered and press-fit into the housing; the Chinese-origin LCD screen will be screwed into the plastic housing and connected to cables; PCBAs will be installed in the plastic housing; and software and firmware will be loaded. Next, the Chinese-origin PCBAs will be installed; the antenna will be assembled and connected; the vibrator motor, speaker, and battery will be installed; decals will be applied; and testing will be performed. Lastly, the finished radio-control transmitters will be packaged.
