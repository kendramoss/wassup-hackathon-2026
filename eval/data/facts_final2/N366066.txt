In your letter dated October 15, 2026, you requested a tariff classification, country of origin, and marking determination ruling. The items under consideration are described as the Kefirko Kefir Fermenters. They are complete fermentation systems designed for the home preparation of milk or water kefir. The product, sold under the KEFIRKO trademark, is available in two models: 900 ml (30 fl oz) and 1400 ml (47 fl oz), each offered in four colors (blue, pink, green, and white) with separate product/style numbers. You state that while the color and size variations have different product/style numbers, they are structurally, mechanically, and functionally identical, differing only in volume, overall dimensions, weight, color, and model/SKU designations. The complete retail packaging for each model includes a food-grade clear soda-lime glass bottle/vessel; a two-piece polypropylene (PP) lid with an integrated strainer; a plastic scraper and stirrer; a plastic juicer attachment; a thermoplastic elastomer (TPE) sealing ring; a printed paper instruction booklet; and printed cardboard retail packaging. The glass bottle is manufactured in China, while the functional plastic accessories are manufactured in Slovenia and Croatia. The instruction booklet is printed in Germany, and the cardboard box is produced in Slovenia. The manufacturing and assembly process occurs in Slovenia, where all individual components are delivered to the headquarters facility. Operations include component inspection, the physical nesting/assembly of the plastic strainer, lid, scraper, stirrer, and sealing ring onto the glass vessel, and final packaging of the assembled unit alongside the instruction booklet into the printed cardboard retail box prior to export to the United States in fully assembled condition.
