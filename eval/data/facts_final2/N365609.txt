In your letter dated October 1, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on an in-line MOS filter. Descriptive literature was provided for our review. The item under consideration is described as an in-line MOS filter (item number 1111200201), which is an in-line water separator and mini air filter designed to attach directly to air compressor hoses, spray guns, or airbrushes to eliminate harmful moisture, oil mist, and solid debris before they reach the tools. The pneumatic accessory operates at 20 CFM at 90 PSI via a 1/4-inch NPT male inlet stem while safeguarding sensitive downstream equipment from internal contaminants. The device features a blue anodized aluminum end cap, a rubber O-ring, a Polypropylene (PP) inner sleeve, filter core, and end base, all enclosed within a transparent Polycarbonate (PC) bowl for instant visual monitoring of trapped contaminants, which is equipped with an integrated manual drain button for regular liquid discharge. The in-line MOS filter is made from components sourced from China. The components include the aluminum used to make the blue end cap, the stainless steel used to make the threaded inlet stem, the O-ring, inner sleeve, filter core, end base and transparent filter bowl. All of the Chinese components are then sent to Malaysia where the compression blue end cap and threaded inlet stem are CNC machined, milled, drilled, grooved, deburred, and inspected. Once the metal components are made, they are then assembled with the remaining Chinese components to form the complete in-line MOS filter.
