In your letter dated September 7, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on windchimes for the purposes of current trade remedies. The product under consideration is a windchime that measures approximately 31.5 inches in height and 12 inches in diameter at its widest point. Each windchime consists of a metal utility hook attached to a short link chain through a small “extended round nut,” and a metal full-bloom sunflower topper featuring a disc floret and ray florets (petals). Suspended beneath this crown is a rigid, circular black metal band that serves as the chime support, which is held in place by three small metal chains connected to a central axis rod. Four metal tubes of varying lengths are hung below the ring by nylon braided cords. A striker (a flat, circular black metal disc) is suspended by a braided cord at the heart of the chime array, and a smaller metal sunflower hangs at the very bottom of the windchime to serve as a wind catcher. In your ruling request submission, you state that the windchimes are assembled in Vietnam using components sourced from both China and Vietnam. The components sourced from China include the utility hooks, iron chains, extended round nuts, and nylon braided cords. The balance of components—namely, the sunflowers (the topper and wind catcher), chime supports, iron tubes (chimes), and strikers—are produced in Vietnam. The factory combines these Vietnamese-origin components with the Chinese-origin parts to create the finished windchimes. The assembly process in Vietnam begins by making the metal disc florets and ray florets (petals) from metal sheets through stamping, and O-rings and cross bars through cutting, then welding these metal components to create complete sunflowers and the chime support. These parts are further combined with the Chinese-originating components to fabricate the finished windchimes. Overall, this manufacturing process in
Vietnam involves metal cutting, stamping, spot welding, polishing, powder coating, paint spraying, and hand-painting (with Vietnamese-originating coating and paint). Finally, the finished products undergo testing and inspection before being packaged for export. The Bill of Materials (BOM) demonstrates that the manufacturing operations in Vietnam contributes approximately 86% of total cost of production and final value of the windchimes.
