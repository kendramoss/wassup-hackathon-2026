In your letter dated October 1, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on a pressure gauge. Descriptive literature was provided for our review. The item under consideration is described as a pressure gauge (item number 1111200195), which is a mechanical instrument designed to measure and display fluid or gas pressure. It features a 50mm dial with a measurement range of 0 to 160 PSI and utilizes a copper Bourdon tube as its internal sensing element, which expands or contracts in response to pressure changes to drive the dial pointer. The internal mechanism is housed in a carbon steel gauge case and connects to external systems via a 1/4-inch bottom mount featuring a 1/4" NPT-18 TPI brass inlet fitting. The configuration constitutes a complete gauge designed to be permanently or semi-permanently threaded into piping or machinery to provide real-time pressure readings without electrical components. The pressure gauge is made from components sourced from China and Malaysia. The Chinese components include the carbon steel (for the gauge case and retaining ring), gauge lens, dial assembly, screws, movement assembly, and Bourdon tube. The inlet fitting is made from Malaysian brass bar stock. All of the Chinese components are then sent to Malaysia where the metal components are constructed by stamping, forming, trimming, deburring and coating. Welding of the Chinese-sourced Bourdon tube to the inlet fitting is also undertaken forming a key functional component of the gauge. The Malaysian brass bar stock is then CNC machined by precision drilling the parts, deburring and inspecting. Once the metal components are made, they are then assembled with the remaining Chinese components to form the complete pressure gauge.
