In your letter dated October 1, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on a pneumatic impact wrench. Product information was submitted with your request. The item under consideration is a pneumatic impact wrench (Item Number 1151200009) to tighten and loosen nuts and bolts for automobile tire maintenance and industrial assembly. The impact wrench features a 1/2-inch square drive anvil, twin hammer impact mechanism, reversible direction, and a quick-connect plug. You state that the impact wrench is capable of delivering up to 1000-1200 Newton-meters of torque at 8,500 rotations per minute. During operation, the user applies pressure to the trigger which allows compressed air to enter the tool. The compressed air pushes against rotor blades which forces the rotor to spin at high speeds. This action provides the primary rotational power which is transferred to the wrench’s anvil. As the wrench encounters resistance from a nut or bolt, its hammer mechanism strikes the anvil with powerful impacts to increase torque output and overcome the resistance. As you described, the impact wrench is assembled in Malaysia from Malaysian and Chinese components. In Malaysia, rough metal blanks from China undergo CNC machining such as milling, drilling, boring, grinding, grooving, and deburring to obtain the form of the finished anvil, hammer cage, cylinder, and rotor. These processes deliver the precise shapes, dimensions, splining, drilled holes, slots, and surfaces which the components rely on for functioning. The anvil, hammer cage, and rotor also undergo heat treatment to achieve specified hardness and mechanical properties. Also in Malaysia, plastic pellets sourced from China undergo injection molding and over-molding to form the wrench’s plastic housing and grip. All Malaysian-manufactured parts undergo testing for performance and inspection for quality.
Additionally, several Chinese-origin components are imported to Malaysia ready for assembly without further processing. These components include, but are not limited to, the aluminum housing cover, the hammers, the rotor blades, the trigger, the quick-connect plug, the reversing button, gasket, bearings, pins, springs, screw rods and O-rings. Final assembly of the impact wrench begins with the installation of the hammer, hammer pin, and anvil into the hammer cage. The twin hammer impact assembly is installed into the housing cover, and a bearing is installed onto the front cover. The rotor blades are installed into the rotor, and the rotor is then installed into the cylinder. After that, the front cover and rotor cover are installed onto the cylinder. The reversing button is installed on the rear cover which is fastened with screws. In the next steps, the cylinder is installed into the housing, the housing cover assembly is installed to the motor housing assembly, and the trigger is installed. Following that, a compressed spring is installed onto the intake valve, an exhaust cover is installed onto the air inlet, and an O-ring is installed. The intake valve and air inlet are installed into the main housing and the quick connect plug is installed onto the air inlet. Finally, the completed impact wrench undergoes testing and packaging.
