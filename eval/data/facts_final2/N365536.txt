In your letter dated September 30, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on decorative iron garden stakes. Photographs and descriptions of the merchandise and manufacturing process were included in your inquiry. The products under consideration are described as RS 32-Inch Solar Butterfly Stakes, SKU # 6112178, 6109235, and 6109236. Each style is a decorative garden stake made of iron with a powder coated finish, and a solar powered light. Each style is shaped like a flower stem with two flowers, two leaves, and a butterfly sitting on each leaf. The different styles represent different color combinations of flowers and butterflies. Each garden stake measures approximately 17.25 inches long, 4.5 inches wide, and 31 inches high. You state these garden stakes are intended for indoor or outdoor use. You describe a production process in which the components of the garden stakes are stamped out of iron, welded together, polished, and powder coated in Cambodia. Those components, along with iron wire, iron tube, and solar lights from China, are then assembled into the finished garden stakes and then packed for shipping in Cambodia.
