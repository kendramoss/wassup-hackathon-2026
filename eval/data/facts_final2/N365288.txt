In your letter dated September 22, 2026, you requested a ruling regarding the classification and country of origin of a siphon tube set. Item number AU8561 is identified as a siphon tube set, also referred to as a multi-use liquid transfer pump kit. The set consists of a manually actuated pump that is retail packaged with two siphon hoses, an inflation hose, an extension hose, hose sleeves, a hose connector, a plastic inflator head, an air chuck assembly, and a stainless-steel needle. The pump and the accessories packaged with the pump allow air or liquids to be transferred. In your letter, you suggest the pump imparts the essential character of the set and therefore the siphon tube set is classified within subheading 8413.20.0000, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS), which provides for Pumps for liquids, whether or not fitted with a measuring device; liquid elevators; part thereof: Hand pumps, other than those of subheading 8413.11 or 8413.19. While we agree the pump imparts the essential character of the set, the design of the pump and the accessories allow it to function as either an air or a liquid pump not just as a liquid pump. Because of the dual-functionality, CBP has previously classified a similar multi-use pump set in accordance with GRI 3(c) within heading 8414, which provides for air pumps. See New York Ruling Letter G89404, dated April 12, 2001.
