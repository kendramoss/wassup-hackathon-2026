In your letter, dated October 13, 2026, you requested a classification, country of origin, and marking ruling for plywood cable reel flanges. Product information and photos were submitted for our review. The product under consideration is cable reel flanges manufactured from plywood. The flanges are circular and range in diameter and thickness. They are drilled in the center for the central axle and precision cut and drilled around the center for functional holes. The flanges form the ends of a cable reel.
