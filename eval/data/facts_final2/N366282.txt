In your letter dated October 22, 2026, you requested a classification and country of origin ruling on a chemical injector kit. Descriptive literature was provided for our review. The product under consideration is described as a chemical injector kit for high-pressure washers (SKU W03K2), which is a retail-packaged downstream injection system that completes the activity of introducing cleaning chemicals into the water stream after being released from a pump. The kit consists of 16 feet of PVC tubing, a regulating valve of brass material, a quick plug, a brass fitting, a black nozzle tip, a filter, and PTFE thread seal tape, packaged together ready for retail sale. In operation, the regulating valve, which is designed to control the flow of chemicals and liquids received from the tubing connected to a pump, functions as a pressure actuating suction valve. The rigid, non-movable brass fitting features a through-hole that transmits this negative pressure to guide the liquid without telescopic movement or retraction. The quick plug enables tool-free connection and disconnection from the high-pressure washer’s water outlet. The black nozzle tip, which features a fixed orifice, is designed to accelerate the mixed fluid of water and foam concentrate, entraining air to produce and project foam from the outlet. Additionally, a filter attached to the suction hose inlet screens out impurities to protect the control valve and nozzle from clogs, while the included PTFE thread seal tape is applied to threaded connections to provide a seal across both high-pressure water paths and suction circuits. In your letter, you suggest the kit is classified within subheading 8424.90.9080, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS), which provides for Mechanical appliances (whether or not hand operated) for projecting, dispersing or spraying liquids or powders; fire extinguishers, whether or not charged; spray guns
and similar appliances; steam or sand blasting machines and similar jet projecting machines; parts thereof: Parts: Other: Other. We disagree. The kit constitutes goods classified in two or more headings, which are put up in a set ready for retail sale.
