In your letter, dated September 21, 2026, you requested a country of origin determination on behalf of your client, L&L International Inc. (South El Monte, CA). You have presented three scenarios involving wild-caught red shrimp (Pleoticus muelleri) harvested in Argentina and subsequently shipped to China for further processing prior to importation into the United States. In the first scenario, the red shrimp is wild caught in Argentina and shipped to China in a raw, frozen state, either head-on or headless. In China, the shrimp is thawed and, when imported head-on, beheaded. The shrimp is then peeled and deveined, with the tails either retained or removed. Thereafter, the shrimp is washed, treated with food additives, rinsed, graded, individually quick frozen (IQF), and packaged in 1-, 2-, 4-, 5-, or 10-pound boxes for exportation to the United States in a frozen condition. In the second scenario, the red shrimp is likewise wild caught in Argentina and shipped to China raw and frozen, either head-on or headless. In China, the shrimp is thawed and, when necessary, beheaded. It is then peeled and deveined, with the tails either retained or removed, and placed on skewers. The shrimp is subsequently washed, treated with food additives, rinsed, graded, IQF frozen, and packaged in 1-, 2-, 4-, 5-, or 10-pound boxes for exportation to the United States in a frozen condition. In the third scenario, the red shrimp is wild caught in Argentina and shipped to China raw and frozen, either head-on or headless. In China, the shrimp is thawed and, when necessary, beheaded. The shrimp is then processed into “EZ Peel” shrimp by cutting the shell along the back and deveining the shrimp. Thereafter, the shrimp is washed, treated with food additives, rinsed, graded, IQF frozen, and packaged in 1-, 2-, 4-, 5-, or 10-pound boxes for exportation to the United States in a frozen condition.
