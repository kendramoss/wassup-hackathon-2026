In your letter, dated November 5, 2026, you requested a binding ruling concerning the country of origin of plywood. Product information was submitted for our review. The product under consideration is plywood. You outline a scenario wherein plywood platforms are constructed in Tanzania and then sent to Indonesia where face and back plies are laminated onto the platform. The platforms are cores that consist of three to seven plies and the grain of each ply runs at an angle to that of the adjacent plies. The thickness of each ply ranges from 1.4mm to 2.7mm. The species of wood are eucalyptus and pine. In Tanzania, the veneers are peeled, cut, glued, laid up, hot pressed, and cold pressed to form the platforms. In Indonesia, the platforms are laminated with a face and back ply of United States-origin maple or oak, China-origin poplar, or Latvia-origin birch. The face and back ply veneers measure 0.6mm to 0.7mm in thickness.
