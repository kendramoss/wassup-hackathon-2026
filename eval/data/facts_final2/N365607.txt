In your letter dated October 1, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on an air pressure regulator. Item number 1111200197 is identified as a 1/4-IN Compressed Air Pressure Regulator. The regulator consists of a pressure gauge positioned in front of valve that is actuated using a knob assembly. The valve consists of a die-cast aluminum valve body with 1/4" NPT female inlet and outlet ports that incorporate a diaphragm assembly, a brass valve spool, springs, an O-ring, an aluminum plug, a valve seat, screws and a bolt. Once connected to a compressed air system, the regulator functions as a valve that stabilizes incoming air pressure to an adjustable constant output pressure for downstream pneumatic tools and equipment. With respect to origin, the final assembly of the regulator occurs in Malaysia using components from China and Malaysia. Prior to the final assembly, the valve body and plug are manufactured in China and exported to Malaysia to undergo a machining process to add threading. Less than three percent of material is removed during the machining process. The final assembly process begins by inserting a screw, a Chinese spring, and a Chinese diaphragm assembly onto a Chinese knob assembly. Afterwards, a valve body is screwed onto the knob assembly. Subsequently, a spool, a spring, an O-ring and plug are manually inserted into the valve body. A Chinese pressure gauge is then screwed onto the valve body. Once complete, the regulator is tested, inspected and packaged ready for shipment.
