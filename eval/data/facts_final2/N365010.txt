In your letter dated September 10, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Trabos S.A.C., for quartz surface products designated as item numbers 3005, 5016, 5019, H1015, and H-019. From the information you provided, quartz sand, unsaturated polyester resin, pigments, and other additives are produced in China and shipped to Peru. In Peru, these materials are mixed together so that they are uniformly agglomerated throughout the body of the product. The blended materials are spread evenly into molds, pressed, and heat-cured. After the surfaces are removed from the mold, they are polished, edge-worked, and otherwise finished before they are packaged for shipping. You state that after importation into the United States, these quartz surface products will be cut to size and shape for installation as kitchen countertops.
