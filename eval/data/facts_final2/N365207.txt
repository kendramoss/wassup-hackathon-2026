In your letter, dated September 18, 2026, you requested a binding country of origin, marking, and applicability of trade programs ruling on behalf of your client, Kuresa S.A. The ruling request was for adhesive paper labels. Product information and photos were submitted for our review. The products under consideration are branded, die-cut, self-adhesive labels packaged in envelopes or boxes. In your letter, you outline a scenario wherein 40”-wide rolls of self-adhesive paper originate in China and are shipped to Peru. In Peru, the rolls are slit to letter width, the label paper is die-cut into labels, and label sheets are cut to size and printed on the front and back with branding and SKU artwork and information. The label sheets are then packaged for retail sale.
