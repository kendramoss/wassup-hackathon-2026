In your letter, dated October 16, 2026, you requested a binding classification, country of origin, and application of trade programs ruling on multilayer wood flooring. Product information was submitted for our review. The product under consideration is multilayer wood flooring. The finished panels are three-ply with a 4.05mm-thick face ply, a 7.9mm-16.2mm cross-ply core layer, and a 2mm-thick back ply. The face will consist of white oak, red oak, or hard maple, the core will be red pine, and the back will be eucalyptus. The face and back are veneer sheets and the core will be lumber blocks.
