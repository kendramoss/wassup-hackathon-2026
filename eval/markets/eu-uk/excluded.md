# Excluded candidates (EU/UK non-preferential origin test set)

One line each. All reasons come from the primary texts fetched on 2026-11-26.

## Court judgments

- C-260/08 HEKO (steel cables 7312, Chinese stranded wire cabled in North Korea): the Court only held that a change of heading is not required; the origin is left to the national court, so no outcome. The customs office said China, the Finanzgericht said North Korea.
- C-26/88 Brother International (electronic typewriters, parts from Japan assembled in Taiwan): the Court gave interpretive criteria and left the origin to the national court; no part values in the text.
- 162/82 Cousin (unbleached cotton yarn from Egypt and the US, dyed and mercerised in Germany): the Court only held Reg. 749/78 invalid; the origin outcome is not stated and the yarn has mixed origins.
- 49/76 Gesellschaft fuer Ueberseehandel (raw casein ground in Germany): the third country of origin is not named in the text, so no expected country.
- C-372/06 Asda Stores (televisions assembled in Turkey): customs said "China and Korea" (split), no value shares in the text; the Court answered validity and chassis questions only.
- C-297/23 P, T-324/21 and Decision (EU) 2021/563 (Harley-Davidson, assembly in Thailand): the texts give no materials and no named country for the "major portion of materials" (the US appears only in the appellants' argument); T-324/21 itself did not open on the Publications Office. Art. 33 DA anti-avoidance branch, not a checkable input.
- C-209/20 Renesola, UK FTT (2018) and UT (2020) stages: counted once under the CJEU judgment; the UT outcome is conditional on the reference and neither decision is on National Archives Find Case Law.

## Regulations

- Implementing Reg. (EU) 2017/2093 (stainless tubes, hot-formed in China, cold-formed in India): terminates an anti-circumvention probe on due-cause grounds, states the Commission does not rely only on customs origin law, no tariff code per product (nine CN codes), no value or origin finding.

## Commission guidance

- Yarn example (primary rule allows natural fibres, chemicals or both): rule illustration with no product facts.
- HS 7227 and 7223 rule wording: rule text, not a case.

## UK tribunal cases

- Manor Park Trading [2010] UKFTT 505 (TC) (cotton bed linen, Pakistani fabric cut and stitched in the UAE, origin Pakistan): the appeal turned on Art. 220 CCC; the text gives no CN code for the linen or the fabric, and printed versus unprinted (6302.21 vs 6302.31) cannot be told, so the good and material codes would both be guesses.
- FMX Food Merchants [2011] UKFTT 20 (TC) (garlic, Cambodia vs China): origin decided on evidence of transhipment and false certificates, no processing facts.
- Kilo Ltd [2022] UKFTT 351 (TC) (citric acid, Malaysia vs China): trading in a free commercial zone with no manufacturing, origin decided on evidence; no processing to test against a rule.
- Truebell Plc [2011] UKFTT 370 (TC) (lamps assembled in Pakistan from Chinese parts): preliminary hearing only; the "over 60% non-originating" figure is the Commission mission's finding, not a tribunal holding.
- Universal Cycles [2025] UKFTT 1208 (TC) and [2026] UKFTT 322 (TC) (bicycles, Sri Lanka vs China): preliminary decisions only; a National Archives search on 2026-11-26 found no merits decision yet.
- Canadian Solar [2024] UKFTT 85 and [2025] UKUT 142 (consigned-from, not origin): not an origin-rule decision.
- Alfred Lamb [2009] UKFTT 220 (TC): preferential origin, out of scope.

## Other sources in the report

- EU public BOI list (CIRCABC): CN code, one-line description and origin only; no materials, shares or processing, so no input can be built.
- National courts (DE, NL, FR) and WCO Origin Compendium: not enumerated in the report and no primary text located; not used.
