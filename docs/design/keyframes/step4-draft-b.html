CBP previously ruled on the current production setup: origin China (N328954). This request concerns a proposed change to that setup: make Motor-fan assembly and PCBA (SMT) in Thailand.

Facts

The merchandise under consideration is a Kobalt 12.5-amp heat gun, model 5208257. It features two fan speeds and a variable temperature control dial and is rated at 1500 watts. The main components are a plastic molded housing that encompasses a heating element, motor-fan assembly, power switch and power cord. It delivers hot air for heat shrinking, stripping paint, bending plastic, drying and thawing. The Kobalt 12.5-amp heat gun, model 5208257 is assembled in Thailand using components sourced from Thailand and China.

The main housing, front cover, heating element, electric motor, fan, motor support and printed circuit board assembly (PCBA) are produced in Thailand. The switch, power cord, wiring, knob, back cover and support ring, heat insulator and fasteners are sourced from China.

In Thailand, the electric motor, fan and motor support will be produced in-house: stamp rotor laminations from silicon steel; stack laminations and wind the rotor; build the stator; assemble the motor; press-fit the fan to the motor spindle.

In Thailand, the printed circuit board assembly (PCBA) will be produced in-house: SMT placement and reflow of the printed circuit board assembly; test the PCBA.

Final assembly in Thailand: press fit the fan to the motor spindle and attach the fan cover; crimp plugs to the heating element wires, add heat shrink tubing, solder wires to the switch and plug the heating element into the switch and PCBA; fasten the PCBA to the main housing, assemble the temperature knob, connect the PCBA to the motor, add the back cover and motor support; secure the power cord, add the switch slider, close the housing with screws, test, label and pack.

Issue

What is the country of origin of Kobalt 12.5-amp heat gun, model 5208257, produced as described above, under the substantial-transformation test?

Requester's position

The requester submits that the merchandise is a product of Thailand, based on these key factors: heating element, electric motor-fan assembly, and PCBA. [REQUESTER TO CONFIRM AND EXPAND WITH COUNSEL.]
