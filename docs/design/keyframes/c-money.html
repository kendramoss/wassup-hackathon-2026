Where "Add money inputs" on the unlocks strip goes. The values are the heat-gun economics: $2.0M imports a year, China 41.7%, Thailand 16.7%, five groups costed at both plants. Three concepts

Duty rates are one per country already in the product, so nobody types a country twice
"Extra cost to move" shows what each move costs before any analysis, without claiming a saving the origin hasn't confirmed yet
C lowers the bar for a first plan: one percentage instead of ten cells, and A's table is one click away
The quick estimate is rough: it gives switch, cord and minor parts $98k where the demo has $105k. Fine for ranking moves, so it says so
B puts money on the form for every user, and most first visits only want the origin
