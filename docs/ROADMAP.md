# Product roadmap

Updated 28 November 2026. Unde is a working hackathon prototype, not a certified enterprise service. This roadmap separates what is already built from unfinished product work and owner-operated infrastructure. It does not promise delivery dates, customers or pilots.

**Status:** **shipped** means implemented; **partial** means a named part works but the full capability does not; **planned** means not implemented. Activation is separate: a feature can be shipped but off. The [current committed deployment switches](../.github/workflows/deploy.yml) and linked release evidence take precedence over older default-off design/status tables. [Scope](scope-and-jurisdiction.md), [measured results](NUMBERS.md) and the [security implementation reference](api/SECURITY.md) provide the detail; a switch alone is not evidence of complete market coverage.

### Acceptance criteria for the planned privacy mode

- **Protect before the boundary.** Photos/PDFs may require local or explicitly approved private OCR; masking text after cloud extraction does not hide the original scan from that service.
- **Preserve legal meaning.** Company names are not the same as origin countries, and prices/relationships can be confidential without being PII. Minimize irrelevant identifiers; use approved processing when a named party or other sensitive fact is legally necessary. Test that tokenization does not change origin conclusions. Restore verified real party details inside the authorized broker/filing export boundary; do not submit placeholder tokens as filing identities.
- **Make disclosure enforceable.** Let an administrator define allowed recipients, fields and exceptions; show the user what leaves the selected boundary. Do not rely only on a prompt telling the agent to be discreet.
- **Verify provider handling.** Document actual model/OCR/embedding recipients, regions, training permissions, retention, caching, logging and deletion limitations. No-training, encryption at rest and zero retention are different claims. Do not advertise unverified provider settings.
- **Keep evidence without another leak.** Record policy decisions and test results without storing raw sensitive content in logs or audit exports. Keep re-identification mappings protected and scoped to the workspace.

The [confidential-data-boundary design](superpowers/specs/2026-11-28-confidential-data-boundary-design.md) defines proposed settings, failure behavior and a bounded final-round release. The [implementation plan](superpowers/plans/2026-11-28-confidential-data-boundary.md) maps it to files, tests and delivery gates. Build execution is tracked separately in [#999](https://github.com/kendramoss/wassup-hackathon-2026/issues/999). Neither document means the controls are shipped. This requires a deliberate revision of the present [model-input policy](design/data-handling.md), not importing the logging sanitizer into inference paths. See the [Altana/cloud-agent privacy research](design/2026-11-28-cloud-agent-document-privacy-research.md) for sources, current code boundaries and unknowns.

## Coverage to explore, not current promises

- **Further USMCA provisions:** encode one missing provision family at a time from primary text, with versioned tariff correlations and explicit missing-fact outcomes. **Dependencies:** authoritative tables and inputs. **Proof:** independent expected checks for thresholds/exceptions and reserved regime-specific cases; keep preferential qualification separate from marking origin.
- **Further agreements:** extend the shared calculator through separate agreement profiles and clause-specific inputs, rather than copying a headline rule. **Dependencies:** usable PSRs, editions and direct-consignment/proof requirements. **Proof:** published examples plus constructed positive/negative/unknown cases; preserve failures and state coverage. Existing calculators are already built, not future inventions.
- **Additional authorities / precedent:** obtain usable primary decisions and rights, normalize dated facts/holdings, exclude test families from retrieval, then evaluate a jurisdiction-specific adapter. **Proof:** rule selection, answer accuracy, abstention and coverage separately. Do not turn existing list-rule checks into broad precedent-model claims.
- **Direct filing:** reuse the prepared package and status backend, then integrate only a documented authorized submission channel. **Dependencies:** access and acceptance rules from the receiving authority. **Proof:** sandbox submission/receipt, idempotency and status reconciliation. Until that access exists, export for broker submission; do not promise a live CBP eRulings API.

New coverage needs regime-specific evidence before a coverage claim. Formal specialist review remains optional post-hackathon work, not a new release gate ([#924](https://github.com/kendramoss/wassup-hackathon-2026/issues/924)). A broker or authority still decides real filings.

## What we can say in the final-round demo

**Target increment, not a completed claim:** a reviewed-text, non-streaming single origin check with a preview/approval, an enforced provider-send gate and a privacy receipt. Raw scans, full agents, remote tools and sensitive MCP exports remain blocked in protected mode until their own tests pass. The [delivery plan](superpowers/plans/2026-11-28-confidential-data-boundary.md) sequences seven tested tasks; the [design](superpowers/specs/2026-11-28-confidential-data-boundary-design.md) specifies settings, deadlines/expiry, unsupported paths, rollout and rollback.

Demonstrate a synthetic identity being replaced, the actual approved provider payload, a blocked stale/foreign-workspace request and a blocked scan/tool attempt. Show privacy, legal-fact preservation, answer coverage and latency measurements separately, only after they exist. No additional redaction-model round trip is planned for the text MVP. The planning estimate and owner dependencies are in the design; without a judging date and passing evidence there is no promise of release before that date.

Until implemented, use public/synthetic documents and label the flow as a design. Cloud AI currently processes authorized inputs; access/log controls do not conceal them from the model. Competitor federation claims do not prove model blindness either.

In every phase: Unde suggests, a licensed broker or authority decides; every published number has a source in [NUMBERS](NUMBERS.md); no customer adoption, security certification or contractual guarantee is implied.
