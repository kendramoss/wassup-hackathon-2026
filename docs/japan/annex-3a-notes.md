# JP-EU EPA and JP-UK CEPA: Annex 3-A notes

The Annex 3-B rule rows of the JP-EU EPA (`eu3.pdf`) and the JP-UK CEPA (`uk3.pdf`)
use MaxNOM, RVC, EXW, FOB, CC, CTH, CTSH, process terms and textile tolerances.
Annex 3-B does not define them. They are defined in Annex 3-A, "Introductory notes
to product specific rules of origin", a separate PDF that was not embedded. Every
note was cited to the schedule PDF, so an Annex 3-A note could not have carried a
correct citation or page link.

## Schema (additive)

A note may carry two optional fields, `source_url` and `citation`, both or neither.
`validateSchedule` rejects half a pair, a padded value, a URL that is not `https://`
or has a `#` fragment, and a pair on a note without a PDF page (page 0) or with a
`ref`. When present, the evidence of the note is cited `"<citation>, PDF page N"`
and links to `"<source_url>#page=N"`, instead of the schedule's citation and URL.
Notes without the pair, and every other agreement, are unchanged.

## What is quoted and how it is scoped

Each quote is the text of its PDF page (or a paragraph of it) as the PDF text layer
gives it, with line breaks and hyphenated breaks joined, without the page footer
and the closing rule line. One note is one chunk on one page, so its page and link
are exact. A chunk that continues on the next page continues in the next note; its
citation names the same Note. EU: 22 notes. UK: 20 notes.

- Schedule scope, no `applies`: Note 1 paragraph 3, Note 2, Note 3, Note 4. They
  define terms for every rule of the schedule.
- Schedule scope with `applies` 25 to 40: Note 5. Its title says "processes
  referred to in Sections V to VII", which are Chapters 25 to 40.
- Section XI, whole section: Notes 6, 7 (paragraphs 1 to 4) and 8 (paragraphs 1 and
  3). Annex 3-B reaches them only through its Section XI note ("see Notes 6, 7 and 8
  of Annex 3-A"); no row cites a Note on its own.
- Section XI with `applies` from the note's own wording: Note 7 paragraph 5 (headings
  51.06 to 51.10 and 52.04 to 52.07), and Note 8 paragraph 2 (Chapters 61 to 63).

Applicability by code range is all the schema can express. A note whose condition
depends on facts (for example Note 3 paragraph 4, which names "Annex 3-C", or the
percentages in Note 7) states its condition in its own words and is not narrowed
further.

## The formulas (the one transcription)

Annex 3-A Note 4 paragraph 2 prints MaxNOM and RVC as typeset fractions, which the
text layer scrambles ("VNM MaxNOM(%) = x100 EXW"). They are written linearly:
`MaxNOM(%) = (VNM / EXW) × 100` and `RVC(%) = ((FOB − VNM) / FOB) × 100`. The build
checks that the page text after the lead-in is exactly the expected scrambled layer,
so a changed PDF fails the build. A reader should compare these two lines with
EU page 8 and UK page 6.

## Cut from Annex 3-A

Not needed by the rule text and not changing qualification: Note 1 paragraphs 1, 2
and 4 (purpose of the annex, the HS 2017 basis and the list of requirement types),
and Note 2 paragraph 4 (meaning of Chapter, heading, Section and subheading). Annex
3-C (UK) and Articles 3.2, 3.3 and 3.5 are other documents and are not embedded; the
quoted text refers to them.
