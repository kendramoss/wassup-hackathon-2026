# JP-EU EPA and JP-UK CEPA: Chapter 3 articles

The Annex 3-B rows of the JP-EU EPA and the JP-UK CEPA are read with the main text
of Chapter 3 (Rules of origin), Section A. Annex 3-A was embedded earlier
([annex-3a-notes.md](annex-3a-notes.md)); the articles below were not. They are
now quoted as source-pinned notes. The product specific rule of Annex 3-B still
governs: the articles are context, and an Unde Labs note says so in the evidence.

Both digests are pinned in `DIGESTS` of `eval/jp/build_psr.py`, next to eu2 and uk2.
The notes are generated by the same script (`CHAPTER_3`), so `schedules.json` stays
reproducible: a rebuild from the pinned PDFs gives the same bytes. The first page of
`eu1.pdf` opens with the tail of the previous chapter and its last page carries
Article 4.1 of Chapter 4; neither is quoted. Each quote is one chunk of one PDF page
as the text layer gives it, line breaks and hyphenated breaks joined, without the page
footer. Evidence is cited `"JP-EU EPA Chapter 3 (Rules of origin), Article 3.x ...,
PDF page N"` (UK: `JP-UK CEPA`) and links to `<source_url>#page=N`.

## Scope

- Schedule scope, no `applies`: the definitions, Article 3.2, Article 3.4, Article 3.5,
  Article 3.6 paragraphs 1 and 2, Article 3.12. They can change the outcome of any
  row (Article 3.12 paragraph 2 reaches change-in-classification and process rows as
  well as wholly obtained ones, so it is not narrowed).
- Wholly obtained rows: Article 3.3 and Article 3.6 paragraph 3 (which speaks of
  products wholly obtained and of rules that require wholly obtained materials)
  carry `applies`. The ranges are the code ranges of the 26 Annex 3-B rows of each
  schedule whose rule text contains "wholly obtained", computed from
  `schedules.json` at build time (`wholly_obtained_ranges`), with ranges that touch
  joined. EU: 0101-0106, 0201-0210, 03, 0401-0410, 0601-0604, 0701-0714, 0801-0814,
  090210-090220, 1001-1008, 1101-1109, 1401-1404, 1507, 1509-1510, 1514-1515,
  1601-1605, 2002-2009, 2106, 2203-2209, 2309. UK: the same except 1102-1103,
  1105-1109, 1601, 1603-1605, and no 2309. A test checks every row range against the
  rows of the schedule: all 26 wholly obtained rows are inside, and no other row
  outside those ranges is reached. Chapter 03 is attached whole because both rows
  of the chapter say "wholly obtained".

## Precedence caveat

One Unde Labs note per agreement (page 0, no URL, text begins "Unde Labs note (not
treaty text)") says that the quoted articles are context for the Annex 3-B rule, do
not replace the listed rule and do not themselves establish origin; that Article 3.2
paragraph 1(b) (products produced exclusively from originating materials) is a route
the Chapter lists, shown as context only, with the listed rule governing unless it
says otherwise; that Annex 3-A is only partly quoted (Note 1 paragraphs 1, 2 and 4 and
Note 2 paragraph 4 are not supplied); that Annex 3-C and Appendix 3-B-1 (UK: also Annex
3-D and Article 3.5 paragraphs 10 to 12), Articles 3.7 to 3.11 and 3.13 to 3.15, the
procedural articles and every Chapter 3 article not listed are not supplied and not
verified; and that a missing fact leaves the question undetermined. It is returned
with the articles on every lookup of the schedule.

## Effect on the development rulings

`jpeval plan -split dev_eval`, offline, 400 cases, 396 eligible requests. Before is
the commit this change is based on; after is the same tree with the regenerated
schedules. Only the evidence blocks of EU and UK requests grow.

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| total request bytes | 7,332,525 | 9,279,221 |
| average request | 18,516 | 23,432 |
| largest request | 35,072 | 36,612 |
| requests that changed | | 182 (181 EU, 1 UK) |
| average EU/UK request | 19,787 | 30,483 |
| bytes added per EU/UK request | | 9,763 minimum, 10,696 average, 13,346 maximum |

A request on a non-wholly-obtained row gains 9,763 bytes (EU, 134 requests), one on a
wholly obtained row 13,346 (EU, 47 requests); the one UK request gains 11,192. This is
an evidence addition. No accuracy change is claimed or measured. `jpeval diagnose` on
the dev and dev_eval splits is identical before and after apart from the data digest.

A lookup hash of every other agreement is byte-identical to the commit before
(`TestOtherEvidenceByteIdentical`). For EU and UK, with the Chapter 3 evidence left
out, the hash of rows, Annex 3-B cell notes and Annex 3-A notes is unchanged
(`TestChapter3OtherAgreementsAndOldEvidenceUnchanged`); the full EU and UK hashes
with the new evidence are pinned in `TestChapter3EvidenceHashes`.
