In your letter dated April 3, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, GHP. The product under consideration is described as the “AF-02 Electric Fireplace.” It is an electric fireplace that includes a heater with fan and features decorative logs and a light emitting diode (LED) produced flame effect that provides the appearance of a burning fire. This fireplace insert is assembled in Malaysia from parts sourced from Malaysia and China. We note that several of the key components such as the heater tube, thermostat, motor, and control components are sourced from China.
