In your letter dated March 25, 2023, you requested a country of origin ruling for a breaker bar tool.The subject breaker bar tool, sometimes referred to as a flex handle, is a hand tool made of steel that has a long handle/neck with a hinged-joint head that is used to tighten or loosen fasteners at various angles. The tool’s head fits onto fasteners via breaker bar socket attachments of various sizes. The socket attachments are neither imported with the article nor included when sold to purchasers. When the tool is grasped by the handle and its hinged-joint head (with socket) is attached to a fastener, it can be positioned at various angles in order to provide the torque necessary to tighten or loosen the fastener.
