In your letter dated January 11, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on 5 models of gaming laptops. The merchandise under consideration is identified as five models of automatic data processing (ADP) machines that are referred to as Razer Blade 18 Gaming Laptop personal computers (PCs), models numbers, RZ09-05827ER3-R3U1, RZ09-05828ER3-R3U1, RZ09-05829ER4-R3U1, RZ09-0582DER9-R3U1, and RZ09-0582EER9-R3U1, which are all equipped with Windows 11 Home NexGen Premium operating systems and Razer’s proprietary software called Razer Synapse. All five models have 3 USB Type-A Ports, 2 USB Type-C Ports, one UHS-II SD card reader, and one HDMU 2.1 output. Each model in consideration is similar in appearance, but has variations in the memory size, GPU type, and storage capacity, based on the model configurations. In your submission, you state that the manufacturing process for all five models of the Razer Blade 18 PCs is the same. The motherboard Printed Circuit Board Assemblies (PCBAs) are assembled in Vietnam using components sourced from various countries of origin. The production of the PCBAs starts with blank printed circuit boards (PCBs) and advances through many steps, such as surface mount technology (SMT), laser etching, solder paste application, pick and placement of the components through a reflow oven, and auto-inspection and testing of the parts. Once the motherboard PCBAs are completed and tested, they are shipped to China where the Razer Blade 18 gaming laptops are assembled. Each assembly process consists of inserting the completed motherboard PCBA onto the bottom cover and attaching parts, such as an LCD panel, keyboard, storage, Wi-Fi antenna, webcam, cooling fans, touchpad, speaker, battery, as well as installation of software (Windows 11 Home Operating System and Razer Synapse software). Lastly, these laptop PCs are inspected, tested, labeled, and packaged for export into the United States.
