In your letter dated August 19, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on a metal lantern wax warmer for purposes of current trade remedies. The product under consideration is metal wax warmer in the form of a lantern. Each wax warmer consists of a finial (iron ring and hanger), a wax melt dish atop, a metal lantern cap, a light bulb, a socket, a protective cage (cage bars and guard ring), a glass cylinder, faux wood accent rings (one circling just under the top cap and the other framing the bottom base), a pedestal base, a power cord, and wax. The submitted literature indicates that all components are imported from China, except for the power cord and the wax, which are produced in Vietnam. The assembly process in Vietnam begins by welding the components imported from China. Specifically, the metal bars and the rings are welded into complete protective cages, and the various components are welded to create the wax melt dish. These parts are then combined with the Vietnamese-made power cord and wax to make the finished wax warmer. The narrated assembly flowchart illustrates that the manufacturing process in Vietnam includes spot welding, polishing, powder coating, paint spraying, and hand-painting (using Vietnamese-origin coating and paint). Finally, the finished products undergo testing and inspection before being packaged for export.
