In your letter dated July 3, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Galaxy Industrial (Thailand) Co., Ltd. The products under consideration are gate valves (ranging from DN50/2" to DN600/24") and butterfly valves (ranging from DN50/2" to DN800/32"). Each butterfly valve consists of a cast iron body, a seat, a valve disc, and a handle for manual actuation. Each gate valve features a cast iron body with flanged ends for pipeline connection, a handwheel for manual actuation, a resilient-seated closure, and a stem. While the valves vary in size, they are all designed to control the flow of fluid. With respect to origin, the final assembly of both types of valves occurs in Thailand using components from China and a valve body from Thailand. Prior to the final assembly, the valve body and other components undergo precision machining to achieve exact dimensions and tolerances. Machining operations include drilling, boring, turning, and milling to ensure a proper fit of the all the components. Afterwards, the valve body is subjected to a surface treatment and a coating process. The final assembly begins by positioning a valve bushing, a rubber seat, a disc and a stem in a valve body in sequence. Additional boring operations are completed to refine the internal dimensions and tolerances necessary to achieve a proper fit of the components. Afterwards, the inserted components are mechanically pressed in place. Once complete, pins are inserted into the assembly to retain the components in their operational positions. Lastly, an actuator is connected and screwed onto the valve. Inspections and testing are completed throughout the assembly process.
