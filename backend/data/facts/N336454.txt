In your letter dated November 9, 2023, you requested a country of origin ruling on Imazethapyr Herbicide.You provided the following description of the processing involved in its manufacture.Imazethapyr Herbicide is formulated in Malaysia. Kenso will import Imazethapyr (CAS # 81335-77-5), a stable dormant solid form molecule, from China in bulk bags. In a product batch, Imazethapyr (CAS # 81335-77-5) will chemically react with Ammonium Hydroxide Solution (CAS # 1336-21-6) in the presence of water to form Imazethapyr Ammonium Salt (CAS # 101917-66-2). The process continues until 40% Imazethapyr Ammonium Salt Manufacturing Concentrate (CAS # 101917-66-2) is produced in liquid form. The 40% Imazethapyr Ammonium Salt Manufacturing Concentrate (CAS # 101917-66-2) is then mixed proportionately with other ingredients and further diluted with water to obtain the end use finished product for shipment to the USA in 1000L IBC Totes.The "country of origin" is defined in
