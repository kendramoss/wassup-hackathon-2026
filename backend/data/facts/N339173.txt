In your letter dated March 28, 2024, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Grupo Industrial Persal SA de CV. The product to be imported consists of part numbers 1290826051 and 1291109131, which are described as an internal steel structure for a wind turbine. Part number 1290826051 is the front assembly of the structure and part number 1291109131 is the rear assembly of the structure. The complete structure measures 40’ length x 8’ width x 9’ height.In your request, you suggest that the internal steel structure is properly classified under subheading 8503.00.9570, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS). We disagree. In noting that the subject merchandise consists of structural components that machinery is mounted to or within, they are not an integral component to the generator without which the wind generator could not function. As such, they are not considered parts of the generator and subheading 8503.00.9570, HTSUS, is not applicable.
