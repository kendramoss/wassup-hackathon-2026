In your letter dated August 7, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on shock absorbers. The articles under consideration are shock absorbers, Part Numbers AQ51010, AQ60416, AQ60604, AY90132, and BQ65083, which are components of suspension struts used in passenger vehicles. Their function is to accelerate and attenuate the vibration of the frame and body, and to improve the ride comfortability. You state that the shock absorbers are imported separately from the suspension struts. Each shock absorber is composed of an inner tube, piston rod, and outer cylinder from Malaysia, as well as other minor components, and components of a valve assembly and piston assembly from China and Malaysia, which are assembled together in Malaysia. The final assembly in Malaysia includes washing components, welding, inserting, compressing, coating/paining, and testing.
