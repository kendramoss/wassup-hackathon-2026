In your letter dated October 27, 2023, you requested a tariff classification and country of origin determination ruling for marking purposes and for purposes of applying trade remedies under Section 301 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, from China, which you filed on behalf of your client Grupo Antolin Silao, S.A. de C.V.The product under consideration is an automotive Load Floor Trunk Automotive V & C 167 load floor HL, which consists of a substrate of a load floor with t-nuts from China, fabric from the Netherlands, adhesive, screws, brackets, and labels from the United States, and clips from Canada. You state that all components are imported into Mexico, where it is assembled together into a complete load floor trunk.
