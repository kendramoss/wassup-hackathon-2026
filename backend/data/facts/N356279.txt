In your letter dated November 20, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Stellar Jewels, LLC. You submitted photographs, product description, and manufacturing information for our review. The subject merchandise is a gold rope chain necklace which is constructed of United States (U.S.) origin gold. The manufacturing steps are as follows: The gold is melted, cast, drawn, coiled, and cut in the United States. The U.S links are exported to Bolivia. The individual links are assembled including weaving, soldering, adding clasps and end rings, cleaning, and polishing in Bolivia.
