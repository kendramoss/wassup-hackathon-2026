In your letter dated February 26, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on Metronidazole Topical Gel 0.75% in dosage form. Metronidazole Topical Gel is a medicinal preparation containing Metronidazole, a synthetic nitroimidazole antimicrobial agent, as the active ingredient. It is indicated for the treatment of inflammatory papules and pustules of rosacea. In your letter, you stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Metronidazole is manufactured in India and that the finished topical gel in dosage form is also made in India. In India, the Metronidazole will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Metronidazole Topical Gel, USP 0.75% in dosage form.
