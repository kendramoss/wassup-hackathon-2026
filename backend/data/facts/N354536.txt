This is in response to your letter dated October 2, 2025, on behalf of Galanz Americas Limited Company (“Galanz Americas”), you request a United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement (USMCA) eligibility for preferential tariff treatment on microwave ovens. The merchandise subject to this ruling request is five models of microwave ovens, all of which will be assembled in Mexico and ultimately imported into the United States. The microwave ovens each consist of the following key assemblies or components: a magnetron, cavity kits, outer enclosures, a door plate assembly, a lamp, a sensor, an outer wrapper assembly, a bottom plate assembly, a door assembly (containing a door hook, frame and shielding board), and an air duct.
