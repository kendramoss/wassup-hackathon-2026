In your letter dated September 24, 2025, you requested a ruling on behalf of your client, Ashi Diamonds LLC . You submitted photographs, product description, and manufacturing information on a gold bracelet. Style 70163FGWG is a white gold bracelet with diamonds. The manufacturing steps are as follows: Product design. This is performed in United States Creating the wax model. This is performed in United States Alloying metal casting. This is performed in the United States. Spruing and investing. This is performed in the United States. Wax Burnout (lost wax process). This is performed in the United States. Cooling and mold break-out. This is performed in the United States. Sprue cutting. This is performed in the United States. Assembling the links. This is performed in India. Setting of gems and final finishing. This is performed in India.
