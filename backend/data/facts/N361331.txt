In your letter dated  May 7, 2026 , you requested a country of origin ruling on a thread depth tire gauge.Descriptive literature was provided for our review.The item under consideration is described as a thread depth tire gauge (item number SP019), which isdesigned for the measurement of remaining vehicle tire tread depth. The gauge helps monitor tire wear,thereby enhancing driving safety, mitigating the risk of hydroplaning, and ensuring compliance with legal tiretread depth regulations. To operate the gauge, users should first clear the tire tread surface of any debris ormoisture. Subsequently, the measuring probe is inserted vertically into a tire tread groove until the base platerests flush against the tire surface. The tread depth value is then obtained by reading the scale on the gauge. Areading within the green zone signifies normal tire condition. The yellow zone serves as a warning, indicatingthat tire wear is nearing the replacement limit. A reading in the red zone necessitates immediate tirereplacement. The device measures 85mm in length, 11.2mm in width, and 26.7mm in height, with a weightof 9.5g. Its construction comprises an ABS base, a carbon steel measuring pin, a stainless-steel hexagonalcolumn, a steel pipe, and a carbon steel pen clip.The thread depth tire gauges are manufactured in Malaysia with components from both Malaysia and China.The Malaysian components include the plastic base, measuring pin, and hexagonal stainless-steel column.The stainless-steel pipe and carbon steel pen clip are sourced from China. The eight-step manufacturingprocess starts in Malaysia with the injection molding of the plastic base before measuring pin stamping andnickel plating. Step three and four both occur in China and include the steel tube cutting and pen clipstamping followed by chromium plating each component. Back in Malaysia, the ruler injection molding andprinting occurs before final assembly, testing and packaging.
