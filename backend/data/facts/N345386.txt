In your letter dated January 24, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Weifang Great Paper Co., Ltd, for the purposes of duty calculation and Section 301 applicability. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review.The items under consideration are packages of facial tissues. They come in double ply and in either 1 or 2 folds. Per your submission, the bulk rolls of facial tissue stock are manufactured in Indonesia. You describe the paper as White/Kraft paper: virgin; layer: 2; with a 12-15 GSM. These parent rolls of paper, which measure 1800 mm in width and have a maximum roll diameter of 1800 mm, are then sent to China, where the paper is embossed, folded, cut, subjected to quality control, and packaged.
