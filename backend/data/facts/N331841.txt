In your letter dated March 29, 2023, you requested a country of origin ruling on Lenalidomide Capsules.Lenalidomide, imported in 2.5 mg, 5 mg, 10 mg, 15 mg, 20 mg, and 25 mg capsules, is in a class of medications called immunomodulatory agents. It is used in combination with dexamethasone for the treatment of adult patients with multiple myeloma (MM).You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Lenalidomide, is manufactured in Latvia and that the finished Lenalidomide Capsules in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Lenalidomide will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Lenalidomide Capsules in dosage form.
