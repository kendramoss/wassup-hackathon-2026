In your letter dated July 11, 2024, on behalf of Dr. Reddy's Laboratories, Inc. you requested a tariff classification and country of origin determination ruling.Doxycycline, imported in 40 mg capsules, is a medicinal preparation containing Doxycycline Monohydrate, an antibiotic, as the active ingredient. It is indicated for the treatment of bacterial infections, including acne, rosacea, urinary and respiratory tract infections, eye infections, gum disease, gonorrhea, chlamydia, and syphilis.
