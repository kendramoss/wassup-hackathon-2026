In your letter dated April 24, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Xstar Global Sourcing LLC, for the purpose of duty calculation and Section 301 applicability. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review. The paper products under consideration are "hand towels" (paper towels, roll towels, and other hand towels) and "kitchen towels." They come in either single, double or triple ply with 5-fold, c-fold, v- fold, multi-fold or no fold. Per your submission, the bulk rolls of hand towel stock are manufactured in Indonesia. You describe the paper as White/Kraft 100% wood pulp virgin paper; layer:1-2-3; 18-47g/sm. These parent rolls of tissue paper, which measure in multiple widths from 1840mm up to 2800mm and have a maximum roll diameter 2800mm, are then sent to China, where the paper is unwound, embossed, perforated, cut to length and width and are either re-rolled or folded. The hand towels are then subjected to quality control and packaged. The bulk rolls of kitchen towel stock are manufactured in Indonesia. You describe the paper as White/Kraft 100% wood pulp virgin paper; layer:2-3; 18-23g/sm. These parent rolls of tissue paper, which measure 2800mm in width and have a maximum roll diameter 2800mm, are then sent to China, where the paper is unwound, embossed, perforated, rewound and cut to length and width. The kitchen towels are then subjected to quality control and packaged.
