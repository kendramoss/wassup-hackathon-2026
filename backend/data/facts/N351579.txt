In your letter dated July 22, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on Mesalamine Extended-Release Capsules USP. Mesalamine, imported in 0.375 gram extended-release capsules, is a medicinal preparation containing Mesalamine, also known as 5-aminosalicylic acid (5-ASA), as the active ingredient. It is indicated for the maintenance of remission of ulcerative colitis in adults. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Mesalamine, is manufactured in Italy and that the finished capsules in dosage form are made in China. In China, the Mesalamine will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Mesalamine Extended-Release Capsules in dosage form.
