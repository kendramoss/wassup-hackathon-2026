In your letter dated June 11, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on an oven latch assembly. The product under consideration is an oven latch assembly used in a gas range where the oven door is closed manually but locked by oven latch. It is powered and controlled by the main board from the gas range to lock and unlock the oven door. You provided a Bill of Materials, which indicates that the oven latch assembly consists of a latch, pin, rod, power driver containing an electric motor, screw and four metal brackets in different shapes. All of the components are of Chinese origin and assembled into the finished oven latch assembly in Thailand. The assembly process in Thailand is described as follows: Riveting components with the metal brackets 1, 2 and 3 Installing the latch Installing the rod Riveting components with the metal bracket 4 Installing the power driver Test and package
