In your letter dated April 1, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on an air fryer on behalf of your client, Midea Consumer Electric (Vietnam) Co., Ltd (“MVN”). The product under consideration is described as the “CN Air Fryer II,” MVN model number MF-CN55M1. This air fryer is a domestic electric countertop device that cooks food by circulating hot air around the ingredients using fan blades. In this version, the motor will be produced in Thailand. The air fryer is manufactured in Vietnam through a three-stage process. The first stage involves three phases: Phase One involves the production of the structural body and other plastic components (i.e., heating insulation block, front cover of the frying bucket, handle, bracket, power supply box, bottom cover, motor cover, air outlet window, circular guide rail, inner bottom plate bracket, top cover, and main body) by an injection molding process. The plastic raw materials are sourced from Vietnam, Thailand, Cambodia, and China. Phase Two involves the production of the metal parts, including the surrounding wall and reflecting cover, from steel coiled materials. The fabrication will be completed in Vietnam from steel sourced from either Vietnam or China. Phase Three involves the production of the heating tube or heater, which is manufactured by a third-party supplier of MVN in Vietnam from various raw materials. In the next stage, the display and power boards are manufactured by a third-party supplier in Vietnam from various raw materials. 

In the final stage, also performed in Vietnam, the Vietnamese-origin body and the other plastic and metal parts, along with the remaining components originating from Vietnam and China, will be placed on the main assembly line to produce the finished ?CN Air Fryer II.?? It will then be tested, inspected, and packaged.
