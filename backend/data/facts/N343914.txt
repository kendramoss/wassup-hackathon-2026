In your letter dated November 7, 2024, you requested a country of origin ruling onpost-industrial recycling (PIR) polyethylene terephthalate (PET) flakes, on behalf of your client Polarpak Company.The merchandise under consideration is referred to as post-industrial recycling (PIR) polyethylene terephthalate (PET) flakes. They are also sometimes referred to as “regrinds”. The PIR PET flakes are produced as a result of manufacturing operations performed at Polarpak’s Canadian manufacturing facility.Virgin PET pellets will be imported from various countries, including China, to its manufacturing facility. At the Canadian facility, the pellets are melted and extruded into PET sheets. The sheets are heated and stamped; the stamped PET is then formed into finished food packaging containers.
