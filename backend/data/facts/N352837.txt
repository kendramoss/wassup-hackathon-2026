In your letter dated August 25, 2025, on behalf of your client, Globe Union North America Group, you requested a country of origin ruling to apply current trade remedies and for marking purposes. The product at issue is a two-handle faucet, model number F8FA0069. The faucet, which can be installed in a lavatory, is comprised of two handles, a body, an aerator, cover plates and cartridge valves. With respect to origin, the final assembly of the faucet occurs in China and begins with cartridge valves from Taiwan undergoing various tests and inspections. Afterwards, two cartridge valves are inserted into the valve chambers of a faucet body and secured in place using a retaining nut. In the next step, the two handles are attached to a valve stem and fastened in place with set screws. Subsequently, the cover plates and an aerator are installed onto the spout section of the faucet body. Once complete, the faucet undergoes final testing.
