In your letter dated February 10, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on Deflazacort Tablets.Deflazacort, imported in 6 mg, 18, mg, 30 mg and 36 mg tablets, is in a class of medications called corticosteroids. It is indicated for the treatment of Duchenne muscular dystrophy (DMD) in patients 5 years of age and older.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Deflazacort, is manufactured in Italy and that the finished Deflazacort Tablets in dosage form are also made in Italy. In Italy, the Deflazacort will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Deflazacort Tablets in dosage form.
