In your letter dated August 12, 2025, on behalf of Fantasia Accessories Ltd. and Fujian Haojin Toiletries Co., Ltd., you requested a country of origin and marking ruling. The Lulu® Nail Polish Collection contains 14 mini nail polishes (0.07 fluid ounce) in various colors including purple, blue, green, pink, yellow, silver glitter, and others. The nail polishes are packaged together for retail sale. You state that the raw materials used to produce the finished nail polishes include humectants, preservatives, solvents, and cosmetic colorants. The raw materials will be sent to either Malaysia or Vietnam where they will be weighed and blended under controlled conditions using a mixing machine to produce a stable bulk. Preservatives and other ingredients will be added in a cooled-down phase. The formulation will then be bulk tested for pH, microbial content, and stability. After testing, the bulk formulation will be sent from Malaysia or Vietnam to China where it will be filled into glass retail containers, affixed with retail labels, and packaged in cartons for exportation to the United States. All packaging materials (bottles, caps, cartons, labels, and cardboard) originate in China.
