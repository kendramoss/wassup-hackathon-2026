In your letter dated December 6, 2018, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of your client, Taskmaster Components of Mt. Pleasant, Texas. Descriptive literature and pictures were provided with your request. The items under consideration have been identified as an ArcWheel 22.5 and 24.5, and ArcWheel Heavy Duty Series, which are steel rims, used in on-road vehicles such as pick-up trucks, trailers, heavy-duty trucks and other on-road type vehicles. You state that the steel is imported from China to Mexico, where it is made into rims of various sizes and types.
