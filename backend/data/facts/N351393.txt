In your letter dated July 16, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling. The merchandise under consideration is identified as the passive USB A to Lightning Assembly. The subject cable consists of a length of insulated conductor with a USB-A style connector at one end and a lightning-style connector at the other. You state that the cable is a passive device used to transmit high-speed data signals between a number of devices, such as computers and smart phones. You state that the manufacturing process begins in Taiwan with a tinned copper wire conductor being extruded, twisted, braided, jacketed and insulated prior to being cut to the desired length. The unfinished cables will be shipped to China for the final manufacturing process. In China, the ends of the cable are prepared and the connectors of Chinese origin are assembled onto each end of the cable. Then the finished cable is packaged for shipment to the United States.
