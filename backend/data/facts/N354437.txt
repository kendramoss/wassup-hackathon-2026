In your letter dated September 30, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on Bisoprolol Fumarate and Hydrochlorothiazide Tablets. Bisoprolol Fumarate and Hydrochlorothiazide, imported in 2.5 mg/6.25 mg, 5 mg/6.25 mg and 10 mg/6.25 mg tablets, is a medicinal preparation containing Bisoprolol Fumarate (a beta blocker) and Hydrochlorothiazide (a diuretic), as the active ingredients. It is indicated for the treatment of hypertension. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs), Bisoprolol Fumarate and Hydrochlorothiazide, are manufactured in India and China, respectively. In India, these two APIs will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Bisoprolol Fumarate and Hydrochlorothiazide Tablets, USP in dosage form.
