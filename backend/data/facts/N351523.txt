In your letter dated July 21, 2025, on behalf of Fantasia Accessories Ltd. and Fujian Haojin Toiletries Co., Ltd., you requested a country of origin and marking ruling. The subject product is described as a lip balm sold under the brand name BBC Beauty®, designed to intensely protect and care for lips. The lip balm will be packaged for retail sale in a 0.12-ounce plastic tube applicator with a cap. The raw materials used to produce the finished lip balm will be sourced from various countries. The ingredients will be sent to either Malaysia or Vietnam for processing. In Malaysia or Vietnam, the ingredients will be weighed and blended under controlled conditions to produce a stable bulk. This includes heating oil and wax in separate phases, then combining the ingredients using a mixing machine. Preservatives and other ingredients will then be added. The formulation will then be bulk tested for pH, microbial content, and stability. The bulk formulation will then be sent from Malaysia or Vietnam to China where it will be filled into retail containers, affixed with retail labels, and packaged in cartons for exportation to the United States. All packaging materials (tubes, caps, and cardboard) originate in China.
