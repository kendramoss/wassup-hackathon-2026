In your letter, dated December 13, 2021, you requested a tariff classification and country of origin ruling on printed artwork. Photos and a product description were provided for our review.The artwork sample shown in your submission depicts a small girl seated by the shore with the ocean and sunset in the background. The picture is digitally printed onto canvas that has subsequently been stretched onto wood stretcher bars. The finished works will range in size from 10” x 13.5” up to 30” x 40”.You propose classification in subheading 4911.91.4040, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS). We agree.
