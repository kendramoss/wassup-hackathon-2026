In your letter dated March 18, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Anqiu Xiangyu Packaging and Printing Co., Ltd, for the purpose of duty calculation and Section 301 applicability. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review The paper towels under consideration are described as packages of “hand roll towels” and “center pull towels." They come in a variety of plies (single, double, and triple). Per your submission, the bulk rolls of hand towel stock are manufactured in Indonesia. You describe the paper as White/Kraft paper: virgin; layer: 1/2; 26-40 g/sm. These parent rolls of paper, which measure in multiple widths from 1400 mm up to 2850 mm and have a maximum roll diameter of 2850 mm, are then sent to China, where the paper is embossed, re-rolled and cut to size. The finished product is then subjected to quality control, and packaged.
