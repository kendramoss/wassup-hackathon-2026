The factory inThailand builds these local components and joins them with the Chinese-origin components to finish theproduct.The manufacturing steps in Thailand include:Filament forming and bracket weldingAssembly and sealing of the bulbEvacuation and gas fillingInstallation of the lamp baseFinal packagingThe provided information shows that the Chinese components make up about 66 percent of the totalcomponent cost.
