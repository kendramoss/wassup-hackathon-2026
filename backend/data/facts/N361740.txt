In your letter dated May 22, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling for arachidonic acid oil on behalf of your client, Louis Dreyfus Company Ingredients GmbH. A narrative description of the manufacturing process accompanied your letter. The merchandise under consideration is “ARA Oil Gold AP.” You have outlined a scenario in which unrefined arachidonic acid (ARA) oil, derived from the fungus Mortierella Alpina, is imported into Germany, from China, and mixed with additional ingredients to produce the “ARA Oil Gold AP.” In Germany, the unrefined ARA oil is combined with fully refined sunflower oil from the European Union, tocopherol rich extract (E 306) from Spain, and ascorbyl palmitate (E 304) from China. Once combined, the mixture undergoes a process of bleaching, refining, filtration, distillation, and deodorization to produce the finished oil. The finished product is then packaged and shipped to the United States, where it will be used for human consumption in food products. You state the “ARA Oil Gold AP” is composed of approximately 80-85 percent mortierella alpina oil, 15-20 percent high oleic sunflower oil, 0.15 percent tocopherol rich extract, and 0.04 percent ascorbyl palmitate.
