In your letter dated June 4, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on Nitroglycerin Ointment, USP 0.4%. Nitroglycerin Ointment is a medicinal preparation containing diluted Nitroglycerin, a vasodilator, as the active ingredient. It is indicated for the treatment of moderate to severe pain associated with chronic anal fissure. In your letter, you stated diluted Nitroglycerin is a mixture of 10% nitroglycerin in propylene glycol. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), diluted Nitroglycerin, is manufactured in the United States and that the finished ointment in dosage form is made in India. In India, the diluted Nitroglycerin will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final ointment in dosage form.
