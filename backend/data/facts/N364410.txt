In your letter dated August 20, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on an automotive knuckle assembly on behalf of Seohan Innobility Co., Ltd. The article under consideration is identified as the MV/ME Knuckle Assembly, part number 51710-DO000. The subject knuckle assembly is a component of a motor vehicle’s front suspension system. It provides mounting points for the shock absorber, lower control arm, tie rod, constant velocity (CV) joint, wheel speed sensor, brake caliper, and wheel. The assembly supports the front wheel load and facilitates functions associated with steering, braking, wheel speed detection, and shock absorption. The finished knuckle assembly consists primarily of the MV/ME Cast Knuckle, part number M51715-DO000, and two sockets that are press-fit into the knuckle. The cast knuckle provides the principal structure of the finished assembly and the mounting locations for the various suspension, steering, braking, and wheel-related components. According to the information submitted, the manufacturing process begins with Chinese-origin aluminum ingot, aluminum scrap, aluminum-strontium alloy, and magnesium. The aluminum is imported into South Korea in ingot block form, while the aluminum scrap consists of defective products and gate/runner cut-off scrap intended for remelting. The aluminum-strontium alloy is imported in round-bar form, and the magnesium is imported in small ingot or block form. In South Korea, these materials undergo melting, tapping, degassing, casting, trimming, cutting, finishing/deburring, heat treatment, X-ray inspection, and packaging. Through these operations, the Chinese-origin materials are formed into the specifically shaped MV/ME Cast Knuckle, part number M51715-DO000. The cast knuckle is subsequently supplied to another manufacturer in South Korea, where it undergoes two stages of machining. The machining operations include facing, boring, drilling, rapping, counterboring, milling, and chamfering of various reference faces, mounting holes, seating faces, and other features
associated with the brake caliper, control arms, ABS sensor, and other components. Following the machining operations, dockets are press-fit into the T/Arm and L/Arm mounting locations. The completed article is then inspected and supplied as the MV/ME Knuckle Assembly, part number 51710-DO000.
