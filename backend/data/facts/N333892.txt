In your letter dated July 10, 2023, on behalf of Encube Ethicals Private Limited, you requested a country of origin ruling on Adapalene Gel USP 0.3%.Adapalene Gel USP 0.3%, imported in 45 grams tubes, is a topical retinoid. It is indicated for the topical treatment of acne vulgaris in patients 12 years of age and older.You indicated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Adapalene, is manufactured in Spain and that the finished Adapalene Gel 0.3% in dosage form is made in India. In India, the Adapalene will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Adapalene Gel in dosage form.
