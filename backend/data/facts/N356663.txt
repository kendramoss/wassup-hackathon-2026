In your letter dated December 2, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on a Non-Locking Doorknob Latch Set on behalf of Taiwan Fu Hsing Industrial Co., Ltd. The article under consideration is described as a Non-Locking Doorknob Latch Set. The set is made primarily of steel and consists of an inside knob assembly, an outside knob assembly, a latch assembly, and a strike plate that are packaged together for sale and ready for installation. The inside and outside doorknob assembly, the strike plate, and the latch assembly are each separate pieces and not attached to each other within the packaging. You describe a manufacturing process in which the internal and external components of the doorknob assembly, which include the spindle, rose, tab, spring cage, and coupler assemblies, and the latch assemblies are produced in Thailand and/or China. Those components are shipped to Taiwan where they will be assembled together with Taiwan-origin doorknobs and the strike plate to create the Non-Locking Doorknob Latch Set. The set is then packaged in Taiwan and shipped to the U.S.
