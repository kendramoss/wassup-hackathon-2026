In your letter dated October 21, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on Phenylephrine Hydrochloride Ophthalmic Solution, USP 2.5%. Phenylephrine Hydrochloride Ophthalmic Solution is a medicinal preparation containing Phenylephrine Hydrochloride, an alpha-1 adrenergic receptor agonist, as the active ingredient. It is indicated to dilate the pupil. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Phenylephrine Hydrochloride is manufactured in Taiwan and that the finished ophthalmic solution in dosage form is made in India. In India, Phenylephrine Hydrochloride will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Phenylephrine Hydrochloride Ophthalmic Solution in dosage form.
