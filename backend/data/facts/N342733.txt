We note that the cables are rated at 125 Volts.In your request, you state that the manufacturing process occurs in Vietnam and begins with Vietnamese single copper wire being stranded to the specific thickness. This conductor is then encapsulated with PVC insulation via the extrusion process before it is cooled and cut to length.
