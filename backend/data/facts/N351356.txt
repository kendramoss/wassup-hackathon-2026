The subject articles, identified as a “Waterproof Featherbed Protector” and “Mattress Box Spring Protector,” are covers designed to encase and protect a featherbed, mattress, and box spring from bed bugs, dust mites and allergens. The protectors are composed of 100 percent cotton woven fabric which has been dyed white and is laminated on the inner surface with a sheeting of compact thermoplastic polyurethane (TPU) to protect from spills, stains and allergens. The items are not stuffed or filled. The protectors feature an opening with a hidden zipper closure located at the bottom end. The featherbed protector includes a 3-inch side gusset, and the mattress/box spring protector comes in three heights (9, 12 and 16 inches). All protectors are available in the following standard bed sizes: Twin, 39" x 75"; Twin XL, 39" x 80"; Full, 54" x 75"; Queen, 60" x 80"; and King, 78" x 80.” The protectors are available in the following SKU and product numbers:
