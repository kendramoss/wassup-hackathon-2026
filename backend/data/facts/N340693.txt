In your letter dated June 12, 2024, you requested a country of origin ruling.There are five items at issue with this request, which are all described as automobile booster cables used to assist in charging the battery of the vehicle. The specific part numbers and descriptions are listed below.782-5251: Commercial Heavy Duty Booster Cable 782-5252: Commercial Heavy Duty Booster Cable 782-5254: Professional Heavy Duty Booster Cable 782-5259: Commercial Heavy Duty Booster Cable782-5260: Commercial Heavy Duty Booster Cable Service Vehicle Permanent Connector Cable The first three cables are comprised of a length of insulated cable conductor at varying length and thickness depending on the model with two clamps at each end. The fourth cable is comprised of a length of insulated cable with clamps on one end and lug connectors on the other. The final cable is comprised of a length of cable with lug connectors on one end and a two-pronged plastic connector at the other. In your request, you state that the manufacturing process for all items is substantially similar and occurs in Thailand through the following steps. Copper wire of Thai origin is twisted and braided before being insulated via the extrusion process. The various connectors and clamps, of Chinese origin, are then assembled and added to the ends of the cables. The finished cables are then retail packaged for shipment to the United States.The “country of origin” is defined in
