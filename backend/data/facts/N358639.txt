In your letter dated February 9, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on a pipe assembly for marking purposes and for purposes of applying additional duties. The article under consideration is a pipe assembly heater (Part 21022-9BT0A), which is used in passenger vehicles. You describe it as a circulation channel of the coolant from the radiator to the engine. It is situated between the radiator and the engine. The pipe assembly heater consists of four pipes (pipe 11, 12, 13, and 14), a flange, and two brackets. According to the flowcharts you supplied, the steel pipes, flange, and brackets are made in South Korea. You state that pipe bending occurs in South Korea. The components are then sent to Mexico where the following processes occur: spot welding of pipe 11 with pipe 14, spot welding of the welded product with one bracket, spot welding of pipe 13 with another bracket, caulking pipe 12 with the flange, expanding and brazing the product, adjusting the shape, leak testing, plating, and inspection.
