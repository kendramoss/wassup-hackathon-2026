In your letter dated March 12, 2026, you requested a tariff classification and country of origin ruling for the purpose of marking on behalf of your client, Transtex LLC. You also inquire whether the unit is eligible for duty free treatment under the United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement (USMCA). The product at issue is referred to as a DClimate Truck AC Unit, part number 910016-01- HC7000U Espar RH. The unit is designed to be installed in heavy trucks for the purpose of changing temperature and humidity levels within the cabin of the truck. The unit primarily consists of a housing that features a top cover, base and an evaporator coil, a compressor, a compressor driver, a converter assembly and a condenser shroud assembly with a blower. The unit is imported packaged with a duct kit and a heater.
