The yarns will be used in commercial settings to produce socks.The manufacturing steps for the gimped elastomeric filament yarns are as follows:China- 100 percent nylon filament yarn is extruded and spooled.- Nylon yarn (heading 5402) is shipped to Peru.Brazil- 100 percent spandex filament yarn is extruded and spooled.- Spandex yarn (heading 5402) is shipped to Peru.Peru:- 100 percent nylon yarn is wrapped around a 100 percent spandex core yarn to create a gimped yarn.- The gimped yarn is packaged on a cone and exported to the United States.
