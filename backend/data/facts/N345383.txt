In your letter dated January 23, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Weifang Great Paper Co., Ltd, for the purpose of Section 301 applicability. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review.The items under consideration are packages of paper napkins, including “dinner napkins,” “luncheon napkins,” “beverage napkins,” and "tall-fold napkins," which come in a variety of sizes and folds. Per your submission, the bulk rolls of tissue paper are manufactured in Indonesia. You describe the paper as White/Kraft paper: virgin; layer: 1/2; with a 12-18 GSM. These parent rolls of tissue paper, which measure in multiple widths from 330 mm up to 1960 mm and have a maximum roll diameter 1960 mm, are then sent to China, where the tissue paper is embossed, cut to size, folded, subjected to quality control, and packaged.
