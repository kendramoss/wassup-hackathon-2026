In your letter dated June 23, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Dongguan Pengxing Paper Co., Ltd, for the purpose of duty calculation and Section 301 applicability. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review. The paper product under consideration is "toilet tissue." They come in either single, double or triple ply rolls. Per your submission, the bulk rolls of toilet tissue stock are manufactured in Indonesia. You describe the paper as White/Kraft 100% wood pulp virgin paper; layer:2; 12.5-14 g/sm. These parent rolls of tissue paper, which measure 2800mm in width and have a maximum roll diameter of 2800mm, are then sent to China, where the paper is unwound, embossed, rewound and cut to size. The toilet tissue is then subjected to quality control and packaged.
