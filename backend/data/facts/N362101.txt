In your letter dated June 5, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling. The merchandise under consideration are wireless video game remote controllers, referred to as the Razer Raiju V3 Pro, models RZ06-05580100-R3UA, RZ06-05580200-R3UA, and RZ06-05580300-R3UA. These are 2.4 GHz wireless radio frequency (RF) gaming remote controllers for the PlayStation 5 and personal computer (PC). These RF remote controllers are imported with a wireless USB Type-A dongle. As per the information provided, these controller models contain a printed circuit board assembly (PCBA) manufactured in Thailand via a surface mount technology (SMT) process utilizing components sourced from various countries. The SMT process includes adhering components, such as integrated circuit (IC) chips, switches, transistors, resistors, capacitors, etc., to a bare printed circuit board (PCB). All major controller functions are enabled by the finished PCBA, including registering player input during gameplay. The functional PCBA will be sent from Thailand to China. In China, the final assembly takes place, which consists of mounting the PCBA into a shell/casing, and installing other parts, such as brackets, buttons, directional pad, thumb sticks, connector, grips, battery, etc. Then, the completed game controllers are inspected, tested, labeled, and packaged.
