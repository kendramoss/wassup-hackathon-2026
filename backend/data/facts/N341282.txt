In your letter dated July 15, 2024, you requested a country of origin ruling on Proparacaine Hydrochloride Ophthalmic Solution.Proparacaine Hydrochloride Ophthalmic Solution USP, 0.5% is a topical local anesthetic for ophthalmic use. It is used to perform tonometry, gonioscopy, removal of corneal foreign bodies and for short corneal and conjunctival procedures.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Proparacaine Hydrochloride is manufactured in Switzerland and that the finished Proparacaine Hydrochloride Ophthalmic Solution in dosage form is made in India. In India, the Proparacaine Hydrochloride will be mixed with inactive ingredients to produce the final Proparacaine Hydrochloride Ophthalmic Solution in dosage form.
