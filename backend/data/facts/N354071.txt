In your letter dated September 22, 2025, you requested the tariff classification and the applicability of Section 232 additional duties of steel coils. The products under consideration are Type 304 stainless steel coils made to ASTM A240. These cold-rolled, annealed coils have a thickness of .0495 inch (1.25 mm), a width 13.21 inches (335.53 mm), and have a PVC coating on one side. The coils will ultimately be used to make step rails for pickup trucks.
