In your letter dated October 29, 2018 you requested a tariff classification and country of origin ruling.There are three Power Distribution Assemblies (bus bars) under consideration which are identified as Part Numbers 633860001, 633970001, and 63320001. Each of the bus bar assemblies consist of two 40” copper conductors which are recognized as the power conductor and the return conductor. Both conductors are affixed to an insulating base with adhesive strips and each has a single multi-pin electrical connector. The conductors, adhesive strips, and insulating base have predrilled holes which are aligned prior to assembly and are used with threaded fasteners to screw the assembly together and presumably to make electrical connections. With the exception of the fasteners for Part Numbers 633860001 and 633970001, the country of origin for all of the individual components is China. You state that the bus bars will be assembled in Mexico.The assembly process involves arranging the copper conductors and adhesive strips onto the top and bottom of the insulating base. Using applied heat and pressure, the conductors are adhered to the base to form a single dual conductor bus bar. Once the lamination process is complete, two multi-pin connectors are installed onto the newly laminated conductors and personnel perform quality inspection, testing, and packing.In your request you suggest the three Power Distribution Assemblies are correctly classified under 8537.10.9170, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS). As the bus bars consist of a base equipped with two or more electrical connectors for the purpose of distributing electricity, we agree with your suggested classification.
