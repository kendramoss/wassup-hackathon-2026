In your letter dated June 25, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on Nystatin and Triamcinolone Acetonide Ointment. Nystatin and Triamcinolone Acetonide Ointment is a medicinal preparation containing Nystatin (antifungal) and Triamcinolone Acetonide (synthetic corticosteroid), as the active ingredients. It is indicated for the topical treatment of cutaneous candidiasis (yeast infection of the skin). In your letter, you stated Nystatin and Triamcinolone Acetonide Ointment contains two active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs). The Nystatin is made in Italy and the Triamcinolone Acetonide is made in India. In India these two APIs will be mixed with inactive ingredients to produce the final topical ointment in dosage form.
