In your letter dated July 15, 2025, on behalf of Fantasia Accessories Ltd. and Fujian Haojin Toiletries Co., Ltd., you requested a country of origin and marking ruling. Dermasil® Dry Skin Treatment Body Lotion is described as an over the counter (OTC) body lotion containing the active ingredient dimethicone. You indicate that it is specially formulated to create a barrier on the skin to lock in moisture and protect against irritation. In addition to dimethicone, other raw materials used to produce the body lotion include emulsifiers, preservatives, thickeners, oils, stabilizers, and moisturizing agents. These ingredients are sourced from various countries and will be sent to either Malaysia or Vietnam. In Malaysia or Vietnam, the ingredients will be weighed, emulsified, homogenized, and blended under controlled conditions to produce a stable bulk using a mixing machine (emulsifier). This process includes heating oil and water phases separately, then combining with high shear mixing operations. Preservatives and other ingredients will be added in a cooled-down phase. The formulation will then be bulk tested for pH, microbial content, and stability. After testing, the bulk formulation will then be sent from Malaysia or Vietnam to China where it will be filled into retail containers, affixed with retail labels, and packaged in cartons for exportation to the United States. All packaging materials (bottles, caps, cartons, and cardboard) originate in China.
