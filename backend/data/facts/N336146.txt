In your letter dated October 26, 2023, filed on behalf of Encube Ethicals Private Limited, you requested a country of origin ruling on Tretinoin Gel (Microsphere) 0.08%.Tretinoin Gel (Microsphere) 0.08%, imported in 50 gram bottles, contains 0.8 mg of Tretinoin, a naturally occurring derivative of vitamin A (retinol), as the active ingredient. It is indicated for the topical application in the treatment of acne vulgaris. You indicated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Tretinoin, is manufactured in Italy and that the finished Tretinoin Gel (Microsphere) in dosage form is made in India. In India, the Tretinoin will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Tretinoin Gel (Microsphere) in dosage form.
