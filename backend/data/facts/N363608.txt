In your letter dated July 23, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on HDMI cable assemblies. There are two HDMI cable assemblies at issue in this request, individually identified by model names 0.75M 48G Pearl Passive HDMI Assembly and 1.5M 48G Pearl Passive HDMI Assembly. Both HDMI cable assemblies are identical except for the variation in length. Both HDMI cable assemblies consist of insulated electrical conductors with HDMI Type-A style connectors attached to each end. In use, the subject HDMI cable assemblies are used to transmit audio and video signals between devices such as televisions, monitors, and other media enabled devices. In your request, you state that the manufacturing process begins in Taiwan, where a copper wire conductor is drawn and twisted, then insulated and covered with aluminum foil tape before a braided copper shield is applied. It is then sheathed with an insulating material prior to being cut to the desired length. The unfinished cables will then be shipped to China for the final manufacturing process. In China, HDMI Type-A style connectors of Chinese origin are assembled onto each end of the cable. Then, the finished cable is packaged for shipment to the United States.
