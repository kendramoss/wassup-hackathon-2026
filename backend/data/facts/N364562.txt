In your letter dated August 25, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on wheel hub bearing assemblies on behalf of Iljin Global Co., Ltd. The merchandise under consideration consists of wheel hub bearing assemblies, identified as Part Numbers IJ123161, IJ113067, IJ123124, IJ223089, and IJ223153. The assemblies are designed for use in motor vehicles and function as the interface between the rotating and non-rotating components of the vehicle’s suspension system. The principal components include a hub, outer ring, inner ring, rollers, cage, seals, hub bolt, and grease. The submission proposes two manufacturing scenarios. Scenario 1 Under Scenario 1, the hub, outer ring, and inner ring are manufactured in China from steel round bar. In China, the hub outer ring undergoes cutting, forging, rough turning, heat treatment, finish turning, drilling, and tapping, as applicable. The inner ring similarly undergoes forging, turning, and heat treatment. At the time these components are exported from China, they have been formed into dedicated hub and bearing-ring components but have not yet undergone precision grinding or super-finishing. The remaining components, including the rollers, cage, open seal, pack seal, encoder seal, hub bolt, and grease, are also of Chinese origin. In South Korea, the hub outer ring, and inner ring undergo precision grinding to form the raceways and achieve the required dimensional tolerances, followed by super-finishing to obtain the required roundness and surface roughness. The components are then washed and inspected. Final assembly also occurs in South Korea. The assembly operations include pressing the hub bolt into the hub, assembling the roller and cage components, machining and pressing the inner ring, orbital forming, installing the outboard seal, performing ABS output inspection, and conducting precision clearance matching. The completed assemblies are subsequently inspected, packaged, and shipped.
Scenario 2 The processing of the hub, outer ring, and inner ring under Scenario 2 is substantially the same as the processing described in Scenario 1. These components are manufactured in China through forging, turning, heat treatment, and other machining operations before being exported to South Korea as semi-finished hub and bearing-ring components. In South Korea, the hub, outer ring, and inner ring undergo precision grinding and super-finishing, followed by washing, inspection, and final assembly. Unlike Scenario 1, the rollers, cage, open seal, pack seal, encoder seal, hub bolt, and grease used in Scenario 2 are manufactured in South Korea or purchased as South Korean-origin components. The final wheel hub bearing assembly is completed and inspected in South Korea.
