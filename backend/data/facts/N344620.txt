In your letter dated December 12, 2024, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Hangzhou Mingxuan Sanitary Products Co., Ltd., for the purpose of duty calculation and Section 301 applicability. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review.The items under consideration are described as packages of “kitchen towels,” “hand towel rolls,” and “hand towel sheets.” They come in a variety of plies (single, double, and triple) and folds (none, multifold, C-fold, V-fold, and 5-fold).Per your submission, the bulk rolls of paper towel stock are manufactured in Indonesia. You describe the paper as White/Kraft paper: virgin; layer: 1/2/3; with a 18-47 GSM. These parent rolls of paper, which measure in multiple widths from 20 mm up to 32 mm and have a maximum roll diameter of 2800 mm, are then sent to China, where the paper is embossed, converted by cutting to width and re-rolling to length, subjected to quality control, and packaged.
