In your letter dated May 31, 2024, filed on behalf of Dr. Reddy’s Laboratories Incorporated, you requested a tariff classification and country of origin determination ruling.Vasopressin Injection USP, imported in 1 mL single dose vials, is a medicinal preparation containing Vasopressin, as the active ingredient. It is a peptide hormone used to increase blood pressure in patients with vasodilatory shock who are resistant to fluid and catecholamine therapy.
