In your letter dated September 23, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on Ranolazine Extended-Release Tablets. Ranolazine, imported in 500 mg and 1,000 mg extended-release tablets, belongs to a class of medications called anti-anginals. It is indicated for the treatment of chronic angina (chest pain). You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Ranolazine, is manufactured in India and that the finished tablets in dosage form are also made in India. In India, the Ranolazine will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Ranolazine Extended-Release Tablets in dosage form.
