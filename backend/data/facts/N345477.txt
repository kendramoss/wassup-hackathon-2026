In your letter dated January 29, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on Apremilast Tablets in dosage form.Apremilast, imported in 10 mg, 20 mg and 30 mg tablets, is a phosphodiesterase 4 (PDE4) inhibitor. It is indicated for the treatment of adults with active psoriatic arthritis and adults with oral ulcers associated with Behcet's Disease. Apremilast is also indicated for the treatment of adult patients with plaque psoriasis who are candidates for phototherapy or systemic therapy.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Apremilast is manufactured in Spain and that the finished tablets in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Apremilast will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final tablets in dosage form.
