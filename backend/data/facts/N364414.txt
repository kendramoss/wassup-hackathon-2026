In your letter dated August 20, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on a windchime for purposes of current trade remedies. The product under consideration is a windchime identified as the RS 31.5in metal chicken windchime, which measures approximately 31.5 inches in height and 8 inches in diameter. Each windchime consists of a dome-shaped metal top piece with metal tubes, flowers, roosters, a clapper, and a flower windcatcher suspended beneath. Three metal chains are connected to a hook at the top of the dome for hanging. In your ruling request submission and subsequent email correspondence, you state that the windchimes are assembled in Vietnam using components sourced from both China and Vietnam. The components sourced from China include the top pieces, flowers, rooster bodies, clappers, and windcatchers. The components of Vietnamese origin are the rooster wings and tubes. The factory combines these Vietnamese-origin components with the Chinese-origin parts to create the finished windchimes. The assembly process in Vietnam begins by welding the imported components. Specifically, the factory in Vietnam welds the Chinese-origin rooster bodies and the Vietnamese-origin wings together to form complete roosters. These roosters are then combined with the other imported components and the Vietnamese-made metal tubes (chimes) to assemble the finished windchime. This manufacturing process includes spot welding, polishing, powder coating, paint spraying, and hand-painting (using coating and paint of Vietnamese origin). Finally, the finished products undergo testing and inspection before being packaged for export. The Bill of Materials (BOM) demonstrates that the manufacturing operations in Vietnam contribute approximately 31% of the total cost of production and final value of the windchimes.
