In your letter dated December 22, 2025, you requested a tariff classification and country of origin determination ruling. Calcitonin Salmon Injection, imported in 400 USP Units/2 mL vials, is a synthetic polypeptide hormone that acts as a calcitonin receptor agonist. It is indicated for the treatment of Paget’s disease, hypercalcemia, and postmenopausal osteoporosis.
