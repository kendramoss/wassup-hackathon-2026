In your letter, dated February 2, 2026, you requested a country of origin determination on behalf of your client, Crane USA, Inc, for three items. The mat consists of three layers: a single layer of 100 percent polyester batting sheet is sandwiched between two layers of 100 percent cotton woven sateen fabric. The three layers are quilted together. The round shaped mat measures 40 inches in diameter and is finished with tassels along the perimeter. The mat is imported without toys such as a mirror, rattle, crinkle paper toys, or a squeaker. The unlined storage bin measures 13 inches in diameter by 12 inches in height and features two rectangular slit handles.
