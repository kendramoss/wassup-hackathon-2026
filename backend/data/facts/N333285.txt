In your letter dated June 2, 2023, you requested a tariff classification and country of origin ruling.An ingredients breakdown, manufacturing flowchart, and a picture of the product accompanied your inquiry.The subject merchandise, “Kimchee Sauce,” is described as a liquid sauce that will be imported in an ambient condition and sold primarily to the food service industry. The product is said to contain tap water (Japan), high fructose liquid sugar (Japan), salt (Japan), garlic (China), apple pulp (Japan), sodium L-glutamate (Brazil), brewed vinegar (Brazil), mandarin orange pulp (Japan), paprika (Spain), fermented vegetables (China), chili pepper (China), bonito extract (Japan), squid extract (Japan), soy protein hydrolysate (U.S.), dried bonito (Indonesia), citric acid (Japan), ginger (China), konbu (Japan), xanthan gum (U.S.), sodium 5-inosinate (Thailand) and paprika pigment (Japan). The product is said to be manufactured in Japan by mixing the raw materials with seasoning, removing metal, filling into 1200 gram (42.32 ounce) plastic containers, capping, labeling, and boxing.
