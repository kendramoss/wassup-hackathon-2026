In your letter, dated September 25, 2025, you requested a binding tariff classification and country of origin ruling on printed porcelain plaques. Product information and photos were submitted for our review. The product under consideration is printed porcelain or porcelain-enameled steel plaques. The plaques are commemorative or memorial plaques used for placement on tombstones. A photo of the person being memorialized is digitally printed onto the porcelain and then fired to vitrify the image.
