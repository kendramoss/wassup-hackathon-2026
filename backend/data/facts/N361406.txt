In your letter dated  May 9, 2026 , you requested a country of origin ruling on digital tire pressure gauges.Descriptive literature was provided for our review.The items under consideration are described as digital tire pressure gauges (model numbers SP003, SP010,SP024, and SP007), which are precise electronic tools that measure a tire’s inflation level by converting airpressure into an electrical signal. The exact measurement reading is displayed on an integrated digital LCDscreen, thereby eliminating the guesswork often associated with traditional analog dial gauges. In operation,each gauge is pressed onto the tire’s valve stem, allowing air pressure to act upon an internal electronicsensor, which then converts the pressure into data displayed on the backlit screen.While all models share fundamental similarities, minor variations exist in their housing design, color, andform. The SP003, measuring 77mm by 31mm by 23mm and weighing 25g, is composed of a top/bottom case,button, LCD screen, printed circuit board (PCB), rubber component, CR2032 battery, and auxiliary materials.The SP010, measuring 102mm by 65mm by 34mm and weighing 110g, includes a brass air inlet nozzle, TPEprotective sleeve, ABS top/bottom case, silicone rubber button, LCD screen, PCB board, rubber component,CR2032 battery, and auxiliary materials. The SP024, measuring 137mm by 56mm by 29mm and weighing45g, consists of an ABS top/bottom case, ABS battery cover, silicone rubber button, LCD screen, PCB board,rubber component, AAA battery, and auxiliary materials. Finally, the SP007, measuring 11mm by 2.4mm by4.4mm and weighing 48g, is composed of an ABS top/bottom case, silicone rubber button, LCD screen, PCBboard, rubber component, CR2032 battery, and auxiliary materials. The digital tire pressure gauges are manufactured in Malaysia through a substantially similar process,utilizing components sourced from both Malaysia and China. Malaysian-sourced components include the topcase, bottom case, button, LCD screen, PCB board, rubber components, and various auxiliary components. Specifically, for the SP010 model, a brass inlet nozzle with a deflation button and a plastic protective sleeveare also sourced from Malaysia. Components from China primarily consist of batteries and raw rubber andplastic materials.The manufacturing process for the SP010 model begins in Malaysia with the CNC machining, electroplating,and assembly of its copper head, followed by the remaining processing steps common to all gauges. InMalaysia, the plastic upper and lower housings undergo injection molding, with spray painting applied ifrequired. This is followed by overmolding injection for the upper and lower covers and then the injection ofglue for the rubber components. The LCD panel, circuit board, and battery are procured as complete unitsand prepared for installation. The assembly sequence involves installing the circuit board and batteryretaining clip, welding the circuit board, battery, and clamp plate, and subsequently installing the LCDscreen, battery, and rubber part. The final steps include screwing together the upper and lower covers,followed by testing and packaging.
