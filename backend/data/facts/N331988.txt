In your letter dated April 4, 2023, you requested a country of origin ruling on Timolol Maleate Ophthalmic Solution.Timolol Maleate Ophthalmic Solution, USP 0.25% and 0.5% (5 mL and 10 mL) is indicated in the treatment of elevated intraocular pressure in patients with ocular hypertension or open-angle glaucoma.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Timolol Maleate, is manufactured in Germany and that the finished Timolol Maleate Ophthalmic Solution is made in India. In India, the Timolol Maleate will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Timolol Maleate Ophthalmic Solution in dosage form.
