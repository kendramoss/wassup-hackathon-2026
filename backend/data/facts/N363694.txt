In your letter dated July 26, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling. The product under consideration is described as aluminum laminated film intended for use with lithium-ion battery cells. You indicate that the aluminum laminated film prevents electrolyte leakage and is engineered to withstand high-pressure manufacturing while maintaining extreme formability and dimensional accuracy. The product consists of an outer layer of nylon/polyethylene terephthalate (PET) measuring 37 microns (0.037 mm) in thickness, a core layer of aluminum foil measuring 60 microns (0.06 mm) in thickness, and an inner layer of cast polypropylene (CPP) film measuring 80 microns (0.08 mm) in thickness. According to your submission, nylon/PET film of Chinese origin and an adhesive of Korean origin are sent to Thailand and made into the final product. In Thailand, the nylon/PET film are dry laminated together and then laminated to the aluminum foil that originates in Thailand by means of the adhesive. Next, the CPP film, which also originates in Thailand, is dry laminated to the foil. Finally, a slip agent coating is applied to improve the surface properties and handling of the film. The nylon/PET layer structurally supports the product and prevents tearing during deep drawing, the aluminum foil provides the primary moisture and gas barrier to ensure a hermetic seal, and the CPP layer provides electrolyte resistance and creates the final heat seal.
