In your letter dated July 10, 2025, you requested a country of origin determination for purposes of Section 301 duties and marking under Section 304 of the Tariff Act of 1930, as amended (19 U.S.C. 1304). Pictures, a bill of materials and descriptions of the manufacturing process were included in your submission. The item under consideration is identified as a “cultihoe” (SKU# 1023423). It is a two-in-one gardening tool that combines a cultivator and a hoe. It has a long handle that is attached to a steel double-sided head. One side of the head has a hoe blade that is used for chopping weeds or breaking up soil. The other side incorporates a three-tine cultivator that is used for loosening and aerating soil. You state that the manufacturing process begins in Vietnam, where Chinese-origin hot-rolled steel is laser-cut into a hoe blade. Also in Vietnam, steel is punched, pressed and bent into the three tines that make up the cultivator. These pieces (i.e., the hoe blade and tines) are then welded together to form the complete double-sided head. The head is sent to China where it polished and powder coated. A handle socket is connected and secured, along with a wooden handle. Finally, middle and end plastic grips are attached to the handle.
