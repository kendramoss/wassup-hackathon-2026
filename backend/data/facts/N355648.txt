In your letter dated November 3, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on on behalf of your client, Metser Demir Celik. The products under consideration are five calcium silicide (CaSi) cored wire variants consisting of a steel tubular sheath that encapsulates the calcium silicide powder that will be added to the steel melt. Based on the chemistry provided, the CaSi cored wire variants meet the definition of a ferroalloy in accordance with Chapter 72, Note 1(c), Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS).
