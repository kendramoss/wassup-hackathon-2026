Item# EG2503010, which you describe as a “printed chenille bathmat,” is a woven floor mat consisting of an exposed upper surface of a 100 percent polyester, chenille woven fabric printed with primitive diamond shaped designs. A nonwoven fabric is laminated to the back of the chenille face. These two layers are laminated to a backing of canvas coated with thermoplastic rubber (TPR) on the bottom surface to provide a non-skid surface. The edges are finished with an overlock stitch. The mat measures 18 ½ x 27 inches and is approximately 1/16 inch thick. The surface is printed with an oriental design. The knitted fabric is laminated to a layer of foam, approximately 3/8 inch thick, and is backed with a nonwoven fabric with an application of polyvinyl chloride (PVC) dots forming a design.
