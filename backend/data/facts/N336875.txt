In your letter dated December 1, 2023, filed on behalf of Dr. Reddy’s Laboratories, Inc., you requested a tariff classification and country of origin determination ruling.Dr. Reddy’s Tilia FE, is a medicinal preparation containing a combination of Norethindrone Acetate (a progestin) and Ethinyl Estradiol (a steroidal estrogen), as the active ingredients. It is indicated for the prevention of pregnancy. In your letter, you stated Dr. Reddy’s Tilia FE also contains a small amount of iron (ferrous fumarate) in each of the 7 inactive tablets taken during the fourth week. Dr. Reddy’s Tilia FE is supplied as a continuous dosage regimen consisting of 21 oral contraceptive tablets (1 mg/0.02 mg (5 tablets), 1mg/0.03 mg (7 tablets), 1 mg/0.035 mg (9 tablets) and seven ferrous fumarate tablets (75 mg). The ferrous fumarate tablets are present to facilitate ease of drug administration via a 28-day regimen.
