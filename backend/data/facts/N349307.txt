The subject merchandise consists of a comforter and 2-piece pillow sham set. You state that the set has not yet been assigned an item number/name. Both the comforter and pillow shams are made from 100 percent polyester printed, woven fabrics. The face fabric for the comforter and pillow shams is printed with a botanical design and heat treated in a pattern to create a striped recessed impression alternating with a raised puckered texture. The fabric on the reverse side of each item is printed with a brush stroke dotted line design. Neither the comforter nor the pillow shams contain any embroidery, lace, braid, edging, trimming, piping or applique. The comforter is stuffed with fiberfill and has a knife edge on all four sides. The shams are not stuffed, have a knife edge on three sides and an envelope closure on the fourth side. While this closure design would generally indicate use as a pillowcase, in this case, we consider it to be a pillow sham because of the texturized fabric that coordinates with the comforter with which it is sold and its marketing as a pillow sham. The manufacturing operations for the comforter and shams are as follows: China Polyester face fabric is woven, heat treated and printed. Polyester face fabric exported to Pakistan in rolls. 

Pakistan Polyester back fabric is woven and printed. Filling for the comforter is formed. Fabric is cut. Comforter shell and shams are assembled and sewn. Fiberfill is inserted into the comforter shell. Comforter is tacked and sewn close. Comforter sets are exported to the United States.
