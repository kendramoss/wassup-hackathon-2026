In your letter dated July 17, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on a spark module for a gas range. The merchandise under consideration is described as a spark module for a gas range stove and is identified by part numbers 311D7051P001, 311D7051P002, 311D7051P003, and 311D7051P004. It is noted that the different variations are virtually identical and only differ by the specific appliance with which they will be used. The spark module is attached to a gas range oven and is responsible for generating a high-voltage spark to ignite the gas at the burners. Two configurations were provided for the manufacturing of the spark module. The first configuration has a high voltage (HV) coil, Printed Circuit Board Assembly (PCBA), cover, and epoxy potting all manufactured in China, then shipped to Thailand. In Thailand, the PCBA and HV coil are assembled together. The primary coil lead and terminal are soldered to the PCBA, then are tested. Once testing is complete, epoxy potting is added to shield the device from the environmental factors and distress. The device is tested again for functionality, and once confirmed, the cover is added, the device is tested, date coded, and packaged in Thailand for export to the United States. The second configuration has an HV coil, cover, and epoxy potting originating in China. The PCBA is manufactured in Thailand. The Chinese components are shipped to Thailand, where the PCBA and HV coil are assembled together. The primary coil lead and terminal are soldered to the PCBA, then are tested. Once testing is complete, epoxy potting is added to shield the device from the environmental factors and distress. The device is tested again for functionality, and once confirmed, the cover is added, the device is tested, date coded, and packaged in Thailand for export to the United States.
