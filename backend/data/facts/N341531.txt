In your letter dated July 23, 2024, you requested a country of origin ruling on Triamcinolone Acetonide Ointment USP, 0.05%.Triamcinolone Acetonide Ointment 0.05 % belongs to a class of medications called corticosteroids. It is indicated for the relief of the inflammatory and pruritic manifestations of corticosteroid responsive dermatoses.You indicated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Triamcinolone Acetonide, is manufactured in India and that the finished Triamcinolone Acetonide Ointment in dosage form is also made in India. In India, the Triamcinolone Acetonide Ointment will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Triamcinolone Acetonide Ointment in dosage form.
