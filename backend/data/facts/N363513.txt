In your letter dated July 22, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Trend Power Technology Private Limited Taiwan Branch. The merchandise under consideration is referred to as the Trend Power Lithium-Ion Rechargeable Battery Pack, model number AMZ-01-20SD. It is described as a battery backup unit for data center backup systems and features a durable, box-shaped casing with a handle and a power and signal connector. Inside the case are cylindrical 18650 lithium-ion battery cells and a printed circuit board assembly that functions as a battery management system for monitoring cell voltage, charging current, and temperature. The battery backup unit has a nominal voltage of 49 VDC, a nominal capacity of 7,250 mAh, and a nominal energy of 355 Wh. It measures approximately 85 millimeters in width, 752.7 millimeters in depth, 76.6 millimeters in height, and weighs 7.3 kilograms. The subject battery backup unit is assembled in China using components sourced primarily from China and with battery cells sourced from Malaysia. Minor electrical devices and materials, such as fuses and solder paste, will be sourced from Japan or Taiwan. The printed circuit board assembly, outer case, cable assembly, and remaining components are sourced within China. The process begins with creating a core pack. This involves loading and locking the battery cells into an endcap assembly, spot welding bus plates, and applying glue and insulator paste to the weld joints. Next, the printed circuit board assembly is seated and fastened to the endcap assembly. Workers then install the fuse, solder and inspect string voltage interconnects, install heat-shrink tubing, and apply hot melt reinforcement. Following the creation of the core pack, the assembly process transitions to the final integration phase. This phase involves mounting the core pack in the lower half of the case, installing the cable assembly and fastening its connector to a bracket cover, and installing the top cover. The battery backup unit is subjected to a charge and discharge cycle, as well as functional testing and inspections. Once completed, labels and barcodes are affixed, and the battery is packaged for export.
