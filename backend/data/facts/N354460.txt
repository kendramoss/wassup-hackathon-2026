The subject merchandise, item #721330, described as a “SS Pillow,” is a decorative household pillow measuring 15 x 15 inches. The outer shell of pillow is made from 100 percent cotton, printed, woven fabric. The shell is stuffed with 100 percent polyester fiberfill. The decorative pillow features a striped printed pattern on both the front and back with 100 percent cotton piping along its edges. The decorative pillow will be imported and marketed under the Festive Voice™ brand at Walgreen stores. The manufacturing operations for the pillow are as follows: India Yarn is formed. Cotton fabric is woven, bleached, dyed and printed. Polyester fiberfill is formed. Piping is formed. Cotton fabric is cut and assembled into a pillow shell with piping by sewing. Pillow shell is stuffed and sewn closed. The finished pillow is tagged, packaged and exported to the United States.
