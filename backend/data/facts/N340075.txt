In your letter dated May 10, 2024, you requested a a tariff classification and country of origin determination ruling. Product descriptions and website for the products were provided for our review. The article under consideration is identified as a 6 Foot Curved VINdustry wine peg and panel kit with a vinyl skin option. This item consists of a curved MDF back panel with predrilled holes for mounting to a wall, a 3mm thick acrylic face panel that mounts on top of the MDF panel, aluminum pegs that screw into the panel, and small hardware pieces that affix the pegs to the panel. It comes in three different peg options, Single Deep Pegs, Double Deep Pegs and Triple Deep Pegs for different wine bottle capacity. This kit is also available with a vinyl skin option. In that option, the vinyl skin will be affixed to the acrylic panel in Canada and functions to provide decorative appeal. The components in the kit are intended to assemble into a wall mounted rack designed to hold wine bottles. You state this 6-foot curved wine rack uses the same components and the same amount and value ratios as the 3 foot rectangular kit described in NY ruling N337479.Classification under the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS) is governed by the principles set forth in the
