In your letter dated May 9, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Dongguan Lopie Paper Co., Ltd., for the purpose of duty calculation and Section 301 applicability. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review. The paper products under consideration are "exam table paper". They come in single ply, rolls. Per your submission, the bulk rolls of exam table paper stock are manufactured in Indonesia. You describe the paper as White/Kraft 100% wood pulp virgin paper; layer:1; 12-18g/sm. These parent rolls of tissue paper, which measure in multiple widths from 457mm up to 686mm and have a maximum roll diameter of 380mm, are then sent to China, where the paper is embossed, cut to length and width with no shaping done. The rolls of exam table paper are then subjected to quality control and packaged.
