In your letter dated January 24, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Weifang Great Paper Co., Ltd, for the purpose of duty calculation and Section 301 applicability. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review.The items under consideration are described as packages of “hand towels." They come in either single or double ply with no fold. Per your submission, the bulk rolls of tissue paper are manufactured in Indonesia. You describe the paper as White/Kraft paper: virgin; layer:1; with a 30-36 GSM. These parent rolls of paper, which measure 2750 mm in width and has a maximum roll diameter of 2750 mm, are then sent to China, where the paper is embossed, converted by cutting to width and re-rolling to length, subjected to quality control, and packaged.
