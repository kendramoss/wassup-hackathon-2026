In your letter dated April 6, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on 10% Calcium Chloride Injection, USP in dosage form. 10% Calcium Chloride Injection, imported in 1 g/ 10 mL, is a sterile, nonpyrogenic, hypertonic solution containing calcium chloride dihydrate, as the active ingredient. It is indicated for the treatment of adult and pediatric patients with acute symptomatic hypocalcemia. In your letter, you stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Calcium Chloride Dihydrate is manufactured in Germany and that the finished injection in dosage form is made in Bangladesh. In Bangladesh, the Calcium Chloride Dihydrate will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Calcium Chloride Injection, USP in dosage form.
