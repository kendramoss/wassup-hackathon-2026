In your letter dated April 4, 2023, you requested a country of origin ruling.A description of the products, a manufacturing flowchart, and pictures accompanied your inquiry.Two scenarios are described in your letter. In the first scenario, pumpkin seeds, products of China, will be imported into Mexico for processing. The work performed in Mexico is said to encompass dehulling (removing the shells), cleaning, and removing any foreign materials. The second scenario mirrors the first, except that the pumpkin seeds will be roasted and salted in Mexico. In both scenarios, the products will be then packaged and exported to the United States for sale.COUNTRY OF ORIGIN AND MARKING:
