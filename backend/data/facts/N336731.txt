In your letter dated November 27, 2023, you requested a country of origin ruling on Acyclovir Ointment 5%.Acyclovir Ointment 5% is a medicinal preparation containing Acyclovir, an antiviral, as the active ingredient. It is used in the management of initial genital herpes and in limited non-life-threatening mucocutaneous Herpes simplex virus infections in immunocompromised patients.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Acyclovir, is manufactured in China and the finished Acyclovir Ointment in dosage form is made in India. In India, Acyclovir will be mixed with inactive ingredients to produce the final Acyclovir Ointment in dosage form.
