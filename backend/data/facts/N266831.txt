In your letter dated July 17, 2015, on behalf of International Nutrient Technologies, Canada, you requested a ruling on classification, country of origin, country of origin marking, and the status under the NAFTA of a dietary supplement from Canada. Additional information was provided via eMail message dated July 28, 2015.An ingredients breakdown, product specification sheet, description of production steps and manufacturing process accompanied your inquiry. 24-0389 Omega 3 Prenatal Soft Gelatin Capsules are said to be filled with FMO-DHA fish oil 65/560 (65 mg/g EPA EE and 560 mg/g DHA EE) from Norway. The shell ingredient is composed of gelatin from the United States (U.S.), glycerin from Malaysia, and purified water from Canada. The enteric coating on the capsules is said to be composed of NS Enteric 29Z19241 Clear, and Surelease E-79040 Clear both from the U.S. The ingredients are blended, and encapsulated in Canada as a dietary supplement for human consumption, and sold in bulk for expectant and nursing mothers, and for women planning a pregnancy.
