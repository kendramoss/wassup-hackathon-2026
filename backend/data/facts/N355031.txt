In your letter dated October 20, 2025, on behalf of your client, Apex Tool Group, you requested a country of origin determination for purposes of Section 301 and IEEPA duties. The items under consideration are identified as a combination wrench, a combination wrench set, a flare nut wrench, a flare nut wrench set and an adjustable wrench. Pictures of the items in different stages of manufacture were included with your submission, along with narratives of the manufacturing processes. The combination wrench incorporates an open-ended wrench on one side with a closed ring-shaped box end wrench on the other side. The combination wrench set will include various sizes of combination wrenches packaged in a plastic holder. Flare nut wrenches are similar to combination wrenches except that both ends are open and slightly offset. The flare nut wrench set will include various sizes of flare nut wrenches packaged in a plastic holder. The adjustable wrench consists of a handle that incorporates the fixed side of the adjustable jaws and the moveable side, which can be adjusted by turning a worm gear. You have stated that the manufacturing process for the combination wrench, combination wrench set, flare nut wrench, and flare nut wrench set is the same. It begins in Vietnam, where the entire body of each wrench is cold forged from steel bar imported from China. The forging produces blanks that closely approximate the final shape of the wrenches, although the working ends of the wrenches are still filled with metal. The blanks are shipped to China for finishing operations that include grinding, machining the ends, heat treatment, vibration polishing, plating, and packaging. The manufacturing process for the adjustable wrench begins in Vietnam, where the handle is produced. A vertical press is used to hot forge a heated steel billet to the approximate shape of the handle, including the fixed side of the jaw. The adjustable side of the jaw is similarly hot forged in Vietnam. The two pieces are 

then sent to China where they are machined on hydraulic flattening machines and milling machines to ensure all critical dimensions are accurate. The pieces are then polished, plated and assembled to connect the moveable jaw and worm gear to the handle. Finally, the finished adjustable wrench is packaged for retail sale.
