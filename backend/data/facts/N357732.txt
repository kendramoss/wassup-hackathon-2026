In your letter dated January 13, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on a pair of pruning shears. Pictures of the merchandise in different stages of manufacture were included with your submission, along with a manufacturing flow chart. The item under consideration is identified as 7.5-inch Floral Pruning Shears. The pair of pruning shears is a hand tool made primarily of steel that consists of two curved steel blades, one concave and the other convex. They are connected at a pivot to plastic handles, which incorporate a spring mechanism for opening and closing, along with a locking mechanism. In operation, the blades bypass each other and are used for pruning ornamental plants. One blade also includes an integrated wire cutter and stem stripper. The manufacturing process begins in Vietnam, where carbon steel sheets are stamped into the final size and shape of the concave and convex blades. Each blade also incorporates an upper metal base to which the plastic handles will be applied. Additionally in Vietnam, holes are punched into each blade for assembly purposes. The blades are then sent to China, where they are heat treated, sharpened, coated, and painted. Chinese-origin injection molded plastic handles are applied to the base of the blades and the blades and handles are assembled with a screw, washer, nut, spring, and a plastic locking mechanism. Finally, the finished pruning shears are quality tested and packaged for retail sale before export to the United States.
