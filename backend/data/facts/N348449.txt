In your letter dated April 30, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on Potassium Chloride Extended-Release Tablets. Potassium Chloride Extended-Release Tablets, imported in 8 mEq (600 mg) and 10 mEq (750 mg) is a medicinal preparation containing Potassium Chloride, as the active ingredient. It is indicated for the treatment and prophylaxis of hypokalemia with or without metabolic alkalosis. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API) Potassium Chloride is manufactured in Israel, and the finished tablets in dosage form are made in China. In China, the Potassium Chloride will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Potassium Chloride Extended-Release Tablets in dosage form.
