This ruling is being issued in response to your letter dated April 21, 2025, requesting a country-of-origin determination on behalf of your client, Medline Industries, Inc., for a pillow. A sample and a product description were provided. The subject article is identified as a pillow insert, item number ABS2504KE-P001. The pillow has a man-made fiber non-woven shell with heat bonded edges and is stuffed with a 100% polyester fill. The pillow measures approximately 20" (L) x 14" (H). The manufacturing operations are as follows: The fabric is formed in Vietnam. It is exported to China where it is cut, the edges heat bonded to form a pillow shell, and the pillow shell filled with Chinese-origin fiber fill and heat bonded shut. The pillow insert is then packaged for export to the United States. COUNTRY OF ORIGIN –
