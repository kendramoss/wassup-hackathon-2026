In your letter dated July 29, 2024, you requested a tariff classification and country of origin determination ruling, which you filed on behalf of your client, GSP Automotive Malaysia Sdn Bhd.The article under consideration is a Wheel Hub Assembly, Model Number 696040, which is a 2nd Generation wheel hub with rollers designed for passenger vehicles, such as sport utility vehicles (SUV) and pick-up trucks. It consists of a single flange, inner ring, rollers, cages, and other accessories. The flange end face is provided with a hole and a bolt, which can be directly connected to the wheel rim. The flange inner hole is provided with the spline slot, which can be used with the constant velocity (CV) axel half shaft.According to the information submitted with your request, the rollers and other accessories, such as a cage, cap, seal, and anti-lock braking system (ABS) ring are made in China and imported to Malaysia to be assembled with the Malaysian made flange and inner ring. In Malaysia, the flange and inner ring are grinded and ultra-fine grinded. The rest of the components are then assembled using the roller loading machine, hydraulic press machine, and grease pump machine. Finally, the product is tested, laser marked, and packaged for shipment.
