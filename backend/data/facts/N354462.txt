In your letter dated October 1, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of Slate Run Pharmaceuticals, LLC on Desvenlafaxine Succinate Extended-Release Tablets. Desvenlafaxine Succinate, imported in 25 mg, 50 mg and 100 mg extended-release tablets, is a Serotonin-Norepinephrine Reuptake Inhibitor (SNRI). It is indicated for the treatment of adults with major depressive disorder (MDD). You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Desvenlafaxine Succinate, is manufactured in India, and that the finished tablets in dosage form is made in China. In China, the Desvenlafaxine Succinate will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Desvenlafaxine Succinate Extended-Release Tablets in dosage form.
