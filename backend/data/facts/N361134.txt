In your letter dated April , 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling.29 The submitted sample will beretained by this office.The merchandise under consideration is a lipstick packaged for retail sale described as “Mary Kay LipstickMexico.”You state that bulk lipstick mass manufactured and filled in the United States is sent to Mexico for labellingand packaging operations. In the United States, raw materials sourced both domestically and internationallyare milled and mixed to produce the bulk lipstick mass. This mass is then melted, filled into molds, andcooled to form the “bullet” shape. The molded lipstick is then placed into individual plastic applicator cases,capped, and packaged for export to Mexico. In Mexico, the lipstick cases are labelled and packed intopre-labeled boxes for retail sale. The finished product is subsequently shipped back to the United States.
