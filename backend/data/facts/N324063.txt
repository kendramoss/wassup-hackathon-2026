In your letter dated January 28, 2022, you requested a tariff classification and country of origin ruling.The item under consideration is the Invinity VS3-022 Vanadium flow battery. The unit is a rechargeable vanadium battery pack with a nominal voltage of 850 VDC and a capacity of 220 kWh. Each pack is composed of six battery modules assembled inside a custom 20-foot container. Within each battery module are two battery stacks, electrolyte storage tanks, and pumps. The vanadium flow battery stores and supplies energy using a reversible electrochemical reaction.
