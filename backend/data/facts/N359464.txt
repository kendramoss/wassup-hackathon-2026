In your letter dated March 5, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on pigments. In your submission, you describe a scenario where a pigment produced in India will be sent to Mexico to be b lended with a pigment produced in Mexico in a dry blending step. No additional grinding or wet mixing takes place. The final product, consisting of 49% Indian pigment and 51% Mexican pigment, will be exported from Mexico to the United States. The pigment produced in India is described as Dalamar Yellow YT 800D-IN, SAP 252521, also known as Pigment Yellow 065 (CAS 6528-34-3). This product consists of Fast Bordo GP Base (CAS 96-96-8), Acetoacet-o-anisidide (CAS 92-15-9), and non-reactive additives. The pigment produced in Mexico is described as Dalamar Yellow YT 800D-MX, SAP 161659, also known as Pigment Yellow 065 (CAS 6528-34-3). This product consists of Fast Bordo GP Base (CAS 96-96-8), Acetoacet-o-anisidide (CAS 92-15-9), and non-reactive additives. These pigments are bright reddish, have very good weather fastness, and high opacity in applications.
