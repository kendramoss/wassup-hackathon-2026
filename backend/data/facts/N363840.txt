In your letter dated July 29, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on “Cooked Sticky White Rice.” Pictorial representations of the retail packaging accompanied your request. The subject merchandise described as “Cooked Sticky White Rice” is composed of 30 to 50 percent rice (United States), 50 to 70 percent water (South Korea), and less than 1 percent glucono delta-lactone (France). The rice is grown, harvested, and dehusked in the United States before being exported to South Korea for further processing. You state that the manufacturing process is conducted in South Korea. The white rice is combined with glucono delta-lactone, soaked in water, dehydrated, and subsequently cooked by steaming at temperatures ranging from 70 to 100 degrees Celsius for 10-15 minutes. The cooked rice is then cooled, dried, and packaged in sealed, microwaveable bowls, each containing 210 grams of product. The finished product will be sold at retail in packages containing one, three, or twelve bowls.
