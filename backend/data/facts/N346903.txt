In your letter dated March 18, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Anqiu Xiangyu Packaging and Printing Co., Ltd, for the purpose of duty calculation and Section 301 applicability. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review. The items under consideration are packages of paper napkins, including “interfold napkins,” “multifold towel,” “dinner napkins,” "tall fold napkins," "low fold napkins," and "beverage napkins," which come in single and double ply and a variety of folds. Per your submission, the bulk rolls of napkin stock are manufactured in Indonesia. You describe the paper as White/Kraft paper: virgin; layer: 2; 12-18 g/sm. These parent rolls of tissue paper, which measure in multiple widths from 715 mm-3800 mm and have a maximum roll diameter 840mm, are then sent to China, where the tissue paper is unwound, embossed, folded into napkin sheets, and cut to size. The finished napkins are then subjected to quality control and packaged.
