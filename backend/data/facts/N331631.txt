This is in response to your letter dated March 17, 2023, requesting a ruling on the classification, country of origin, marking and the eligibility of the USMCA on “Super seed cereal - Apple Cinnamon.” An ingredients breakdown, a cost breakdown, and a photo of the product were included with your inquiry.The subject merchandise, “Super seed cereal – Apple Cinnamon,” is described as a dry blend composed of quick oats, hemp hearts, brown sugar, diced apples, flaxseed, ground cinnamon and salt. The finished product will be packed in individual sachets, 5 single serving sachets in a master pouch for retail sale. The user is instructed to add water to the product and heat in the microwave for one minute.You have proposed two different scenarios for consideration.Scenario 1: The quick oats, hemp hearts and flaxseeds are products of Canada. The brown sugar is a product of Brazil. The diced apple is a product of China. The grounded cinnamon is a product of Vietnam, and the salt is a product of the U.S.A.Scenario 2: The quick oats and salt are products of the U.S.A. The hemp hearts and flaxseeds are products of Canada. The brown sugar is a product of Brazil. The diced apple is a product of China, and the ground cinnamon is a product of Vietnam.Classification:
