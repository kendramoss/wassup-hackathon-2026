In your letter dated August 12, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, CTEK Inc. The merchandise under consideration is described as a battery charger, model number NXT 2.3 Powersport, which is designed to charge and maintain all types of lead-acid 12-volt batteries, as well as lithium batteries, that are found in conventional vehicles. The battery charger is comprised of a Printed Circuit Board Assembly (PCBA) enclosed in an outer housing with LED indicators and an ON/OFF button with power cables affixed to either end. The unit is fully automatic which features a built-in automatic temperature sensor and a combination of advanced charging modes allowing it to charge in all weather conditions. The battery charger also has a special reconditioning function that restores stratified and deeply discharged batteries, unique maintenance charging, and the charging of demanding AGM batteries. In your submission, you state that the manufacturing process begins in Malaysia by populating a bare Printed Circuit Board (PCB) via Surface Mount Technology (SMT) with various electronic components, mainly of Chinese origin, such as resistors, capacitors, transformers, diodes, fuses, and integrated circuits, among other components to fully assemble the PCBA. The completed PCBA is then tested and inspected prior to being exported to China. In China, electrical cables are routed through the upper enclosure and soldered to the PCBA. Once the PCBA is seated within the upper enclosure, the lower housing is welded to the upper assembly to seal the battery charger unit. The finished battery charge is then tested and packaged for shipment into the United States.
