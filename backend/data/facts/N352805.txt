In your letter dated August 22, 2025, on behalf of your client Ningbo Eyecos Cosmetic Co., Ltd., you requested a country of origin ruling. The subject products are described as mascara (Formula Code F0424-8), eyebrow mascara (Formula Code F0424-12), and concealer (Formula Code F0424-14). You provided a bill of materials and a production process description for each item. You indicate that the bulk cosmetics will be mixed in South Korea from raw materials sourced from various countries and that no single country will be the source for the majority of the raw materials. The processing in South Korea will consist of multiple mixing steps performed under controlled temperatures. Once mixed, the bulk cosmetics will be subjected to quality control and filtering operations. Afterwards, the bulk cosmetics will be shipped to China for filling and packaging operations. In China, the retail containers will be filled with the bulk cosmetic products and packaged for retail sale for export to the United States. All packaging materials originate in China.
