In your letter received on April 16, 2024, you requested a tariff classification ruling.Images were provided in lieu of a sample.The products under consideration are described as molded plastic parts. These items are unfinished electrical installation boxes made of polyvinyl chloride (PVC) plastic. Per your submission, once imported into the United States, they will undergo assembly to make these electrical installation boxes functional using screws and backplates. They will then be packaged for sale within the United States. Specifications for three different versions were provided, each varying slightly in size. We note that they lack any internal power connections or electrical components and therefore would be classified according to their constituent material.In your original request, you stated that these boxes would not be considered products of Chinese origin due to their further processing in the United States. We disagree. As imported, they are from China and should be marked accordingly. Further, the processing steps performed in the United States would be considered simple assembly and would not alter the basic nature of these boxes.
