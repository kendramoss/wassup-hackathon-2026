In your letter dated June 19, 2024, you requested a country of origin ruling on Cyclophosphamide Capsules USP.Cyclophosphamide, imported in 25 mg and 50 mg capsules, is an antineoplastic and immunosuppressant drug in the class of alkylating agents. It is indicated for the treatment of various cancers such as Hodgkin’s disease, lymphocytic lymphoma, mixed-cell type lymphoma, histiocytic lymphoma, Burkitt’s lymphoma, multiple myeloma, leukemias, mycosis fungoides, neuroblastoma, adenocarcinoma of ovary, retinoblastoma, and breast carcinoma.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Cyclophosphamide, is manufactured in India and that the finished capsules in dosage form are also made in India. In India, the Cyclophosphamide will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final capsules in dosage form.
