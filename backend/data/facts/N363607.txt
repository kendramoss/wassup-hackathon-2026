In your letter dated July 23, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on RJ45 Patch Cords. There are two RJ45 Patch Cords at issue in this request, individually identified by model names 0.75M RJ45 Patch Cord Cinnamon and 1.0M RJ45 Patch Cord Cinnamon. Both patch cords are identical except for the variation in length. Both patch cords consist of insulated electrical conductors with RJ45 style connectors attached to each end. In use, the subject patch cords are used to connect network devices and computers. In your request, you state that the manufacturing process begins in Taiwan, where a copper wire conductor is extruded, twisted, braided, jacketed, and insulated prior to being cut to the desired length. The unfinished cables will then be shipped to China for the final manufacturing process. In China, RJ45 connectors of Chinese origin are assembled onto each end of the cable. Then, the finished cable is packaged for shipment to the United States.
