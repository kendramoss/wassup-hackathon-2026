In your letter received on May 31, 2024, you requested a tariff classification and country of origin ruling.A sample was submitted and will be returned as requested. The product under consideration is described as the “Low Profile Comfort Knee Pad Inserts,” style number 24603. The inserts are made of a soft plasticized foam surface laminated to a polyester jersey backing that serves as mere reinforcement. The orange plastic foam features protruding nubs that measure approximately 3/8 of an inch in depth and mold to the contour of the knee. Each insert measures 9.5 inches in length by 6 inches in width. They are sold as a pair and are meant to be inserted into men’s or women’s work pants with built-in knee pad pockets to provide cushioning when working on a hard surface.
