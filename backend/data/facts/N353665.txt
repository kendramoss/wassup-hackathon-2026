In your letter dated September 12, 2025, you requested a tariff classification and country of origin determination for purposes of Section 301 and IEEPA duties. The merchandise under consideration is a showerhead installation kit. The kit includes a wide jaw adjustable wrench, a 30-foot-long roll of plumber’s thread seal tape, and a pack of wet wipes. The items are used in conjunction with each other to remove and install showerheads. The installation guide you provided states that the wrench is used to remove or install showerheads, the wet wipes are used to clean off old tape, rust or sealant from the threads of a shower arm, and the thread seal tape is used to facilitate a watertight seal. The adjustable wrench is of Vietnamese origin, and the wet wipes and thread seal tape are of Chinese origin. The items will be packaged for retail sale in China before importation into the United States. The pictures provided of the packaging show a rectangular box with a clear window display in the center, allowing the consumer to see a portion of the items inside.
