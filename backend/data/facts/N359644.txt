In your letter dated March 11, 2026, you requested a classification, country of origin, and marking ruling for four DIY Crochet Shoe Kits. You submitted descriptions and photographs for four DIY Crochet Shoe Kits. These kits are very similar to the DIY Knit Shoe Kit covered under ruling N355798, but they differ in how the shoe uppers are formed. Unlike N355798, which contained balls of cotton yarn, these four DIY Crochet Shoe Kits contain pre-cut felt and yarn components in various color and size variations to crochet and create shoe uppers. The outer sole and felt components feature pre-punched holes along their edges. The included yarns are to be used for assembling the uppers with the outer soles and for creating crochet details along the upper edges and topline. All four models are DIY kits that provide the consumers with the opportunity to handcraft uppers and to assemble the components into pairs of wearable shoes or boots after purchase.
