In your letter dated March 20, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on pencils. The products under consideration are yellow pencils, product SKU: 800387. In your letter, you outline a scenario in which the material components for the wood cased lead pencils are sourced in the United States, China, and Vietnam. The wood is sourced in the United States. The ferrule is produced in China and shipped to Vietnam. The production process that takes place in Vietnam is divided into five phases including: 1) slat production, 2) grooving, lead inserting, and shaping, 3) painting, and 4) finishing: printing, end-cutting and eraser installation, 5) packaging. In your letter, you assert that all processing occurs at the manufacturer facility in the Vietnam.
