In your letter dated  May 13, 2026 , you requested a country of origin ruling on valve caps.Item numbers SP005, SP017 and SP012 are referred to as plastic valve caps. The valve stem caps aredome-shaped and threaded to be used on tire valves.With respect to origin, the manufacturing process begins by subjecting raw polypropylene material fromChina to an injection molding process. The resulting product then undergoes trimming and a sorting processin Malaysia. For item number SP012, an O-ring from Malaysia is installed onto the cap. Lastly, each valvecap is inspected and packaged.
