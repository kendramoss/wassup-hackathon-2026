In your letter dated March 13, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on an air fryer on behalf of your client, Midea Consumer Electric (Vietnam) Co., Ltd (“MVN”). The product under consideration is described as the “MVN Air Fryer IV,” model number AF1 SS (Vortex ClearCook OdorEase). This air fryer is a domestic electric countertop device that cooks food by circulating hot air around the ingredients using fan blades. The air fryer is manufactured in Vietnam through a two-stage process. The first stage involves three phases: Phase One involves the production of the structural body and other plastic components (i.e., the large upper body, power box, lower body, heat insulation block, left and right sealing strips, motor cover, guide rail, power board cover, ventilation window mesh, handle cover, micro-motion cover plate, micro-motion bracket, spring sleeve, lower heating insulation ring, bottom cover clamp plate, air outlet connector, bucket front cover, lens hood, fret ejector, oven lamp holder, frying bucket handle base, upper body frame and door insulation cover) by an injection molding process. The plastic raw materials are sourced from Vietnam, Thailand, Cambodia, and China. Phase Two involves the production of the metal parts, including the internal base plate, surrounding wall, reflecting cover, frying bucket and baking tray from steel coiled materials. The fabrication will be completed in Vietnam from steel sourced from either Vietnam or China. Phase Three involves the production of the heating pipe or heating element, which is manufactured by a third-party supplier of MVN in Vietnam from various raw materials. 

In the second stage, also performed in Vietnam, the Vietnamese-origin body and other plastic and metal components will be placed with the remaining components originating from Vietnam and China on the main assembly line to produce subassemblies (i.e., the bucket and base subassemblies) followed by final assembly of the finished MVN Air Fryer IV. It will then be tested, inspected, and packaged.
