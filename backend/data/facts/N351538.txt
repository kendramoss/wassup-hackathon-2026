In your letter dated July 21, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling for the purpose of marking, Section 301 Trade Remedies and other duties. Descriptions of the production processes were provided in the submission. The diffuser and impeller are components of an Electric Submersible Pump (ESP) system used to lift crude oil from a wellbore. The ESP system, which is used in the oil and gas industry, is configured as a multiple stage pump that incorporates multiple impellers and stationary diffusers. Together, the impeller and diffuser increase fluid pressure that pumps oil through the well. The circular-shaped diffuser and impeller feature a ring of enclosed vane patterns and a center bore that is circular in shape. With respect to origin, the manufacturing of the diffuser and impeller begins in China where they are cast into a casting tree that consists of groups of iron circles attached to a central gating system. The groups of circles do not feature vanes, or a center bore. Once imported into Malaysia, the castings of the diffuser and impeller are subject to shell treatment, an initial sandblasting phase, cutting and separation processes, a manual saw cutting process, a second sandblasting phase, two machining phases, and polishing and turning operations. During these processes, approximately 66 percent of material from the diffuser's shell and casting, and 85 percent of material from the impeller's shell and casting, are removed. Once complete, a tungsten carbide component is installed and glued into the center of the diffuser and a washer is installed into the diffuser.
