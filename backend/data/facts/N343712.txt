In your letter dated November 1, 2024, you requested a country of origin ruling on hypodermic needles.The products under consideration are hypodermic needles for animal use only. The manufacturing process is described as follows:In South Korea, hollow steel tubes will be cut to length and cut or sharpened to a sharp tip on one end, resulting in a basic steel needle. The needles will be exported to China, where a hub will be attached to the blunt end with adhesive, then packaged, and exported to the United States.
