The following facts are based on your April 14, 2025, ruling request as well as a follow-up submission dated October 30, 2025, and a separate classification ruling regarding the IMDC issued April 14, 2023. See New York Ruling Letter (“NY”) N331604, dated April 14, 2023. According to your request, the IMDC is a 60-foot steel container equipped with racking, 24 immersion tanks, 24 power distribution units, four power hubs, pumps, valves, piping and wiring, access doors, and lighting. The IMDC will be used to house and supply power to various types of machinery, which are installed within the immersion tanks. As outlined in NY N331604, the subject IMDC is classified under subheading 8537.10.91, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (“HTSUS”), which provides for: “Boards, panels, consoles, desks, cabinets and other bases, equipped with two or more apparatus of heading 8535 or 8536, for electric control or the distribution of electricity, including those incorporating instruments or apparatus of chapter 90, and numerical control apparatus, other than switching apparatus of heading 8517: For a voltage not exceeding 1,000 V: Other:” The subject IMDC is assembled in Canada. The product is comprised of various originating and nonoriginating materials purchased from manufacturers in China, France, 
 2 Taiwan, Germany, Poland, and Denmark. You have provided U.S. Customs and Border Protection (“CBP”) with a costed bill of materials (“BOM”), which outlines the value and proposed tariff classification of the various components used in the IMDC. According to the BOM, the IMDC is comprised of approximately 91,547 components, with a total component value of $1,131,354.46 and a transaction value of $1,990.000. The aggregate value of materials originating in USMCA countries is $609,696.82, and the value of non-originating materials is $521,657.64.
