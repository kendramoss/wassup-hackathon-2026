In your letter dated July 2, 2024, you requested a country of origin ruling on Clindamycin Phosphate and Benzoyl Peroxide Gel, 1.2% / 5%, on behalf of Encube Ethicals Private Limited.Clindamycin Phosphate and Benzoyl Peroxide Gel, is a combination topical drug containing 12 mg of Clindamycin Phosphate (antibiotic) and 50 mg of Benzoyl Peroxide (antiseptic), as the active ingredients. It is indicated for the topical treatment of inflammatory acne vulgaris in patients 12 years and older.In your letter, you stated Clindamycin Phosphate and Benzoyl Peroxide Gel, 1.2%/5% contains two active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs). The Clindamycin Phosphate is made in China and the Benzoyl Peroxide is made in Sweden. In India these two APIs will be mixed with inactive ingredients to produce the final topical gel in dosage form.
