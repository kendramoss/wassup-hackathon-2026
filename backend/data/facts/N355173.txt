In your letter dated October 23, 2025, you requested a tariff classification and country of origin ruling. Images were submitted in lieu of a sample. The product under consideration is described as an inflatable silicone seal, part number BF13-28-1032. It is intended for use in a Technopak 25-kilogram milk powder packaging machine. Your submission indicates that this product is initially molded in China from “Elastosil R Plus 4000/40 K2 CN” silicone plastic. It will then be exported to New Zealand where it undergoes a proprietary heat treatment and quality assurance process that fully crosslinks the material and produces the final FDA-compliant component.
