In your letter dated August 21, 2025, you requested a ruling on behalf of your client, Richline Group. You submitted photographs, product description, and manufacturing information on 14-karat gold pendant necklaces for our review. Style no. BX4837 are 14-karat gold pendant necklaces. The manufacturing steps performed in the United States are as follows: Ring designed Wax model and wax tree created Fine gold is melted together with alloy metals to create a karated casting grain Casting grain is re-melted and injected into the flasks, creating a karat gold duplication of the wax tree Individual castings are cut off the gold tree and inspected Approved castings are sent to either China, Thailand, or India for final assembly The manufacturing steps performed in China, Thailand, or India are as follows: Gold castings are filed, stamped with gold karat and trademarks, and pre-polished Diamonds of India origin are mounted and set Colored gems of China, Thailand, or India origin are mounted and set Polishing Rhodium is applied on the diamond section of pendant
