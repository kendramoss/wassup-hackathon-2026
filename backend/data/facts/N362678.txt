In your letter dated June 27, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on a sun visor for motor vehicles. The article under consideration is a sun visor assembly, Part Number JHZ-Z0029L, designed for installation on the roof of the driver’s and front passenger’s seats of a motor vehicle. The sun visor assembly consists of a foam body, inner frame, bracket assembly, mirror assembly, conductive plate assembly, roller shaft, micro switch, warning label and sun visor hook. The manufacturing process includes injection molding, EPP foam molding, fabric cutting, assembly, testing, and inspection. According to the submitted bill of materials (BOM), the cover fabric, upper foam, lower foam, bracket frame, bracket buckle, rotating shaft, end cap, spring plate bracket, inner frame, roller shaft, mirror frame, mirror cover, light guide plate, hook, and hook clip are sourced from Thailand. The plug, spring plate, conductive contact, conductive plate, roller pin, mirror, mirror spring plate, mirror cover shaft, mirror bulb, bumper, bulb wire harness, heat shrink tube, micro switch, seat belt warning label, and child seat warning label are sourced from China. The components are assembled in Thailand through a process that includes injection molding, EPP foam molding, fabric cutting, bracket sub assembly, mirror sub assembly, sun visor assembly, inspection, and packing.
