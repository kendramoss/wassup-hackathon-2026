In your letter dated January 20, 2024, you requested a country of origin ruling on Battery Cooling Plates, which you filed on behalf of your client, Daeshin S and C of South Korea.The articles under consideration are three aluminum cooling plates, the Top Plate, the U-frame, and the Cooling Plate, which are designed to be used with electric vehicle batteries. They are critical components of battery packs and attach to the battery cells to maintain a uniform temperature inside the battery pack by controlling the heat to maintain the battery life and prevent fire.
