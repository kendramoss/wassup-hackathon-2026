This ruling is being issued in response to your letter dated December 25, 2024, requesting a country-of-origin determination for a pillow. In lieu of a sample, a product description and picture were provided.The subject article is identified as the Adorable Dog Shaped Pillow, item number 25C6SWTP04. It is a decorative pillow in the form of a small, long, short-haired dog with large floppy ears and a short upright tail. It has a beige 100% polyester knit plush outer fabric (210 gsm) and a non-removable dark green 100% polyester tufted green sweater with two embroidered strings of multi-colored Christmas tree lights. The pillow is stuffed with 100% polyester fiber fill. The pillow measures approximately 20" (L) x 13" (H) x 3" (D) and weighs approximately 0.8 lbs.The manufacturing operations are as follows: The two fabrics that comprise the pillow shell are made in China. They are exported to Vietnam where they are cut, sewn and filled with Vietnamese-origin fiber fill prior to packaging and export to the United States.COUNTRY OF ORIGIN –
