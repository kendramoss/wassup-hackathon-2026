In your letter dated August 21, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on a broom. Product images were submitted in lieu of samples. The product under consideration is described as a heavy-duty, mixed-fill sweeping broom designed for industrial, commercial, and domestic sweeping applications. Per the submitted images, it is a push broom featuring a wide head with a handle that measures approximately 47 inches in length. The broom is available in six SKUs, with heads that range from 11 inches to 18 inches in width. While the manufacturing process and materials are the same for all variants, the ratio of natural to synthetic fiber bristles may vary depending on the SKU. The broom consists of a wooden head made from timber block sourced from Sri Lanka, bristles made of natural bassine/palmyra fiber from India and synthetic polyethylene terephthalate (PET) fiber from Italy, a plastic bracket, metal handle, and metal scraper all sourced from China, and metal staple wire from France. In your submission, you outline a manufacturing scenario in which all components are shipped to the United Kingdom. In the United Kingdom, holes are precision drilled into the timber block and filled with a combination of natural bassine fiber and PET synthetic fiber which are permanently secured into the timber block using wire staples. The filled broom head is trimmed, dressed, and inspected to achieve the required sweeping profile and manufacturing specification. The plastic socket is attached, the metal handle and metal scraper are fitted, and the completed broom is inspected before packaging for export from the United Kingdom.
