This is in reply to your letter dated April 23, 2026, requesting a country of origin determination for a faux rabbit fur pillow, which will be imported into the United States. A photo has been submitted with your request in lieu of a sample. DPCI# 067-08-0299, described as a “Faux Rabbit Fur Pillow” is a decorative pillow with an outer shell of 100 percent polyester warp knit pile fabric that is stuffed with polyester fiber. This square pillow features a long, shaggy faux fur pile fabric on both sides. The pillow is sewn along the edges with a knife edge finish. The “Faux Fur Pillow” measures 20 x 20 inches and is marketed under the THRESHOLD™ brand sold at Target stores. The manufacturing operations for the faux rabbit fur pillow are as follows: China: Polyester faux rabbit fur fabric is knit. Polyester faux rabbit fur fabric undergoes dyeing, washing, drying, napping, shearing, decating and tip printing. Polyester faux rabbit fur fabric is rolled and shipped to Vietnam. Vietnam: Loose polyester fiber filling and labels are manufactured. Fabrics are cut into panels. Panels are sewn into pillow shells, stuffed, and sewn closed with labels. Finished faux fur rabbit pillows are inspected, packaged and shipped to the United States.
