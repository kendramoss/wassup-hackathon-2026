In your letter dated September 5, 2023, you requested a country of origin ruling on Midodrine Hydrochloride Tablets.Midodrine Hydrochloride, imported in 2.5 mg, 5 mg and 10 mg tablets, is a vasopressor and antihypotensive agent. It is indicated for the treatment of symptomatic orthostatic hypotension (OH).You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Midodrine Hydrochloride, is manufactured in Italy and that the finished Midodrine Hydrochloride Tablets in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Midodrine Hydrochloride will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Midodrine Hydrochloride Tablets in dosage form.
