In your letter dated April 11, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Chaozhou Soft Paper Products Co., LTD, for the purpose of duty calculation and Section 301 applicability. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review. The paper products under consideration are boxes of "facial tissue." They come in a variety of plies (double and triple) and folds (1/2 and z). Per your submission, the bulk rolls of facial tissue stock are manufactured in Indonesia. You describe the paper as White/Kraft 100% wood pulp virgin paper; layer: 2-3; 11.5-15.5 g/sm. These parent rolls of tissue paper, which measure in multiple widths from 180mm up to 200mm and have a maximum roll diameter 2700mm, are then sent to China, where the tissue paper is embossed, cut and folded to length and width. The finished facial tissues are then subjected to quality control and packaged.
