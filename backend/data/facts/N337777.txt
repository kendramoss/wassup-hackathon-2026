In your letter dated January 26, 2024, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Theophylline Extended-Release Tablets.Theophylline, imported in 300 mg and 450 mg extended-release tablets, is a vegetable alkaloid belonging to a group of medicines known as bronchodilators. It is indicated to treat the symptoms and reversible airflow obstruction associated with chronic asthma and other chronic lung diseases, such as emphysema and chronic bronchitis.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Theophylline, is manufactured in Germany and that the finished tablets in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Theophylline will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final tablets in dosage form.
