In your letter dated June 2, 2026, you requested a country of origin determination for marking purposes under Section 304 of the Tariff Act of 1930, as amended (19 U.S.C 1304). Item SP004 is identified as a tire iron. The tool is made of a single flattened piece of steel that is slightly bent and curved on each end. It is designed for removing and installing vehicle tires during repair or replacement. It helps pry tire beads away from the wheel rim, enabling safe and convenient tire removal, replacement, and maintenance. You have stated that the tire iron is manufactured entirely in Malaysia from raw carbon 40Cr steel that is sourced from China. The manufacturing processes that occur in Malaysia include cutting the raw steel to the required dimensions, forging, pressing, letter stamping, grinding, polishing, and chrome plating.
