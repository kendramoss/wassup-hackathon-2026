In your letter dated March 11, 2024, you requested a country of origin ruling on a garden cart, which you filed on behalf of your client, Meridian International Co., Ltd. USA.The article under consideration is a Garden Cart, which is a utility cart used to transport heavy items. It consists of a high-density polyethylene (HDPE) plastic base, polypropylene (PP) plastic wheels, and a steel handle, stator, and wheel frame. You state that the raw HDPE and PP are sourced from Thailand and made into the base and wheels in Thailand. The completed base and wheels are then shipped to China, where they are assembled together with the Chinese steel handle, stator, and wheel frame. The metal handle is attached to the plastic base with screws and the wheels are attached to the bottom of the base to create the finished garden cart.
