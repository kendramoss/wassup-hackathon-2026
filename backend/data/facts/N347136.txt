In your letter dated March 26, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on Dicamba DGA Herbicide. We referred the information provided to our laboratory for review. We regret the delay. The merchandise under consideration is Dicamba DGA Herbicide formulated in Malaysia. You indicate that it is an agricultural herbicide used for weed control in asparagus, conservation reserve programs, corn, cotton, fallow croplands, general farmstead (non-cropland), sorghum, grass grown for seed, hay, proso millet, pasture, rangeland, small grains, sod farms, farmstead turf, soybean, and sugarcane. The herbicide will be imported in 1,000 liter IBC totes. You provided the following description of the manufacturing process. Kenso will import dicamba acid (CAS No. 1918-00-9), a stable dormant solid form molecule, from China to Malaysia. The dicamba acid will chemically react with diglycolamine (DGA) (CAS No. 929-06-6) in the presence of water to form Dicamba DGA Salt Concentrate (CAS No. 104040-79-1). The Dicamba DGA Salt Concentrate is then mixed with a surfactant and further diluted with water to obtain the end use finished product, Dicamba DGA Herbicide.
