In your letter dated September 4, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on Epidural Needles. The provided sample has been reviewed and disposed of. Epidural Needles are medical devices used for the administration of anesthesia. Each needle consists of a stainless-steel shaft with a bevel (you refer to it as a cannula) , a plastic hub, plastic plunger, and a plastic needle protector. The provided Reli® Epidural Needles Datasheet indicates that the needles range from 16-gauge to 22-gauge and 2.5” to 6” in length. You describe the manufacturing process as follows: 1. Production in Japan: Stainless-steel hollow tubes are cut to length to form cannula. Tip of cannula is bent to produce an epidural point. Epidural point is grit blasted to ensure smooth heel on needle. The above processes, including the sourcing of raw materials for the stainless-steel cannula, are performed in Japan. 2. Assembly in China: The Japanese-origin cannula is exported to China. Plastic hub and plunger, made from raw materials sourced from Japan, are affixed to the cannula using adhesive glue of Japanese origin. A needle protector, molded in China using Japanese raw materials, is assembled onto the cannula. The completed needles are packaged in units of 100 and then boxed in cardboard cartons. The cardboard boxes are sourced in China. The primary function of the completed Epidural Needles is to inject local anesthetics into patients to provide regional anesthesia. They will only be used by trained healthcare professionals.
