In your letter dated July 16, 2023, you requested a country of origin ruling on Balsalazide Disodium Capsules.Balsalazide Disodium, imported in 750 mg capsules, is an anti-inflammatory drug. It is indicated for the treatment of mildly to moderately active ulcerative colitis in patients 5 years of age and older.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Balsalazide Disodium, is manufactured in Taiwan and that the finished Balsalazide Disodium Capsules in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Balsalazide Disodium will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Balsalazide Disodium Capsules in dosage form.
