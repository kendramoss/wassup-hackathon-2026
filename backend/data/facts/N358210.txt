In your letter dated January 26, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Green Planet Paper Co., Ltd, for the purpose of duty calculation and Section 301 applicability. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review. The items under consideration are packages of paper napkins, including “dinner napkins,” “dispenser napkins,” and “beverage napkins.” The finished napkins are produced in various sizes, with widths ranging from approximately 152 mm to 400 mm and lengths ranging from approximately 230 mm to 620 mm. They are manufactured in either single-ply or double-ply, and are folded into 1/4, 1/6, 1/8, and 1/9 folds. Per your submission, the bulk rolls of napkin stock are manufactured in Indonesia. You describe the paper as white, 100% wood pulp produced from virgin fiber; layer:1-2; 12-17 gram per square meter. These parent rolls of tissue paper, which measure in multiple widths from approximately 304 mm to 715 mm with a maximum roll diameter of approximately 1150 mm, are then sent to China, where the paper is embossed, cut and folded to length and width. No shaping is done. The napkins are then subjected to quality control and packaged.
