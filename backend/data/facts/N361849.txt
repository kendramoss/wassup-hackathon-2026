In your letter dated May 28, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Accelink Technologies, for three models of optical amplifiers (OA). The items concerned are three models of OA, identified as part numbers RPM-C/L-14.5-09-06-LU-5B, EDFA-MW-VGA-L60-15/25-24-0-0-LC/UPC, and EDFA-MW-A16-963/1220-5/38-L. These OA devices are used in fiber-optic communication systems to boost the strength of an optical signal without converting it into an electrical signal. This allows data to travel longer distances with minimal loss. In the OA, the printed circuit board assembly (PCBA) powers the pump lasers, which generate high-energy pump light. That pump light is then combined with the incoming signal light using a wavelength-division multiplexer (WDM). The combined light travels through the optical fiber, where the pump energy transfers to and strengthens the signal, amplifying it so it can continue across the network with significantly less attenuation. The manufacturing process for the OA occurs in Malaysia, using components, including the Pump Laser (Japan), Photodetector (China), PCBA (Malaysia), WDM (China), passive optical splitter (China), optical component (China), and Collimator (China). The first step is the PCBA assembly, which is as follows: 1. The printed circuit board will undergo solder paste printing. 2. The board will undergo an initial solder paste inspection (SPI). 3. The board’s solder side will be surface-mounted by means of a pick-and-place machine that populates the solder side of the board with its dedicated PCBA components. 4. The solder side will undergo solder reflow to permanently adhere the components to the board. 5. The PCBA will undergo automated optical inspection (AOI). 6. The PCBA will undergo an automated 3D x-ray inspection. 7. The PCBA will undergo another round of SMT subprocess, repeating all previous steps. 

8. The PCBA will then undergo conformal coating to protect the PCBA and its components from moisture, dust, or other forms of damage. The coating is then dried and baked. 9. The PCBA will undergo a manual visual inspection via microscope. Butterfly-shaped pump forming machines and pin dedicated automatic forming machines are then used to shape the pins to the correct height and length. Electrical installation is performed using equipment such as a computer, ion fan, exhaust fan, electric screwdriver, forceps, dispensing machine, glue application fixture and 3M fine insulating tape. The process includes installing the cover plate onto the mechanical housing, as well as pump installation while maintaining proper welding iron temperature controls. During soldering, all welding holes are filled with solder, and the solder surface must exhibit strong adhesion to the pins. The resulting solder joints must be uniform, complete, and exhibit a smooth, shiny finish, with no evidence of short circuits, poor soldering, or missing solder. Next, passive optical components for each module’s serial number are placed into a tray in the correct order. Fiber coils are arranged carefully to prevent them from contacting sharp components that could cause damage. Finally, the manufacturer uses specialized optical test equipment, to verify that each module meets performance specifications. The finished OA is inspected and packaged for export into the United States.
