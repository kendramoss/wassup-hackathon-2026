In your letter dated September 1, 2026, on behalf of Horizon Hobby, LLC, you requested a country of origin ruling. The merchandise under consideration is a radio control transmitter, model No. DX6e, item No. SPMR6655A, which is a handheld remote controller used to remotely control toy airplanes, helicopters, and multirotor aircraft through a 2.4 GHz radio frequency. This radio control transmitter is comprised of components that are manufactured in a variety of countries. According to the information provided, the two key printed circuit board assemblies (PCBAs), the Plano RF Transmitter Module PCBA and the MBA Main Computer PCBA will be manufactured in the United States (U.S.) using surface mount technology (SMT). This process encompasses dual in-line packaging (DIP) and wave soldering, including populating a bare printed circuit board (PCB) with various electrical and electronic components, such as resistors, capacitors, fuses, RF chips, and other components. The Plano RF Transmitter Module PCBA is the main radiofrequency (RF) transmitter for communication with the receiver of the toy car or boat. The MBA Main Computer PCBA is the main computer board responsible for converting analog inputs into digital information to be transmitted by the Plano RF Transmitter Module. Model DX6e also includes an Austin Receiver PCBA, which performs the function of receiving control data at low power from a second transmitter. All non-Chinese components, including the Plano RF Transmitter Module PCBA, MBA Main Computer PCBA, and Austin Receiver PCBA, will be shipped to China for final assembly into the radio control transmitters.
The CBA PCBA, which functions as a serial connection to auxiliary devices; the Roller Wheel Board, which supports the roller wheel, tactile button, and encoder; and the Trim PCB Board, which functions as the board for trim switches, are produced in China. The final assembly also takes place in China. This process involves the following: the Chinese plastic housing will be screwed and snapped together; the gimbals or sticks will be screwed, pressed, and soldered with wire harnesses; cables and switches will be soldered and press-fit into the housing; the Chinese LCD screen will be screwed into the plastic housing and attached to cables; the PCBAs will be installed into the plastic housing; software will be installed; the antenna will be connected; the battery will be installed; decals will be applied; and testing will be performed. Lastly, the radio control transmitter will be packaged and shipped to the United States.
