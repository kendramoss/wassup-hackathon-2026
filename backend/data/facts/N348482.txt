In your letter dated May 1, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on Ethosuximide Capsules, USP. Ethosuximide Capsules, imported in 250 mg, is in a class of medications called anticonvulsants. It is indicated for the control of absence (petit mal) epilepsy. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Ethosuximide, is made in the Netherlands, and that the finished capsules in dosage form are made in China. In China, the Ethosuximide will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Ethosuximide Capsules in dosage form.
