IIn your letter dated January 14, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Wan An (M) SDN. BHD. The article under consideration is a non-splined wheel hub assembly (model number 515040). The assembly consists of a double row of tapered roller bearings pressed into a spindle that features bolt holes that directly connect to the wheel rim. The bearings are designed to reduce friction and the spindle functions as a housing for the bearings. In use, the assembly reduces friction between moving parts of a vehicle, helping to improve fuel efficiency, reduce noise and extend the life of other components, which ensures optimal performance, safety and comfort on the road. You state that the inner and outer rings and spindle are imported from China in a rough semi-manufactured state and are precision machined in Malaysia. They undergo multiple turning operations, such as drilling or tapping, grinding and super-finishing to create a finished product. Other components, such as rollers, cages and sealing rings are made in China and imported to Malaysia, where the components will be inspected and undergo final assembly using a finishing machine, a component optical matching machine, press machines and a spin riveting machine. Each wheel hub assembly is inspected, laser marked, rust-proofed, tested and packaged for shipment.
