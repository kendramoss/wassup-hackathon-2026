In your letter dated September 9, 2024, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client LG Chem Ltd., for a doped and coated single crystal cathode material that subsequent to its importation into the United States will eventually be part of the cathode of a lithium-ion battery that will be incorporated into an electric vehicle. The manufacturing process for the instant material involves different levels of production in different countries (China, Korea) related to the descriptive process described below.In China, an inorganic precursor compound, a lithium compound, and several metal oxide dopants are mixed and then heated at a certain temperature for a certain amount of time. An additional lithium compound is then added to this mixture which is also heated at a certain temperature for a certain amount of time. After this process is completed, the material is sent to Korea where it is doped and mixed with a different material than what was performed in China, coated with a metal oxide, and heated at a certain temperature for a certain amount of time in a different type of kiln than what is normally used in these types of processes. After this process is completed, the material is sent to the United States for its ultimate incorporation into the lithium-ion battery.
