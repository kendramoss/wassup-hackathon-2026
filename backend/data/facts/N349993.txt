In your letter dated June 10, 2025, you requested a country of origin determination on a garden fork (SKU# 4413085) for purposes of Section 301 duties and marking purposes under Section 304 of the Tariff Act of 1930, as amended (19 U.S.C. 1304). Pictures, a bill of materials and descriptions of the manufacturing process were included in your submission. The garden fork has a steel head that incorporates ten slightly bent tines, which is attached to a wooden handle. It measures 149.8 cm in length and 26.7 cm in overall width. The tool is primarily designed for soil preparation, including loosening, lifting, and turning over soil, as well as aerating and mixing in compost. The manufacturing process for the fork begins in Vietnam, where Chinese-origin hot-rolled steel is laser-cut into the individual tines of the fork. The tines are further heated, bent and welded together to form the head of the fork. The fully formed fork head is then sent to China where it is polished, powder coated and assembled with a Chinese-origin socket and wood handle. Finally, a plastic grip is attached to the end of the handle.
