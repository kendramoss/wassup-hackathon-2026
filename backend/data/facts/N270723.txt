In your letter dated November 16, 2015, you requested a classification and country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Lauzon Distinctive Hardwood Flooring, Inc. Product information was submitted for our review.You outline a scenario wherein engineered flooring panels, with an unfinished face ply of either white oak or black walnut, are made in China and subsequently imported into Canada. You state that the flooring panels from China are of a plywood construction. While you do not indicate if the panels are tongued and grooved upon importation into Canada, your statement of processing in Canada does not include such working.
