In your letter dated October 21, 2019, on behalf of Pegatron USA Corporation, you requested a ruling on the classification, country of origin, and marking for dome cameras.The merchandise under consideration is dome network cameras, model DH-IPC-HDBW2531R-ZS/VFS. These progressive CMOS surveillance cameras can capture “real time” video and transmit it to a location outside the camera for remote recording or viewing. In addition, the subject cameras can record video internally onto an SD card. However, the dome cameras in question cannot record still images.In your request, you suggest that the subject surveillance cameras are classified in subheading 8525.80.30, HTSUS, which in pertinent part, provides for television cameras. However, these cameras also have the function of recording video images internally.
