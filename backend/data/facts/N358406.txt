In your letter, dated December 17, 2025, you requested a binding country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Lopie International (HK) Limited. The ruling was requested returned to you for additional information, which was received by this office on January 31, 2026. The ruling was requested for record paper for purposes of trade remedy applicability. Product information and a sample were provided for our review. The merchandise under consideration is a roll of thermal record paper. You describe a scenario wherein thermal paper jumbo rolls are manufactured in Indonesia. The paper is then shipped to China where it is unwound, printed on the back with advertising information, slit into small rolls and packaged. The record paper rolls are then shipped to the United States from China.
