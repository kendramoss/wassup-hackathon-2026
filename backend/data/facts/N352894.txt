In your letter dated August 26, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on Flecainide Acetate Tablets. Flecainide Acetate, imported in 50 mg, 100 mg and 150 mg tablets, is an antiarrhythmic. It is indicated for the prevention of paroxysmal supraventricular tachycardias (PSVT), paroxysmal atrial fibrillation/flutter (PAF) associated with disabling symptoms, and documented ventricular arrhythmias. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Flecainide Acetate is manufactured in India and that the finished Flecainide Acetate Tablets in dosage form is made in China. In China, the Flecainide Acetate will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Flecainide Acetate Tablets in dosage form.
