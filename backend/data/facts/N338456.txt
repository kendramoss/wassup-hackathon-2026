In your letter dated February 26, 2024, you requested a country of origin ruling on Darunavir Tablets.Darunavir, imported in 600 mg and 800 mg tablets, is a human immunodeficiency virus (HIV-1) protease inhibitor indicated for the treatment of HIV-1 infection in adult and pediatric patients 3 years of age and older. Darunavir must be co-administered with Ritonavir (Darunavir/Ritonavir) and with other antiretroviral agents. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Darunavir, is manufactured in India and that the finished tablets in dosage form are also made in India. In India, the Darunavir will be mixed with inactive ingredients from various countries to produce the final tablets in dosage form.
