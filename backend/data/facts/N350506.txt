In your letter dated June 23, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Dongguan Pengxing Paper Co., Ltd, for the purpose of duty calculation and Section 301 applicability. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review. The paper products under consideration are "hand towel sheets," "center-pull towels," and "hand roll towels." They come in single ply rolls. Per your submission, the bulk rolls of hand towel stock are manufactured in Indonesia. You describe the paper as White/Kraft 100% wood pulp virgin paper; layer:1; 19-42 g/sm. These parent rolls of towel stock paper, which measure in multiple widths from 930mm up to 2850mm and have a maximum roll diameter of 2850mm, are then sent to China, where the paper is unwound, embossed, cut to length and width and rewound or folded. The paper towels are then subjected to quality control and packaged.
