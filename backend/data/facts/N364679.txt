In your letter dated August 27, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on a tapered roller bearing wheel hub assembly on behalf of Wan An (M) Sdn. Bhd. The article under consideration is a tapered roller bearing (TRB) wheel hub assembly, identified by the unique product numbers 25819667, WH151097, and A515097, which corresponds to the universal model number 515097, and is designed for use in passenger motor vehicles. The wheel hub assembly facilitates the rotation of the vehicle’s wheels and supports the vehicle while enabling smooth wheel movement. The assembly incorporates an anti-lock braking system (ABS) ring and is designed to reduce friction between moving components of the vehicle. The principal components of the wheel hub assembly include a spindle, outer ring, inner ring, tampered rollers, cages, sealing rings, ABS ring, sensor, and related components. The spindle, outer ring, and inner ring are manufactured from S55 steel in Malaysia. The remaining components, including the rollers, cages, sealing rings, sensor, and related components, are manufactured in China and imported into Malaysia for incorporation into the finished wheel hub assembly. In Malaysia, S55 steel is heated and subjected to a forging process to form rough blanks for the spindle, outer ring, and inner ring. The resulting forgings subsequently undergo finish turning using computer numerical control (CNC) machine tools to obtain the required dimensions and configuration. The components are then drilled as necessary and subjected to precision grinding operations to create the finished surfaces and bearing raceways. The raceway surfaces are subsequently honed and superfinished to achieve the required dimensional tolerances and surface characteristics. Following these manufacturing operations, the finished spindle, outer ring, and inner ring are assembled in Malaysia with the tapered rollers, cages, seals, ABS components, sensor, and other components. The assembly operations include installation and machining of the rollers and cages, pressing the components together, application of grease, installation of seals and other components, and roll-greasing of the completed
unit. The wheel hub assembly is then subjected to ABS testing and final inspection before being laser marked, rust-proofed, and packaged for shipment.
