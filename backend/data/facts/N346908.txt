In your letter dated March 18, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Anqiu Xiangyu Packaging and Printing Co., Ltd, for the purpose of duty calculation and Section 301 applicability. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review. The paper products under consideration are toilet seat covers. Per your submission, bulk rolls of toilet seat covers are manufactured in Indonesia. You describe the toilet seat covers as White/Kraft paper: virgin; layer: 1;12-18 g/sm. These parent rolls of toilet seat covers, which measure 380mm in width and have a maximum roll diameter 380mm, are then sent to China, where the tissue paper is embossed, folded, cut to size, subjected to quality control, and packaged.
