In your letter dated January 23, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Weifang Great Paper Co., Ltd, for the purpose of Section 301 applicability. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review.The item under consideration is toilet tissue. The toilet tissue comes in a variety of plies (single, double, and triple). Per your submission, the bulk rolls of tissue paper are manufactured in Indonesia. You describe the tissue paper as White/Kraft paper: virgin; layer: 2; with 12.5-15 GSM. These parent rolls of tissue paper, which measure 2740mm/ 2760mmmm in width and have a maximum roll diameter 2760mm, are then sent to China, where the tissue paper is embossed, converted by cutting to width and length and re-rolling onto smaller tubes, subjected to quality control, and packaged.
