In your letter, dated September 11, 2025, you requested a binding country of origin ruling on two baskets for the purpose of duty calculation. You filed the request on behalf of your client, Walgreens Boots Alliance. The request was returned to you for additional information. The information was received by this office on October 10, 2025. Product information and photos were provided for our review. The products under consideration are a seagrass tray and a water hyacinth cutlery caddy. Both are basket-type containers made from plaiting materials. In your letter, you set forth a scenario wherein all manufacturing, from harvest of the sea grass or water hyacinth through manufacturing to packaging all occurs in Vietnam.
