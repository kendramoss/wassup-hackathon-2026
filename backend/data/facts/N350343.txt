In your letter dated June 18, 2025, you requested a country of origin determination for purposes of Section 301 and IEEPA duties. Pictures of the merchandise in different stages of manufacture were included with your submission, along with a narrative of the manufacturing processes. The subject articles are a metal spring clamp and a plastic spring clamp. The metal spring clamp consists of nickel-plated steel jaws and handles with polyvinyl applied to the handles and over the tips of the jaws. The plastic spring clamp consists of plastic jaws and handles with plastic swivel pads attached to the tips of the jaws. They are used for clamping and securing various materials. The manufacturing process for the metal spring clamp begins in Vietnam. Raw steel roll, sourced from Vietnam and other countries, is stamped into the final size and shape of the left and right jaws and handles of the clamp. These two articles are then sent to China, where they are nickel plated and assembled with a spring and a screw. Finally, the polyvinyl is applied to the handles and over the tips of the jaws The manufacturing process for the plastic spring clamp also begins in Vietnam. Plastic powder, sourced from Vietnam and other countries, is injection molded into the final size and shape of the left and right jaws and handles of the clamp. These two articles are then sent to China, where they are assembled with a spring and a screw. Finally, the swivel pads are attached to the tips of the jaws.
