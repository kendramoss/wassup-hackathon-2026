In your letter dated March 22, 2019 you requested a tariff classification ruling.There are two items under consideration which are identified as Pushbutton Stations PB-01 and PB-02. Pushbutton Station PB-01 is described as a stainless steel enclosure containing a single pushbutton switch mounted inside. Pushbutton Station PB-02 is described as a carbon steel enclosure containing a single pushbutton switch mounted inside. You state that for both Pushbutton Stations the enclosures are produced in the United States and the pushbutton switches are manufactured in France, and that the station is assembled in Canada.
