In your letter dated May 6, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Dongguan Lopie Paper Co., Ltd, for the purpose of duty calculation and Section 301 applicability. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review. The paper products under consideration are "toilet tissue" and "jumbo roll tissue." They come in either single, double or triple ply with no fold. Per your submission, the bulk rolls of toilet tissue stock are manufactured in Indonesia. You describe the paper as White/Kraft 100% wood pulp virgin paper; layer:1-2; 12-18 g/sm. These parent rolls of tissue paper, which measure 2760 in width and have a maximum roll diameter 2760mm, are then sent to China, where the paper is unwound, embossed, rewound, cut and folded to length and width with no shaping done. The toilet tissue is then subjected to quality control and packaged.
