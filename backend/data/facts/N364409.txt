In your letter dated August 20, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on an automotive knuckle assembly on behalf of Seohan Innobility Co., Ltd. The article under consideration is identified as the LX3 Knuckle Assembly, part number 51710-P9000, which is designed for use in the front suspension system of certain motor vehicles. The knuckle assembly provides mounting points for the shock absorber, lower control arm, tie rod, constant velocity (CV) joint, wheel speed sensor, brake caliper, and wheel. The assembly supports the front wheel load and facilitates functions associated with steering, braking, wheel speed detection, and shock absorption. The finished LX3 Knuckle Assembly consists primarily of an aluminum LX3 Cast Knuckle, part number M51715-P000, and a socket, part number 51798-3M100, that is press-fit into the knuckle. The cast knuckle provides the principal structure of the finished assembly and the mounting locations for the various suspension, steering, braking, and wheel-related components. According to the information submitted, the manufacturing process begins with Chinese-origin aluminum ingot, aluminum scrap, aluminum-strontium alloy, and magnesium. The aluminum is imported into South Korea in ingot block form, while the aluminum scrap consists of defective products and gate/runner cut-off scrap intended for remelting. The aluminum-strontium alloy is imported in round-bar form, and the magnesium is imported in small ingot or block form. The prepared molten aluminum is subjected to an electro-stirring high-pressure casting process. During this operation, the molten aluminum is introduced into a dedicated casting mold and solidified into the shape of the LX3 Cast Knuckle. Following casting, the article is cooled and undergoes trimming and gate/runner cutting to remove excess material. The cast knuckle is then heat treated and subjected to visual and X-ray inspections. The LX3 Cast Knuckle subsequently undergoes several precision machining operations using three-axis and four-axis machining centers. These operations include machining the datum face and step, bore, cover, for-hole locations, and caliper features. Additional machining is performed on the U-arm, T-arm, L-arm, and
ABS features and includes cutting, drilling, boring, reaming, chamfering, and tapping operations. The machined knuckle is inspected using various gauges to verify the required dimensions, positions, and tolerances. The socket, part number 51798-3M100, is of Korean origin and is sourced from a Korean supplier. Following completion of the machining operations, the socket is press-fit into the machined knuckle. The completed article undergoes final inspection and becomes the LX3 Knuckle Assembly, part number 51710-P9000.
