In your letter dated March 31, 2023, you requested a country of origin ruling.The merchandise under consideration, identified by model number CAT01, is described as a 14-foot CAT5E ethernet cable. The cable consists of a 14-foot length of insulated cable affixed with RJ45 connectors at both ends. The subject cable is used to connect various network devices and computers.You state that the manufacturing process is fully completed in the Philippines. This process begins with bulk wire conductor being extruded, twisted, braided, jacketed, and insulated prior to being cut to the desired length. The ends are then prepared for the addition of the Chinese RJ45 connectors. The connectors are crimped onto each end of the cable before the cables are tested and packaged for shipment to the United States.The “country of origin” is defined in
