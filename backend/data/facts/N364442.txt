In your letter dated August 20, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, STL Optical Connectivity NA, LLC. The merchandise under consideration is a fiber optic cable identified by model number MCD2KRE2625LFXXX20. The fiber optic cable is further described as a connectorized fiber optic cable consisting of a fiber optic core made of 16 fibers enclosed within a jacket and terminated at each end with a fiber optic connector. The subject cable is used as ultra-high-density data-center links and short-reach interconnections to transmit optical data signals. In your request, you state the manufacturing process begins with the creation of optical fibers in India. This process begins with the melting and stretching of the glass raw material into a glass bar. This bar is then ground and cleaned before it is further stretched and drawn into the appropriate length and diameter for the individual optic fiber. This fiber is then coated with protective polymer, trimmed at the ends, and tested for strength and function before it is exported to China or Vietnam. In China or Vietnam, the fiber core is colored, tested, and jacketed with plastic and aramid yarn. The cable is then cut to length, and the ends are then prepared for the addition of the connectors. U.S. origin connectors are then assembled onto the ends in China or Vietnam, and the finished cable is inspected, tested and packaged for export to the U.S.
