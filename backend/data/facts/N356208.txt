The Serta Ultra Loft Mattress Pad is a mattress pad comprised of three layers designed to be used on a mattress to provide additional comfort. The top layer of the mattress pad is made of 100 percent polyester microfiber plain woven fabric that has been bleached. The bottom layer of the mattress pad is 100 percent polyester nonwoven fabric. Between the two fabrics is a layer of 100 percent polyester garnetted stock batting fabric. The three layers are quilted together with a single needle stitch in an 8-inch box pattern. A 15-inch deep 100 percent polyester knit skirt with a fully elasticized edge is attached to the mattress pad on all four sides. The mattress pad is imported in Twin, Twin XL, Full, Queen and King sizes. The Serta Sleep Chill Mattress Pad is a mattress pad comprised of three layers designed to be used on a mattress to provide additional comfort. The top layer of the mattress pad is made of 50 percent polyester and 50 percent nylon double knit fabric that has been bleached. The bottom layer of the mattress pad is 100 percent polyester nonwoven fabric. Between the two fabrics is a layer of 100 percent polyester staple fiber bonded batting fabric. The three layers are quilted together in a wavy pattern. A 15-inch deep 100 percent polyester knit skirt with a full elasticized edge is attached to the mattress pad on all four sides. The mattress pad is imported in Full, Queen and King sizes. The manufacturing operations for the mattress pads are as follows: 

VIETNAM Polyester fabric (top layer) for Serta Ultra Loft Mattress Pad woven. Polyester double knit (top layer) for Serta Sleep Chill Mattress Pad knitted. Woven and knit polyester greige fabrics exported to China on rolls. CHINA Polyester batting fabrics formed. Polyester nonwoven backing fabrics formed. Polyester skirt fabric knitted. Elastic narrow fabric is formed. Fabrics are cut and assembled by sewing and quilted into mattress pads. Mattress pads are packaged and exported to the United States.
