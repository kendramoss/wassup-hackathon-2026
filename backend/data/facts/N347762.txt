In your letter dated April 14, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling. The product under consideration, “Solar Buddies,” is described as a refillable sunscreen applicator intended for applying sunscreen to the skin in a smooth, mess-free manner. The applicator consists of five components: a plastic bottle, a screw-top lid, a rollerball housing (to be integrated into the lid), the rollerball head, and a sponge applicator. The foam sponge will be manufactured in the United Kingdom. The other components are sourced from China. The parts will be assembled in the United Kingdom and then packaged for sale.
