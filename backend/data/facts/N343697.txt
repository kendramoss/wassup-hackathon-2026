In your letter dated November 1, 2024, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Hyundai Motor Company.There are two items at issue with this request, which are each described as wire harnesses for use specifically with motor vehicles. The first harness is identified by part number 39222-2S200 and is comprised of a group of insulated conductor wires bundled together and covered with plastic and terminated at the various ends with an assortment of connectors. You state that this harness is installed within the engine compartment and attached to the water temperature sensor in order to transfer electrical signals throughout the vehicle’s cooling system.The second harness is identified by part number 47891-4G000 and is also comprised of a group of insulated conductor wires bundled together and covered with plastic and terminated at the various ends with an assortment of connectors. You state that this item is installed within the suspension system of the vehicle in order to transfer the necessary electrical signals.In your request, you state that the manufacturing process for both parts is virtually identical and described herein. Insulated cable conductors as well as the various raw materials for the terminals and connectors are manufactured in South Korea and sent to China for further processing. In China, the cables are cut to the desired length and taped together in order to create the unterminated harness. The ends of the cables are then stripped and the connectors and terminals are assembled thereon. The finished harness is then shipped back to South Korea for final testing and packaging for shipment to the U.S.The “country of origin” is defined in
