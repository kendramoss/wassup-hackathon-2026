This replaces Ruling Number, N352331, dated September 12, 2025, which contained a clerical error. The United States-Korea Free Trade Agreement (UKFTA) analysis was omitted from the final ruling. A complete corrected ruling follows. The subject merchandise is Banchandanji Jeotgal (Seasoned Squid). The ready-to-eat product is composed of salted squid, starch syrup, red pepper powder, frozen minced garlic, paprika extract, sugar, frozen red chili pepper, fresh garlic, sesame seeds, mipung (umami seasoning blend), matsurae (flavor enhancer), ground ginger, anchovy fish sauce, glycine, xanthan gum, polymix-m (preservative blend) and compound phosphate. The manufacturing process involves cutting the squid into stripes, salting them and then blending them with the aforementioned seasoning ingredients. You state that the squid is procured and salted in China, then exported to Korea. In Korea, it is mixed with a proprietary seasoning mix. Jeotgal Seasoned Squid is commonly consumed as a side dish or as a condiment. You proposed classification of the product in subheading 1605.54.6030, HTSUS. We agree. Classification:
