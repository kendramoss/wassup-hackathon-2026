In your letter dated May 7, 2025, you requested a tariff classification and country of origin determination ruling on a steel chisel. Pictures and a description of the manufacturing process were provided with your submission. A chisel is a hand tool with a cutting edge that is used for carving or cutting a hard material, such as wood. This can be performed through hand pressure or by striking the end opposite to the blade with a tool, such as a hammer or mallet. You have stated that the cutting part of the tool does not contain by weight over 0.2 percent of chromium, molybdenum, or tungsten or over 0.1 percent of vanadium.
