The subject merchandise, item number 26C6SWTP10, described as the “Christmas Snowy Mountain Night Scene Decorative Pillow,” is an 18 × 18 inch decorative pillow. The pillow is made with an outer shell formed from two fabrics. The face side is made of 100 percent polyester slub woven fabric that is printed with a snowy Christmas scene including Santa Claus in his sleigh, a snowman, two mice, pine trees, two cabins, snow covered mountains and a ski lift and embroidered with stars on the top of three pine trees, a reindeer and a third mouse. The back is 100 percent polyester mink velvet fabric that is dyed blue. The back mink velvet fabric is lined with a nonwoven fabric. The fabrics form an outer shell and lining for the pillow that is stuffed with polyester fiber. The pillow is sewn with a knife edge. The manufacturing operations for the pillow are as follows: CHINA: Slub face fabric is woven. Slub face fabric is exported to Vietnam. VIETNAM: Mink velvet back fabric is woven. 

Nonwoven lining for back panel formed. Polyester staple fiber is manufactured. Slub face fabric is printed and embroidered. Fabric is cut and assembled by sewing the panels to form a pillow shell. Pillow shell is stuffed with polyester staple fiber and sewn closed. Finished pillow is packaged and exported to the United States.
