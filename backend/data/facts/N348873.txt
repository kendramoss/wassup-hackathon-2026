In your letter dated May 9, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Dongguan Lopie Paper Co., Ltd., for the purpose of duty calculation and Section 301 applicability. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review. The paper products under consideration are "toilet seat covers". They come in single ply with either 1/2 or 1/4 fold. Per your submission, the bulk rolls of toilet seat cover stock are manufactured in Indonesia. You describe the paper as White/Kraft 100% wood pulp virgin paper; layer:1; 12-18g/sm. These parent rolls of tissue paper, which measure in multiple widths from 420mm up to 430mm and have a maximum roll diameter of 380mm, are then sent to China, where the paper is embossed, cut and folded to length and width. The toilet seat covers are then subjected to quality control and packaged.
