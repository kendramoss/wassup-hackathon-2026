In your letter dated August 15, 2025, on behalf of Ingenus Pharmaceuticals, LLC, you requested a country of origin ruling. PROCARDIA XL, imported in 30 mg, 60 mg and 90 mg extended-release tablets, is a medicinal preparation containing Nifedipine, as the active ingredient. It is a calcium channel blocker indicated for the management of hypertension and vasospastic angina. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Nifedipine, is manufactured in Spain and that the finished tablets in dosage form are made in China. In China, the Nifedipine will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Procardia XL extended-release tablets in dosage form.
