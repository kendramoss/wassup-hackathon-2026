In your letter dated May 28, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on a sump pump. The product at issue is a Sump Pump, part number 80745. The submersible pump is commonly installed in basements of homes and buildings to automatically remove accumulated water to prevent flooding. The pump features a power cord and a float switch and consists of a stainless-steel body that encases an impeller and a power motor. With respect to origin, the final assembly of the pump occurs in Thailand using components from Thailand and China. Prior to the final assembly, the motor assembly occurs in Thailand. This process begins by attaching an O-ring, a wire bushing, a tapping screw, a lock washer, a grounding lead, and a spring washer to the top of a motor housing from China. Afterwards, the rotor and stator, both from Thailand, are assembled into the motor housing and enclosed using screws. Once complete, the motor is lubricated. The final assembly begins by threading and securing a power cord from Thailand through a hole on the top of the main housing from Thailand. Afterwards, a capacitor from China, a cable sheath from China, a cable retainer from Thailand, and a block from Thailand are secured in place using tapping screws. In the next step, a main pump housing assembly from Thailand is installed onto a motor assembly and screwed in place using rings, bearings, and washers. The process continues by placing an impeller from China onto the motor-pump subassembly. Lastly, a floating switch from Thailand is affixed onto the side of the main pump housing. Various tests and inspections are completed during the assembly process.
