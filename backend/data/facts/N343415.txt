In your letter dated October 18, 2024, you requested a country of origin ruling on a coated polyethylene microporous sheet on behalf of your client, SEMCORP America Inc.The product under consideration is referred to as a High-Performance Coated Separator for EV batteries. The product is composed of a high molecular weight polyethylene substrate with a single-application coating of polymer and ceramic materials. It is intended for use as a battery separator in electric vehicle (EV) batteries.
