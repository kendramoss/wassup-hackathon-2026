In your letter dated December 12, 2024, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Hangzhou Mingxuan Sanitary Products Co., Ltd., for the purposes of duty calculation and Section 301 applicability. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review.The items under consideration are packages of paper napkins, including “dinner napkins,” “lunch napkins,” “low fold napkins,” “tall fold napkins,” “off-fold napkins,” “interfold napkins,” and “beverage napkins.” They come in a variety of plies (single, double, and triple) and folds (1/2, 1/4, 1/6, 1/8, 1/12, and off-fold).Per your submission, the bulk rolls of napkin paper are manufactured in Indonesia. You describe the paper as White/Kraft paper: virgin; layer: 1/2; with a 12-18 GSM. These parent rolls of paper, which measure in multiple widths from 230 mm up to 2800 mm and have a maximum roll diameter of 1440 mm, with a core diameter of 76 mm, are then sent to China, where the paper is embossed, cut to size, folded, subjected to quality control, and packaged.
