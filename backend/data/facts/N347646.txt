This is in response to your letter dated April 10, 2025, requesting a tariff classification and country of origin determination, for the purpose of section 301, on one style of manila folder. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review. SKU 810838 is a letter-size 1/3 tab manila file folder. We note that previously we ruled on the same style folder (same SKU#) but with different countries of origin for the materials and labor; see ruling number N347187. Each separate ruling applies to the specific circumstances outlined in each, regardless of the matching SKU numbers, which you now state you will amend to include the two-digit country code. Per your submission, the jumbo rolls of paper are made in Taiwan. They are then sent to Malaysia, where the paper will be colored, die-cut to shape, and folded. Still in Malaysia, the folders are packaged in retail boxes and then packed for export to the United States.
