This is in response to your request dated April 16, 2026, on behalf of the exporter Mirka Ltd., requesting reconsideration of New York (“NY”) ruling N360536, dated April 15, 2026. We have reviewed your request and affirm NY N360536. NY N360536 describes the subject merchandise as follows: The merchandise at issue is described as the Mirka Iridium SR PSA and Mirka Iridium SR Grip. They are abrasive discs designed to be affixed to tools in order to sand epoxy, acrylic, and other resin-based surfaces on road, aerospace, rail, and marine vehicles. From the information you provided, the manufacturing process of the Mirka Iridium SR PSA is as follows: the “Jumbo J1 roll,” which consists of artificial silicon carbide abrasive grains affixed to a PET film backing, is imported into Finland from China. A roll of “release paper” is imported into Finland from Italy. In Finland, a pressure-sensitive adhesive is applied to the back of the Jumbo J1 roll. The Jumbo J1 roll is then laminated onto the release paper. This material is laser-cut, resulting in multiple individual discs on the uncut roll of release paper. In use, the discs are peeled off the release paper and affixed to tools using the pressure-sensitive adhesive. For the Mirka Iridium SR Grip, the Jumbo J1 roll is imported into Finland from China. “Grip fabric,” which has a hook-and-loop backing, is imported into Finland 
2 from Italy. In Finland, the Jumbo J1 roll is laminated to the grip fabric. This material is then laser-cut into separate, individual discs. In use, the discs are affixed to tools using the hook-and-loop backing. NY N360536 concluded that the Jumbo J1 roll manufactured in China was identifiable upon importation into Finland as abrasive grain affixed to a Polyethylene Terephthalate (“PET”) film backing.
