In your letter, dated October 29, 2024, you requested a classification and country of origin determination on behalf of your client, Channel Seafood International (Boca Raton, FL).You have outlined a scenario in which whole, uncleaned, and frozen squid with the scientific name Uroteuthis Duvauceli (also known as the Indian Ocean Squid or Indian Squid) of Indian origin will be shipped to China. In China, the squid will be defrosted, eviscerated to eliminate innards, cleaned, cut into rings, packed in various combinations, frozen, and shipped for import to the United States.
