In your letter dated March 17, 2023, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of your client, Covertech Flexible Packaging.The product under consideration is a metallized polyethylene terephthalate (PET) film with a backing of polyethylene and an anti-corrosive lacquer coating. You indicate that the film is an anti-corrosive/fire retardant product used in HVAC insulation, pipe wrap and various other reflective insulation products. The film will be imported into the United States in rolls. You suggest classification of this product in 3921, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS). We disagree.
