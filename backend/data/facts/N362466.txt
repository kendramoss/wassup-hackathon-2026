In your submission dated June 22, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling for purposes of Section 301 duties, other tariffs and marking. Pictures and descriptions of the manufacturing processes were included in your submission. The item under consideration is identified as a pry bar, SKU 57721. It is a hand tool that incorporates a plastic handle attached to a steel shaft with a tapered working end and a nail-pulling slot. The tool operates by applying manual force to create leverage, allowing the user to lift, separate, or remove materials and extract nails. The manufacturing process for the pry bar begins in Thailand, where Chinese-origin steel coil is straightened to ensure dimensional accuracy and is cut to the required lengths of the shaft. One end of the shaft is then pressed and formed into the tapered working end, excess material is removed, and the steel is machined to the required dimensions and profile. The other end of the shaft is mechanically pressed to form the side wings/flanges that will attach to the plastic handle. The working end of the shaft is subsequently bent to the required angle to create the leverage geometry necessary for the pry bar’s intended function. Product identification and branding are permanently pressed into the shaft before it is polished to remove burrs and surface imperfections. The shaft is then sent to China where it is heat treated, electroplated and assembled with a Chinese-origin injection-molded plastic handle.
