This is in response to your letter dated January 2, 2025, on behalf of your client, Jinan Hehe Paper Co., Ltd, requesting a country of origin determination of paper napkins for purposes of Section 301 duties. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review.The items under consideration are packages of paper napkins, including “dinner napkins,” “luncheon napkins,” “beverage napkins,” “interfold napkins,” "tall napkins,” and “low fold napkins.” They come in a variety of plies (single, double, and triple) and folds (1/4, 1/2, 1/6, 1/8).Per your submission, the bulk rolls of napkin stock are manufactured in Indonesia. You describe the paper as White/Kraft paper: virgin; layer: 1/2; with a 12-19 GSM. These parent rolls of paper, which measure in multiple widths from 340 mm up to 1360 mm and have a maximum roll diameter 1440mm, are then sent to China, where the paper is embossed, cut to size, folded, subjected to quality control, and packaged.
