In your letter dated July 4, 2023, you requested a country of origin ruling for a pear head extendable ratchet tool produced in two countries, China and South Korea.The subject ratchet tool is a hand tool composed of a long handle/neck with a pear-shaped ratcheting head that is used to tighten fasteners. The tool’s ratcheting head fits onto fasteners via socket attachments of various sizes that are not imported with this product or included with this product when it is sold (sockets are bought separately and used in association with the ratchet handle). When the tool’s ratcheting head (with socket) is attached to a fastener and the tool is grasped by the handle and turned in one direction it turns freely, and when it is turned in the other direction, it engages to tighten the fastener. The tool’s handle is extendable to enhance leverage.
