In your letter dated June 19, 2024, you requested a country of origin ruling on Clindamycin Phosphate Gel, USP 1%.Clindamycin Phosphate Gel, USP 1% is a medicinal preparation containing Clindamycin Phosphate, an antibiotic, as the active ingredient. It is indicated for the treatment of acne vulgaris.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Clindamycin Phosphate, is manufactured in China and the finished Clindamycin Phosphate Gel in dosage form is made in India. In India, the Clindamycin Phosphate will be mixed with inactive ingredients to produce the final Clindamycin Phosphate Gel in dosage form.
