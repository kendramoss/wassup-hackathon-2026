In your letter dated May 29, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on tire repair plugs Item number SP001, SKU number 1687681, Tire Repair Plugs, are composed of polyester yarn that has been completely coated or embedded within a mixture composed primarily of butyl rubber. The product is a tubeless tire repair product. Butyl rubber, polyester yarn and a petroleum hydrocarbon resin are sourced in Malaysia. A vulcanizing agent and an accelerator are sourced in China. In Malaysia, the butyl rubber, petroleum hydrocarbon resin, vulcanizing agent and accelerator are weighed out according to a precise formula. They are added to a batching tank, heated and agitated to soften the rubber. They are then transferred to a mixer for complete blending. The polyester yarn is unwound from the reel and dipped into the rubber mixture to fully coat the yarn. The coated yarn enters a curing tunnel to be dried and cured at a set temperature. After drying, the rubber-coated yarn is rewound into rolls for subsequent processing. Multiple rubber-coated yarns are then fed into the laminating equipment where they are pressed into a flat composite slab. The laminated slab is slit into continuous rubber strips of specified widths. The slit rubber strips are stacked and loaded into a high-temperature test chamber. They are preheated to a set temperature and then vulcanized at the set temperature and time to form a stable three-dimensional cross-linked structure. They undergo product testing and are packaged for exportation to the United States.
