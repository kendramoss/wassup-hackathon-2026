In your letter dated February 6, 2023, you requested a tariff classification, country of origin and marking ruling.A description and video of the manufacturing process, product specification sheets, and photos accompanied your letter.The subject merchandise consists of three products: dehydrated lemon pieces, dehydrated lemon wheels and dehydrated lemon powder. The products are said to contain 100 percent of the characteristic fruit with no other flavorings, additives or preservatives. The fruit will be grown and harvested in Spain, South Africa, Egypt, Mexico, the United States or Turkey and will be processed in Canada by washing, slicing and conventional dehydration. The dehydrated lemon powder will also be subjected to grinding into powder form. The products will be imported in bulk and retail containers. When imported for retail, they will be packaged in 1-kilogram (kg.) standup pouches measuring 11 x 14 inches. When imported in bulk, they will be packaged in poly bags ranging from 7 to 10 kg.
