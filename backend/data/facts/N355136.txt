In your letter dated October 22, 2025, you requested a tariff classification and country of origin determination ruling. Descriptive information was provided with the submission. The item under consideration is a Timing Chain Kit, item 9-4200SA. The items in the kit consist of a timing chain guide, a timing chain, a tensioner assembly, and two sprockets (a camshaft and a crankshaft sprocket) packaged together ready for retail sale. The items, which are primarily constructed of steel, are used to replace the timing system of an internal combustion engine used in motorized vehicles (sedans, sport utility vehicles, crossover utility vehicles, minivans and cargo vans). The repair kit, which constitutes goods classified in two or more headings, is put up in a set ready for retail sale.
