In your letter dated May 1, 2025, you requested a ruling regarding the tariff classification of copper electrode blanks from the Netherlands and the country of origin of finished copper electrodes. Technical information was submitted with your request. The items under consideration for classification are described as copper electrode blanks used in the manufacture of finished copper electrodes to be incorporated into plasma arc cutting machine torches. In your letter, you explain that at importation, the blanks are not ready for direct use but have undergone machining processes such that the blanks possess the dimensions and shape of a finished copper electrode. In the Netherlands, copper rods are intricately machined to precise shape and tolerances using a Swiss-style computer numerical control (CNC) lathe. The machining processes include turning, boring, threading, drilling, milling, and grooving. Upon importation, the copper electrode blanks are ready for coating, laser marking, and assembly with other components as preparation for direct use.
