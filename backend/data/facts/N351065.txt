In your letter dated July 7, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Dongguan Weisheng Paper Products Co, Ltd. for the purpose of duty calculation and Section 301 applicability. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review. The paper product under consideration is packages of "toilet tissue." They come in double and triple ply with 1/2 fold. Per your submission, the bulk rolls of toilet tissue stock are manufactured in Indonesia. You describe the paper as White/Kraft 100% wood pulp virgin paper; layer:2; 11.5-13.5 g/sm. These parent rolls of tissue paper, which measure in multiple widths from 400mm up to 1440mm and have a maximum roll diameter of 1440mm, are then sent to China, where the tissue paper is unwound, embossed, and cut and folded to length and width. The toilet tissue is then subjected to quality control and packaged.
