In your letter dated January 26, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Green Planet Paper Co., Ltd, for the purpose of duty calculation and Section 301 applicability. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review. The paper product under consideration is packages of “toilet paper jumbo roll.” They come in rolls in either single-ply or double-ply. Per your submission, the parent rolls of toilet paper stock are manufactured in Indonesia. You describe the paper as white, 100% wood pulp produced from virgin fiber; two-ply with a basis weight ranging from approximately 13.5 to 15 grams per square meter. These parent rolls of tissue paper, which measure in multiple widths from approximately 2800 mm to 2830 mm with a maximum roll diameter of approximately 2760 mm, are then sent to China, where the paper is unwound, embossed, rewound onto cores, and cut to size. The toilet paper is then subjected to quality control and packaged.
