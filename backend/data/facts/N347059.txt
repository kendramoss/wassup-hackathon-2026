In your letter dated March 24, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on medical drapes. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review. The items under consideration are paper medical drapes used to provide patients with personal privacy during medical exams. This is a single-use, disposable, non-sterile product. These drapes feature a double or triple ply paper construction for enhanced strength. They will come in widths of 40” and lengths of 48" to 90" and are available in white, blue and pink. Per your submission, the jumbo rolls of paper are sourced from Indonesia. You describe the paper as White, 100% wood pulp virgin paper; 1-ply layer; 16-20 g/sm. These jumbo rolls of tissue paper, which measure 1020mm in width and have a diameter of 1150mm, are then shipped to China, where the paper is cut to size, folded and packaged.
