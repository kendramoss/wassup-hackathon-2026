In your letter dated June 25, 2025, you requested a country of origin determination for purposes of Section 301 and IEEPA duties. Pictures of the merchandise in different stages of manufacture were included with your submission, along with a narrative of the manufacturing processes. The item under consideration is a fire rake. It is a hand tool used for digging trenches, clearing underbrush, and creating fire breaks. The tool consists of a double-sided head connected to a fiberglass handle. One side of the head is a hoe blade and the other side is a rake with six tines. The manufacturing process for the fire rake begins in Vietnam, where raw steel is laser-cut into the final shape of the double-sided rake head. This includes the six tines of the rake and the hoe blade. The fully formed rake head is then sent to China where it is riveted and welded to a connection point that accommodates the handle. Finally, the rake head and connection point are powder coated and assembled with a fiberglass handle.
