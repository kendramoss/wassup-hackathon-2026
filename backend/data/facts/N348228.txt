In your letter dated April 24, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Xstar Global Sourcing LLC, for the purpose of duty calculation and Section 301 applicability. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review. The paper products under consideration are "dinner napkins," "lunch napkins," "beverage napkins," "tall- fold napkins," "low -fold napkins," "off-fold napkins," and "interfold napkins." They come in either single, double or triple ply with off-fold, 1/2, 1/4, 1/6, 1/8 or 1/12 fold. Per your submission, the bulk rolls of napkin stock are manufactured in Indonesia. You describe the paper as White/Kraft 100% wood pulp virgin paper; layer:1-3; 12-21g/sm. These parent rolls of tissue paper, which measure in multiple widths from 230mm up to 2800mm and have a maximum roll diameter 1500mm, are then sent to China, where the paper is printed, embossed, perforated, cut, and folded. The napkins are then subjected to quality control and packaged.
