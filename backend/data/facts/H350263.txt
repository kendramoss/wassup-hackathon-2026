The disc launcher is an unassembled kit for children ages 8-12 comprised of all necessary components to assemble the toy. All components are imported into the United States unassembled packaged together in a build box. The individual components are: a generic 3V DC motor, a flywheel, a wood board including a plywood trigger and a guide rail (collectively “wood boards”), a battery pack and batteries, plastic discs, EVA foam-double stickers, paper tubes, plastic tab, bolts, nuts, O-rings, and rubber bands. The motor will originate from Japan, the Republic of Korea, Taiwan, or Vietnam. The remaining components originate from China. The importer may source the flywheel from the same countries as the motor in the future.
