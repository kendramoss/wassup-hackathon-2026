In your letter dated October 31, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on sheets of CR39 or polycarbonate. The product under consideration is noted to be safety plates composed of CR39 or polycarbonate. The plates are rectangular in shape, measuring either 2 inches by 4 inches or 4 inches by 5 inches. The plates have not been optically worked in any way. They will be incorporated into welding helmets to protect the eyes from projectiles. In your request, you suggest classification of the safety plates in heading 9001, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS). We disagree.
