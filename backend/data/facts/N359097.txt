In your letter dated February 23, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on Doxycycline Hyclate Tablets in dosage form. Doxycycline Hyclate, imported in 100 mg tablet form, is a broad-spectrum antibiotic derived from oxytetracycline. It is indicated for the treatment or prevention of infections that are proven or strongly suspected to be caused by bacteria. In your letter, you stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Doxycycline Hyclate is manufactured in Macau and that the finished tablets in dosage form is made in India. In India, the Doxycycline Hyclate will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Doxycycline Hyclate Tablets USP in dosage form.
