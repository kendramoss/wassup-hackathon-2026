This mixture will be chemically synthesized with non-originating sodium hydroxide and liquid ammonia. Washing and dehydration processes will be performed. The semi-finished good A is defined as a “precursor” in the commercial market.Process 2: In Canada, the non-USMCA originating semi-finished good A will be mixed with lithium hydroxide and doping compounds under a special manufacturing process where [X] (“proprietary substance 1”) and [X] (“proprietary substance 2”) are added and then fired at high temperature. The semi-finished good B is called a “doped-cathode active material” in the commercial market. The semi-finished good B will be imported into the United States for use in lithium-ion batteries or will be put into Process 3 below.Process 3: In Canada, the semi-finished good B will be coated under a special coating process where USMCA originating or non-originating boric acid of subheading 2810.00.00, HTSUS, will be added at a high temperature to produce coated and doped Cobalt Lithium Manganese Nickel Oxide, CAS No. 182442-95-1 (“finished good”) of subheading 3824.99.39, HTSUS, which will be further de-sieved, de-ironed, and packaged.
