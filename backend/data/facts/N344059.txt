In your letter dated November 15, 2024, on behalf of Encube Ethicals Private Limited, you requested a country of origin ruling on Azelaic Acid Gel, 15% (ANDA 208724).Azelaic Acid Gel, 15%, imported in 50 grams tubes, is an aqueous gel which contains azelaic acid, a naturally-occurring saturated dicarboxylic acid. It is indicated for topical treatment of the inflammatory papules and pustules of mild to moderate rosacea.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Azelaic Acid, is made in Germany, and that the finished gel in dosage form is made in India. In India, the Azelaic Acid will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Azelaic Acid Gel in dosage form.
