In your letter dated September 22, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on LED lights for purposes of applying current trade remedies. The merchandise at issue is four models of LED lights identified as under-cabinet light, headlamp, utility light, and pivot spotlight. LED under-cabinet light Each light is comprised of a total of twenty-one (21) individual components. The major components include an LED PCBA (Printed Circuit Board Assembly), a control PCBA, an ABS (Acrylonitrile Butadiene Styrene) cover, an ABS battery compartment, a lens, a polarity connector, AAA batteries, etc. Particularly, the control PCBA consists of nineteen (19) discrete components, and the LED PCBA consists of six (6) discrete components. Headlamp Each light is comprised of a total of nineteen (19) individual components. The major components include a PCBA, which the control PCBA and LED PCBA are combined in a circuit board, an ABS cover, an ABS battery compartment, a lens, a Li-Ion rechargeable battery, etc. Particularly, the control PCBA consists of nineteen (19) discrete components, and the LED PCBA consists of thirteen (13) discrete components. Utility light Each light is comprised of a total of forty-nine (49) individual components. The major components include an LED PCBA, a control PCBA, an ABS cover, an ABS battery compartment, a lens, a Li-Ion rechargeable battery, etc. Particularly, the control PCBA consists of forty-one (41) discrete components, and the LED PCBA consists of sixty-one (61) discrete components. 

Pivoting spotlight Each light is comprised of a total of seventy-two (72) individual components. The major components include an LED PCBA, a control PCBA, an ABS handle, an ABS battery compartment, a lens, a polarity connector, rechargeable batteries, etc. Particularly, the control PCBA consists of one hundred and twenty-one (121) discrete components, and the LED PCBA consists of two (2) discrete components. You present a manufacturing process scenario for the LED lights which is summarized as follows: The components are imported into Thailand where they will first be made into the PCBA and the SMD LED sub-assembly with the discrete components (circuit board, LED, resistor, capacitor, infrared induction probe, etc.) through the LED chip-mounting operation and the Surface Mount Technology (SMT) process to fabricate the LED light source. The LED chip-mounting operation undergoes the procedures of stencil alignment, solder paste printing, solder paste inspection, LED chip placement, reflow soldering, automated optical inspection and functional testing. Specifically, the SMT process that creates the PCBAs undergo the procedures of pre-production preparation, stencil preparation and solder paste printing, automated component placement, reflow soldering, and manual soldering of positive/negative wires. Subsequently, these PCBAs and the SMD LED sub-assemblies will be sent to China and assembled with the balance of the Chinese-origin components, e.g., housings (including battery compartments), lenses, wires and buttons, metal hardware and accessories, etc., to produce the finished LED lights.
