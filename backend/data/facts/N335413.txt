In your letter, dated September 19, 2023, you requested a classification and country of origin determination on behalf of your client, Channel Seafood International (Boca Raton, FL).You have outlined a scenario in which whole in-shell scallops with the scientific name Patinopecten Yessoensis will be harvested from an aquaculture facility in Japan, frozen, and shipped to China. In China, the scallops will be defrosted, washed, shucked, sorted, sized, glazed, metal-detected, inspected, and weighed, with the abductor muscle of the scallop then individually quick frozen. The finished product, frozen scallop meat, will be packaged and shipped frozen from China for import to the United States.You seek a determination as to the tariff classification and country of origin of the above-described product.
