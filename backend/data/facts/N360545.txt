In your letter dated April 8, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on Cevimeline Hydrochloride Capsules, USP in dosage form. Cevimeline Hydrochloride, imported in 30 mg capsules, is a cholinergic agonist and a parasympathomimetic agent. It is indicated for the treatment of dry mouth in patients with Sjögren’s Syndrome. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Cevimeline Hydrochloride, is manufactured in India and that the finished capsules in dosage form are also made in India. In India, Cevimeline Hydrochloride will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Cevimeline Hydrochloride Capsules in dosage form.
