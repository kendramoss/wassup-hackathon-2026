In your letter, dated October 7, 2025, you requested a ruling on tariff classification and a country of origin determination. The subject merchandise is a Black Tea product called Tsit Wing Blue Label Tea. It is said to contain a blend consisting in equal parts of Sri Lankan and China Dust tea. The 50-50 tea product is grown, harvested, processed, and packaged as raw materials in Sri Lanka and China. The raw materials are then shipped to Hong Kong for inspection, blending, packaging, and shipping. The product is imported as loose tea to the United States in five-pound metalized film bags.
