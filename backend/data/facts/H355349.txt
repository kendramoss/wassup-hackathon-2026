The product at issue is Yellowfin and Bigeye tuna caught in the RMI’s Exclusive Economic Zone (“EEZ”). The catching vessels are flagged in the People’s Republic of China (“China”) and the Federated States of Micronesia (“Micronesia”). The tuna is caught and processed in the following manner: H&G Tuna is caught by line. On the catching vessel, the tuna is gutted and gilled.
