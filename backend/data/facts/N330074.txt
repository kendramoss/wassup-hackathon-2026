In your letter dated January 4, 2023, on behalf of Dr. Reddy’s Laboratories, Inc., you requested a tariff classification and country of origin determination ruling.Sunitinib Malate, imported in 12.5 mg, 25 mg, 37.5 mg and 50 mg capsules, is a receptor tyrosine kinase inhibitor. It is indicated for the treatment of renal cell carcinoma (RCC) and imatinib resistant gastrointestinal stromal tumor (GIST).
