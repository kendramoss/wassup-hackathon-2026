In your letter dated April 7, 2023, you requested a country of origin ruling on Methylene Blue Injection USP, 50 mg/10 mL.Methylene Blue Injection is indicated for the treatment of pediatric and adult patients with acquired methemoglobinemia.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Methylene Blue, is manufactured in Italy and that the finished Methylene Blue Injection in dosage form is made in India. In India, the Methylene Blue will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Methylene Blue Injection in dosage form.
