In your letter dated March 18, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Anqiu Xiangyu Packaging and Printing Co., Ltd, for the purpose of duty calculation and Section 301 applicability. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review. The paper products under consideration are packages of "facial tissue." They come in a variety of plies (single, double, and triple) and 1/2 fold. Per your submission, the bulk rolls of facial tissue stock are manufactured in Indonesia. You describe the paper as White/Kraft paper: virgin; layer: 1-3; 11-20 g/sm. These parent rolls of tissue paper, which measure in multiple widths from 150mm up to 250mm and have a maximum roll diameter 250mm, are then sent to China, where the tissue paper is embossed, folded into sheets, and cut to size. The finished facial tissues are then subjected to quality control and packaged.
