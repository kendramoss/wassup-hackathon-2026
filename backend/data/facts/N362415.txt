In your letter dated June 17, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on a ball joint for marking purposes and for purposes of applying trade remedies under Section 301 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, from China. The article under consideration is a cross axis ball joint (CABJ), Part Number 1044454-00-B / CCA0116, which is a precision component of a passenger-vehicle suspension. The CABJ connects the control arm to the steering knuckle and is mounted inside the suspension link in place of a conventional rubber bushing. By increasing the lateral (transverse) stiffness of the link, it minimizes changes in suspension geometry and improves ride comfort and handling stability. During the vertical motion of the wheel (bump and rebound), the ball stud of the CABJ articulates (rotating and flexing) while transmitting load. The CABJ therefore performs a core kinematic function of the suspension system. The CABJ is composed of the following seven components: pillow ball, boot, plug, lower clamp ring, upper clamp ring, and ball seat, all sourced from Korea, and socket housing from China. These components are joined into a single precision ball-joint mechanism through precision assembly and torque-verification processes performed in Korea.
