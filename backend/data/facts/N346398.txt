In your letter dated March 1, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on an air fryer on behalf of your client, Midea Consumer Electric (Vietnam) Co., Ltd (“MVN”). The product under consideration is described as the “MVN Air Fryer IX,” model number MF-CY55MS1. This air fryer is a domestic electric countertop device that cooks food by circulating hot air around the ingredients using fan blades. The air fryer is manufactured in Vietnam through a three-stage process. The first stage involves three phases: Phase One involves the production of the structural body and other plastic components (e.g., the bottom cover, motor cover, power box, heat insulation block, internal base plate bracket, injection molded upper housing, injection molded lower housing, ring rail, injection molded front bucket cover, air outlet window, control panel bracket, left and right windshield brackets) by an injection molding process. The plastic raw materials are sourced from Vietnam, Thailand, Cambodia, and China. Phase Two involves the production of the metal parts, including the internal base plate, sheet metal upper housing and lower housing, edge trim (or surrounding wall), reflecting cover and sheet metal front bucket cover from steel coiled materials. The fabrication will be completed in Vietnam from steel sourced from either Vietnam or China. Phase Three involves the production of the heating pipe or heating element, which is manufactured by a third-party supplier of MVN in Vietnam from various raw materials. 

In the next stage, the display and power boards are manufactured by a third-party supplier in Vietnam from various raw materials. In the final stage, also performed in Vietnam, the Vietnamese-origin body and other plastic and metal components will be placed with the remaining components originating from Vietnam, China, and Malaysia on the main assembly line to produce subassemblies (e.g., the bucket and base subassemblies) followed by final assembly of the finished MVN Air Fryer IX. It will then be tested, inspected, and packaged.
