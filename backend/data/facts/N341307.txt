In your letter dated July 16, 2024, you requested a tariff classification and country of origin determination ruling.The product under consideration is referred to as a Ceramic Coated Lithium-Ion Battery Separator. It is described as a thin microporous sheet of high-density polyethylene (HDPE) film. The film is coated with a boehmite/acrylic resin. The film is used as a lithium-ion battery separator. Its purpose is to physically block direct contact between the anode and cathode in the lithium-ion battery. It is a secondary battery component that prevents fire and explosion. The presence of the coating is said to improve thermal stability and wettability. It will be imported on a roll.
