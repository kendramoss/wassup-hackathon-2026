The subject merchandise, style “SK Silex UL Thailand,” is double-knit fabric with a silicone coating. The coated fabric is composed of 39.7 percent silicone coating, 60.3 percent polyester filament fibers, by weight. The silicone coating consists of one outer compact plastic layer. The combined material weighs 615g/m2. The fabric is being imported in widths of 54 inches and used for upholstery. Based upon our observation, the silicone coating is visible to the naked eye. The manufacturing operations for the coated upholstery fabric are as follows: Thailand Fabric is knitted, bleached and dyed. Fabric is shipped to China. China Fabric is coated with a silicone outer compact plastic layer. The finished fabric is packaged and then shipped to United States.
