In your letter dated October 21, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on Lidocaine and Prilocaine Cream. Lidocaine and Prilocaine Cream, is a medicinal preparation containing a combination of Lidocaine and Prilocaine, as the active ingredients. It is indicated as a topical anesthetic for use on normal intact skin for local analgesia and genital mucous membranes for superficial minor surgery and as pretreatment for infiltration anesthesia. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs), Lidocaine and Prilocaine, are manufactured in Taiwan and Belgium, respectively. The finished cream in dosage form is made in India. In India, the Lidocaine and Prilocaine APIs will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final topical cream in dosage form.
