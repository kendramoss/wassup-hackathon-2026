The item identified as the “Quincy Quilt” is a quilt constructed in five layers: face and back panels of 100 percent cotton woven fabric; two internal layers of 100 percent olefin nonwoven scrim and a middle layer of 100 percent polyester batting. The outer face is printed to resemble a pieced patchwork pattern in alternating pink, blue, and cream vintage floral motif squares. The back panel is printed with a single all-over floral motif on a cream background. The five layers are quilted together in a diamond pattern. The edges of the quilt are finished with a ½ inch wide strip of 100 percent cotton woven printed binding with mitered corners. The quilt contains no embroidery, lace, braid, edging, trimming, piping, or applique work. The quilt is available in two sizes: Full/Queen (90 x 92 inches) SKU# 600P25252001 and King (108 x 92 inches) SKU# 600P25253001. The item identified as the “Quincy Sham” is a coordinating quilted face pillow sham in the same fabrics, print and construction as the Quincy Quilt. The back of the sham matches the back of the quilt and features an opening with a hidden zipper closure near the binding at the bottom of the sham. The sham contains no embroidery, lace, braid, edging, trimming, piping, or applique work. The quilted sham is available in two sizes: Standard (20" x 26") SKU# 600P25255001 and King (20" x 36") SKU# 600P25254001. The quilt and sham are packaged and sold separately under the Lauren Ralph Lauren Home® brand.
The manufacturing operations for the quilt and sham are as follows: VIETNAM: 100 percent cotton greige fabric is woven. 100 percent polyester batting is formed. 100 percent olefin nonwoven scrim is formed. 100 percent polyester sewing thread is formed. Cotton woven greige fabric for the front and back panels is exported to China. Fabrics are cut into panels and binding fabric is cut into strips for both comforter and shams. Polyester batting and olefin nonwoven scrim panels are placed between the face and backing panels and quilted. Hidden zipper is sewn along bottom edge of the sham’s backing fabric. Binding is sewn to all edges. The quilt and quilted sham are packaged separately for retail sale and exported to the United States. CHINA: The cotton fabrics undergo printing, bleaching, and shrinking. Zipper is manufactured. Finished fabric and zipper component are exported to Vietnam.
