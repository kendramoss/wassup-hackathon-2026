In your letter dated August 20, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on a plastic serving dish and plastic dipping bowls. The item under consideration is a plastic serving dish and three plastic dipping bowls, item WIC 666789. The items are all made of melamine plastic. The oblong-shaped plastic serving dish accommodates and holds three round plastic dipping bowls. The articles are designed for serving of snacks, sauces, and condiments. The plastic serving dish measures approximately 15 inches long by 6 inches wide by 2 inches high. Each plastic dipping bowl measures approximately 4 inches in diameter and is 2 inches high. They will be packaged and sold together for retail sale. Based on the information you provided, the raw materials are sourced from Vietnam. The production process that takes place in Vietnam is divided into four phases including: 1. manufacturing, including preparation of melamine compound into serving dish and bowl shapes, 2. assembling, polishing, and decal printing, 3. packaging, 4. testing and inspection. In your letter, you state that all processing occurs at the manufacturer facility in Vietnam.
