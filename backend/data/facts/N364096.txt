In your letter dated August 6, 2026, you requested a tariff classification and country of origin ruling on behalf of Conglom Inc. In lieu of samples, illustrative literature and product descriptions were provided for review. Item 1, the “Laundry Cabinet Kit (SKU QL114),” is a storage cabinet, utility sink, two-handle pullout faucet, and strainer kit. The cabinet is constructed from particleboard, medium density fiberboard (MDF), and the cabinet’s exterior is finished with painted melamine. The sink is constructed of Acrylonitrile Butadiene Styrene (ABS) and is 14.5" deep. The two-handle pullout faucet and the strainer are constructed of stainless steel. The overall product dimensions are approximately 24.24" in width, 33.85" in height, and 21.45" in length. Upon importation into the United States, the kit will be packaged in a single carton and intended for residential laundry and utility purposes. Item 2, the “Laundry Cabinet Kit (SKU QL113),” is a storage cabinet, utility sink, single lever pulldown faucet, and strainer kit. The cabinet is constructed from particleboard, MDF, and the cabinet’s exterior is finished with painted melamine. The sink is constructed of stainless steel and is 9.5" deep. The single lever pulldown faucet and the strainer are also constructed of stainless steel. The overall product dimensions are approximately 24.17" in width, 33.5" in height, and 21.34" in length. Upon importation into the United States, the kit will be packaged in a single carton and intended for residential laundry and utility purposes. The ruling request seeks classification of item 1 and item 2 in subheading 9403.60.8093, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS). Further, for item 1, the ruling request outlines a sourcing operation wherein the ABS sink, faucet, and the strainer will be sourced in China. For item 2, the ruling request outlines a sourcing operation wherein the stainless-steel sink, faucet, and the strainer will be sourced in China. The material components will be
shipped and exported to Vietnam. In Vietnam, for item 1 and item 2, the cabinet raw material components will be locally sourced. The manufacturing operations undertaken in Vietnam for the cabinet involves (1) wood cutting of the particleboard and the MDF panels, (2) edge banding of the panels, (3) drilling of the panels, (4) assembly of the cabinet doors to the frame, and (5) painting of the cabinet doors and the frame. For item 1 and item 2, in Vietnam, the Chinese ABS sink, stainless steel sink, faucets, and the strainers will not undergo a manufacturing operation that results in a physical change such as die cutting, grinding, riveting, threading, hole punching, welding, soldering, fusing, machining, knurling, or crimping. In Vietnam, the Chinese origin components will be unpacked and repackaged with a specific Vietnamese cabinet. In Vietnam, the laundry cabinet kits will be inspected, packaged, and then exported to the U.S. The Harmonized Commodity Description and Coding System Explanatory Notes (ENs) constitutes the official interpretation of the Harmonized System at the international level. The ENs to Chapter 94 of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule (HTS), for “Furniture,” states “the term ‘furniture’ means: (A) Any ‘moveable’ articles (not included under other more specific headings of the Nomenclature), which have the essential characteristic that they are constructed for placing on the floor or ground, and which are used, mainly with a utilitarian purpose, to equip private dwellings, hotels, theatres, cinemas, offices, churches, schools, cafes, restaurants, laboratories, hospitals dentists’ surgeries, etc., or ships, aircraft, railway coaches, motor vehicles, caravan-trailers or similar means of transport. (It should be noted that, for the purposes of this Chapter, articles are considered to be ‘movable’ furniture even if they are designed for bolting, etc., to the floor, e.g., chairs for use on ships). Similar articles (seats, chairs, etc.,) for use in gardens, squares, promenades, etc., are also included in this category.” The ENs to Chapter 94, heading 9403 of the HTS, states “This heading covers furniture and parts thereof, not covered by the previous headings. It includes furniture for general use (e.g., cupboards, show cases, tables, telephone stands, writing desks, escritoires, book cases, and other shelved furniture (including single shelves presented with supports for fixing them to the wall), etc.), and also furniture for special uses.”
