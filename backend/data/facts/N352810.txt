In your letter dated August 23, 2025, you requested a classification, marking, country of origin, and eligibility of the USMCA ruling. Images were provided in lieu of a sample. The product under consideration is described as the “Remote Daddy.” It is made by 3D printing using a polylactic acid (“PLA”) plastic filament. It is intended to hold a TV/streaming remote control during normal operation to protect the remote from being chewed on by pets, children removing the batteries, and impact damage from accidentally being dropped. You indicate this item lacks any electrical components. As the “Remote Daddy” would be considered an article of plastic, and as it is not more specifically provided for elsewhere, the applicable subheading will be 3926.90.9989, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS), which provides for “[o]ther articles of plastics and articles of other materials of headings 3901 to 3914: [o]ther: [o]ther: [o]ther.” The column one, general rate of duty is 5.3 percent ad valorem. Per your submission, this article is 3D printed in Alberta, Canada from non-originating plastic filaments.
