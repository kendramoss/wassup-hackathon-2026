In your letter dated October 17, 2023, you requested a country of origin ruling on Tavaborole Topical Solution 5%.Tavaborole Topical Solution 5% is an oxaborole antifungal indicated for the treatment of onychomycosis of the toenails due to Trichophyton rubrum or Trichophyton mentagrophyte.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Tavaborole, is manufactured in India and that the finished topical solution in dosage form is also made in India. In India, the Tavaborole will be mixed with inactive ingredients from various countries to produce the final topical solution in dosage form.
