In your letter dated December 30, 2024, you requested a country of origin ruling on Divalproex Sodium Delayed-Release Capsules.Divalproex Sodium, imported in 125 mg delayed-release capsules, is an anticonvulsant. It is indicated for the treatment and management of seizure disorders, mania and migraine headaches.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Divalproex Sodium is manufactured in Taiwan and that the finished delayed-release capsules in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Divalproex Sodium will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final capsules in dosage form.
