In your letter dated June 23, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Dongguan Pengxing Paper Co., Ltd, for the purpose of duty calculation and Section 301 applicability. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review. The paper products under consideration are "beverage napkins," "luncheon napkins," and "dinner napkins." They come in either single or double ply with 1/2, 1/4 or 1/8 fold. Per your submission, the bulk rolls of napkin stock are manufactured in Indonesia. You describe the paper as White/Kraft 100% wood pulp virgin paper; layer:1-2; 12-25 g/sm. These parent rolls of tissue paper, which measure in multiple widths from 400mm up to 1000mm and have a maximum roll diameter of 1000mm, are then sent to China, where the paper is embossed then cut and folded to length and width. No shaping is done. The napkins are then subjected to quality control and packaged.
