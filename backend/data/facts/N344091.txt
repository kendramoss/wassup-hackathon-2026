In your letter dated November 17, 2024, on behalf of your client, LT Precision Co., Ltd., you requested a country of origin ruling for marking purposes and a determination on the eligibility of duty free treatment for a cylindrical battery can under the USMCA.The merchandise under consideration is referred to as a 4680 Cylindrical Can Assembly. U.S. Customs and Border Protection reviewed the merchandise in New York ruling N342365, dated September 16, 2024, and classified the assembly under subheading 8507.90.8000, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS). The subject Cylindrical Can Assembly will be produced in Mexico using steel sourced from Korea, and incorporates a rivet and gasket that are also sourced from Korea.
