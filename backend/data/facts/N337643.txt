The sample, Item #N6485, is grey bathmat consisting of an upper surface of polyester fabric of looped pile knitted construction. Polyester chenille yarns form the pile. The knitted fabric is laminated to a layer of foam approximately ½ inch thick and is backed with a non-skid polyvinyl chloride (PVC) coated polyester fabric. The bathmat, measuring 20 × 34 inches, is finished along the edges with a knitted binding. The manufacturing operations for the bathmat are as follows: ChinaChenille yarns are formed.Knitted fabric that forms the upper surface is formed and dyed.Polyester coated with PVC is formed.Knitted binding is formed.VietnamFoam is formed.Foam and PVC coated polyester layers are laminated to knitted fabric.Fabric is cut to size.Binding is sewn along the edges.Finished mat is packaged and exported to the United States.
