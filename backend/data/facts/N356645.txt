In your letter, dated December 2, 2025, you requested a tariff classification and country of origin determination ruling on wooden tongue depressors. Product information and photos were submitted for our review. The products under consideration are flat wooden sticks that are rounded at both ends with a smooth polished surface. The flat wooden sticks are constructed of birch wood and are available in two sizes, 150 mm long by 17 mm wide by 1.6 mm thick or 140 mm long by 16 mm wide by 1.6 mm thick. You indicate that the flat wooden sticks are not coated or chemically treated.
