In your letter dated May 21, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Acer, Inc. The merchandise under consideration is described as four models of automatic data processing (ADP) machines that you refer to as personal computers (PCs). The first PC is identified as the Chromebox Desktop Computer (Chromebox PC), Model Number CXM1, which is described as a 16 cm by 12 cm by 3 cm enclosure containing the motherboard printed circuit board assembly (PCBA) motherboard, system memory, solid state storage (SSD), and a heat sink. The Chromebox PC has multiple USB connection ports, two HDMI ports, and an RJ45 Ethernet port. In your submission, you state that the Chromebox PC’s motherboard PCBA is manufactured in Thailand by a surface mount soldering process of integrated circuits, diodes, capacitors, resistors, etc. onto a bare printed circuit board. Also in Thailand, the processor, memory, and SSD, which are all produced in Taiwan, are added to the motherboard PCBA. After testing, the motherboard PCBAs is then shipped to China where the heat sink, gaskets, a CMOS battery, and a wireless antenna are added and the assembly is inserted into the housing. Following the assembly of the Chromebox PC, it is tested, the operating system (OS) is installed, and the devices are prepared for export to the United States. The second PC under consideration is identified as the Mini PC Desktop Computer (Mini PC), Model Number N1502G-LNL, which is described as a 16 cm by 12 cm by 3 cm enclosure containing the motherboard PCBA, system memory, SSD, and a heat sink. The Mini PC has multiple USB connection ports, two HDMI ports, and an RJ45 Ethernet port. In your submission, you state that the Mini PC’s motherboard PCBA is manufactured in Vietnam by a surface mount soldering process of integrated circuits, diodes, capacitors, resistors, etc. onto a bare printed circuit board. The Tower PC has multiple USB connection ports, two HDMI ports, audio jacks, and an RJ45 Ethernet port.
