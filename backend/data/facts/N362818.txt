In your letter dated July 2, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on Tretinoin Gel, USP 0.01%, in dosage form. Tretinoin Gel, 0.01% is a medicinal preparation containing Tretinoin, a naturally occurring derivative of vitamin A (retinol), as the active ingredient. It is indicated for the topical application in the treatment of acne vulgaris. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Tretinoin, is manufactured in Italy and that the finished gel in dosage form is made in India. In India, Tretinoin will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Tretinoin Gel in dosage form.
