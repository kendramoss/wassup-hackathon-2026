In your letter dated April 24, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Xstar Global Sourcing LLC, for the purpose of duty calculation and Section 301 applicability. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review. The paper products under consideration are packages of "facial tissue." They come in a single, double or triple ply with 1/2 fold. Per your submission, the bulk rolls of facial tissue stock are manufactured in Indonesia. You describe the paper as White/Kraft 100% wood pulp virgin paper; layer:2-3; 12-15 g/sm. These parent rolls of tissue paper, which measure 2800mm in width and have a maximum roll diameter of 2800mm, are then sent to China, where the tissue paper is embossed, cut to length and width, and folded. The facial tissue is then subjected to quality control and packaged.
