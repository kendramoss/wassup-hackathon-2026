In your letter dated July 11, 2025, you requested a country of origin determination for purposes of Section 301 duties and marking under Section 304 of the Tariff Act of 1930, as amended (19 U.S.C. 1304). Pictures, a bill of materials and descriptions of the manufacturing process were included in your submission. The item under consideration is a sidewalk ice scraper (SKU# 4413823). It is a hand tool with a long handle and a flat sharp blade, which is used to remove ice and compacted snow from sidewalks, driveways, and other paved surfaces. You state that the manufacturing process begins in Vietnam, where Chinese-origin hot-rolled steel is laser-cut into the ice scraper blade. Also in Vietnam, 12" long round steel is punched, bent and welded onto the blade in order to form a connection point with the handle socket. The fully formed ice scraper head is then sent to China, where it is polished and powder coated. A handle socket is connected and secured, along with a wooden handle. Finally, middle and end plastic grips are attached to the handle.
