The subject merchandise, Item# ABS202408230033, is a chenille bath mat of tufted construction. The pile surface is made from twisted, uncut loops of polyester chenille yarns that are tufted into a pre-existing base consisting of a nonwoven layer of polyester and a woven mesh layer of polypropylene. The tufts are made in two pile heights, approximately 6 and 13 millimeters (mm), to form a design on the surface. The tufted fabric is laminated to a 15 mm thick layer of polyurethane (PU) foam which is then backed with a 3-dimensional diamond mesh spacer fabric to create a non-skid surface. The edges of the rug are capped with a narrow strip of knit binding fabric. The finished mat measures 20 × 32 inches. The manufacturing operations for the bath mat are as follows: CHINA: Chenille yarns are machine tufted into two primary backing fabrics, a nonwoven and a mesh fabric. Diamond mesh spacer fabric is formed. Knitted binding fabric is formed. 

Fabrics are exported to Vietnam. VIETNAM: Foam is formed. Foam and diamond mesh spacer fabric are laminated to the tufted chenille fabric. Laminated material consisting of chenille tufted fabric, foam and spacer fabric is cut to size. Knitted binding is sewn around the edges. Completed bath mat is packaged and exported to the United States.
