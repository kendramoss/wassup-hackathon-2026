In your letter dated August 18, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on an automotive airbag module, which you filed on behalf of your client, ZF Passive Safety Korea Co., Ltd. The article under consideration is described as an automotive airbag module with illumination, part number A0055U2615J B, which is used in the steering wheel of a passenger vehicle. The airbag module is a critical component of the vehicle’s safety system. It rapidly deploys the airbag upon collision detection to protect occupants from impact. Its design ensures precise timing and reliable inflation to minimize injury. The airbag module consists of an inflator from the United States, airbag cushion and plate from Thailand, cover and light from Korea, hardware from China, and label from the European Union. The final assembly of all components into a complete airbag module occurs in Korea, by inserting components into each other, attaching by screws, height inspection, horn test, and final inspection.
