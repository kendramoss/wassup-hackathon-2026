The subject merchandise consists of an infant’s 2-ply blanket. The front/face side of the blanket is made from 100 percent polyester knit pile fabric. The back side is made from 100 percent polyester jersey knit fabric. Both fabrics are dyed pink. The two layers are joined and a satin binding is sewn along the four edges creating a 1 ½ inch border. The blanket measures 40 × 30 inches. The blanket is designed for protection against the cold. The manufacturing operations for the blanket are as follows: Indonesia Fabric front/face side made of 100 percent polyester pile fabric is knit, dyed and finished. Fabric is shipped in rolls to Vietnam. Vietnam Fabric back side made of 100 percent polyester jersey knit fabric is knit, dyed and finished. Binding fabric made of 100 percent polyester is woven, dyed and finished. Fabrics are cut and sewn into blanket. Finished blanket is exported to the United States.
