In your letter dated November 15, 2024, on behalf of Encube Ethicals Private Limited, you requested a country of origin ruling on Clobetasol Propionate Topical Solution, 0.05%.Clobetasol Propionate Topical Solution, 0.05%, imported in 25 mL and 50 mL bottles, is a synthetic corticosteroid. It is indicated for short-term topical treatment of inflammatory and pruritic manifestations of moderate to severe corticosteroid-responsive dermatoses of the scalp.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Clobetasol Propionate, is made in Italy, and that the finished topical solution in dosage form is made in India. In India, the Clobetasol Propionate will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Clobetasol Propionate Topical Solution in dosage form.
