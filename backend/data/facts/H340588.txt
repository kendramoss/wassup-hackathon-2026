The final product under consideration is pure nickel powder. The nickel powder is manufactured by hydrometallurgy in Australia and does not have a specific application, and it consists of 100% nickel. The nickel powder is then shipped to Canada where it is screened and cut into different sizes which may be sold as is or blended together with other sizes. The powder is packaged in either pails with 25 kg of powder or drums holding 250 kg of powder.According to your request, and confirmed by the NCSD, the subject merchandise is classified under subheading 7504.00.00, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (“HTSUS”), which provides for: “Nickel powders and flakes.”
