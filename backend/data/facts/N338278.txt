In your letter dated February 19, 2024, you requested a country of origin ruling on Zinc Sulfate Injection.Zinc Sulfate Injection USP, imported in 10 mg/10 mL, 30 mg/10 mL, 25 mg/5 mL, is a parenteral nutritional solution for intravenous infusion into a central or peripheral vein. It is indicated for use in adult and pediatric patients as a source of zinc for parenteral nutrition when oral or enteral nutrition is not possible, insufficient, or contraindicated. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Zinc Sulfate, is manufactured in the Czech Republic and that the finished Zinc Sulfate Injection in dosage form is made in India. In India, the Zinc Sulfate will be processed into the final Zinc Sulfate Injection in dosage form.
