In your letter dated July 7, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on a steering knuckle assembly, filed on behalf of Viet Nam Haiya Technology Co., Ltd. The article under consideration has been identified as a steering knuckle assembly, Part Number HYA1448-2 (Customer Part Number LK004), which is used in motor vehicles. It is sometimes commercially referred to as a loaded knuckle. The knuckle is an automotive chassis component linking the front wheel hub, suspension and steering systems. It is installed on the inner side of the front wheels at both ends of the front axle to realize wheel steering and vertical suspension travel. It serves as the connecting point between tie rods, control arms, and braking components. The finished assembly consists of a steering knuckle, wheel bearing, flange, and a standard hardware kit. The steering knuckle blank, is an unfinished, raw piece of metal sourced from China, that is intended for further processing in Vietnam. You state that the knuckle blank lacks the features to execute its intended end-function of becoming a finished knuckle that is subsequently integrated into the steering knuckle assembly. It is then exported to Vietnam where it undergoes significant machining operations, including turning, milling, drilling, grinding, and finishing to produce a functional steering knuckle body. The wheel hub assembly is entirely forged, machined, and assembled in Vietnam through a complex process that involves forging ring materials into upper flange outer ring banks and upper flange inner ring blanks. The product then undergoes computer numerical control (CNC) machining, grinding, drilling assembly and bolt pressing, and spin riveting in order to produce the finished wheel hub component that is ready for integration into the steering knuckle assembly. The hardware kits are prepared in Vietnam and include bolts, nuts, shims, and bushings but sometimes vary depending on the components from car makers. 

Finally, all components are integrated into a finished steering knuckle assembly through pressing the wheel hub into the knuckle body, installing the back plate, performing quality testing, installation of hardware kits, and packaging the finished steering knuckle assemblies for export.
