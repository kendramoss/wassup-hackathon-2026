In your letter dated December 12, 2024, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Liming USA Company, for the purpose of duty calculation and 301 applicability. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review.The items under consideration are packages of paper napkins, including “dinner napkins,” “luncheon napkins,” “beverage napkins,” “off-fold napkins,” “interfold napkins,” tall napkins,” and “low fold napkins.” They come in a variety of plies (single, double, and triple) and folds (1/4, 1/2, 1/6, 1/8).Per your submission, the bulk rolls of tissue paper are manufactured in Indonesia. You describe the paper as White/Kraft paper: virgin; layer: 1/2; with a 12-18 GSM. These parent rolls of paper, which measure in multiple widths from 600 mm up to 2800 mm and have a maximum roll diameter of 1440 mm, with a core diameter of 76 mm, are then sent to China, where the paper is embossed, cut to size, folded, subjected to quality control, and packaged.
