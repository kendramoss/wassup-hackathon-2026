In your letter dated July 26, 2024, you requested a country of origin determination for Beet Concentrate Powder on behalf of your client, Van Drunen Farms (Chicago, IL).You have presented a scenario whereby your client proposes to import beet concentrate powder made from raw beets grown in either the United States or France, since your client has a supplier in each country. The beet concentrate powder is derived from raw beets through a multi-stage process that involves concentrating key nutrients in raw beets while removing sugar, water, fiber, and other water-soluble elements. You state that the first four stages of production take place either entirely in the United States or in France, apart from a process called chromatography, which further concentrates certain nutrients in the beet concentrate and occurs in China.
