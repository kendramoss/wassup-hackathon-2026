This is in response to your letter dated May 5, 2022, requesting a ruling on the country of origin and marking of Organic Hemp Hearts. Organic Hemp Hearts are described as 100% hulled hemp seeds dehulled from whole hemp seeds. Whole hemp seeds (Product of USA) are received, heated, destoned; sorted, dehulled and packaged in Canada. Organic Hemp Hearts will be packaged in pouches for retail sale with a net weight of 189 grams. Country of Origin Marking
