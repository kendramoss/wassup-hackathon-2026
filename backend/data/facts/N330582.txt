This is in response to your letter dated January 19, 2023, requesting a ruling on tariff classification, country of origin, and eligibility under the USMCA of a Potting Mix for Plants. A sample was not submitted, but material safety data sheets were included with your inquiry.The subject merchandise is Molly’s Aroid Potting Mix. The product is composed of coco chips (also known as coconut coir, 59 percent, Canada), Pinus radiata bark (18 percent, New Zealand), expanded clay (Germany), perlite (Canada), pro-mix HP (Canada), worm castings (Canada), coco pith (2 percent, coconut coir, Canada), and horticultural charcoal (Canada). The product, which is intended to support tropical and indoor plants, will be imported in five-quart bags from Canada and sold to consumers on Amazon and through retail garden centers in the United States.Classification
