In your letter dated July 5, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Kaishan Compressor USA. The product at issue is referred to as the KRSD Series air compressor. The compressor is an assembly that consists of various components: an outer housing (a base frame and panels), an air/oil separator tank, a cooling mechanism, an electrical system, piping assemblies, and a motor. The air compressor is used in air conditioning systems and has an output range of 15-200 horsepower. With respect to origin, the air compressor is assembled in Turkey using components primarily from China, motors from Brazil and few a components sourced from Turkey. The final assembly begins by installing an oil return pipe to a top cover, followed by the mounting and adjustment of a cover plate. Next, a safety valve, a pressure gauge, and an oil observation window are installed, and shock absorbers are bolted to a base frame from Turkey. An airend assembly is then bolted to the exterior of the chassis. Subsequently, a motor support bracket, bracket mountings, a coupling, a flange, and a motor adapter are installed onto a Brazilian motor, which is then bolted to a screw block. The process continues with the installation of a transmitter, fittings, pipes, and a regulating valve. The suction valve gasket and valve are secured to the airend using a nut and spring, followed by the installation of a flexible air duct and gasket. Thin pipes from a Turkey-sourced tank are then connected, sealed, and bolted to the suction, control, and discharge valves. Additionally, a drain line hose, nipples, and O-rings are installed onto a cooler, a fan, and a radiator, followed by the installation of hoses onto a thermal valve. Air outlet connection pipes and nipples are connected and covered with a protective cap. A fan top cover is rotated, and fan blades featuring a motor from Brazil are installed. To complete the compressor, outer panels and a door from Turkey are bolted in position, and the electrical connections are finalized. Finally, a top cover and air filter hose are installed, adhesive is applied, and the finished compressor is labeled, packaged, and ready for shipment.
