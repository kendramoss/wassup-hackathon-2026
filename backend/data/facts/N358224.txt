In your letter dated January 26, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Green Planet Paper Co., Ltd, for the purpose of duty calculation and Section 301 applicability. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review. The paper products under consideration are “folded paper towel,” “paper towel roll,” and “kitchen towel.” They come in either single or double ply with 1/2-fold, 1/3-fold, C-fold, or in rolls. Per your submission, the bulk rolls of paper stock are manufactured in Indonesia. You indicate the tissue paper used to produce the paper towel products are white or brown in color, 100% wood pulp produced from virgin fiber; layer:1-2; 20-32 grams per square meter. These parent rolls of paper, which measure in multiple widths from approximately 1160mm up to 2840mm and have a maximum roll diameter of approximately 1500mm, are then sent to China, where the paper is unwound, embossed, then either cut and fold to length and width or rewound and cut for rolls. The paper towels are then subjected to quality control and packaged.
