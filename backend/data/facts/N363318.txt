In your letter dated August 13, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling for purposes of country of origin marking and applying current trade remedies. The merchandise under consideration includes an electric salt grinder and an electric pepper grinder (the “salt and pepper grinders” or “grinders”), which are identical in construction. The difference between the two grinders is limited to their use with either salt or pepper. Thus, the manufacturing process for the grinders in Taiwan is identical. The manufacturing process is summarized as follows: The grinders will be assembled in Taiwan using components sourced from China and components produced in Taiwan. The components sourced from China are the stainless steel upper section housing, motor with its gear set, PCB (Printed Circuit Board), battery, thrust washer, spring, base plate, aluminum shaft, and coarseness adjustment knob. The remaining components—the plastic base, spindle support/centering bracket, retaining clip, adjustment rod, base locking insert, welded retaining ring, and stainless steel threaded insert—are made in Taiwan. The factory in Taiwan fabricates these components through plastic injection molding and metal machining. In the final assembly, the imported components will first be assembled into an upper section (referred to as the “SS upper housing") and then combined with the Taiwan-originating components to make the finished product. The provided information indicates that the Chinese-origin components, which mainly form the upper section and the drive mechanism, contribute approximately 80 percent of the total cost.
