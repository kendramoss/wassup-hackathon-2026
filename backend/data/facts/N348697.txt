In your letter dated May 6, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Dongguan Lopie Paper Co., Ltd, for the purpose of duty calculation and Section 301 applicability. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review. The paper products under consideration are "interfold napkins," "dispenser napkins," "beverage napkins," "luncheon napkins," and "dinner napkins." They come in either single, double or triple ply with off-fold, 1/2, 1/4, 1/6 or 1/8 fold. Per your submission, the bulk rolls of napkin stock are manufactured in Indonesia. You describe the paper as White/Kraft 100% wood pulp virgin paper; layer:1-3; 12-25g/sm. These parent rolls of tissue paper, which measure in multiple widths from 380mm up to 1550mm and have a maximum roll diameter 1550mm, are then sent to China, where the paper is printed, embossed, perforated, cut and folded to length and width and shaped. The napkins are then subjected to quality control and packaged.
