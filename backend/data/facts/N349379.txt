In your letter dated May 12, 2025, you requested a classification and country of origin ruling for worm gear operators. The items under consideration are referred to as S05 worm gear operators. The operators consist of a handwheel joined to a housing that encases a worm gear, a segment, bearings, O-rings, a cover plate, and a declutch cam and lever. Once a worm gear operator is joined to a valve, the user engages the declutch lever to connect the worm gear to the segment. The handwheel is then rotated, which causes the teeth of the worm and the segment to mesh, allowing the torque needed to open and close the valve to be amplified.
