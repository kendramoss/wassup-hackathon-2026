In your letter dated October 17, 2023, you requested a country of origin ruling on Betamethasone Dipropionate Ointment, USP 0.05% in dosage form.Betamethasone Dipropionate Ointment USP, 0.05% is a topical corticosteroid indicated for the relief of the inflammatory and pruritic manifestations of corticosteroid-responsive dermatoses.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Betamethasone Dipropionate, is manufactured in India and that the finished ointment in dosage form is also made in India. In India, the Betamethasone Dipropionate will be mixed with inactive ingredients from various countries to produce the final ointment in dosage form.
