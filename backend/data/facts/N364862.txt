In your letter dated September 4, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on a pressure washer adapter set and turbo nozzle. Descriptive literature was provided for our review. The first item under consideration is described as a pressure washer adapter set (SKU A02-B03K), which is a multi-piece hardware kit designed to provide seamless, leak-free connections between a variety of high-pressure cleaning components. The set consists of a stainless-steel straight swivel adapter, and a brass quick connect, garden hose connect, and garden hose male plug adapter. The universal kit is designed to withstand extreme operating pressures up to 5,000 PSI, making it compatible with both commercial gas and residential electric power washers. The set is primarily utilized to convert threaded connections into fast-snap joints and reduce setup and breakdown times by allowing users to quickly swap out spray guns, extension wands, surface cleaners, and high-pressure hoses. The second item under consideration is described as a pressure washer turbo nozzle (SKU T01-N01-B). This accessory is designed for attachment to high-pressure spray lances to facilitate the cleaning of unpainted hard surfaces. The device utilizes an internal ceramic rotor that, when subjected to water pressure, rotates to convert a concentrated, zero-degree stream into an oscillating, 360-degree cone-shaped spray pattern. It combines the force of a pencil jet with the coverage area of a fan nozzle to remove grime, moss, and rust. The nozzle is composed of brass and stainless-steel components, encased in a rubberized protective sleeve, and features a standard 1/4-inch quick-connect inlet plug. The adapters and nozzles are made from components sourced from China and Malaysia. The Chinese components include the stainless-steel (for the quick connect and quick plug), compression springs, retaining rings, O-rings, locking balls, rubber grip, and plastic filter elements. The Malaysian component is the brass bar stock used for the brass nut, brass nozzle body, brass sealing fitting, brass stem, coupler body, and
locking sleeve. All of the components are then sent to Malaysia where the adapters and nozzles are constructed by CNC machining the stainless-steel or brass depending on the component, precision drilling the parts, deburring and inspecting to form the finished components. Once the metal components are made, they are then assembled with the remaining Chinese components to form the complete adapters and nozzles. The adapters and nozzles are then tested and packaged for retail sale. Item one is then placed into white retail box for shipment to the United States. Item two is placed in a brown retail box.
