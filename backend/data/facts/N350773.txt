In your letter dated June 30, 2025, on behalf of your client, KD Navien, you requested a classification and country of origin ruling for marking purposes. The heat pump, model number GDHP820035DR1, is an outdoor unit. The outdoor unit consists of a chassis that incorporates a compressor, a condenser, a motor and fan assembly, a four-way reversing valve, an expansive valve assembly, a control panel and an accumulator. Once paired with an indoor unit, which is imported separately, the system heats and cools spaces.
