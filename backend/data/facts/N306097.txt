In your letter dated September 4, 2019, you requested a tariff classification and country of origin ruling on behalf of Johnson Matthey Inc. Additional information was provided via email on September 17, 2019. Spray Dried Copper Zeolite Catalysts are a chemical mixture that will be applied to a ceramic substrate and used as a catalytic converter in a vehicle. The process to produce the Spray-Dried Copper Zeolite Catalysts consists of mixing zeolite crystals, water, and metal salt together, pumping the slurry through a spray dryer to form agglomerated dry particles, then sending through a calcining oven to fix the copper onto the zeolite and removing all excess moisture for packaging.
