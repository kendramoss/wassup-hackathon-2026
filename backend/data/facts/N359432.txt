In your letter dated March 4, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on Ivermectin Tablets, USP in dosage form. Ivermectin, imported in 3 mg tablets, is a semisynthetic, anthelmintic agent. It is indicated for the treatment of Strongyloidiasis of the intestinal tract and Onchocerciasis, two conditions caused by parasitic worms. In your letter, you stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Ivermectin is manufactured in China and that the finished tablets in dosage form is made in India. In India, the Ivermectin will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Ivermectin Tablets, USP in dosage form.
