In your letter dated November 30, 2023, on behalf of Dr. Reddy’s Laboratories, Inc., you requested a tariff classification and country of origin determination ruling.Nortriptyline Hydrochloride, imported in dosages of 10 mg, 25 mg, 50 mg and 75 mg capsules, is a tricyclic antidepressant. It is indicated for the treatment of depression.
