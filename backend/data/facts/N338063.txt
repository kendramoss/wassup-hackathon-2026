In your letter dated February 8, 2024, filed on behalf of Dr. Reddy’s Laboratories, Inc., you requested a tariff classification and country of origin determination ruling.Dr. Reddy’s Azurette® Tablets is a medicinal preparation containing a combination of Desogestrel (a progestin) and Ethinyl Estradiol (a steroidal estrogen), as the active ingredients. It is indicated for the prevention of pregnancy. The product is a combination oral contraceptive consisting of a cycle of 28 tablets. Each tablet dispenser contains 21 active dark blue tablets containing Desogestrel (0.15 mg) and Ethinyl Estradiol (0.02 mg), 5 active green tablets containing Ethinyl Estradiol (0.01 mg) and 2 inactive white tablets containing inert ingredients as a reminder tablet to facilitate ease of drug administration via a 28-day regimen. In your letter, you stated the active ingredients are from the Netherlands and or Italy but the finished tablets in dosage form will be made in China.
