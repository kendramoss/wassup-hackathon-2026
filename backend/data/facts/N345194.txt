In your letter dated January 15, 2025, on behalf of your client, Dr. Reddy’s Laboratories, Incorporated, you requested a tariff classification and country of origin determination ruling.Formoterol Soln Neb, imported in 20 mcg /2 mL vials, is a medicinal preparation containing Formoterol Fumarate Dihydrate, a long-acting beta agonists (LABAs), as the active ingredient. It is indicated for the treatment of bronchoconstriction in patients with Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD).
