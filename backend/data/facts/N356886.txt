The subject merchandise, item number SWLH26C02, described as the “Merry Christmas Pillow” is a 12 × 20 inch decorative pillow. The front of the pillow is comprised of 100 percent polyester woven slub fabric, that is embroidered with the words “Merry Christmas” and holly leaves and berries. The back of the pillow is comprised of 100 percent polyester velvet that is printed with an all over design of pine needles, holly leaves and holly berries. The pillow is stuffed with polyester staple fiber. The pillow features knife edges and a red pompon at each corner. The manufacturing operations for the pillow are as follows: CHINA: Slub face fabric is woven. Pompons are formed. Slub face fabric and pompons are exported to Vietnam. VIETNAM: Velvet back fabric is woven and printed. Polyester staple fiber and thread are manufactured. 

Slub face fabric is embroidered. Fabric is cut and assembled by sewing the panels to form a pillow shell. Pompons are attached. Pillow shell is stuffed with polyester staple fiber and sewn closed. Finished pillow is packaged and exported to the United States.
