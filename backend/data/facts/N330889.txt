In your letter dated February 10, 2023, you requested a tariff classification and country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, LG Sourcing Inc. Photographs and product descriptions were submitted for our review. The subject article, which you referred to as the “Kobalt 15” Tool Bag,” item 2416932 is a tool bag. It is constructed with an outer surface of 100 percent polyester, which is a man-made textile material. The bag is designed to provide protection, portability, organization, and storage to various hand tools and related accessories. The article features a zippered closure around three sides, an adjustable shoulder strap, and two handles. The exterior features three front open pockets.You have indicated that the locations of the manufacturing operations are as follows:China:The polyester fabric and all materials are produced.Cambodia:All cutting, sewing, and assembly are performed.CLASSIFICATIONTool bags are provided for eo nomine in heading 4202, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS), which provides, in relevant part, for, “Trunks, suitcases, vanity cases, attaché cases, briefcases, school satchels, …. tool bags, sports bags, bottle cases, jewelry boxes, powder cases, cutlery cases and similar containers, …. of textile materials.”
