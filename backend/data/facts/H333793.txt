The subject merchandise is “strawberry mochi,” which is round in shape and strawberry pink in color. Then they are weighed, steamed at 112 degrees Celsius, mixed, and stirred. Afterwards, the various additives, i.e., trehalose, gelatin, strawberry flavor, sorbitol (E420) (sweetener), FD&C Red No. 40 (E129), monoglycerides and diglycerides (E471) (emulsifier), and potassium sorbate (E202) (preservative) are prepared and added to the mixture. The mixture is shaped into round balls and becomes the final product. One individual bag contains 8 strawberry mochis and has a net weight of 216 g. Each outer box has 12 individual bags and a total gross weight of 3.2 kg.The strawberry mochi is exported from Taiwan in an ambient state and sold mainly in bulk to Asian supermarkets and restaurants. According to the requestor, the strawberry mochi is intended to be consumed as a snack or sweet and is sold in the dessert section of stores alongside other confectioneries.
