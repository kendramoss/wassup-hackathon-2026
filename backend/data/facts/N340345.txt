In your letter dated May 24, 2024, you requested a country of origin ruling on an angiographic syringe kit. Additional information was provided via email, dated June 10, 2024.The imported product is a sterile angiographic syringe kit housed in a high-impact polystyrene tray, which consists of one or two angiographic syringes, tubing with two Luer connectors and two dust caps (tubing assembly), a filling spike (spike body and spike sheath) with a dust cap and a vent cover cap (spike assembly), and/or a filling straw. There are two types of syringes. Type 1 is composed of a protective cap, a helical Luer nut, a blue umbrella, a plunger seal, a lower cover, a piston cover, and a syringe barrel. Type 2 has similar components but without the blue umbrella and the lower cover. The protective cap, helical Luer nut, blue umbrella, plunger seal, lower cover, piston cover, syringe barrel, tubing with two Luer connectors, spike body, spike sheath, dust caps, vent cover cap, and filling straw are made in China. The packaging materials (tray, Tyvek pouch, Tyvek label, master carton, and inner carton) are made in Costa Rica.In Costa Rica the syringe components are attached together to form syringes using jigs, fixtures, pneumatic presses, and assembly tables. During the process, the proper attachments are verified; and the individual components of the assembled syringe are inspected. The dust caps are threaded onto tubing by hand to become tubing assemblies. The spike sheaths, spike bodies, the vent cover caps, and the duct caps are connected together by hand to become spike assemblies. The components of the kits are put into a blister tray and sealed with a Tyvek label or Tyvek pouch with PE film printed with manufacturing information. The assembled syringe kits are then packed into corrugated fiberboard cartons which may or may not contain an inner carton in a master carton. The corrugated fiberboard cartons' artwork is printed on at a previous point in the process. Then the master carton is wrapped in PE film and sterilized. The finished goods are then inspected for compliance with specifications.
