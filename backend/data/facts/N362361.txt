In your letter dated June 16, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on electric marine propulsion systems. There are two items under consideration described as electric marine propulsion systems, identified by model numbers KAHE POD 600 and KAHE POD 160. KAHE POD 600 is designed to be attached to light watercrafts such as small boats, kayaks, float tubes and paddleboards to provide propulsion. It can also be converted as an underwater scooter for snorkeling. This model is comprised of a main propulsion unit with a propeller, remote control, mounting kits for various watercrafts, and an underwater scooter conversion kit. KAHE POD 160 is a smaller unit designed to be attached to paddleboards and kayaks. It is comprised of a main propulsion unit with a propeller, remote control, and mounting kits. In your submission, you state that entire assembly process for both models takes place in France. The main propulsion units are assembled in France using electric motors, battery, propeller, outer housing/enclosure of Chinese origin. All sub-assemblies are integrated using connectors, wires, and screws along with basic press-fitting. Once fully assembled and tested, the main propulsion unit is packaged with a remote control, charger, and mounting/conversion kits of Chinese origin for shipment to the United States.
