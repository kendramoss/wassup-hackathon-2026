In your letter dated July 10, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Babcock Power Services Inc. The merchandise under consideration is referred to as a boiler wall panel for a power generating boiler used in a fossil fuel utility. The panels are composed of carbon or alloy steel tubes and are used as replacement or repair components for existing boiler walls. The boiler wall panel is assembled using sub panel assemblies manufactured in China, where ten carbon or alloy steel tubes (manufactured to ASTM A210 specification) are joined with membrane bars. An automated panel welding machine that uses either a metal inert gas welding process or a submerged arc welding process welds the membrane bars and tubes to form a single uniform wall panel. The ten-tube sub panel assemblies are sent to Vietnam where two panels are welded together to create a twenty-tube assembly. Next, an overlay is applied to one face of the panel. If required, some panels are bent at one or more places to match the design of the boiler wall. Lastly, the panels are tested and inspected through both visual inspections and verifying the accuracy of the final dimensions.
