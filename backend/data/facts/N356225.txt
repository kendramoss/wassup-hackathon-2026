In your letter dated November 19, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on Butalbital, Acetaminophen and Caffeine Capsules. Butalbital, Acetaminophen, and Caffeine, imported in capsule form, is a medicinal preparation containing 50 mg of Butalbital (a barbiturate), 300 mg of Acetaminophen (an analgesic) and 40 mg of Caffeine (a stimulant), as the active ingredients. It is indicated for the relief of symptoms associated with tension or muscle contraction headaches. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs), Butalbital, Acetaminophen and Caffeine, are manufactured in Belgium, India and China, respectively. The finished capsules in dosage form are manufactured in the United States. In the United States, the Butalbital, Acetaminophen and Caffeine APIs will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final capsules in dosage form.
