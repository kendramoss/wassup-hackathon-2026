In your letter dated February 13, 2023, you requested a tariff classification and country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, LG Sourcing Inc. Photographs and product descriptions were submitted for our review. The article at issue, which you referred to as the “Kobalt 18-in Cargo Bag,” item number 2416942, is a tool bag.In your request, you stated that the article is constructed of 100 percent polyester, which is a man-made textile material. You also stated that the outer surface is uncoated. The tool bag is designed to provide protection, portability, organization, and storage to various hand tools and related accessories. It features a zippered top closure and two handles. The front exterior has two open pockets. The rear exterior has three open pockets. The bag measures 18.11” (W) x 13 (H) x 8.66” (D). You have indicated that the locations of the manufacturing operations are as follows:China:The polyester fabric and all materials are produced.Cambodia:All cutting, sewing, and assembly are performed.CLASSIFICATIONTool bags are provided for eo nomine in heading 4202, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS), which provides, in relevant part, for, “Trunks, suitcases, vanity cases, attaché cases, briefcases, school satchels, …. tool bags, sports bags, bottle cases, jewelry boxes, powder cases, cutlery cases and similar containers, …. of textile materials.”
