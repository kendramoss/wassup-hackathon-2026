In your letter dated August 29, 2023, you requested a country of origin ruling. The product to be imported is described as stabilized ferrosilicon powder, which will be used as a flux powder for welding rod.In accordance with Section XV, Note 8(b) and Chapter 72, Note 1(c), the applicable subheading for the ferrosilicon powder will be 7202.29.0050, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS), which provides for Ferroalloys: Ferrosilicon: Other, Other. The rate of duty will be free.You describe a scenario wherein lump ferrosilicon is purchased from China, measuring 10-50 mm. The product is sent to Japan for further processing. In Japan, a milling and crushing operation breaks the ferrosilicon down into a powder. The ferrosilicon also undergoes a surface stabilization treatment before it is packaged for export to the United States.
