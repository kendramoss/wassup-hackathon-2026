In your letter dated May 12, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on a plastic cellphone case. The product under consideration is described as a mobile phone case, model number ACS08956. It is designed to cover and safeguard a cellphone from physical damage such as scratches, drops, and impacts. This case is made from a combination of polycarbonate (“PC”) and thermoplastic polyurethane (“TPU”) plastic. The case covers the back, sides, and corners of the phone, but not the screen, and has cutouts on the back for the phone’s camera. Per your submission, the PC plate and TPU resin are sourced from China and the protective film and paper packaging which will be produced in Thailand. In China, the PC plate will undergo machine processing, a lamination process, and have a magnetic ring attached. In Thailand, the processed PC plate will be added to a mold and the TPU plastic will be injected and formed around the plate to create the case. Afterward, the protective film will be laminated to the case and further finishing procedures will be completed.
