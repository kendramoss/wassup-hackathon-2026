In your letter dated May 7, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on Potassium Chloride Extended-Release Capsules. Potassium Chloride, imported in 8 mEq and 10 mEq extended-release capsules, is a medicinal preparation containing Potassium Chloride, as the active ingredient. It is indicated for the treatment and prophylaxis of hypokalemia with or without metabolic alkalosis. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Potassium Chloride is manufactured in the United States and that the finished capsules in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Potassium Chloride will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final capsules in dosage form.
