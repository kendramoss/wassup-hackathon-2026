In your letter dated September 4, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on pressure washer nozzles and a sewer jetter kit. Descriptive literature was provided for our review. The first item under consideration is described as a 3-piece sewer jetter nozzle set (SKU T01-N02K). The item is packaged together as a set for retail sale and is designed exclusively for mechanical attachment to high-pressure hydro-jetting equipment or pressure washer hoses to clean, clear, and flush commercial and residential drainpipes. The nozzles are made of corrosion-resistant stainless-steel and contain three distinct spray nozzles designed to direct high-pressure fluid streams. The first is a rotating nozzle featuring an internal spinning mechanism driven by water flow to provide 360-degree scouring action against grease and roots. The second is a button nose nozzle with rear-facing thrust orifices designed to propel the hose forward while flushing loose debris. The third is a pointed jetter nozzle (also referred to as a penetrating or chisel nozzle) configured with a sharp profile and a forward-facing jet to punch through tough blockages and obstructions. The second item under consideration is described as a sewer jetter kit (SKU W02-P02K). The retail-packaged assortment is designed to convert a standard mechanical gas or electric pressure washer into a high-pressure pipe and drain cleaning system. The kit contains a high-pressure thermoplastic hose measuring 1/4-inch in internal diameter, reinforced with dual-layer high-tenacity terylene textile braiding and jacketed in weather and abrasion-resistant polyvinyl chloride (PVC). The hose is terminated at both ends with 1/4-inch Male National Pipe Thread (M-NPT) brass or stainless-steel fittings and possesses a rated maximum working pressure of 5,800 PSI and a burst pressure of 11,000 PSI. Imported alongside and packaged with the hose are several interchangeable hydro-jetter nozzles, including a stainless-steel button-nose nozzle and a brass rotating nozzle. The assortment further incorporates an M22
female-to-1/4-inch NPT female coupler, a 1/4-inch quick-disconnect plug for attachment to a pressure washer spray gun, a roll of polytetrafluoroethylene (PTFE) thread seal tape, two specialized open-ended spanners for fitting installation, and a set of cleaning pins for clearing nozzle orifice obstructions. The components are put up together in a single package for retail sale and function in an interdependent manner to project, disperse, and spray high-velocity water under pressure for the maintenance and cleaning of conduits. The nozzles, hose and adapters are made from components sourced from China and Malaysia. The Chinese components include the stainless-steel (for the nozzle washer, nozzle body, button nose nozzle, pointed nozzle, and fittings), screws, washers, O-rings, PVC plastic pellets for the hose, crimp sleeve, crimp sleeve, springs, retaining ring, and locking balls. The Malaysian components include the brass bar stock used for the nozzle rotating sleeve, female thread bushing, connector, coupler body, and locking sleeve. The plastic pellets are also imported from Malaysia to make the anti-slip sleeve. All of the components are then sent to Malaysia where the metal components are constructed by CNC machining the stainless-steel or brass depending on the component, precision drilling the parts, deburring and inspecting. Once the metal components are made, they are then assembled with the remaining Chinese components to form the complete nozzles and fittings. The hose is made by melting the white PVC plastic pellets and extruding the inner tube. Next, the reinforcing cord is wound around the outer surface of the inner tube to provide support and reinforcement. Then, the black PVC pellets are then extruded over the reinforcing cord to form the outer layer of the hose. The metal pieces are then added to the end of the hose using a crimping machine. All of the nozzles from item one are then tested and packaged in a white retail box. All of the items from item two are then tested and packaged in a brown retail box.
