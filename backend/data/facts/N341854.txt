In your letter dated August 6, 2024, you requested a country of origin ruling on Tretinoin Cream, USP 0.1%.Tretinoin Cream, USP 0.1% is a medicinal preparation containing Tretinoin, a naturally occurring derivative of vitamin A (retinol), as the active ingredient. It is indicated for the topical application in the treatment of acne vulgaris.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Tretinoin, is manufactured in Germany and that the finished Tretinoin Cream in dosage form is made in India. In India, the Tretinoin will be mixed with inactive ingredients to produce the final Tretinoin Cream in dosage form.
