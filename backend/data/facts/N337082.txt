In your letter dated December 14, 2023, you requested a country of origin ruling on Naloxone Hydrochloride Injection.Naloxone Hydrochloride Injection, imported in 0.4 mg/mL, is a synthetic morphinane alkaloid belonging to a class of drugs known as opioid antagonists. It is indicated for the complete or partial reversal of opioid depression, including respiratory depression, induced by natural and synthetic opioids, including propoxyphene, methadone and certain mixed agonist-antagonist analgesics: nalbuphine, pentazocine, butorphanol, and cyclazocine. Naloxone Hydrochloride Injection is also indicated for diagnosis of suspected or known acute opioid overdosage.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Naloxone Hydrochloride, is manufactured in France and that the finished Naloxone Hydrochloride Injection in dosage form is made in India. In India, Naloxone Hydrochloride will be mixed with inactive ingredients to produce the final Naloxone Hydrochloride Injection in dosage form.
