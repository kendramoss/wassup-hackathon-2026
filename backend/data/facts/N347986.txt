In your letter dated April 17, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Hangzhou Fuyang Liming Industrial Co., Ltd, for the purpose of duty calculation and Section 301 applicability. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review. The paper products under consideration are "hand towels." They come in either single or double ply with no fold. Per your submission, the bulk rolls of hand towel stock are manufactured in Indonesia. You describe the paper as White/Kraft 100% wood pulp virgin paper; layer:1; 32 g/sm. These parent rolls of tissue paper, which measure 2850mm in width and have a maximum roll diameter 2570mm, are then sent to China, where the tissue paper is unwound, embossed, cut to size and rewound. The hand towels are then subjected to quality control and packaged.
