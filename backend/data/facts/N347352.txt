In your letter dated April 2, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on Tacrolimus Ointment 0.1%. Tacrolimus Ointment 0.1%, imported in 30 g, 60 g and 100 g, is a macrolide immunosuppressant produced by Streptomyces tsukubaensis. It is indicated as second-line therapy for the short-term and non-continuous chronic treatment of moderate to severe atopic dermatitis in non-immunocompromised adults who have failed to respond adequately to other topical prescription treatments for atopic dermatitis, or when those treatments are not advisable. You indicated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Tacrolimus, is manufactured in India and that the finished Tacrolimus Ointment in dosage form is also made in India. In India, the Tacrolimus will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Tacrolimus Ointment 0.1 % in dosage form.
