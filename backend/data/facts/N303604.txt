In your letter dated March 22, 2019 you requested a tariff classification ruling.The merchandise under consideration is identified as the Electrical Junction Boxes, PN RTB-005-04 which consists of electrical components mounted within a NEMA compact enclosure that is used in electrical distribution applications. The subject junction box is described as a populated enclosure containing 56 terminal blocks, a bus bar, two cross connectors, and various mounting hardware. You state the enclosure, bus bar, and cross connectors are from Germany, the terminal blocks are from Romania, and the various remaining parts and mounting hardware are of numerous foreign origins.
