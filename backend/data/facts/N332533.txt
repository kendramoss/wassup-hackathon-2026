In your letter dated April 27th, 2023, you requested a country of origin ruling and marking determination for a duo eyeliner on behalf of Elegant Best Investment Limited (“Elegant Best”).The merchandise under consideration is a duo eyeliner. You state that the raw materials for the bulk material originate in multiple countries. You have included raw material lists for the product showing that no single country produced the majority of the raw material for the bulk product. The process that is the subject of this ruling is the container hot filling, and packaging process. Processing in South Korea is described as follows. The raw materials are assembled, weighed and stored in South Korea, then bulk mixed, and prepared, before being put into bulk storage in South Korea. The mixed and prepared product in bulk storage, is shipped from South Korea to China. No additional ingredients are added to the (bulk) product in China. In China, the bulk product is placed in hot filling machinery, retail containers are filled with the product, labeled, placed in individual cartons then assembled in retail boxes. All packaging is of Chinese origin. An image of the product was sent with your request. For purposes of this ruling, the packaging is assumed to be disposed of after the cosmetic is used up.Images that you supplied of the container shows a cosmetic in pencil form. You state that the container is made of plastic/aluminum and plastic. The duo eyeliner appears to be in retail packaging.
