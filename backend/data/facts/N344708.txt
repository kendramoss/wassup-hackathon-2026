This is in response to your letter dated December 17, 2024, on behalf of your client Elemental LED, requesting a country of origin determination on the LED tape light for purposes of the applicability of Section 301 duties.The merchandise at issue is a 5-meter-long strip of LED lights wound onto a spool and packaged in a static shielding bag. Depending on the application, the 5-meter strip may be cut into shorter lengths at designated locations called solder pads, designated by the scissor icon and vertical dashed line in the center marked in the tape. Alternatively, several 5-meter strips may be connected to one another to create longer lengths.You present a manufacturing process scenario, in which the factory in Thailand uses the materials and components of Chinese or Taiwanese-origin to manufacture the LED tape light. In particular, the LED package is from Taiwan, which consists of the unmounted LED die, plastic frame with copper solder pads and gold wires. The LED package manufacturing process was described in ruling N343880, dated December 5, 2024, and the country of origin was determined to be Taiwan therein.Major processing operations of the LED lights in Thailand are summarized as follows:Flexible Printed Circuit Board (FPCB) CodingSolder Paste PrintingComponent Placement via Surface Mount Technology (SMT) and LED Diode MountingReflow SolderingQuality Control InspectionLED FPCB Sheet SolderingCleaningQuality Control and Age TestingBlowing Heat Shrink TubingPCB Split and ReelingSurface InspectionTape application
