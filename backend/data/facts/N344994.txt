In your letter dated January 8, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on candy canes. A manufacturing flowchart, list of product ingredients, spec. sheets and product samples accompanied your request. The product samples were disposed of.The subject merchandise is described as candy canes composed of white granulated sugar (product of Thailand), corn syrup (product of China), water, natural and artificial flavoring (product of China), titanium dioxide, and FD&C No. 40 (both are products of India).You have stated that all manufacturing is conducted in Cambodia. White granulated sugar, corn syrup and water are boiled, air is pumped out and the pot is cooled. Natural and artificial flavoring, titanium dioxide and FD&C Red No. 40 are added and blended evenly. The preparation is cooled and mixed (different colored sugar masses together) and the sugar stands are pulled into candy cane shapes. The candy canes will be packed into paper boxes, 12 candy canes per box.
