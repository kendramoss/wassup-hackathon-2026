In your letter dated August 11, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on razor clams. The subject merchandise is Canned Razor Clams in Olive Oil. The merchandise consists of live clams harvested in Ireland. The clams are shipped live to Spain where the clams are cooked in their shells, after which the meat is manually extracted, placed into cans, and covered in olive oil of Spanish origin. The cans are then hermetically sealed, marked with batch numbers, and subjected to thermal sterilization before exportation to the United States. You seek a determination as to the country of origin of the above-described product.
