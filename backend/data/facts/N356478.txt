In your letter dated November 26, 2025, you requested a ruling on behalf of your client, Gemasia USA Corp. You submitted photographs, product description, and manufacturing information on a pair of gold earrings. Style ER-019810 is a pair of gold earrings set with diamonds. Manufacturing Steps: Step 1: Product design. This is performed in the United States. Step 2: Creating the wax model. This is performed in India. The wax models are sent to the U.S. casting manufacturer for production of the finished castings. Step 3: Alloying fine gold with base metals. This is performed in the United States. Step 4: Spruing and investing. This is performed in the United States. Step 5: Wax Burnout (lost wax process). This is performed in the United States Step 6: Cooling and mold Break-out. This is performed in the United States. Step 7: Sprue cutting. This is performed in the United States. Step 8: Pre-finishing and Tumbling. This is performed in the United States. Step 9: Attaching findings including posts and nuts. This is performed in India. Step 10: Setting diamonds and final polishing. This is performed in India. Step 11: Logo engraving, and packaging. This is performed in India. The finished earring is then exported to the United States.
