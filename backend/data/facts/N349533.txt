In your letter dated May 28, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Dongguan Lopie Paper Co., Ltd, for the purpose of duty calculation and Section 301 applicability. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review. The paper products under consideration are "dispenser napkins" They come in either single or double ply with 1/4, 1/6 or 1/8 fold. Per your submission, the bulk rolls of napkin stock are manufactured in Vietnam. You describe the paper as White/Kraft 100% wood pulp virgin paper; layer:1-2; 13.5-22g/sm. These parent rolls of tissue paper, which measure in multiple widths from 380mm up to 1495mm and have a maximum roll diameter 1595mm, are then sent to China, where the paper is embossed, cut and folded to length and width. No shaping is done. The napkins are then subjected to quality control and packaged.
