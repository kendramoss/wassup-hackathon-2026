In your letter dated July 10, 2025, you requested a country of origin determination for purposes of Section 301 duties and marking under Section 304 of the Tariff Act of 1930, as amended (19 U.S.C. 1304). Pictures, a bill of materials and descriptions of the manufacturing process were included in your submission. The item under consideration is a garden hoe (SKU# 4413302). It is a hand tool with a long handle and a flat, sharp steel blade that is secured at a right angle to the handle. It is primarily used for weeding, cultivating soil, and creating planting rows in gardens. You state that the manufacturing process begins in Vietnam, where Chinese-origin hot-rolled steel is laser-cut into the hoe blade. Also in Vietnam, 12" long round steel is pressed and bent into a right angle and welded onto the hoe blade. The fully formed hoe head is then sent to China, where it is polished and powder coated. A handle socket is connected and secured, along with a wooden handle. Finally, middle and end plastic grips are attached to the handle.
