In your letter dated May 2, 2025, you requested a classification and country of origin ruling on unfinished shafts. The unfinished shaft is used in turbochargers, which are referred to as compressors. The aluminum alloy shaft, which is powered by a linkage assembly, transfers torque and establishes the position between the actuator and the adjustment ring, which adjusts the speed of the turbine wheel, and subsequently the compressor wheel.
