In your letter dated March 31, 2023, you requested a country of origin ruling.The merchandise under consideration is identified as the Type C to HDMI Adapter, Model Number CD-130A1 (Adapter), which is described as signal converting adapter having one male USB-C plug on one end, a short length of cable, and a housing having an HDMI female socket connector on the other end. The subject adapter is intended to be used for personal electronics and is comprised of a plastic molded housing containing a printed circuit board assembly (PCBA), wires, and connectors. You state the adapter is manufactured in the Philippines from components sourced from China and the Philippines. Based on the information supplied, the PCBA is assembled in the Philippines from a bare printed circuit board, integrated circuits, resistors, and capacitors, by a surface mount soldering process to produce the PCBA. The PCBA, which functions to convert the video signals received from a personal computer’s USB-C socket to an HDMI format, is then assembled into the plastic housing after the wires are soldered onto PCBA. Also in the Philippines, the USB-C connector is soldered to wires on the opposite end of the cable. The adapter is inspected and tested prior to ultrasonically welding the enclosure and final packaging.
