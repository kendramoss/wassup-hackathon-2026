In your letter dated July 24, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on four models of gaming laptops. The merchandise under consideration is identified as four models of automatic data processing (ADP) machines that are referred to as Razer Blade 14 Gaming Laptop personal computers (PCs), models RZ09-05305ES3-R3U1, RZ09-05306ES3-R3U1, RZ09-05306EM3-R3U1, and RZ09-05306ES4-R3U1, which are all laptops equipped with Windows 11 Home NextGen Premium operating system and Razer’s proprietary software called Razer Synapse. All models have two USB Type-A ports, two USB Type-C ports, one MicroSD card reader (UHS-II), and one HDMI 2.1 output. Moreover, each model in question has a similar appearance, but has variations in the processor speed, memory size, GPU type, color, and storage capacity, based on the model configurations. In your submission, you state that the manufacturing process for all four models of the Razer Blade 14 gaming laptops is the same. The motherboard Printed Circuit Board Assemblies (PCBAs) are assembled in Vietnam using components sourced from various countries of origin. The production of the PCBA starts with a blank printed circuit board (PCB) and advances through many steps, such as surface mount technology (SMT), laser etching, solder paste application, pick and placement of the components through a reflow oven, and auto-inspection, and testing of the parts. Once the motherboard PCBA is completed and tested, it is shipped to China where the Razer Blade 14 gaming laptops are assembled. Each assembly process consists of inserting the completed motherboard PCBA onto the bottom cover and attaching the webcam, OLED panel, wireless antenna, keyboard, fan, lithium battery, etc., into the housing. Lastly, these laptop PCs are inspected, tested, labeled, and packaged for export into the United States.
