The item under consideration is a packaged set of five polypropylene insertable plastic dividers with pockets and tabs (SKU# 7278216176000MX), each measuring approximately 9 ¼ ” by 11 1/8”. The surface of the backer material and tab is write and erase. The dividers can be inserted into a notebook or binder by the ultimate consumer.The dividers will first undergo manufacturing in China and then be further processed in Mexico.In China, plastic sheets are cut to specific dimensions to form the front pocket piece, including a diagonal cut delineating the top of the pocket. They are scored along the bottom and sides to form an overlap where the edges will be sealed. A separate backer piece, the “divider body” is cut to the finished divider dimensions, including a tab. The unfinished dividers are then sent to Mexico.In Mexico, the unfinished dividers are finished via the following steps: The plastic pocket piece is placed on top of the divider body in the heat sealing machine. The three scored edges of the pocket piece are folded to overlap the divider body and heat sealed together. Three holes are punched along the left hand side. The dividers are collated into sets of five multi-colored tabbed dividers and packaged as such with a literature sheet in a clear polypropylene bag. The paper elements and bag are sourced in Mexico. Finally, the sets are packed into a shipper case for export to the United States.
