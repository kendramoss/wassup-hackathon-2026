In your letter dated February 2, 2024, on behalf of Dr. Reddy’s Laboratories, Inc., you requested a tariff classification and country of origin determination ruling.Zovia® 1/35 (Ethynodiol Diacetate and Ethinyl Estradiol Tablets, USP) is a medicinal preparation containing a combination of Ethynodiol Diacetate (a progestin) and Ethinyl Estradiol (a steroidal estrogen), as the active ingredients. It is indicated for the prevention of pregnancy. The product is a combination oral contraceptive consisting of a cycle of 28 tablets. Each tablet dispenser contains 21 active pale pink tablets containing Ethynodiol Diacetate (1 mg) and Ethinyl Estradiol (0.035 mg) and 7 inactive white tablets containing inert ingredients as a reminder tablet to facilitate ease of drug administration via a 28-day regimen.
