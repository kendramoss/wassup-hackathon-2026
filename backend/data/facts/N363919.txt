In your letter dated July 31, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on wheel hub units, which you filed on behalf of Motorcar Parts of America, Inc. The articles under consideration are wheel hub units incorporating either ball bearings or tapered roller bearings. Parts WH515096, DLG515096, and A515096 incorporate splined inner flanges. The spline feature allows the hub to mesh with the drive axle and transmit torque. Part WH515054 is a non-splined unit, where the bearing/hub assemblies do not contain internal splined teeth that engage a drive axle to transmit torque. Each wheel hub unit mainly consists of an inner flange, outer flange, inner rings, cage, tapered rollers (or balls), anti-lock braking system (ABS) sensor, screws, tooth ring, shim, seals, and bolts of Chinese origin. The inner rings, outer flange, and inner flange are sourced from China in a raw, unfinished form and sent to Malaysia for further processing. The remaining components are not subject to any further processes in Malaysia and will be incorporated into the wheel hub units in the condition as imported. In Malaysia, the inner rings undergo grinding, superfine grinding, and auto-matching. The flanges undergo computer numerical control (CNC) lathe machining, bolt installation, grease application, seal installation, bearing set pressing, roll form (riveting), crack testing, examination, cap pressing, retainer installation, ABS sensor installation, internal End of Line (EOL) testing, voltage testing, spline broaching (for Parts WH515056, DLG515096, and A515096), anti-rust application, laser etching, and durability testing. Finally, the products are packaged and labeled ready for shipment.
