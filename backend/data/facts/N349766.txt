In your letter dated June 3, 2025, you requested a tariff classification and country of origin determination. In lieu of a sample, photographs of the pet bed were provided with your request. Item #1943569, described as a “Pumpkin Donut Bed,” is a pet bed intended to be used as a comfortable place for a pet to sleep. The pet bed consists of a pet bed cover and two removable cushion inserts. The pet bed cover is composed of two types of textile fabrics. A dyed 100 percent polyester knitted faux rabbit fur fabric comprises the sides and top of the bed and a dyed 100 percent polyester woven oxford fabric coated with dioctyl terephthalates (DOTP) resin comprises the bed bottom. The DOTP coating applied to the bottom fabric prevents the pet bed from skidding. The bolster and bottom insert are composed of an outer shell of 100 percent polypropylene nonwoven fabric stuffed with 70 percent polyurethane shredded foam and 30 percent polyester fiberfill. The cushions are inserted into a zippered opening along the base of the sides and at the bottom center of the cover to form the completed bed. The completed circular-shaped pet bed measures 36 inches in diameter and 9 ½ inches in height. The pet bed cover is machine washable. You state the manufacturing operations for the pet bed are as follows: China: • Top and Sides: 100 percent polyester faux rabbit fur fabric is knitted, dyed, and shipped in rolls to Vietnam or Cambodia. • Bottom: 100 percent polyester oxford fabric is woven, dyed, coated with DOTP resin and shipped in rolls to Vietnam or Cambodia. • Cushion Inserts: 100 percent polypropylene fabric is produced, dyed and shipped in rolls to Vietnam or Cambodia. • Zippers and rubber cord is produced and shipped to Vietnam or Cambodia. 

Vietnam: • 100 percent polyester thread is produced and shipped in spools to Vietnam manufacturer or Cambodia. • 100 percent polyester fiberfill is produced and shipped in bales to Vietnam manufacturer or Cambodia. • 100 percent polyurethane is produced and shipped in bales to Vietnam manufacturer or Cambodia. Vietnam/Cambodia: • Fabric is cut and sewn. • Cushion inserts are stuffed, inserted into pillow cover, and zippered closed. • The finished pet bed is packaged and shipped to the United States.
