In your letter dated February 13, 2025, on behalf of JDI Display America Inc., you requested a country of origin ruling.The merchandise under consideration is a thin-film transistor (TFT) liquid crystal display (LCD) module, part number LPM096G522A. This display is designed for integration into automotive systems for the rearview mirror. Its primary operational functions encompass the following: (1) signal reception, inclusive of Low Voltage Differential Signaling (LVDS); (2) signal distribution to individual pixels; and (3) image displaying. Based on the information provided, the subject TFT-LCD module does not contain any video signal conversion capabilities.In Japan, the following steps occur. First, the process starts with a glass substrate, which entails multiple steps in the chemical vapor deposition machines to build a TFT array structure on the substrate. The next step consists of the color filter built-up. Then, the LCD panel is filled using a one-drop fill (ODF) process, applied to the entire TFT array substrate, in which the liquid crystal material is compressed between the TFT array sides, and the color filter glass substrate is added and aligned on top. This process creates a sheet of TFT-LCD cells that will later be cut into individual cells.In China, the substrate is cut into “chips” (individual LCD panels) and the module is assembled. The assembly consists of taking the LCD cell (the "chip"), and affixing driver integrated circuits (ICs), adding the polarizers, the flexible printed circuit(s) (FPC(s)), a backlight, and a frame. Lastly, it passes through outgoing quality testing.
