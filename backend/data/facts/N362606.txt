In your letter dated June 26, 2026, you requested a country of origin determination for purposes of Section 301 and other duties. Pictures of the item in different stages of manufacture were included with your submission, along with a narrative of the manufacturing processes. The merchandise is identified as an 18" Indexing Flat Pry Bar. It is a hand tool constructed from carbon steel that is used for prying, lifting and separating materials, and removing nails. It is constructed with a rotatable claw-shaped head that can swivel 180 degrees and lock at 16 positions by pressing an indexing gear located on the side of the claw. The tool also incorporates a raised striking surface and narrow jab end to provide strong leverage for prying materials and pulling nails. The tool has a length of 18 inches and weighs 2.20 pounds. You propose two manufacturing scenarios. The first scenario begins in Vietnam, where carbon steel is forged into a flat rough shape of the claw head of the tool. This article is then sent to China, where it is further forged and bent into its final claw-shaped angle head of the pry bar. A V-shaped notch is machined into the claw for the nail puller slot and holes are drilled into it for assembly with the handle and an indexing gear. Teeth are then milled into one of the holes, to allow the indexing gear to swivel the head and lock it into position once assembled. Further in China, the metal handle is forged, drilled with assembly holes and milled with teeth for assembly with an indexing gear and the claw head. The handle and the claw also undergo quenching, tempering, sandblasting, and electrophoretic coating. Finally, the handle and claw are assembled with a Chinese origin indexing gear, end cap, spring, and plastic handle grip. The second manufacturing scenario is similar to the first, except a rough shape of the handle is forged in Vietnam. It is then sent to China, along with the flat rough shape of the claw head, where it is drilled with assembly holes and milled with teeth for assembly with a Chinese-origin indexing gear and the final claw shaped head.
