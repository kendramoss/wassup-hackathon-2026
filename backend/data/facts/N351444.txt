In your letter dated July 17, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on Methenamine Hippurate Tablets, USP. Methenamine Hippurate, imported in 1 gram tablets, is a urinary tract antiseptic and antibacterial drug. It is used for the prophylaxis and treatment of frequently recurring urinary tract infections requiring a long-term therapy. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Methenamine Hippurate, is manufactured in India and that the finished tablets in dosage form are also made in India. In India, Methenamine Hippurate will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Methenamine Hippurate tablets in dosage form.
