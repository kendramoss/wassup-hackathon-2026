In your letter dated January 15, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Zhejiang Lingrong Crafts Co., Ltd for the purpose of Section 301 applicability. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review.The item under consideration is toilet tissue, which comes in two-ply and three-ply.Per your submission, the bulk rolls of tissue paper are manufactured in Indonesia. You describe the tissue paper as White/Kraft paper: virgin; layer: 3; with a 17 GSM. These parent rolls of tissue paper, which measure 420mm in width and have a maximum roll diameter 1440mm, are then sent to China, where the tissue paper is embossed, converted by cutting to width and length and re-rolling onto smaller tubes, subjected to quality control, and packaged.
