In your letter dated August 17, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on rubber mallets The imported mallets are identified as 16-ounce Husky Fiberglass handle white and black rubber mallet, SKU #1006100802; and 24-ounce Husky Fiberglass Handle Gray Rubber Mallet, SKU number 1006432316. Each mallet consists of s styrene-butadiene rubber (SBR) hammer head and a fiberglass, plastic coated handle. The mallets are intended for use in damage-free tamping tasks associated with tile installation, furniture assembly, carpentry, automobile repair, etc. You have stated that each mallet is manufactured in the same manner. The rubber hammer heads are manufactured in Thailand from inputs originating in Thailand. The fiberglass handles are manufactured in China from inputs originating in China. The final assembly of the rubber hammer heads with the fiberglass handles takes place in China. The detailed manufacturing process is as follows: Rubber Hammer Head production process in Thailand: 1) Rubber compound mixing: Pre-weighed bales of SBR copolymer, originating in Thailand, are loaded into the internal mixer and ground. Fillers, pigment, process aid and rubber accelerator, all originating in Thailand, are added and mixed together until uniform dispersion is achieved. 2) Final mixing (Curative incorporation): The resulting masterbatch is fed into the open two-roll mixing mill. Once the compound bands around the rolls, sulfur, a vulcanizing agent, vulcanization activators, and scorch retarders are added evenly across the sheet. The compound is repeatedly cut, folded into triangular packages and passed through the rolls until fully dispersed. The mixed compound is sheeted to a uniform thickness, then laid flat to cool, then stored for the next operation.
3) Precision Pre-forming (Blank Cutting): The compounded rubber sheet is rolled into a cylindrical charge and loaded into the pre-forming machine. The target blank weight is set on the controller. The machine extrudes and cuts the compound into uniform pre-weighed blanks. The cut blanks are placed on racks/staging trays and held for the subsequent molding operation. 4) Compression molding and Vulcanization (Hammer Head Forming): The press is set to the temperature and cure time specified for each SKU number. Once the mold reaches the set temperature, the pre-formed blank is placed in the mold cavity, the mold is closed, and pressure is applied to vulcanize and form the rubber into the hammer-head shape. After curing, the mold is opened, the molded head is demolded and a visual appearance inspection in performed. The finished hammer heads are packaged and shipped to China for final assembly. Fiberglass Plastic-Coated Handle production process in China: 1) Fiberglass core rod forming: Continuous fiberglass roving and liquid polyester resin are fed into the pultrusion machine together. The composite material is pulled through a heated die and cured at a fixed temperature and traction speed to form a solid, straight fiberglass core rod of standard diameter. The long rods are cut to fixed lengths matching product specifications. 2) First coating: white polypropylene (PP) injection and logo molding: The fiberglass core rod is placed into the first injection mold. White PP material is injected to form the first coating and integrated brand logo. After molding, the semi-finished handle is cooled and shaped for subsequent operations. 3) Second coating: black PP injection: The white PP with logo, semi-finished handle is placed into the second mold. Black PP material is melted and secondarily injected over the outer layer of the white PP base. The black PP layer covers the designated area and forms the main outer plastic structure of the handle. The handle is cooled and fixed after processing. 4) Third coating: Thermoplastic elastomer (TPE) tail injection: Load the double-layer PP coated handle, semi-finished product into the third-set coating mold. Heat and melt the TPE materials and perform targeted injection coating on the tail section of the handle. This coating forms a non-slip, wear-resistant and flexible TPE layer at the handle tail. Final assembly and packaging process in China: Input materials: Rubber hammer head (manufactured in Thailand) and Fiberglass plastic-coated handle (manufactured in China) 1) Adhesive applied to the connecting end of the handle. 2) The handle is press-fitted firmly into the reserved mounting hole of the rubber hammer head. 3) A plastic fastening ring is installed around the exterior of the joint to reinforce the connection and prevent loosening or separation during use. 4) Product labels are affixed as required by the customer. 5) Final inspection and sorting of all finished goods are conducted. 6) The finished mallets are packed into standard cartons for shipment.
