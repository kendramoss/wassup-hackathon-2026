In your letter, dated September 30, 2025, you requested a binding country of origin ruling on paper plates. The ruling was requested for marking purposes. Product information and photos were submitted for our review. In your letter, you outline a scenario wherein jumbo rolls of paper are manufactured in South Korea and sent to China for printing. Subsequently, the rolls of paper are shipped to Taiwan, where they are cut to shape (for example, squared with scalloped edges and octagonal) and molded to three-dimensional form. Finally, packaging and inspection is done in Taiwan.
