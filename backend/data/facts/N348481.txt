In your letter dated May 1, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on Icosapent Ethyl Capsules. Icosapent Ethyl Capsules, imported in 0.5 g and 1 g, is in a class of drugs called lipid-lowering agents. It is available by prescription only as an adjunct to diet to reduce triglyceride (TG) levels in adult patients with severe ( 500 mg/dL) hypertriglyceridemia. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Icosapent Ethyl, is manufactured in South Korea and that the finished Icosapent Ethyl Capsules in dosage form is made in China. In China, the Icosapent Ethyl will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Icosapent Ethyl Capsules in dosage form.
