In your letter dated June 20, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on chassis components. The articles under consideration are a ball joint (bolt-on), Part Number WGHBJ-007, stabilizer, Part Number WGHLG-0492, ball joint (press-in), Part Number WGHQT-0344, tie rod end, Part Number WGHLG-0544, and sway bar link, Part Number WGHLG-0738, which you refer to as chassis components. You state that these components connect the body to the wheels, ensuring stability, handling, and comfort. These articles are core components of a suspension and steering system and serve as the direct connection points between the front wheel assemblies and the vehicle's frame. According to the bills of materials (BOMs) submitted with your request for each product, Parts WGHBJ-007, WGHLG-0492, and WGHQT-0344 each contain a Vietnamese ball socket and packaging, as well as a Chinese nut, ball stud, ball seat, lubricant grease, and clips. PartsWGHLG-0544 and WGHLG-0738 each contain a Vietnamese ball socket, dust cover, seal plate, and packaging, and a Chinese ball stud, ball seat, lubricant grease, and clips. The Chinese subparts are imported into Vietnam, where they are assembled with the Vietnamese subparts.
