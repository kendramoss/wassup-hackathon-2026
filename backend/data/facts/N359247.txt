In your letter dated February 26, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on an Ignition Distributor for an automotive engine. A distributor in an ignition system acts as a specialized rotary switch, routing high-voltage electricity from the ignition coil to the correct spark plug in the proper firing order. Driven by the camshaft, the distributor ensures precise timing of the spark for combustion.
