In your letter dated September 2, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling of a steering knuckle on behalf of Q-Thai Parts Co., Ltd. The item is a steering knuckle assembly, model 10801138BP1JT, which you describe as a critical structural component used in automotive steering, suspension, and wheel-end systems. It provides wheel support, steering positioning, wheel rotation, and mounting interface for the braking components, and contributes to vehicle safety, handling stability, and reliability during vehicle operation. The assembly consists of a steering knuckle, wheel hub bearing assembly, and other minor components. You state that the steering knuckle is produced entirely in Thailand from sand, pig iron, and other raw materials. The process starts with the casting of the knuckle by sand mixing, molding, melting, automatic pouring, shot blasting, and automating grinding. Then, the knuckle undergoes electrophoretic coating, computer numerical control (CNC) machining of the bearing bore and dust shield hole, and precision machining. After that, the knuckle is assembled with the dust shield, bearing, snap ring, seal, and wheel hub, which are imported from China. Finally, the product is tested, laser marked, quality checked, and packaged for shipping.
