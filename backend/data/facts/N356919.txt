In your letter dated December 10, 2025, you requested a ruling on behalf of your client, Aarush Diam LLC. You submitted photographs, product description, and manufacturing information on a pair of gold earrings. Style 925 is a pair of silver earrings set with diamonds. Manufacturing Steps: Step 1: Product designing. This is performed in the United States. Step 2: Creating the wax model. This is performed in India. The wax models are sent to the U.S. casting manufacturer for production of the finished castings. Step 3: Alloying fine silver with base metals. This is performed in the United States. Step 4: Spruing and investing. This is performed in the United States. Step 5: Wax Burnout (lost wax process). This is performed in the United States. Step 6: Cooling and mold Break-out. This is performed in the United States. Step 7: Sprue cutting. This is performed in the United States. Step 8: Pre-finishing and Tumbling. This is performed in the United States. Step 9: Setting diamonds and final polishing. This is performed in India. Step 10: Attaching findings including hooks and backs. This is performed in India. Step 11: Logo engraving, and packaging. This is performed in India.
