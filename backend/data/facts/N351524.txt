In your letter dated July 21, 2025, on behalf of Fantasia Accessories Ltd. and Fujian Haojin Toiletries Co., Ltd., you requested a country of origin and marking ruling. The subject product is described as Johnson Parker® Spiced Cranberry Scented Body Butter with Shea Butter packaged for retail sale in an 8 fluid ounce tube. The body butter contains shea butter, fragrance, dimethicone and various other ingredients. It is indicated for use on the hands. The ingredients include emulsifiers, preservatives, fragrances, oils, solvents, and moisturizing agents sourced from various countries. The ingredients will be sent to either Malaysia or Vietnam where they will be weighed, emulsified, homogenized, and blended under controlled conditions to produce a stable bulk using a mixing machine (emulsifier). This process includes heating oil and water phases separately, then combining with high shear mixing operations. Preservatives and other ingredients will be added in a cooled-down phase. The formulation will then be bulk tested for pH, microbial content, and stability. The bulk formulation will then be sent from Malaysia or Vietnam to China. In China, it will be filled into retail containers, affixed with retail labels, and packaged in cartons for exportation to the United States. All packaging materials (bottles, caps, cartons, and cardboard) originate in China.
