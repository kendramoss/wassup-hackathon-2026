In your letter dated September 8, 2025, on behalf of Ingenus Pharmaceuticals, LLC, you requested a country of origin ruling. Diltiazem Hydrochloride, imported in 120 mg, 180 mg, 240 mg, 300 mg and 360 mg extended-release capsules, is a medicinal preparation containing, Diltiazem Hydrochloride, as the active ingredient. It is a calcium ion cellular influx inhibitor (slow channel blocker or calcium antagonist) indicated for the treatment of hypertension and angina. In your letter, you stated Diltiazem Hydrochloride is manufactured in Italy and the finished capsules in dosage form are made in China. In China, the Diltiazem Hydrochloride will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Diltiazem Hydrochloride extended-release capsules in dosage form.
