In your letter dated August 13, 2024, you requested a country of origin ruling on Doxepin Tablets.Doxepin, imported in 3 mg and 6 mg tablets, is a medicinal preparation containing, Doxepin Hydrochloride, a tricyclic antidepressant, as the active ingredient. It is indicated for the treatment of insomnia characterized by difficulties with sleep maintenance.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Doxepin Hydrochloride, is manufactured in Italy and that the finished Doxepin Tablets in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Doxepin Hydrochloride will be mixed with inactive ingredients to produce the final Doxepin Hydrochloride Tablets in dosage form.
