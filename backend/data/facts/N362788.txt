In your letter dated July 1, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on Rifabutin Capsules, USP in dosage form. Rifabutin, imported in 150 mg capsules, is an antimycobacterial agent. It is indicated for the prevention of disseminated Mycobacterium avium complex (MAC) disease in patients with advanced HIV infection. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Rifabutin, is manufactured in Italy and that the finished capsules in dosage form are made in India. In India, Rifabutin, will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Rifabutin Capsules in dosage form.
