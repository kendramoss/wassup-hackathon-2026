In your letter dated January 6, 2019, you requested a country of origin rulingThe merchandise at issue is described as an emergency roadside kit. The kit is meant to be kept within the user’s vehicle and contains a variety of items that allow the user to address a number of roadside emergency situations. The full list of contents is as follows: a PVC carrying case, jumper cables, tow strap, flashlight, D-cell battery, emergency poncho, knit gloves, first aid kit, and a registration card. Though classification is not a specific issue with this request, this office finds it prudent to establish the correct classification prior to proceeding with the country of origin analysis.
