In your letter dated November 3, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on Phenylephrine Hydrochloride Ophthalmic Solution, USP 10%. Phenylephrine Hydrochloride Ophthalmic Solution, 10% is a medicinal preparation containing Phenylephrine Hydrochloride, an alpha- 1 adrenergic receptor antagonist, as the active ingredient. It is indicated to dilate the pupil. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Phenylephrine Hydrochloride is manufactured in Taiwan and that the finished ophthalmic solution in dosage form is made in India. In India, Phenylephrine Hydrochloride will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Phenylephrine Hydrochloride Ophthalmic Solution in dosage form.
