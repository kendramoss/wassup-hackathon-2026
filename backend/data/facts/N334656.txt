In your letter dated August 14, 2023, you requested a country of origin ruling on Metronidazole Topical Cream 0.75%.Metronidazole Topical Cream is a member of the imidazole class of antibacterial agents. It is indicated for topical application in the treatment of inflammatory papules and pustules of rosacea.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Metronidazole is manufactured in India and that the finished cream in dosage form is also made in India. In India, the Metronidazole will be mixed with inactive ingredients from various countries to produce the final topical cream in dosage form.
