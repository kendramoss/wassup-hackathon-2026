In your letter dated June 23, 2025, on behalf of Fantasia Accessories Ltd. and Fujian Haojin Toiletries Co., Ltd., you requested a country of origin and marking ruling on scented liquid hand soap. In your submission, the subject product is described as a scented hand soap in liquid form sold under the brand name Johnson Parker®. You state that the hand soap gently cleanses skin with a fresh scent and will be sold in a plastic bottle featuring an applicator pump. The raw materials used to produce the finished hand soap will be sourced from various countries. The ingredients used to produce the finished hand soap, along with their respective countries of origin and functions, are summarized in your submission. The ingredients will be sent to either Malaysia or Vietnam. In Malaysia or Vietnam, the ingredients will be weighed, homogenized, blended under controlled conditions to produce a stable bulk, and then combined with high shear mixing operations using a mixing machine. Preservatives and other ingredients will be added in a cooled-down phase. The formulation will then be bulk tested for pH, microbial content, and stability. After testing, the bulk formulation will then be sent from Malaysia or Vietnam to China. There, the product will be filled into 14 fl. oz. retail containers, affixed with retail labels, and packaged in cartons for exportation from China to the United States. All packaging materials (bottles, pumps, cartons, and cardboard) originate in China.
