In your letter dated May 6, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on Dutasteride Capsules in dosage form. Dutasteride, imported in 0.5 mg capsules, is a synthetic steroid analogue. It is indicated for the treatment of symptomatic benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH) in men with an enlarged prostate. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Dutasteride, is manufactured in Italy and that the finished capsules in dosage form are made in China. In China, Dutasteride will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Dutasteride capsules in dosage form.
