In your letter dated September 26, 2025, on behalf of Ingenus Pharmaceuticals, LLC, you requested a country of origin ruling. Dolishale, imported in 0.09 mg/0.02 mg tablets, is an oral contraceptive containing a combination of Levonorgestrel (a progestin) and Ethinyl Estradiol (an estrogen) as active ingredients. It is indicated for the prevention of pregnancy in women. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs), Levonorgestrel and Ethinyl Estradiol, are manufactured in Germany and the Netherlands, respectively. The finished tablets in dosage form are manufactured in China. In China, the Levonorgestrel and Ethinyl Estradiol APIs will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final tablets in dosage form.
