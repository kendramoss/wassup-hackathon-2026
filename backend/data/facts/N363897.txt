In your letter dated July 31, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on Triamterene and Hydrochlorothiazide Capsules USP, in dosage form. Triamterene and Hydrochlorothiazide, imported in 37.5 mg/25 mg capsules, is a medicinal preparation containing Triamterene (a potassium-sparing diuretic) and Hydrochlorothiazide (a thiazide diuretic), as the active ingredients. It is indicated for the treatment of hypertension or edema in patients who develop hypokalemia on hydrochlorothiazide alone. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs), Triamterene and Hydrochlorothiazide, are manufactured in Italy and India, respectively. In India, these two APIs will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Triamterene and Hydrochlorothiazide Capsules in dosage form.
