In your letter dated June 4, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on Tretinoin Cream, USP 0.1%. Tretinoin Cream, USP 0.1% is a medicinal preparation containing Tretinoin, a naturally occurring derivative of vitamin A (retinol), as the active ingredient. It is indicated for the topical application in the treatment of acne vulgaris. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Tretinoin, is manufactured in Italy and that the finished cream in dosage form is made in India. In India, Tretinoin will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final cream in dosage form.
