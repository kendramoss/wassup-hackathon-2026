This is in response to your letter dated January 2, 2025, on behalf of your client, Jinan Hehe Paper Co., LTD, requesting a country of origin determination of hand towels for purposes of Section 301 duties. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review.The products under consideration are rolls of single-ply paper hand towels.Per your submission, bulk rolls of paper are manufactured in Indonesia. You describe the paper as White/Kraft paper: virgin; layer:1; with a 16-38 GSM. These parent rolls of paper, which measure in multiple widths from 690 mm up to 800 mm and have a maximum roll diameter 1440 mm, are sent to China where the paper is embossed, converted by cutting to width and length and re-rolling onto smaller tubes, subjected to quality control, and packaged.
