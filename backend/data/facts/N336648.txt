In your letter dated November 20, 2023, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Mosaique Surface Inc.The merchandise under consideration is referred to as “Coco Signature Soft,” “Ashford Black,” and “Odysseus Cold.” They are all mosaic tile designs which are designed to be affixed to an existing floor. Samples of Coco Signature Soft and Ashford Black were submitted with your ruling request and were retained by this office. The Coco Signature Soft mosaic is an abstract design, consisting of long triangular pieces of varying lengths and widths arranged around a gold-colored, flower-shaped center. From the information you provided, the surface area of this mosaic consists of 97% Calacatta Dore marble and 3% gold stainless steel. The sample we received measured approximately 30 centimeters long by 27 centimeters wide by 1 centimeter thick at its widest points. You state that the Calacatta Dore marble is mined and manufactured into tiles in Italy. These tiles may be in standard sizes, as well as end-of-cut tiles which may not adhere to standard tile sizes. The end-of-cut tiles range from approximately 30 centimeters long by 30 centimeters wide to 61 centimeters long by 91 centimeters wide, and are approximately 1 centimeter thick. The surface of the tiles is ground to a rough-honed finish. They are then exported to China. In China, the tiles are surface-ground or polished to a fine-honed or polished finish, and cut to the sizes needed for the mosaic design. The gold stainless steel tiles are manufactured in China. The Calacatta Dore marble tiles and the gold stainless steel tiles are assembled into the mosaic design, affixed to a mesh backing, and the gaps filled with grout. The finished mosaic is then packaged and sent to Canada before importation into the United States. The Ashford Black mosaic is a repeating geometric design, consisting of octagonal, trapezoidal, and triangular shapes. You indicate that the surface area of this mosaic consists of 45% Cappraia Breccia marble, 29% Calacatta Dore marble, and 26% Neo Panthera marble. The sample we received measured approximately 30 centimeters long by 38 centimeters wide by 1 centimeter thick at its widest points.From the information you provided, the Cappraia Breccia and Calacatta Dore marble is mined and manufactured into tiles in Italy. These tiles may be in standard sizes, as well as end-of-cut tiles which may not adhere to standard tile sizes. The end-of-cut tiles range from approximately 30 centimeters long by 30 centimeters wide to 61 centimeters long by 91 centimeters wide, and are approximately 1 centimeter thick. The surface of the tiles is ground to a rough-honed finish. They are then exported to China. The Neo Panthera marble is mined and manufactured into tiles in China, and the surface of the tiles is ground to a rough-honed finish. At the assembly plant in China, The Cappraia Breccia marble, Calacatta Dore marble, and Neo Panthera marble are ground or polished to a fine-honed or polished finish. The tiles are then cut to the sizes needed for the mosaic design, affixed to a mesh backing, and the gaps filled with grout. The finished mosaic is then packaged and sent to Canada before importation into the United States. The Odysseus Cold mosaic consists of white swirls on a gray background. You state that the surface area of this mosaic consists of 76% Carrara Velato marble and 24% White Thassos marble. From the information you provided, the Carrara Velato marble is mined and manufactured into tiles in Italy, while the White Thassos marble is mined and manufactured into tiles in Greece. These tiles may be in standard sizes, as well as end-of-cut tiles which may not adhere to standard tile sizes. The end-of-cut tiles range from approximately 30 centimeters long by 30 centimeters wide to 61 centimeters long by 91 centimeters wide, and are approximately 1 centimeter thick. The surface of the tiles is ground to a rough-honed finish. They are then exported to China from their respective countries. In China, the tiles are surface-ground or polished to a fine-honed or polished finish. The tiles are then cut to the sizes needed for the mosaic design, affixed to a mesh backing, and the gaps filled with grout. The finished mosaic is then packaged and sent to Canada before importation into the United States.
