This ruling is being issued in response to your letter dated January 10, 2025, requesting a tariff classification and country of origin determination for a seat cushion, which will be imported into the United States. In lieu of samples, pictures and a product description were provided.FACTSThe subject article is identified as the Dual Purpose Back/Seat Cushion, SKU number B01Z3145001, and is described as a memory foam cushion covered in 100% polyester knit velour fabric that measures approximately 15.7" x 15.4" x 3.5". The foam is contoured to serve as both a seat and lumbar cushion and the packaging material displays its use in an automobile, although the requester states that it can be used in a variety of settings.The manufacturing operations are as follows: The 100% polyester knit velour fabric is made in China and exported to Cambodia where it is cut, sewn and a zipper is affixed. A memory foam cushion blank is made in Cambodia using a foaming process. The cushion blank is inserted into the cover to produce the finished cushion. Subsequent to final inspection, it is packaged for export to the United States.
