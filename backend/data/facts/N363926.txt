In your letter dated August 2, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on a Medium Voltage Substation on behalf of your client, Sungrow USA Corporation. The merchandise under consideration is described as Medium Voltage Substation (MVS) which is identified by model number MVS5140-LS-US. The MVS is mainly comprised of a Smart Control Cabinet (SCC) and an oil-immersed transformer housed in an open frame container. SCC is an enclosed cabinet that contains the electrical aggregation, protection, auxiliary power distribution, monitoring, and control system of the MVS. The transformer converts low-voltage AC into medium-voltage AC when discharging electricity to the grid and steps down medium-voltage AC into low-voltage AC when charging the compatible Battery Energy Storage System (BESS). The MVS is deployed in medium to large-scale energy storage power plants functioning as a bridge between the BESS and the grid to allow a bidirectional flow of electricity to either charge the BESS or discharge power back to the grid. The unit ensures the safe integration of the BESS by executing step-up or step-down voltage conversions required for seamless charging and discharging operations within the power plant. In your submission, you state that the entire manufacturing process takes place in Thailand. The manufacturing process begins with an empty cabinet of Thai origin. It is assembled using various components from Thailand, China, and other countries. An assortment of different electrical and auxiliary components are added to the cabinet, such as lighting, emergency stop switches, smoke detectors, limit switches, circuit breakers, and single and three-phase transformers. These transformers are not related to the oil-immersed transformers but dedicated solely to supplying power to the auxiliary equipment and control system within the SCC. The completed SCC is then lowered to the open frame container where an oil-immersed transformer from either Thailand or Malaysia is attached. The oil-immersed transformer from Thailand or Malaysia is a finished transformer and does not undergo any further manufacturing. The SCC and the oil-immersed transformer are then wired to connect to each other. The finished MVS is then tested and packaged, ready for shipment to the United States.
