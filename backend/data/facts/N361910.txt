Afterwards, theframe is shot blasted, deburred, and subjected to other mechanical finishing operations. Afterwards, the lower cushion, the Chinese body assembly, the upper cushion, and the wear plateare inserted inside the frame. The cap is secured to the top of the frame using hex bolts and washers, whichare then torqued to specification.
