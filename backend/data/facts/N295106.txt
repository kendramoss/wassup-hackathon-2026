In your letter dated November 3, 2010, you requested a ruling on the NAFTA status of Frozen Lobster Tails imported from Canada on behalf of La Renaissance (Quebec, Canada).You indicate that live lobster (Homarus americanus) which is grown and harvested in the United States will be initially shipped to Canada where it will be processed in the following ways: the lobster tails are separated from the whole lobster before it is sized, cleaned and soaked in water for a few hours. The product is subsequently graded, weighed and cut mechanically into a butterfly style. The lobster tails are frozen and placed in boxes that weigh either 10 pounds or 520 grams. The product is not in an airtight container or soaked in brine at completion of packaging. The Frozen Lobster Tails are palletized and returned to the United States.
