In your letter dated December 12, 2024, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Hangzhou Mingxuan Sanitary Products Co., Ltd., for the purposes of duty calculation and Section 301 applicability. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review.The item under consideration is packages of “toilet tissue”, SKU number MXJRT-11. It comes in a variety of plies (single, double, and triple).Per your submission, the bulk rolls of toilet paper stock are manufactured in Indonesia. You describe the paper as White/Kraft paper: virgin; layer: 1/2/3; with a 13-21 GSM. These parent rolls of paper, which measure in multiple widths from 20 mm up to 32 mm and have a maximum roll diameter of 2800 mm, are then sent to China, where the paper is embossed, cut, rolled to length, subjected to quality control, and packaged.
