In your letter dated August 29, 2024, filed on behalf of Dr. Reddy’s Laboratories, Incorporated, you requested a tariff classification and country of origin determination ruling.Treprostinil Injection, imported in solution vials is a medicinal preparation containing Treprostinil, a synthetic analog of prostacyclin (PGI2), as the active ingredient. It is a prostacyclin vasodilator indicated for the treatment of pulmonary arterial hypertension.
