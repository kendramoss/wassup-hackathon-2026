In your letter dated April 2, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on Metronidazole Tablets, USP, in dosage form. Metronidazole, imported in 250 mg and 500 mg tablets, is a medicinal preparation containing Metronidazole, a synthetic nitroimidazole antimicrobial agent, as the active ingredient. It is indicated for the prevention and treatment of certain bacterial and protozoal infections. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Metronidazole, is manufactured in Italy and that the finished tablets in dosage form are made in India. In India, Metronidazole will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Metronidazole Tablets in dosage form.
