In your letter dated June 4, 2025, you requested a country of origin determination on a cultivator rake (SKU# 4433344) for purposes of Section 301 duties and marking under Section 304 of the Tariff Act of 1930, as amended (19 U.S.C. 1304). Pictures and descriptions of the manufacturing process were included in your submission. The cultivator rake features four tines arranged in a straight alignment that are offset at a right-angle to a long wooden handle. It is commonly used for breaking up compacted soil, removing weeds, and preparing soil for planting. You have stated that the manufacturing process begins in Vietnam, where Chinese-origin hot-rolled steel is laser cut into a flat outline of the cultivator head. The tines are further divided and forged, and bent into their final shape. The fully formed cultivator head is then sent to China where it is polished, powder coated and assembled with a Chinese-origin wood handle. Finally, a round plastic grip is attached to the end of the handle.
