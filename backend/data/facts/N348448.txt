In your letter dated April 30, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on Venlafaxine Hydrochloride Extended-Release Capsules. Venlafaxine Hydrochloride, imported in 37.5 mg, 75 mg, and 150 mg extended-release capsules, is an antidepressant. It is indicated for the management of major depressive disorder (MDD), generalized anxiety disorder (GAD), social anxiety disorder (social phobia) and panic disorder. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Venlafaxine Hydrochloride, is made in India, and the finished capsules in dosage form are made in China. In China, the Venlafaxine Hydrochloride will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Venlafaxine Hydrochloride Extended-Release Capsules in dosage form.
