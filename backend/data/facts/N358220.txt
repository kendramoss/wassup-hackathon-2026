In your letter dated January 26, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Green Planet Paper Co., Ltd, for the purpose of duty calculation and Section 301 applicability. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review. The paper product under consideration is packages of “facial tissue.” They come in double ply with ½ fold. Per your submission, the parent rolls of facial tissue stock are manufactured in Indonesia. You describe the paper as white, 100% wood pulp produced from virgin fiber; two-ply with a basis weight ranging from approximately 12.5 to 13.5 grams per square meter. These parent rolls of tissue paper, which measure in multiple widths from approximately 1260 mm to 1470 mm with a maximum roll diameter of approximately 1150 mm, are then sent to China, where the paper is embossed, interfolded, and cut to size with no shaping. The facial tissues are then subjected to quality control and packaged.
