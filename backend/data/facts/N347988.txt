In your letter dated April 17, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Hangzhou Fuyang Liming Industrial Co., Ltd, for the purpose of duty calculation and Section 301 applicability. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review. The paper products under consideration are packages of "toilet paper" and "toilet paper-jumbo roll, "which come in either single, double or triple ply with no fold. Per your submission, the bulk rolls of toilet paper stock are manufactured in Indonesia. You describe the paper as White/Kraft 100% wood pulp virgin paper; layer:1; 12-32 g/sm. These parent rolls of tissue paper, which measure in widths 2750mm/2800mm and have a maximum roll diameter 2800mm, are then sent to China, where the tissue paper is printed, embossed, and cut and shaped to size. The toilet paper is then subjected to quality control and packaged.
