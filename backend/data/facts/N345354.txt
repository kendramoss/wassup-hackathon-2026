In your letter dated January 23, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on Rocuronium Bromide Injection in dosage form.Rocuronium Bromide Injection, imported in 50 mg/5 mL and 100 mg/10 mL multiple dose vials, is a nondepolarizing neuromuscular blocking agent. It is indicated as an adjunct to general anesthesia to facilitate both rapid sequence and routine tracheal intubation, and to provide skeletal muscle relaxation during surgery or mechanical ventilation.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API) Rocuronium Bromide, is manufactured in Spain and that the finished Rocuronium Bromide Injection in dosage form is made in India. In India, Rocuronium Bromide will be mixed with inactive ingredients to produce the final Rocuronium Bromide Injection in dosage form.
