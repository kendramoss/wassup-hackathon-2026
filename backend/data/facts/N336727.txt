In your letter dated November 27, 2023, you requested a country of origin ruling on Desoximetasone Cream, USP 0.25%.Desoximetasone Cream, imported in dosages of 15 g, 60 g and 100 g tubes, is a topical corticosteroid. It is indicated for the relief of the inflammatory and pruritic manifestations of corticosteroid-responsive dermatoses.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Desoximetasone, is manufactured in India and that the finished cream in dosage form is also made in India. In India, the Desoximetasone will be mixed with inactive ingredients from various countries to produce the final cream in dosage form.
