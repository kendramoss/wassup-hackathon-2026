In your letter dated  April 21, 2026 , you requested a tariff classification and country of origin ruling.Images were provided in lieu of samples.The primary product under consideration is described as the “OC 250 Dome Splice Closure Kit,” part number024985. It is designed to store and secure spliced fiber optic cables and features multiple splice trays housedin a weatherproof dome cover that is clamped and sealed to a base endplate. We agree this enclosure issimilar to the one described in New York ruling letter N331709, dated April 19, 2023, and we will classify itin the same manner under heading 9013.In your request, you indicated your supplier wanted to classify this complete kit under subheading8517.62.0090, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (“HTSUS”). We note that there are noactive electrical components that facilitate the transmission or reception of data. This tariff number coversactive data transmission apparatus that receives, converts, and transmits a data signal. A passive,nonelectrical article such as the subject closure kit would not be classified within heading 8517. As such, itwould not be appropriate to classify this product within subheading 8517.62.0090, HTSUS.Additionally, you requested the classification of the following components if imported separately: 250 benchadapter, hinge adapter (red), bench clamp fixture, hinge adapter (orange), dome cover, and dome clampassembly. You indicate they are all made of molded plastic, and we would agree with your proposedclassification within heading 3926.
