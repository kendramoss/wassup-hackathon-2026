In your letter dated April 17, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Hangzhou Fuyang Liming Industrial Co., Ltd, for the purpose of duty calculation and Section 301 applicability. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review. The paper products under consideration are packages of paper napkins, including “dinner napkins,” “luncheon napkins,” “beverage napkins,” “off fold napkins,” “tall fold napkins,” “low fold napkins," and "airlaid napkins," which come in a variety of sizes and folds. Per your submission, the bulk rolls of napkin stock are manufactured in Indonesia. You describe the paper as White/Kraft 100% wood pulp virgin paper; layer:1-2; 12-18 g/sm. These parent rolls of tissue paper, which measure in multiple widths from 320mm up to 2570mm and have a maximum roll diameter 2570mm, are then sent to China, where the tissue paper is printed, embossed, and cut to size. The paper napkins are then subjected to quality control and packaged.
