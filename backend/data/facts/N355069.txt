In your letter dated October 21, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling. The good under consideration is the Razer Wolverine V3 Tournament Edition 8K PC gaming controller, Model RZ06-05550100-R3U1. It is a wired gaming controller that is compatible with a personal computer only. According to the information provided, the subject controller contains a printed circuit board assembly (PCBA) that is manufactured in Vietnam via a surface mount technology (SMT) process, utilizing components sourced from various countries. The SMT process includes adhering integrated circuit chips, transistors, resistors, capacitors, etc., to a bare printed circuit board (PCB). The functional PCBA will be exported from Vietnam to China for final assembly. The final assembly in China consists of placing the PCBA in a plastic casing and attaching other parts, including brackets, buttons, a directional pad, thumbsticks, connectors, grips, a battery, etc. The completed game controllers are inspected, tested, labeled, and packaged in China.
