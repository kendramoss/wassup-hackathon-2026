In your letter dated November 27, 2023, you requested a tariff classification, country of origin and status under the US-PTA determination ruling on behalf of your client CPF Floors LLC.The products under review are vinyl flooring tiles with click lock installation edging. The tiles consist of the following layers: a UV coating layer, polyvinyl chloride (PVC) wear layer and a PVC decorative layer, a stone plastic composite (SPC) core comprised of a mixture of PVC and calcium carbonate, and an ethylene-vinyl acetate (EVA) acoustic padding layer. Imported in boxes, the subject planks are typically manufactured in lengths of 1220 mm (4 feet) and widths of 183 mm (approximately 7 inches).
