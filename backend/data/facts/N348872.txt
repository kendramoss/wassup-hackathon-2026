In your letter dated May 9, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Dongguan Lopie Paper Co., Ltd, for the purpose of duty calculation and Section 301 applicability. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review. The paper products under consideration are "paper towels" (hand towel sheet, center-pull towel, hand roll towel and kitchen roll towel). They come in either single, double or triple ply with 1/3 fold, 1/6 fold or no fold. Per your submission, the bulk rolls of paper stock are manufactured in Indonesia. You describe the paper as White/Kraft 100% wood pulp virgin paper; layer:1-2; 17-42g/sm. These parent rolls of tissue paper, which measure in multiple widths from 1300mm up to 2850mm and have a maximum roll diameter of 2850mm, are then sent to China, where the paper is embossed, cut and folded to length and width with no shaping done. The hand towels are then subjected to quality control and packaged.
