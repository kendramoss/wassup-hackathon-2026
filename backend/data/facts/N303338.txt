This replaces Ruling Number N302029, dated December 18, 2018, which contained an omission regarding the applicability of Section 301 trade remedies. A complete corrected ruling follows.In your letter dated November 30, 2018, you requested a binding ruling for country of origin on behalf of your client, Foxconn. Illustrative literature and a product description were received. Foxconn item, the “Computer Server Cabinet,” is a floor-standing moveable steel storage cabinet on four swivel castors designed to secure a computer server and hardware. The cabinet measures approximately 44” in length, 24” in width, and 80” in height.
