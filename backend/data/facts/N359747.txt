In your letter dated March 13, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on Ibuprofen Capsules in dosage form. Ibuprofen, imported in 200 mg capsules, is a nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drug (NSAID). It is indicated for the temporary relief of minor aches and pains due to headaches, toothaches, backaches, menstrual cramps, the common cold, muscular aches, minor pain of arthritis, and temporarily reduces fever. In your letter, you stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Ibuprofen is manufactured in the United States and that the finished capsules in dosage form is made in China. In China, the Ibuprofen will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Ibuprofen Capsules in dosage form.
