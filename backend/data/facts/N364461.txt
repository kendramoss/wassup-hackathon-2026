In your letter dated August 21, 2026, you requested a country of origin and marking ruling on an automatic wristwatch from Japan. Styles 1903WC001-1 (black dial) and 1903WC001-2 (blue dial) are automatic wristwatches. The watches feature Miyota 9039 movements containing 24 jewels, stainless-steel cases, and leather straps. You state that the watch movements are manufactured in Japan, while the cases and straps are manufactured in China. All components are assembled into complete watches in China. According to HQ 306338, dated May 14, 2021, “CBP’s long-standing position has been that the origin of a watch (excluding the strap, band or bracelet) is the country of assembly of the watch movement. Although the addition of the hands, dial, case or watchband may add definition to the timepiece, it does not substantially change the character or use of the watch movement, which is the essence of the watch.” In this case, the movements of styles 1903WC001-1 and 1903WC001-2 are manufactured in Japan.
