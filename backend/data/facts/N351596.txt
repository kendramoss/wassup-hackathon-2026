In your letter dated July 22, 2025, on behalf of your client Midea Consumer Electric (Vietnam) Co., Ltd. (“MVN”), you requested a country of origin determination ruling for fan heaters for the purposes of applying current trade remedies. The merchandise under consideration is identified as model numbers NTH15-22GR, NTH15-22ER, and NTH15-24HR, positive temperature coefficient (PTC) Fan Heater I (“PTC Heaters I”). The PTC Heaters I operate by using a PTC heating element to heat air as it passes over the PTC heating element. The fans in the PTC Heaters I then circulate the heated air to the room or other surrounding area where the PTC Heaters I are located. The oscillating mechanism in the PTC Heaters I aids the fan blade in distributing the heated air throughout the surrounding area. Except for the minor variations on the color, accessories and shape, all such units will use the same platform and share the same production process. You present the following manufacturing process scenario, in which the PTC Heaters I are produced in Vietnam through a manufacturing and assembly process comprising three main stages. The bill of materials (BOM) indicates that Vietnam accounts for the majority or a significant portion of the production costs of the PTC Heaters I. Manufacturing process details in Vietnam are outlined as follows: In the first stage, key components are manufactured in Vietnam from various raw materials. MVN will manufacture the front and rear shells, display window, bottom cover, base, and oscillator support for the PTC Heaters I and the air duct cover for the NTH15-22GR and NTH15- 24HR models from raw materials via plastic injection molding operations. In the second stage, The PTC heating element of the PTC Heaters I is manufactured by a third-party supplier of MVN in Vietnam from various raw materials. The production process in Vietnam specifically includes adhering the ceramic sheet and the conductive thermal strip together through numerous processing steps. 

Finally, in the third stage, MVN will place the body components, the PTC heating element, and the remaining components on the main assembly line to produce the finished PTC Heaters I. The final production process consists of many steps and can generally be divided into three different phases: (1) manufacturing/assembly, (2) inspection and testing, and (3) packaging. Each step is performed by different workers in an assembly line.
