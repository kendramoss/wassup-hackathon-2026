In your letter dated July 14, 2025, on behalf of Fantasia Accessories Ltd. and Fujian Haojin Toiletries Co., Ltd., you requested a country of origin and marking ruling. The subject product is described as a face cream sold under the brand name Dermasil®, containing the active ingredient dimethicone, enhanced with rose water and witch hazel. You indicate that the formula is designed to hydrate and nourish the skin while providing a gentle toning and balancing effect. In addition to dimethicone, other raw materials used to produce the face cream include emulsifiers, preservatives, thickeners, oils, stabilizers, and moisturizing agents. These ingredients are sourced from various countries and will be sent to either Malaysia or Vietnam. In Malaysia or Vietnam, the ingredients will be weighed, emulsified, homogenized, and blended under controlled conditions to produce a stable bulk using a mixing machine (emulsifier). This process includes heating oil and water phases separately, then combining with high shear mixing operations. Preservatives and other ingredients will be added in a cooled-down phase. The formulation will then be bulk tested for pH, microbial content, and stability. The bulk formulation will then be sent from Malaysia or Vietnam to China where it will be filled into retail containers, affixed with retail labels, and packaged in cartons for exportation to the United States. All packaging materials (bottles, caps, cartons, and cardboard) originate in China.
