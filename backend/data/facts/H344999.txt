In NY N344694, the e-scooter is identified as the “Lightfoot,” a solar and regenerative braking-powered seated e-scooter. It will be primarily used as a personal conveyance by adults to commute to work or to other activities of up to a dozen miles a day, primarily in urban or other locations with bike lanes. As stated in NY N344694, the inner frame, stem, suspension, brakes, seating, and lights are all sourced from vendors within Taiwan. The battery is sourced from Germany. The wheel hub motors, tires, tubes, throttles, grips, end caps, display, wiring harness, horn, on-board charger, solar panels1, and Maximum Power Point Tracking (“MPPT”) system are all manufactured in China. The Lightfoot e-scooter will ship fully assembled from a factory located in Taiwan. It will arrive in the United States ready to ride out of the box. The Lightfoot e-scooter, while solar powered, does not have the capability to charge other devices.
