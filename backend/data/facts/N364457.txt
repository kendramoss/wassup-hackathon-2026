The sample, SKU #8714038009, described as a “ICON HD Rope,” is a braided synthetic rope intended to be used for industrial lifting, towing, mooring, and other heavy-duty applications. The braided rope consists of three layers: a hollow High-Modulus Polyethylene (HMPE) load-bearing core; a protective mid-layer composed of a 100 percent polypropylene (PP) non-woven sand-filter fabric laminated to a plastic film, which is wraps around the core to keep dirt and solid particles out; and an outer 100 percent HMPE braided protective cover. The core is composed of 12 interwoven load-bearing strands of twisted 100 percent HMPE continuous filament yarns coated with polyurethane (PU). The braided protective cover is composed of 32-strands of 100 percent HMPE filament yarns. The finished braided rope has a diameter of 38 mm, a linear density of 10,722,000 decitex, and a total weight of 1005 grams per meter. The rope features a small metal identification tag utilized solely for product traceability purposes.
