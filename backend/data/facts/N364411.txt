In your letter dated August 20, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on automotive carrier on behalf of Seohan Innobility Co., Ltd. The article under consideration is identified as the TM PE Carrier LH, part number M52715-S1AA0, which is designed for use in the rear suspension system of the TM PE vehicle model. The carrier provides mounting points for various suspension, steering, braking, and wheel-speed detection components, including the lower arm, upper arm, assist arm, trailing arm, constant velocity joint, wheel speed sensor, and brake caliper. The carrier also supports the rear wheel load of the vehicle. According to the information provided, the TM PE Carrier LG is manufactured in South Korea from aluminum ingot, aluminum scrap, strontium, and magnesium of Chinese origin. In South Korea, the aluminum ingot and scrap are melted and combined with the strontium and magnesium additives. The molten aluminum undergoes modification and degassing treatments before being subjected to an electromagnetic-stirring high-pressure casting process. During the casting operation, the prepared aluminum is formed into the specifically shaped TM PE Carrier LH. The casting is subsequently cooled, trimmed to remove the gates and excess material, and heat treated. The completed carrier then undergoes visual and X-ray inspections before being packaged for shipment.
