In your letter dated July 7, 2025, you requested a country of origin determination for purposes of Section 301 duties and marking under Section 304 of the Tariff Act of 1930, as amended (19 U.S.C. 1304). Pictures, a bill of materials and descriptions of the manufacturing process were included in your submission. The item under consideration is a bow rake (SKU# 4433289). It features a rake head with sixteen curved steel tines. Two steel bows extend from the sides of the rake head and attach it to a handle at a right angle. The rake is commonly used for spreading mulch, weed removal, tamping soil, breaking up compacted soil, and removing roots and rocks from cultivated beds. You state that the manufacturing process begins in Vietnam, where Chinese-origin hot-rolled steel is laser-cut into a flat outline of the rake head. The tines of the rake head are then bent into their curved shape. Also in Vietnam, round steel is punched and pressed into two bows, which are then welded to the sides of the rake head at a right angle. These welded pieces are sent to China, where they are polished and powder coated. A handle socket is connected and secured to the bows, along with a wooden and fiberglass handle. Finally, middle and end plastic grips are attached to the handle.
