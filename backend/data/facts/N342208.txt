In your letter dated August 22, 2024, you requested a country of origin ruling on Valsartan Tablets.Valsartan, imported in dosage tablets of 40 mg, 80 mg, 160 mg and 320 mg, is a nonpeptide, orally active and specific angiotensin II receptor blocker acting on the AT1 receptor subtype. It is indicated for the treatment of hypertension and heart failure in patients who are intolerant to ACE inhibitors.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Valsartan, is manufactured in China and that the finished tablets in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Valsartan will be mixed with inactive ingredients to produce the final Valsartan Tablets in dosage form.
