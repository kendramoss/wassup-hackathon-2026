In your letter dated August 19, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on Ammonium Lactate Cream 12% in dosage form. Ammonium Lactate Cream 12% is a formulation of 12% lactic acid neutralized with ammonium hydroxide. It is available by prescription only for the treatment of ichthyosis vulgaris and severe xerosis. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Ammonium Lactate, is manufactured in India and that the finished cream in dosage form is also made in India. In India, Ammonium Lactate will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Ammonium Lactate Cream in dosage form.
