In your letter dated August 15, 2025, on behalf of your client, Globe Union North America Group, you requested a country of origin ruling to apply current trade remedies and for marking purposes. The product at issue is a single-handle faucet, model number D225458. The faucet, which can be installed in the sink of a bathroom or a lavatory, is comprised of a single handle, a cover plate assembly, an aerator, a body and a cartridge valve. With respect to origin, the final assembly of the faucet occurs in China and begins with a cartridge valve from Vietnam undergoing various tests and inspections. Afterwards, the cartridge valve is inserted into a faucet body and secured in place using a retaining nut or clip. In the next step, a handle is attached to the valve stem and tightened in place with a set screw. Subsequently, a cover plate assembly and an aerator are installed. Once complete, the faucet undergoes final testing.
