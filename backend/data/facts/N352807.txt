In your letter dated August 22, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Ningbo Eyecos Cosmetic Co., Ltd. The subject product, moisture tone-up primer (Formula Code F0424-17), is described as a primer that mattifies and moisturizes the wearer’s complexion leaving it prepared for makeup. You provided a bill of materials and a production process description. You indicate that the bulk cosmetic will be mixed in South Korea from raw materials sourced from various countries. The processing in South Korea will consist of multiple mixing steps performed under controlled temperatures. Once mixed, the bulk cosmetic will be subjected to quality control and filtering operations. Afterwards, the bulk cosmetic will be shipped to China for filling and packaging operations. In China, the retail containers will be filled with the bulk cosmetic product and packaged for retail sale for export to the United States. All packaging materials will originate in China.
