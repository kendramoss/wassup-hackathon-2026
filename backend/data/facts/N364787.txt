In your letter dated September 2, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on a steering knuckle. The article under consideration is an automotive steering knuckle, identified as model number 10801126JT. The steering knuckle is a structural component of a vehicle's steering and suspension systems, serving as a load-bearing link between the wheel assembly and the chassis. It facilitates wheel support, positioning, and steering. It also withstands the steering forces, braking forces, lateral forces, and road-induced impact loads generated during vehicle operation. The steering knuckle consists of a steering knuckle body manufactured in Thailand and three bushings manufactured in China. In Thailand, the steering knuckle body undergoes multiple manufacturing and finishing operations, including shot blasting, grinding, electrophoretic coating, and precision machining. The machining operations include matching the bearing mounting bore, precision machining of key mounting and functional surfaces, and machining the mounting holes for the dust shield. These operations establish the dimensions and functional surfaces necessary for the steering knuckle body to perform its intended function. Following the machining operations, the three Chinese-origin bushings are positioned at designated mounting locations and press-fit into the steering knuckle body using a hydraulic press. The completed assembly is then inspected and packaged for shipment.
