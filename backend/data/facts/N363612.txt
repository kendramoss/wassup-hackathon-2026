In your letter dated July 24, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on Mupirocin Ointment USP, 2% in dosage form. Mupirocin Ointment is a prescription topical antibiotic used to treat skin infections. It is indicated for the treatment of impetigo due to susceptible isolates of Staphylococcus aureus and Streptococcus pyogenes. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Mupirocin, is manufactured in India and that the finished ointment in dosage form is also made in India. In India, Mupirocin, will be mixed with inactive ingredients to produce the final Mupirocin Ointment, USP in dosage form.
