In your letter, dated April 12, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling for a Gummy Balloon Dog. A manufacturing flowchart, specification sheets, list of product ingredients, photos, and a product sample accompanied your request. Please be advised the product sample was disposed of. The subject merchandise is described as a Gummy Balloon Dog composed of corn syrup (product of China), sugar (product of Thailand), water (product of Cambodia), gelatin (product of China), citric acid (product of China), modified corn starch (product of China), artificial flavor (product of China), and FD&C Red No. 40 (product of India). You have stated that all manufacturing processes are conducted in Cambodia. Raw ingredients that include corn syrup, sugar, water, and corn starch are boiled. Artificial flavoring and FD&C Red No. 40 are added and blended. The product is then molded and cooled. The resulting product is shaped like a balloon dog and inserted in a clear plastic shell that is placed in an outer box. Each item consists of a single red gummy balloon dog imported and sold with a weight of 285 grams. The merchandise is destined for the candy market.
