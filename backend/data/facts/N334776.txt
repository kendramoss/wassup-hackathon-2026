In your letter dated August 17, 2023, you requested a country of origin ruling on Maropitant Citrate Tablets.Maropitant Citrate, imported in 16 mg, 24 mg, 60 mg and 160 mg tablets, is a veterinary antiemetic. It is indicated for the prevention of acute vomiting and the prevention of vomiting due to motion sickness in dogs.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Maropitant Citrate, is manufactured in Italy and that the finished Maropitant Citrate Tablets in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Maropitant Citrate will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Maropitant Citrate Tablets in dosage form.
