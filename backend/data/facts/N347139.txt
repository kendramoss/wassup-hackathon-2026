In your letter dated March 26, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on ignition coils. In your request, you state that the ignition coils are fully produced and assembled in Thailand. The raw materials are either of Chinese or Thai origin. You presented documents/slides and a video showing the production work done in Thailand. The first item under consideration has been identified as an ignition coil, part # KD-9003. The ignition coil is a crucial component in a vehicle's ignition system. Its primary function is to convert the low voltage from the car's battery into the high voltage needed to create a spark in the spark plugs. This spark ignites the air-fuel mixture in the engine cylinders, enabling the engine to run. This product incorporates plastic housing, copper wire, plastic skeleton, iron core, module, and rubber boot. You state that the procedure for producing part # KD-9003 is the following: Primary connector is used to connect the ignition coil primary and wiring harness to ECU.
