In your letter dated July 15, 2023, you requested a tariff classification and country of origin determination ruling.The article under consideration is an Embraer Phenom 300 Aircraft (SN50500146). You state that the aircraft was manufactured in Brazil in 2013, and it was originally sold and imported into China. The aircraft has less than 1000 hours and the seller confirmed that no modifications have been made to the aircraft, as it is in the same condition it was when it left the factory. The Phenom 300 is a 7-passenger aircraft and airworthy. Its unladen weight is 11,583 pounds.
