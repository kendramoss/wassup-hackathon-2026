In your letter dated May 26, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on valve cores. The items under consideration are four red valve cores packaged together ready for retail sale (item number SP016). Each valve core functions as a tire valve that consists of a thimble, a core sleeve, a threaded head, a spring and a sealing ring. With respect to origin, each valve core is assembled in Malaysia using components from Malaysia and a spring ring from China. Prior to final assembly, raw material (copper rods) undergoes machining to form a thimble, a core sleeve, and a threaded head. Afterwards, the thimble, the core sleeve and the threaded head are electroplated. For the spring from China, glue is applied and afterwards it is wound and electroplated. Final assembly involves inserting or screwing a thimble, a core sleeve, a threaded head, and a rubber seal, all manufactured in Malaysia, into one another. A spring is also installed. The components are then pressed together, and two crimp/rivet points are used to secure the components. Lastly, each valve core is packaged on a blister card to complete item number SP016.
