In your letter dated March 8, 2024, you requested a country of origin ruling on a loaded strut.The product under review is a loaded strut assembly (XS843115220AZ), which is intended to be sold as an aftermarket good for passenger vehicles. The strut assembly includes a shock absorber, strut mount, coil spring, and additional components. The main function of the assembly is to generate a damping force through the spring and strut to absorb the corresponding vibration and keep the car body stable.You provided the bill of materials (BOM) with your request indicating that the components of the loaded strut are made in China, Vietnam, and Thailand. The Chinese and Thai components are imported into Vietnam for final assembly with the Vietnamese components.The "country of origin" is defined in
