In your letter dated February 11, 2025, you requested a tariff classification and country of origin ruling. Descriptive literature was provided for our review.The item under consideration is described as the WD-10UA Single-Stage Carbon Block Under Sink Water Filtration System, which provides clean water directly from a faucet. The device connects to the cold-water line leading to the sink. The filter is designed to reduce chlorine, contaminates, and odor for better tasting water. The filters are to be replaced by a consumer approximately every twelve months and discarded. In addition to the main filter, there are also several other components that help the user to install the unit. All of the items are packaged together into one retail package. The package contains a stainless-steel water supply pipe, timer, nuts, Teflon tape, a spring, screw, O-rings, ¼ craw, connector switch cover, connector switch, fixed ring, joint upper cover, joint cup holder, stent, and T-joint. The Explanatory Notes to the Harmonized Tariff System provide guidance in the interpretation of the Harmonized Commodity Description and Coding System at the international level.
