In your letter dated December 12, 2024, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Hangzhou Mingxuan Sanitary Products Co., Ltd., for the purposes of duty calculation and Section 301 applicability. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review.The item under consideration is packages of facial tissues. They come in either double or triple ply, and in either 1 or 2 folds. Per your submission, the bulk rolls of facial tissue stock are manufactured in Indonesia. You describe the paper as White/Kraft paper: virgin; layer: 2/3; with a 12-16 GSM. These parent rolls of paper, which measure in multiple widths from 18 mm up to 20 mm and have a maximum roll diameter of 2800 mm, are then sent to China, where the paper is embossed, folded, cut, subjected to quality control, and packaged.
