In your letter dated September 30, 2022, on behalf of your client, Computime North America, Inc., you requested a tariff classification and country of origin ruling.The item under consideration is described as the control unit for a water softener. The basic function of the control unit is to monitor the water hardness through connection to sensors in the water softener assembly, and, once the hardness is measured, and based on the desired hardness settings, the program instructs the peripheral devices in the softener unit to release the necessary materials or chemicals into the water to maintain the target hardness levels. As you suggested in your letter, the applicable subheading for the water softener control unit will be 9032.89.6085, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS), which provides for “Automatic regulating or controlling instruments and apparatus; parts and accessories thereof: Other instruments and apparatus: Other: Other: Other.” The general rate of duty will be 1.7% ad valorem.Duty rates are provided for your convenience and are subject to change. The text of the most recent HTSUS and the accompanying duty rates are provided on the World Wide Web at https://hts.usitc.gov/current.
