In your letter dated January 21, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling. Images were provided in lieu of a sample. The product under consideration is described as abalone color bar shell stones, style number 10016. Your submission indicates the abalone shells are sourced from China and then shipped to your factory in Thailand. Once in Thailand, the factory cuts the shells into slices. These slices are then lapped to achieve the appropriate thickness of the finished article. It will then be sharpened, which shapes the sliced and lapped material into its final form. In this scenario, this will be your “Danielle” shape. After being shaped, the shell is ground to achieve a flat surface and then finally it will be polished.
