In your letter, dated April 21, 2023, you requested a country of origin ruling for marking and duty purposes on behalf of your client, Staples, Inc., on two styles of paper file folders. A detailed description of the manufacturing operations, values, and photos of the items were submitted for our review.The finished goods to be imported are two file folders: Item TR875411, a hanging file folder, and Item ST413781, a pressboard classification file folder.TR875411 is a hanging file folder measuring approximately 20.75” x 11.62” made of 170 GSM paper stock. It will be imported packaged 25 folders per box and come in assorted colors. PVC tabs and paper inserts for labeling the folders will be included.ST413781 is a classification folder measuring approximately 11.75” x 10”. The folder features one divider, tabs, metal fasteners, and an expansion binding. They will be packaged five folders per box. First, we will address tariff classification.
