In your letter dated August 21, 2023, you requested a country of origin ruling on Ivermectin Cream, 1%.Ivermectin Cream, 1% is a medicinal preparation containing Ivermectin, a semisynthetic anthelminitic agent as the active ingredient. It is a semi-synthetic derivative isolated from the fermentation of Streptomyces avermitilis that belongs to the avermectin family of macrocyclic lactones. Ivermectin Cream, 1%, is indicated for the treatment of inflammatory lesions of rosacea.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Ivermectin, is manufactured in Macau and that the finished cream in dosage form is made in India. In India, the Ivermectin will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final cream in dosage form.
