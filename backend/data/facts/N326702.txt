In your letter dated June 17, 2022, on behalf of your client, Duro Industries, you requested abinding ruling on the eligibility of a walk-behind lawn mower under the United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement (USMCA).The imported merchandise is identified as a YARDMAX walk-behind lawnmower with a 3-kW gasoline-powered engine. You suggested classifying the lawn mower within subheading 8433.11.0010, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS), which provides for “Harvesting or threshing machinery, including straw or fodder balers; grass or hay mowers…, parts thereof: Mowers for lawns, parks or sports grounds: Powered, with the cutting device rotating in a horizontal plane: Electric mowers, including battery operated.” We disagree. The information you provided clearly states that the lawn mower has a gasoline engine, not electric.
