In your letter dated March 25, 2019 you requested a tariff classification ruling. The sample submitted will be disposed of.The subject merchandise is “Village Holly Berries”. The item is green candies in the shape of a leaf and red candies in the shape of a ball, packaged and sold in a glass bottle. The stated ingredients for the candy are dextrose, glucose (corn syrup), cirtic acid, calcium strearate, tapioca dextrin, titanium dioxide, confectioner’s glaze, canauba wax, artificial flavors and colors (FD&C Red 40, Yellow 5 and Blue 1). The candy, manufactured in Canada, will be shipped to China and packaged in 200ml glass bottles of Chinese origin. Each glass bottle contains 200g, net weight, of candy. The product will be returned to Canada and then shipped from Canada to the United States where it will be put up for retail sale.
