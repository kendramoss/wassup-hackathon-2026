In your letter dated September 26, 2025, on behalf of Ingenus Pharmaceuticals, LLC, you requested a country of origin ruling. Estradiol/Norethindrone Acetate, imported in 0.5 mg/0.1 mg and 1 mg/0.5 mg tablets, is a fixed-dose oral hormone replacement therapy (HRT) containing a combination of Estradiol Hemihydrate (an estrogen) and Norethindrone Acetate (a progestin), as the active ingredients. It is indicated for the treatment of moderate to severe symptoms due to menopause and the prevention of osteoporosis in postmenopausal women. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs), Estradiol Hemihydrate and Norethindrone Acetate, are manufactured in Germany or the Netherlands depending on their availability. The finished tablets in dosage form are manufactured in China. In China, the Estradiol and Norethindrone Acetate APIs will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final tablets in dosage form.
