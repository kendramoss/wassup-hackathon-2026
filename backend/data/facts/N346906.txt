In your letter dated March 18, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Anqiu Xiangyu Packaging and Printing Co., Ltd, for the purpose of duty calculation and Section 301 applicability. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review. The paper products under consideration are packages of "regular toilet tissue" and "jumbo toilet tissue." They come in a variety of plies (single, double, and triple). Per your submission, the bulk rolls of toilet tissue stock are manufactured in Indonesia. You describe the paper as White/Kraft paper: virgin; layer: 1-2; 12-18 g/sm. These parent rolls of tissue paper, which measure in multiple widths from 2750mm up to 2800mm and have a maximum roll diameter 2800mm, are then sent to China, where the tissue paper is embossed, rewound, cut to size, subjected to quality control, and packaged.
