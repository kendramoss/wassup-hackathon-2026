In your letter dated April 11, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Chaozhou Soft Paper Products Co., LTD, for the purpose of duty calculation and Section 301 applicability. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review. The paper products under consideration are packages of toilet tissue (soft pack). They come in double ply and 1/2 fold. Per your submission, the bulk rolls of toilet tissue stock are manufactured in Indonesia. You describe the paper as White/Kraft 100% wood pulp virgin paper; layer: 1-3; 11.5-15.5 g/sm. These parent rolls of tissue paper, which measure 1360mm in width and have a maximum roll diameter 2800mm, are then sent to China, where the tissue paper is embossed and cut to size. The finished toilet tissue is then subjected to quality control and packaged.
