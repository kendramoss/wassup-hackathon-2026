In your letter dated November 14, 2024, you requested a country of origin ruling on Ciclopirox Topical Solution, 8% (Nail Lacquer) on behalf of Encube Ethicals Private Limited.Ciclopirox Topical Solution, 8% (Nail Lacquer), imported in 6.6 mL bottles, is a broad-spectrum topical antifungal agent. It is indicated as topical treatment in immunocompetent patients with mild to moderate onychomycosis of fingernails and toenails without lunula involvement, due to Trichophyton rubrum.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Ciclopirox, is manufactured in Finland and that the finished Ciclopirox Topical Solution in dosage form is made in India. In India, the Ciclopirox will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final topical solution in dosage form.
