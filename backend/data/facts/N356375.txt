The good under consideration is the Pop Mart Mega Space Molly 400% Optimus Prime doll. It is a plastic doll that measures approximately 12 inches in height and features a moveable head, mask, and arms. It is designed principally for the amusement of persons ages 15 and older. The manufacturing operations used to produce the doll are as follows: All components, including the head, arms, torso, legs, mask, oxygen tank and tubes, and gun are molded of acrylonitrile butadiene styrene (ABS) plastic in China and exported to Mexico. In Mexico, the components are assembled via screwing and gluing. The finished doll is packaged for retail sale and exported directly to the United States.
