In your letter dated February 12, 2025, you requested a tariff classification and country of origin determination ruling.The item under consideration is described as a tape and dispenser set, which includes two rolls of heavy-duty tape along with a tape dispenser. The items are packaged together and imported under item number 7063910. The heavy-duty tape measures 1.89 inches x 2,548.8 inches (70.8 yards) and is used for sealing shipping packages. The tape dispenser is made of metal with a plastic handle, an adjustable tension brake for easy dispensing, and a blade that cuts through tape for removal.
