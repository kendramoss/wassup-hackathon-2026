In your letter dated March 6, 2023, you requested a country of origin ruling on Acyclovir Cream 5%.Acyclovir Cream 5% is a medicinal preparation containing Acyclovir, an antiviral, as the active ingredient. It is indicated for the treatment of recurrent herpes labialis (cold sores) in immunocompetent adults and adolescents 12 years of age and older.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Acyclovir, is manufactured in Spain and that the finished Acyclovir Cream in dosage form is made in India. In India, Acyclovir will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Acyclovir Cream in dosage form.
