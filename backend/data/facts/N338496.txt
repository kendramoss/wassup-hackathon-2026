In your letter dated February 27, 2024, you requested a country of origin ruling on Clindamycin Phosphate Topical Solution USP, 1%.Clindamycin Phosphate Topical Solution USP, 1% is a medicinal preparation containing Clindamycin Phosphate, an antibiotic, as the active ingredient. It is indicated for the treatment of acne vulgaris.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Clindamycin Phosphate, is manufactured in China and the finished Clindamycin Phosphate Topical Solution in dosage form is made in India. In India, the Clindamycin Phosphate will be mixed with inactive ingredients to produce the final Clindamycin Phosphate Topical Solution in dosage form.
