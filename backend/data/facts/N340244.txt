In your letter dated May 21, 2024, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Hyundai Motor Company.There are two items at issue with this request, which are each described as wire harnesses specifically designed for use in motor vehicles. The first harness is identified by part number 394122M410. The subject harness is comprised of a group of insulated conductor wires bundled together and covered with plastic and terminated at the various ends with an assortment of connectors. You state that this harness is installed within the engine compartment of a vehicle in order to transfer signals between the intake and exhaust systems.The second harness is identified by part number 58595DS000 and is comprised of a bundle of insulated wires terminated at each end with specially designed terminal connectors. You state that this harness is installed within a vehicle’s engine compartment and is used to transmit electrical signals through the brake system.In your request, you state that the manufacturing process for both parts is virtually identical and described herein. Insulated cable conductors are manufactured in South Korea and sent to China in bulk. In China, the cables are cut to the desired length and taped together in order to create the unterminated harness. The ends of the cables are then stripped and the connectors and terminals are assembled thereon. The finished harness is then shipped back to South Korea for final testing and packaging for shipment to the U.S.The “country of origin” is defined in
