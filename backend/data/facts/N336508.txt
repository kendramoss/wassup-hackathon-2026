In your letter dated November 13, 2023, you requested a tariff classification and country of origin determination ruling on behalf of your client, Fluidmaster.The subject of this request are two types of hoses used to connect a variety of appliances (e.g. washing machines, dishwashers, and ice makers) to water sources. Item 1 consists of a polyvinyl chloride (PVC) hose reinforced with braided stainless steel, with fittings.Item 2 consists of a PVC hose reinforced with braided polymer, with fittings.
