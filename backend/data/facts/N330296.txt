In your letter dated January 16, 2023, you requested a country of origin ruling determination.The first item under consideration is referred to as a surveillance camera, Part Number 0235C3Q2. This camera can record and capture both still and digital images onto an internal SD card. The camera consists of a lens, a sensor board, an encoding board, a power board, a shell, and cables. The sensor board is a printed circuit board assembly (PCBA) that can transform the optical signal from the lens to the electrical signal. The encoding board is also a PCBA that can encode the electrical signal of the video or image data. Lastly, the power board is a PCBA that provides power to the camera. The manufacturing process of the three PCBAs occurs in Vietnam, where the bare printed circuit board (PCB) of Chinese origin is first inspected through a 3D solder paste inspection. The PCB is populated with many electronic components of Chinese, Taiwanese, and U.S. origin, using Surface Mount Technology (SMT). Next, the PCBA subassembly undergoes a reflow process and wave soldering, which permanently secures the electrical components onto the PCB. The PCBA subassembly passes through an automated optical inspection (AOI), then it goes through X-Ray inspection and testing. Lastly, the PCBA subassembly goes through a final packing process for importation into China for final assembly.In China, the Chinese origin lens is aligned and bonded with the sensor board with screws or glue and is attached to the housing. Then, the other PCBAs and cables are added, to produce a finished camera. Next, the camera goes through a series of tests such as a waterproof test, shock drop test, and environmental stress screening test, before the camera is packaged for exportation to the U.S.The second item is the Network Video Recorder (NVR), Part Number 0235C328. The NVR consists of a PCBA, a panel, a shell, and a hard disk drive (HDD). The subject NVR records video sent from the camera.Based on the information provided, the hard disks are of Thailand or Philippines origin but are not added to the PCBA during the production process. The subject NVR is imported into the United States without a HDD. The manufacturing of the PCBA occurs in Vietnam where the bare PCB of Chinese origin is first inspected through a 3D solder paste inspection. The PCB is populated with many electrical components of Chinese, Taiwanese, and Japanese origin, by using an SMT process. Then, the PCBA subassembly undergoes a reflow process and wave-soldering, which permanently secures the electrical components onto the PCB. Next, the PCBA subassembly passes an AOI and X-Ray inspection, before it is prepared for exportation to China for final assembly.In China, the PCBA, shell, and housing is assembled to produce the NVR without a HDD.
