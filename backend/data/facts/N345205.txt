In your letter dated January 15, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Zhejiang Lingrong Crafts Co., Ltd. for the purpose of Section 301 applicability. Photos and a detailed description of the manufacturing operations were provided for our review.The items under consideration are packages of paper napkins, including “dinner napkins,” “luncheon napkins,” “beverage napkins,” and "guest napkins," which come in a variety of sizes and folds.Per your submission, the bulk rolls of tissue paper are manufactured in Indonesia. You describe the paper as White/Kraft paper: virgin; layer: 2/3; with a 17-18 GSM. These parent rolls of tissue paper, which measure in multiple widths from 250mm up to 330mm and have a maximum roll diameter 1440mm, are then sent to China, where the tissue paper is embossed, cut to size, folded, subjected to quality control, and packaged.
