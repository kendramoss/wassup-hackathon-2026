In your letter dated April 21, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on a LED Mini Light Bar assembled in China from Vietnamese and Chinese components on behalf of your client, Buyers Products, located in Mentor, Ohio. In your email to this office, dated April 29, 2025, you indicated that you are seeking this country-of-origin determination for Section 301 Trade Remedy purposes. The item under consideration has been identified as a 15 Inch Octagonal LED Mini Light Bar, Buyers Part Number 8891062. You state in your request that this LED light bar is used on the Snowplow Truck, Work Truck, Pickup Trucks, and Trailers. It is designed to be placed outside on top of a work truck’s cab as a signaling device to other vehicles/pedestrians to proceed with caution around this vehicle. The LED light bar consists of a lens, reflector, printed circuit board assembly (PCBA) light board containing 68 LEDS, a sealing ring, plastic housing/frames, as well as a magnetic base. The light bar also includes a 15 ft power cord with an accessory power plug with an on/off switch. The magnetic base allows the light bar to attach to the cab, or it can be permanently attached with the mounting feet. The amber light comes with a SAE J845 Class 1 rating and the item features ten adjustable flash patterns. The terms PCB (Printed Circuit Board) and PCBA (Printed Circuit Board Assembly) are often used interchangeably, but they refer to different stages of production. While a PCB refers to the bare board that provides a foundation for electronic circuits, a PCBA is the complete assembly where components are soldered onto the PCB to create a functional product. The PCBA light boards are manufactured in Vietnam via Surface Mount Technology process. The PCBA is populated with an IC chip, as well as various fuses, diodes, capacitors, varistors, etc. The process involves: Screen printing 

Glue dispensing Component placement Reflow soldering Cleaning Final inspection Once completed, the PCBA light boards are packaged and shipped to China as a completed unit.
