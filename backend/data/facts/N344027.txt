In your letter dated November 14, 2014, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Bumetanide Tablets USP.Bumetanide, imported in 0.5 mg, 1 mg and 2 mg tablets, is a diuretic. It is indicated for the treatment of edema associated with congestive heart failure, hepatic and renal diseases, including the nephrotic syndrome.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Bumetanide, is manufactured in Italy and that the finished tablets in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Bumetanide will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final tablets in dosage form.
