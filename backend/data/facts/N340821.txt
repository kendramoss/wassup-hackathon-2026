In your letter dated June 19, 2024, you requested a country of origin ruling on Theophylline Extended-Release Tablets.Theophylline, imported in 300 mg and 450 mg extended-release tablets, is a vegetable alkaloid belonging to a group of medicines known as bronchodilators. It is indicated to treat the symptoms and reversible airflow obstruction associated with chronic asthma and other chronic lung diseases, such as emphysema and chronic bronchitis.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Theophylline, is manufactured in Germany and that the finished extended-release tablets in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Theophylline will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final extended-release tablets in dosage form.
