In your letter dated June 2, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on Cyclophosphamide for Injection in dosage form. Cyclophosphamide for Injection, imported in 500 mg/vial, 1 gm/vial, and 2 gm/vial, is an alkylating drug which is a type of chemotherapy drug used primarily in the treatment of various cancers. It is indicated for the treatment of malignant lymphomas, multiple myeloma, leukemias, mycosis fungoides, neuroblastoma, adenocarcinoma of the ovary, retinoblastoma, and breast carcinoma. Cyclophosphamide for Injection is also indicated for the treatment of minimal change nephrotic syndrome in pediatric patients. You state the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Cyclophosphamide, is manufactured in India, and the finished Cyclophosphamide Injection in dosage form is made in China. In China, the API (Cyclophosphamide) will be processed into the final Cyclophosphamide for Injection in dosage form.
