In your letter dated September 19, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on Latanoprost Ophthalmic Solution. Latanoprost Ophthalmic Solution, imported in 2.5 mL, is a medicinal preparation containing Latanoprost (a prostaglandin F2a analogue), as the active ingredient. It is indicated for reducing elevated intraocular pressure in patients with open-angle glaucoma or ocular hypertension. In your letter, you stated the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API) is manufactured in Taiwan and that the finished ophthalmic solution in dosage form is made in India. In India, the Latanoprost will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Latanoprost Ophthalmic Solution, 0.005% in dosage form.
