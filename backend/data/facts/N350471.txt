In your letter dated June 20, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on Adapalene and Benzoyl Peroxide Topical Gel, 0.3%/2.5%. Adapalene and Benzoyl Peroxide Topical Gel, 0.3%/2.5% is a medicinal preparation containing Adapalene (topical retinoid) and Benzoyl Peroxide (antiseptic), as the active ingredients. It is indicated for the topical treatment of acne vulgaris in adults and pediatric patients 12 years of age and older. In your letter, you stated Adapalene and Benzoyl Peroxide Topical Gel, 0.3%/2.5% contains two active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs). The Adapalene, USP is made in India and the Benzoyl Peroxide is made in Italy. In India these two APIs will be mixed with inactive ingredients to produce the final topical gel in dosage form.
