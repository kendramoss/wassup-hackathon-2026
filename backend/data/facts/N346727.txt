In your letter dated March 13, 2025, you requested a country of origin ruling on a Suspension Control Arm and Ball Joint Assembly. Pictures, videos, and other descriptive literature was provided with your request. The article under consideration is a Suspension Control Arm and Ball Joint Assembly, Part No. TF-NA-CA-001, which is a component of a suspension system in passenger vehicles. It provides a smooth ride and creates stable road control, drivability and handling. The control arm weighs around 9 pounds, and it is made of steel, with plastic and rubber components. You state that rubber accessories, such as bushings and packaging materials are procured exclusively from certified local suppliers in Thailand. The control arm, ball joint, and ball joint seat (RPCA-0001, RPBP-0001, and RPBJ-0001, respectively) are imported from China to Thailand. All rough parts undergo machining, including precision drilling, chamfering, thread tapping, boring, and chamfering with advanced CNC machines in Thailand. The assembly and packing of all components occur in Thailand.
