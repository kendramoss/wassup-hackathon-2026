In your letter dated July 31, you requested a ruling on tariff classification, country of origin, and eligibility under the USMCA for Original Bioactive Fiber. A sample was submitted with your ruling request and was forwarded to the Customs and Border Protection Laboratory for analysis. This analysis has been completed.The subject merchandise is composed of milled hemp seed hulls (50 percent) and psyllium husk powder (50 percent). You state that the two ingredients are not manufactured by Fresh Hemp Foods Ltd rather they are purchased then blended, tested, and packaged at a facility owned by the company located in Canada. The retail packed merchandise will be sold to consumers through retail stores upon importation into the United States.The tariff classification and country of origin for the stated ingredients are as follows: milled hemp seed hulls (2308.00.9890, Canada) and psyllium husk powder (1212.99.9200, India).Classification
