In your letter dated July 7, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on Tranexamic Acid Tablets, USP in dosage form. Tranexamic Acid, imported in 650 mg tablets, belongs to the class of medications known as antifibrinolytics. It is indicated for the treatment of cyclic heavy menstrual bleeding. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Tranexamic Acid, is manufactured in India and that the finished tablets in dosage form is also made in India. In India, Tranexamic Acid, will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Tranexamic Acid Tablets in dosage form.
