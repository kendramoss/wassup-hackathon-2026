In your letter, dated May 20, 2024, you requested a classification and country of origin determination on behalf of your client, Channel Seafood International (Boca Raton, FL).You have outlined a scenario in which whole, uncleaned, and frozen squid with scientific name Illex Argentinus of Vanuatu origin will be shipped to China. In China, the squid will be thawed, separated into tubes, tentacles, skins, and wings, eviscerated to eliminate innards, cleaned, cut into rings, packed in various combinations, frozen, and shipped for import to the United States.You seek a determination as to the tariff classification and country of origin of the above-described product.
