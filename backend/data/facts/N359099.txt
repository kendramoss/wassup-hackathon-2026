In your letter dated February 23, 2026, you requested a country of origin ruling on Doxycycline Hyclate Capsules in dosage form. Doxycycline Hyclate, imported in 50 mg and 100 mg capsule form, is a broad-spectrum antibiotic derived from oxytetracycline. It is indicated for the treatment or prevention of infections that are proven or strongly suspected to be caused by bacteria. In your letter, you stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Doxycycline Hyclate is manufactured in China and that the finished capsules in dosage form is made in India. In India, the Doxycycline Hyclate will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Doxycycline Hyclate Capsules USP in dosage form.
