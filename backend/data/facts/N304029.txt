In your letter dated April 18, 2019, you requested a country of origin ruling. Illustrative literature and a product description were provided. Tumac items, the “wooden drawer boxes,” are ultraviolet (UV) finished, poplar wood, drawer box blanks of varying sizes. The sides measure between 2” to 4” in width, 1.5” in depth, and 32” in length. The front measures between 2” to 4” in width, 1.5” in depth, and 18” in length. The back measures between 2” to 4” in width, 1.5” in depth, and 18” in length. The base measures between 2” to 4” in width, 1/4” in depth, and 18” in length.
