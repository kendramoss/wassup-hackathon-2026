This is in reply to your letter dated February 5, 2014, requesting a classification and country of origin determination for a babies’ coverall which will be imported into the United States.You submitted sample of a babies’ coverall, which you refer to as a romper, which is constructed of 100 percent merino wool knit fabric. The coverall is a full body suit with long sleeves, hand covers, and has a 8 metal snap opening extending from the front neckline and down the left pant leg to right above the footed bottom. The manufacturing operations for the coverall are as follows: The sheep are raised in New Zealand; the scouring, carding, combing spinning of merino fibers is done in China; The knitting, dyeing and finishing if the merino fibers is done in New Zealand. The finished fabric rolls are then sent to Fiji, where the fabric is cut, sewn, and finished into garments. The finished garments will be imported in to United States directly from Fiji.
