In your letter dated June 17, 2013, you requested a ruling on the tariff classification, country of origin and status of men’s trousers from Mexico under the NAFTA. The sample submitted will be returned.Style 2670-005 is a pair of men’s trousers constructed from 65% polyester, 35% cotton, solid color woven twill fabric. The pants have a flat waistband with seven belt loops, a zippered fly front opening with a single button closure on the waistband, slant pockets in the front, two rear welt pockets, a button closure on the left welt pocket, and hemmed leg openings.
