In your letters dated June 4, 2015 and July 7, 2015 you requested a ruling on the tariff classification, marking, country of origin and status under the United States-Korea Free Trade Agreement (UKFTA) of a cleaning cloth from Korea. A sample was provided.The submitted sample is a microfiber cleaning cloth. The cleaning cloth is made from 80 percent polyester and 20 percent polyamide printed knit fabric. It measures approximately 5 ¾ X 7 ½ inches and has pinked edges. You indicate the cloth is suitable for cleaning optical lenses, phones and computer screens.
