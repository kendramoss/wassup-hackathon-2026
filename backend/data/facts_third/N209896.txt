In your letter dated March 17, 2012, on behalf of Precept Inc., you requested a tariff classification ruling.The instant sample, not identified with a style name or number, consists of a spunbonded nonwoven fabric that is stated to be composed of 100 percent polypropylene man-made fibers (filament). You state that this material will be imported as roll goods measuring 60 inches in width and 30 inches in diameter. Your letter indicates the fabric is manufactured in China and then shipped to Mexico where it is merely cut and packed before being sent on to the United States. The instant sample was informally weighed and was found to weigh approximately 35 grams per square meter.
