Style # Bikini 100 is a women’s knit panty constructed of 95% nylon, 5% elastane. The panty features a self-start waistband measuring approximately 1 ½ inches, a change in knit pattern for the crotch portion and elasticized leg openings. In China, the tubular panel is knit with the self-start waistband and lines of demarcation and then the panel is dyed. In Thailand, the crotch and leg openings are cut along the lines of demarcation, elastic is attached to the leg openings, the crotch seam is closed and the leg openings are sewn.Style # Bralette 200 is a women’s knit bralette constructed of 95% nylon, 5% elastane. The garment features an elasticized V-neckline, elasticized straps, an elasticized back edging, a front inner lining including pockets with removable foam cups, and a self-start bottom band measuring approximately 1 ½ inches. In China, both the outer and inner tubular panels are knit to shape with lines of demarcation for the body of the bralette and a self-start bottom band and then the panel is dyed. In Thailand, both tubular panels are cut along the lines of demarcation, the outer and inner layers are sewn together, elastic straps and edging are attached and removable foam cups are inserted.
