The subject merchandise consists of an unfinished pillow shell made of polyester woven fabric. It measures approximately 19-1/2” square.The manufacturing operations for the unfinished pillow shell are as follows: The fabric is woven, printed, and dyed in Taiwan, then shipped in rolls to China where it is cut and sewn into pillow shells, leaving one seam with a 5” opening. When the unfinished pillow shell is imported into the United States, it will be filled and finished by the importer to be sold as a throw pillow.
