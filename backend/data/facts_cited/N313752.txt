In your letter dated August 5, 2020, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of Prominent Target Sdn Bhd (“Prominent”). In lieu of samples, illustrative literature and product descriptions were provided.Based on the information provided, follow-up conversations and email with GDLSK, Prominent will manufacture multiple lines of wooden kitchen cabinets in Malaysia for export to the United States. As U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP) cannot provide a classification and country of origin ruling on all of the articles in the submitted seven-page spreadsheet, GDLSK/Prominent has selected five representative kitchen cabinets for consideration.
