In your letter dated April 5, 2022, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Triazolam Tablets.Triazolam, imported in 0.125 mg and 0.25 mg tablets, is a triazolobenzodiazepine hypnotic agent. It is indicated for the short-term treatment of insomnia.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Triazolam, is manufactured in Italy and that the finished Triazolam Tablets in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Triazolam will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final tablets in dosage form.
