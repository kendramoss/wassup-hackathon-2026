In your letter dated January 14, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling concerning marking and the application of 301 trade remedies on behalf of your client, FGX International, Inc.The merchandise at issue is described as non-prescription reading glasses with frames made of either metal or plastic. In your request, you detail multiple manufacturing scenarios for the subject glasses that are detailed in the following paragraphs. The first scenario presented begins with the importation of empty eyeglass frames of Chinese origin into the Philippines. Corrective, non-prescription reading lenses are manufactured in the Philippines and placed within the empty frames to create finished reading glasses. The completed articles are then inspected, cleaned, tagged, and packaged for shipment to their final destination.The second scenario begins with the importation of empty eyeglass frames of Chinese origin into Vietnam. Corrective, non-prescription reading lenses are manufactured in Vietnam and placed within the empty frames to create finished reading glasses. The completed articles are then inspected, cleaned, tagged, and packaged for shipment to their final destination.In the third scenario, unassembled frames of Chinese origin, consisting of fronts and temples, are imported into the Philippines where they are assembled into empty frames. Corrective, non-prescription reading lenses are manufactured in the Philippines and placed within the empty frames to create finished reading glasses. The completed articles are then inspected, cleaned, tagged, and packaged for shipment to their final destination.The fourth and final scenario begins with the importation of Chinese origin unassembled frames, consisting of fronts and temples, into Vietnam where they are assembled into empty frames. Corrective, non-prescription reading lenses are manufactured in Vietnam and placed within the empty frames to create finished reading glasses. The completed articles are then inspected, cleaned, tagged, and packaged for shipment to their final destination.
