In your letter dated January 7, 2020, you requested a tariff classification ruling.The submitted sample is an adult size women’s costume identified as The Commander’s Wife, item # 113424, which consists of a cloak, dress and a pair of gloves. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The flimsy, full-body cloak imparts the essential character. Although the cloak has band collar, it is closed with a thin elastic loop, the interior seams feature large, loose overlock stitching that rips easily, the armholes, front opening and bottom sweep feature raw, unfinished edges with hanging threads. You state in your letter that the costume will also be offered in women’s plus sizes as item # 113425. Both costumes will be identical except for size.
