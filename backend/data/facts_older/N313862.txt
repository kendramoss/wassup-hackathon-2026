Rabbit Anti-Mouse IgG-HRP Conjugated antibodyIn your letter you stated the HRP conjugated antibody (also called secondary antibody) can detect a specific primary antibody on a western blot membrane. The primary antibody provides the specificity to the protein of interest present on the membrane, and the HRP enzyme conjugated antibody acts as a substrate and produces or emits a light signal at 428nm. The antibodies are stored in a buffer storage solution and imported in 100-1000 microliters volume per tube at a concentration of 1mg/ml. These antibodies will be used for in-vitro scientific research purposes only and will be produced, packed and imported from India directly for retail sale to customers.
