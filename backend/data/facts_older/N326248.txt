In your letter dated May 23, 2022, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Fenofibrate Tablets.Fenofibrate, imported in 54 mg and 160 mg tablets, is an antilipemic agent. It is indicated for the treatment of adult patients with primary hypercholesterolemia or mixed dyslipidemia and severe hypertriglyceridemia.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Fenofibrate, is manufactured in Spain and that the finished tablets in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Fenofibrate will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final tablets in dosage form.
