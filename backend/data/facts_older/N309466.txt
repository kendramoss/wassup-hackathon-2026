In your letter dated December 20, 2019, on behalf of your client, Theory LLC, you requested a ruling on the country of origin of a men’s pullover. Your sample will be retained in our office for training purposes.Style K0483701 “Basic SS Polo R” is a men’s pullover constructed from knit fabric with a stated fiber content of 78% linen (flax), 18% polyamide, and 4% other fibers. The outer surface of the garment’s fabric measures more than 9 stitches per two centimeters in the direction the stitches were formed. Style K0483701 features a rib knit spread collar, a partial front opening with three left-over-right button closures, short sleeves with rib knit ends that are formed by a change in knit pattern, and a rib knit bottom band that is formed by a change in knit pattern, and which provides tightening at the bottom of the garment.The manufacturing operations for Style K0483701 are as follows:All of the garment’s components (the front and back panels, the sleeves, the collar, and the placket trim) are knit to shape in Hong Kong and exported to China. In China, the majority of the components are assembled by looping and linking; the placket trim is cut to the length used in the finished garment and attached to the front panel and the right side of the collar by looping and linking, and to the left side of the collar by stitching; the buttonholes are formed on the placket; buttons are sewn to the placket; and the finished garment is washed and ironed.
