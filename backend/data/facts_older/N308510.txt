In your letter dated December 10, 2019, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Voyetra Turtle Beach Inc. The product that is the subject of this country of origin ruling request is a gaming headset identified as: Recon 200. Voyetra Turtle Beach Inc. is planning to manufacture the gaming headsets within Vietnam. The manufacturing process that occurs within Vietnam is as follows:Printed circuit boards (PCB) which are manufactured in Vietnam are populated with electrical components (i.e. capacitors, resistors, switches, etc.) from China. A process called Surface Mount Technology (SMT) along with DIP automatic insertion is used to place and connect the components to the PCB forming a printed circuit board assembly (PCBA). The resulting PCBA forms the electrical control module of the headset. Bulk rolls of electrical cable, plastic resin and connectors from China, are manufactured into cable assemblies. Plastic resin from China is used to create various components (housing, ear cups, microphone boom, etc.) using a hot resin injection press manufacturing process.Speakers, microphones, headbands, ear cushions, and various other components necessary for the assembly of the finished product of both Chinese and Vietnamese origin are assembled and/or electrically connected to the assembly.Finally the headset is tested and packaged.A detailed explanation of all manufacturing and assembly steps has been provided.
