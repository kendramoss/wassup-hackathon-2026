The item is an LED (light-emitting diode) straight linear tube lamp used in a linear luminaires with G13 bases to replace traditional fluorescent tube in the indoor light fixtures. It consists of end caps, an aluminum heat sink/PCBA (printed circuit board assembly), LED lamps and a translucent plastic tube.
