In your letter dated June 18, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Clomipramine Hydrochloride Capsules.Clomipramine Hydrochloride, imported in dosages of 25 mg, 50 mg and 75 mg capsules, is a tricyclic antidepressant drug. It is indicated for the treatment of obsessions and compulsions in patients with Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder (OCD). You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Clomipramine Hydrochloride, is manufactured in Ireland and that the finished Clomipramine Hydrochloride Capsules in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Clomipramine Hydrochloride will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final capsules in dosage form.
