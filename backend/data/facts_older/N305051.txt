In your letter dated June 25, 2019, you requested a tariff classification ruling.The submitted sample, style number CS20-0067, is a toddler size Halloween Pumpkin Costume, which consists of an upper body garment, pants and a stem hat. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The upper body garment of 100% polyester knit fabric imparts the essential character of the set, and has visible, loose overlock stitching at the neck, armholes and hem. It also has thin elastics at the hem, and a hook and loop tab closure with visible, loose overlock stitching at the closure edge.
