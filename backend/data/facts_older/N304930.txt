In your letter dated June 21, 2019, on behalf of Jerry Leigh of California, you requested a tariff classification ruling.The submitted sample, style number B21957, Boy’s Pirate Costume, consists of a top, pants, eyepatch, head scarf and belt. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The 100% knit polyester top imparts the essential character, and has visible, loose overlock stitching at the neck, arms and hem. It also has a hook and loop tab closure with visible, loose overlock stitching at the closure edge.
