In your letter dated February 10, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Betamethasone Dipropionate Cream USP, 0.05%.Betamethasone Dipropionate Cream USP, 0.05% is a topical corticosteroid indicated for the relief of the inflammatory and pruritic manifestations of corticosteroid-responsive dermatoses. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Betamethasone Dipropionate, is manufactured in Italy and that the finished cream in dosage form is made in India. In India, the Betamethasone Dipropionate will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final cream in dosage form.
