The sample was destroyed during laboratory analysis.The laboratory has reported, style CW6954 is constructed of 91.9 percent cotton and 8.1 percent cashmere knit fabric. The outer surface of the garment’s fabric measures 16 stitches per two centimeters measured in the direction the stitches were formed. The pullover features a round rib-knit neckline, long sleeves with rib-knit cuffs and a rib-knit bottom. Additional features include embroidery in a contrasting color spelling “Chill Out” on the front panel and outlined embroidered stars in a contrasting color throughout. The pullover extends to below the waist.
