In your letter dated February 8, 2022, filed on behalf of Dr. Reddy’s Laboratories, Inc., you requested a tariff classification ruling.Naproxen Sodium, imported in 275 mg and 550 mg tablets, is a nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drug (NSAID). It is indicated for the relief of moderate to severe pain, fever, inflammation and stiffness caused by conditions such as rheumatoid arthritis (RA), osteoarthritis (OA), ankylosing spondylitis (AS), tendinitis, bursitis, acute gout and primary dysmenorrhea (PD).
