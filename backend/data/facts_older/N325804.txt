In your letter dated April 29, 2022, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Ibuprofen Tablets.Ibuprofen Tablets (Orange), imported in 200 mg tablets, is a medicinal preparation containing Ibuprofen, a nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drug (NSAID), as the active ingredient. It is used to reduce fever and to relieve pain from various conditions such as headache, dental pain, menstrual cramps, muscle aches and arthritis.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Ibuprofen, is manufactured in the United States and that the finished tablets in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Ibuprofen will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final tablets in dosage form.
