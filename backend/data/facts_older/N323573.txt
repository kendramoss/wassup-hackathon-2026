In your letter dated January 6, 2022, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of your client, California Costume, Inc.You submitted a sample of a child size costume identified as Neon Nightmare Clown, item numbers3022-046 and SP3022-046, which consists of a pullover dress and a separate collar. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The flimsy man-made textile dress imparts the essential character of the set and features a deep back opening with small hook and loop tabs for closure, the neck and long sleeve ends are finished with large, visible, loose overlock stitching and the hem has raw, jagged cut edges. The dress is constructed with long, loose straight stitching. Although the dress exhibits some minor styling features, such as pieced sleeves, it is not well made.
