In your letter dated December 4, 2019 requesting a ruling on an acceptable country of origin marking for imported frozen shrimps. You did not submit a marked sample nor representative label with your letter for review.You stated that the subject merchandise is wild caught shrimps (Pandalus borealis) harvested from the Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO) of the United Nations Major Fishing Area 21 (Northwest-Atlantic) and FAO 27 (Northeast Atlantic) by vessels bearing the flags of Norway, Iceland, Faroe Island, Russia, Canada, Denmark and the United Kingdom. The shrimps are exported to Iceland where they are brined (salt water), steamed, peeled, beheaded and the tails removed. The shrimps are placed in salt water once more before they are flash frozen then packed in retail bags with a net weight of 11 ounces each. The finished packaged product will be sold to super markets upon importation into the United States.
