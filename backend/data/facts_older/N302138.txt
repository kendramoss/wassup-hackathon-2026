In your letter dated December 7, 2018, you requested a tariff classification ruling. We apologize for the delay as the information was sent to our laboratory for analysis. Levoleucovorin Injection, imported in 175 mg/17.5 ml and 250 mg/25 ml vials, is a medicinal preparation containing Calcium Levofolinate (also known as Levoleucovorin Calcium), as the active ingredient. Levoleucovorin injection is described as a folate indicated for rescue after high-dose methotrexate therapy in osteosarcoma. It is also indicated for diminishing the toxicity and counteracting the effects of impaired methotrexate elimination and of inadvertent overdosage of folic acid antagonists.
