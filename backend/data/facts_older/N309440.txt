In your letter dated February 02, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling determination of a cosmetic product on behalf of, Elegant Best Investment Limited (“Elegant Best”). The merchandise under consideration is called the Duo Brow-Pencil and Liquid Brow Pencil.The process that is the subject of this ruling is the extrusion and filling process. Attached as Exhibit A is an outline of the processing and packaging that occurs. This process is used for one item: the Duo Brow-Pencil and Liquid Brow Pencil.We note that this is a single item with a dual dispensing function. Processing in South Korea and China: For this item, all bulk raw materials are of South Korean origin. The raw materials are weighed and stored in South Korea. The brow pencil raw material is bulk mixed, extruded, shaped into lead and cut in South Korea, and the Liquid Brow is bulk mixed and stored. More specifically, the raw material for the brow pencil is weighed and labeled before being put in bulk storage. Next, the raw material for the brow pencil is melted, mixed, and ground. It is then inspected and stored. The material will be fed into the extrusion machine which shapes the material and dries it before it is cut and heated. It is then placed in a refrigerator and subsequently cut again. The liquid brow raw material is mixed, released, inspected and stored. Then, both the brow pencils and the liquid brow are shipped from South Korea to China for assembling and packaging.. No additional ingredients are added to the product in China. In China, the liquid brow fluid is filled into pens through auto assembly. Then, barrels are added to the pen assemblies and the lead is inserted into the barrels. The containers are plastic and aluminum, or just aluminum. Next, caps are placed on the containers, and the containers are then inspected, batch coded and packaged for retail sale and shipping. This process is similar to the process described in NY J86656 (7/24/03).
