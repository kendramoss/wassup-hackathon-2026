In your letter dated July 10, 2019, you requested a tariff classification ruling.The submitted sample, style number CS20-0141, is an adult size Fortune Teller Booth Costume, which consists of an upper body garment in the form of a fortune teller’s booth, a headband and two plastic rods inserted into the top of the garment to maintain the shape of the booth. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The upper body garment imparts the essential character and is constructed of 100% polyester non-woven fabric, with a cutout in front that serves as a window with an attached plastic crystal ball on the lower window “frame.” To wear, the user slips the booth over their head, so that it covers their body except for their face which is seen through the cutout window. Straps on the inside of the booth hold the booth to the user’s shoulders.
