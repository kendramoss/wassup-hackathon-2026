In your letter, dated December 18, 2019, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of your client, Cottage Door Press. Product descriptions and photos were submitted for our review.Three books are under consideration. Item 1, “Solo Para Chicos,” is a hard cover, spiral-bound book in Spanish with 96 pages bearing text and illustrations. The story content is for adolescents and addresses nutrition and the importance of exercise along with other topics relevant to a changing adolescent.Item 2, “365 Bedtime Stories and Rhymes,” is a hardbound, 384 page book containing stories aimed at children. Although many pages are illustrated, the illustrations are subordinate to the text.Item 3, “A Treasury to Read with Mommy,” is a hardbound, 192 page storybook containing such classic stories as “Goldilocks and the Three Bears” and “Chicken Little.” Although many pages are illustrated, the illustrations are subordinate to the text.
