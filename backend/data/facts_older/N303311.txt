The outer surface of the garment’s fabric measures more than nine stitches per two centimeters in the direction the stitches were formed. The pullover features a rib-knit crew neckline, long sleeves with rib-knit cuffs, and a rib-knit bottom.The front panel is knit to shape with a self-start bottom, self-finished sides with full fashion shaping, a contoured front neckline, and full fashion marks at the armholes. The back panel is knit to shape with a self-start bottom, self-finished sides with full fashion shaping, a contoured back neckline, and full fashion marks at the armholes and shoulders. The sleeves are knit to shape with a rib knit bottom and full fashion marks at the armholes.The crew neck collar is knit to shape with a self-start bottom and self-finished sides.All components are knit to shape in Hong Kong and linked and looped in China.
