In your letter dated December 23, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Isosorbide Dinitrate Tablets.Isosorbide Dinitrate, imported in 5 mg, 10 mg, 20 mg, 30 mg and 40 mg tablets, is a vasodilator. It is indicated for the prevention of angina pectoris due to coronary artery disease.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Isosorbide Dinitrate, is manufactured in Italy and that the finished Isosorbide Dinitrate Tablets in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Isosorbide Dinitrate will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final tablets in dosage form.
