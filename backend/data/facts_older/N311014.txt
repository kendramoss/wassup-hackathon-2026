In your letter dated March 23, 2020 you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Walgreens Co.The merchandise under consideration is identified as a Wall Charger, Model HKAP1152-18US, which consists of a retail package containing a 5 V power adapter plug with a dual USB output sockets. The subject charger is intended to be used for personal electronics and is comprised of a white plastic molded housing containing a double-sided printed circuit board assembly (PCBA), wires, and plug terminals. Based on the information supplied, all of the component materials that make up the Wall Charger are sourced from China and the assembly of the article occurs in Vietnam. In your request, you state that the adapter plug is assembled in Vietnam by soldering 50 electrical components, such as resistors, capacitors, integrated circuits, fuses, and diodes, onto a bare printed circuit board. The female USB Type-A and USB Type-C sockets are then soldered onto the PCBA, and the entire assembly is encapsulated in a plastic case. You indicate that testing and quality control procedures are conducted on the parts as well as the finished product throughout the various stages of the assembly process. With regard to the origin of the Wall Charger, the marking statute, section 304, Tariff Act of 1930, as amended (
