In your electronic submission dated March 23, 2021, you requested a tariff classification ruling on five styles of footwear. You have submitted photographs, a ruling request letter, samples of five styles, and copies of laboratory reports.Style 96670 Ladies Side Cut Out Booties/AOP are women’s, closed toe/closed heel, slip-on, booties. The external surface area of the uppers (esau) and outer soles consist of 100 percent rubber/plastics. The boots measure approximately 6 ½ inches tall. They feature a side cut on the lateral side and a slide fastener on the medial side of the upper. The ankle bone of the wearer is mostly covered. They have approximately three-inch-tall heels resembling stacked leather heels. They do not have a foxing band or a foxing-like band and are not considered protective against water or inclement weather. The F.O.B. value is $6.55 per pair.
