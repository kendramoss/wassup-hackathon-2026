In your letter dated December 10, 2019, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Voyetra Turtle Beach Inc. The product concerned is referred to as a gaming headset (ear buds) identified as “Battle Buds”. Voyetra Turtle Beach Inc. is planning to manufacture the gaming headsets in Vietnam. The manufacturing process that occurs within Vietnam is as follows:This product contains two printed circuit board assemblies (PCBAs). The bare printed circuit boards (PCBs) are manufactured in Vietnam. Then in Vietnam, those PCBs are populated with electrical components (i.e. capacitors, resistors, switches, etc.) from China. A process called Surface Mount Technology (SMT) is used to place and connect the components to the PCBs forming the printed circuit board assemblies (PCBAs). The resulting PCBAs provide the volume up/down/on/off/mute and other interactive control functionalities to the headset. Bulk rolls of electrical cable, plastic resin and connectors from China, are manufactured into cable assemblies in Vietnam. The electrical cable is cut to length, electrical connector elements are attached and placed within molds where the resin pellets are heated to molten form and poured into the mound forming a finished cable/connector assembly. Plastic resin pellets from China are used in Vietnam to create the outer surface of the headsets as well as several other mechanical plastic parts using a hot resin injection press manufacturing process. These pellets are melted into molten forms in Vietnam, then poured into molds to create the various parts. Speakers, microphones, headbands, ear cushions, and various other components, necessary for the assembly of the finished product of both Chinese and Vietnamese origin, are assembled and/or electrically connected to the assembly.Finally the headset is tested and packaged.A detailed explanation of all manufacturing and assembly steps has been provided.
