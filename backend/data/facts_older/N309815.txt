In your letter dated December 20, 2019, on behalf of your client, Theory LLC, you requested a ruling on the country of origin of a men’s sweater. Your sample will be returned.Style K0483704 “Maragall CRD” is a men’s cardigan-styled garment constructed from knit fabric with a stated fiber content of 75% linen (flax), 24% polyamide, and 1% other fibers. The outer surface of the garment’s fabric measures 7 stitches per two centimeters in the direction the stitches were formed. Style K0483704 features a V-styled neckline, a full front opening with four left-over-right button closures, sides that are formed by a change in knit pattern, long sleeves with cuffs that are formed by a change in knit pattern, and a bottom band that is formed by a change in knit pattern, and which provides tightening at the bottom of the garment.The manufacturing operations for Style K0483704 are as follows:All of the garment’s components (the front and back panels, the sleeves, the placket trim, the neck trims, and the bottom trims, are knit to shape in Hong Kong and exported to China. In China, all of the components are assembled by looping and linking; the cuff ends are folded over and looped and linked to the sleeves; the buttonholes are formed on the front placket; the buttons are sewn to the front placket; and the finished garment is washed and ironed.
