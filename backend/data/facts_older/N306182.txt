In your letter dated September 10, 2019, you requested a ruling on the country of origin for a Network Camera.The merchandise under consideration is identified as a Stick Up Cam Battery, which is a 1080p Wireless Network Camera. The Stick Up Cam Battery can capture images and convert them into an electronic signal that is transmitted as a video image to a location outside the camera for viewing or remote recording. It does not have any internal recording capability. Moreover, this indoor/outdoor camera can sit on a flat surface or can be wall mounted. According to the information provided, the following manufacturing processes were performed in Taiwan: Surface Mount Technology (SMT) process, IC programming, part forming, routing, DIP, downloading software, testing and PCBA packaging.
