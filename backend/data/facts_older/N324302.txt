In your letter dated February 11, 2022, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of your client, California Costume, Inc.You submitted a child size costume, identified as “Evil Spirit,” item numbers 3122-048 and SP3122-048, which consists of a full-length robe, separate hood, and a “head component,” which is a plastic headpiece worn to provide the spirit with a faceless effect. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The flimsy man-made textile robe imparts the essential character of the set and features large, visible, loose overlock stitching at the neck. The deep back opening has raw, unfinished edges with only small hook and loop tabs for closure. The long sleeves have raw, jagged cut edges and the bottom hem of the robe is unfinished with a raw edge. The robe is constructed with a single row of long, loose straight stitching. Although the robe includes a panel for the wearer to see through, the robe is not well made.
