In your letter dated June 19, 2019, you requested a tariff classification ruling.The submitted sample, style number CS20-0120, adult size Tiki Costume, also referred to as Halloween Tiki Guy, consists of a shirt and a mask. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The 100% polyester woven fabric shirt imparts the essential character of the set, and features visible, loose overlock stitching at the neck, armholes and hem, raw edges at the collar, and single straight stitched seams. It also has a front opening with hook and loop tab closures with faux buttons and visible, loose overlock stitching.
