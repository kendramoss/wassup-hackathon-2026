In your letter dated May 13, 2021, you requested a tariff classification and country of origin ruling.Micafungin for Injection, imported in 50 mg and 100 mg vials, is an antifungal medication containing Micafungin Sodium, as the active ingredient. It is indicated for the treatment of Candidemia, Acute Disseminated Candidiasis, Candida Peritonitis and Abscesses in adult and pediatric patients four months of age and older.
