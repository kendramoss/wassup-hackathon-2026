In your letter dated March 15, 2021, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Memantine Hydrochloride Extended-Release Capsules.Memantine Hydrochloride, imported in 7 mg, 14 mg, 21 mg and 28 mg extended-release capsules, is an orally active NMDA receptor antagonist. It is indicated for the treatment of moderate to severe dementia of the Alzheimer's type. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Memantine Hydrochloride, is manufactured in Italy and that the finished capsules in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Memantine Hydrochloride will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Memantine Hydrochloride Extended-Release Capsules in dosage form.
