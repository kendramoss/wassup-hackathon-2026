In your letter dated August 25, 2021, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of your client California Costume.You submitted a child size costume identified as Doll Parts, item numbers 3021-191 and SP3021-191, which depicts a zombie doll and consists of a dress, bloomer pants, separate collar with detachable skeleton cameo and mask with attached bow and hair. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The flimsy woven polyester dress imparts the essential character of the set, and features large, visible, loose overlock stitching at the neck and hem bottom. The large back opening is finished with large, visible, loose overlock stitching and closed with a single small hook and loop tab. The sleeves and back waist feature narrow exposed elastic. The dress is constructed with large, loose, single straight stitching.
