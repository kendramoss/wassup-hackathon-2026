In your letter dated November 8, 2019, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Labetalol Hydrochloride Tablets. Labetalol Hydrochloride, imported in 100 mg, 200 mg and 300 mg tablets, is an adrenergic receptor blocking agent. It is indicated for the management of hypertension. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Labetalol Hydrochloride, is manufactured in Italy and that the finished tablets in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Labetalol Hydrochloride will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final tablets in dosage form.
