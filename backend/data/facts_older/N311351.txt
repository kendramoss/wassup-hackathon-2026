In your letter dated January 11, 2020, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of your client, California Costume, Inc.You have submitted a sample of a costume identified as the child size, Bad Hare Day, item numbers 3220-053 and SP3220-053, which consist of a jumpsuit, hip padding, a mask and a waist tie. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The flimsy jumpsuit imparts the essential character and is constructed with loose, single straight stitched seams, and narrow elastic at the sleeve and leg openings. The neck has large loose visible overlock stitching and the large back opening is raw and unfinished and is closed by small hook and loop tabs.
