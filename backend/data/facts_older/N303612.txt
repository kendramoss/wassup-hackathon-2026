The apron is composed of 100 percent woven polyester fabric, cut and hemmed on all four sides. The item is manufactured in five sizes: 30" x 40", 30" x 42", 30" x 44", 30" x 46" and 30" x 48". The reversible apron is designed to be folded in half along the long axis and worn around the user’s waist held in place by means of a large safety pin.
