In your letter dated January 14, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Leflunomide Tablets.Leflunomide, imported in 10 mg and 20 mg tablets, is an immunosuppressive disease-modifying antirheumatic drug (DMARD). It is indicated for the treatment of active rheumatoid arthritis (RA) in adults. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Leflunomide, is manufactured in Taiwan and that the finished tablets in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Leflunomide will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final tablets in dosage form.
