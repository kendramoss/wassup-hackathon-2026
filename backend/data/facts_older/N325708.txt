In your letter dated April 27, 2022, on behalf of Dr. Reddy’s Laboratories, Inc., you requested a tariff classification and country of origin ruling.Clofarabine injection, imported in 20 mg/20 mL vials, is a purine nucleoside metabolic inhibitor with antineoplastic activity. It is indicated for the treatment of lymphoblastic leukemia (a type of cancer of the white blood cells) in children and young adults who have already received at least two other treatments.
