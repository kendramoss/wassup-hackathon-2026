In your letter dated October 31, 2019, on behalf of your client, Theory LLC, you requested a ruling on the country of origin of a men’s pullover. Your sample will be returned.Style K026711R “Jago Hooded” is a men’s cardigan-style garment constructed from knit fabric with a stated fiber content of 50% viscose (rayon), 30% polyester, 17% nylon, and 3% spandex. The outer surface of the garment’s fabric measures 18 stitches per two centimeters in the direction the stitches were formed. Style K026711R features a self-fabric hood, a full-front opening with a double-zippered closure, long sleeves with rib knit ends that are formed by a change in knit pattern, vertical pockets below the waist, and a rib knit bottom that is formed by a change in knit pattern. The manufacturing operations for Style K026711R are as follows:The front and back panels, the sleeves, the hood, the hood trim, the pocket trim, the pocket bags, the pocket bag trim, the placket trim, and the placket facing trim are knit to shape on flat-knitting machines in Hong Kong and exported to China. In China, the majority of the components are looped and linked to form the finished garment, the zipper is attached, the placket facing trim is cut to the length used in the finished garment and stitched to the front panels, the hood, and the hood trim, and the garment is washed and ironed.
