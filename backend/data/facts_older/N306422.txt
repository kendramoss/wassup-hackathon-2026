In your letter dated September 13, 2019, you requested a tariff classification ruling.The submitted sample, style number OEM16-0737, Halloween Butterfly Cape Wings, are purple monarch butterfly wings. The wings have elastic bands that slide over the wearer’s shoulders and hold the wings in place, so that they can be worn like a cape. There are small elastic bands on the front corners of the wings that attach to the wearer’s wrists for additional stability. The butterfly wings are made of polyester woven fabric and sized for ages three and older.
