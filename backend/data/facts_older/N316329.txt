In your letter dated December 7, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Minocycline Hydrochloride Capsules. Minocycline Hydrochloride, imported in 50 mg, 75 mg and 100 mg capsules, is a semisynthetic derivative of tetracycline. It is indicated for the treatment of Rocky Mountain spotted fever, typhus fever, respiratory tract infections and Lymphogranuloma venereum infections due to susceptible strains of the designated microorganisms. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Minocycline Hydrochloride, is manufactured in Portugal and that the finished capsules in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Minocycline Hydrochloride will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final capsules in dosage form.
