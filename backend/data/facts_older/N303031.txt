This is in response to your letter dated February 6, 2019, requesting a country of origin and marking ruling on behalf of your client, Senko Advanced Components, Inc. This request concerns the country of origin of a fiber optic cable assembly (Model # ENC-0916-1008) from China. The cable assembly consists of optical fiber(s), ferrules, a spring, black post, crimp ring, mini boot, pin keepers, pin clamps, etc. The optical fiber is of United States (U.S.) origin. The other components are from various other countries of origin, i.e. Japan, Taiwan, and China.The processing of the components is as follows: 1. U.S. origin optical fiber is imported on reels into China.2. The fibers are then jacketed with Kevlar.3. The cable is cut to size. 4. The connector parts are attached.5. An outer jacket and the coating are removed to bare the fiber.6. Epoxy is added into the ferrule holes; then fibers are added into the ferrule.7. A heated oven cures the epoxy, which affixes to the fiber.8. An outer housing is added.9. The ferrule surface is polished with a polishing machine.10. The cable is inspected.
