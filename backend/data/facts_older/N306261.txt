In your letter dated September 9, 2019, on behalf of California Costumes, Inc., you requested a tariff classification ruling. You submitted a sample of an Ancient Egyptian Tunic Costume, which consists of a textile tunic, a collar, a belt, cuffs and a drape. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The flimsy single layer tunic imparts the essential character, and has visible, loose overlock stitching at the neck, raw edges at the armholes and hem, and single, straight stitched seams.The Ancient Egyptian Tunic Costume will be imported under item number 5220-005 in an adult size and under item number 3220-001 in a child size. Both costumes are constructed exactly the same except for size.
