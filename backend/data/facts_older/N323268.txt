In your letter, dated December 16, 2021, you requested a tariff classification and country of origin marking ruling. A detailed description and photos of the item were submitted for our review. The items under consideration are sets of “Printed Student Activity Sheets” for sale to educational institutions and educators for use in classroom and other educational settings as part of a suite of curriculum materials for the Positive Action program. A “Student Activity Sheet” is a single sheet of paper measuring 8.5” x 12” with text and artwork used to prompt a child to learn by engaging in drawing, coloring, and/or writing on it, cutting and/or folding it, and/or otherwise crafting with it. For example, one exercise prompts the child to design their own patch by drawing designs or figures that represent the child’s interest, hobby, accomplishment, or personal characteristic and then cut out the patch to wear. Other exercises ask the child to write responses to prompts such as, “Find two facts about your favorite animal” or fill in blanks in sentences, such as “Sometimes I wish I could (blank).” Each set of sheets consists of approximately 32-44 unique designs with 30 copies of each design, for a total of 962 to 1,322 single sheets. The pages are printed via offset printing. There is a different set for each grade level from Pre-Kindergarten through Grade 3.The Harmonized Commodity Description and Coding System Explanatory Notes (ENs) constitute the official interpretation of the Harmonized System. While not legally binding nor dispositive, the ENs provide a commentary on the scope of each heading of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS) and are generally indicative of the proper interpretation of these headings. The General EN to Chapter 49, HTSUS, provides in pertinent part: “… this Chapter covers all printed matter of which the essential nature and use is determined by the fact of its being printed with motifs, characters or pictorial representations...” The essential nature and use of the activity sheets is determined by them being printed.
