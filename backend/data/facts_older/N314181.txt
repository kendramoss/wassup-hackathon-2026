In your letter dated August 25, 2020, you requested a tariff classification and country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, California Innovation, Inc. You have submitted photos in lieu of samples. The subject articles, which you referred to as a “12 can zipperless hardbody cooler-style number 3476WG” and a “24 can zipperless hardbody cooler-style number 3477WG,” are insulated cooler bags. Each article is constructed with an outer surface of 90% polyester and 10% of polyvinyl butyral (PVB). In your correspondence, you stated that an outer surface of each bag is not coated with plastic sheeting. The cooler bags provide storage, protection, portability, and organization to canned beverages during travel. They are also designed to maintain the temperature of the beverages. The two cooler bags are identical except for the size. Each cooler bag has one removable interior storage compartment with a plastic lining and a top hook-and-loop closure. Each front exterior features one zippered pocket and one adjustable shoulder strap. The locations of the manufacturing operations for the subject articles are as follows: ChinaThe polyester fabric, PVB fabric, and all raw materials (PEVA lining material, material for shoulder strap, binding material, TPU patch, back saver pad, AZ woven label, and plastic insert (lids, shelf and plastic liner) are produced.VietnamAll cutting, sewing, and assembly are performed.CLASSIFICATION
