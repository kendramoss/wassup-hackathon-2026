This is in response to your letter dated June 11, 2021, requesting a ruling on the country of origin of a running board for purposes of applying trade remedies under Section 301 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended, from China, which you filed on behalf of your client, JAC Products, Inc.The product under review is an aluminum die cast running board used on a Nissan King Cab (pickup truck). You state that the base of the board is produced in China and imported to Canada, where the supplier applies a transformative pretreatment and proprietary finishing treatment. During the pretreatment process, an e-coat and powder coatings are applied. These processes protect the product from rust and corrosion. Otherwise, the product is useless and cannot be installed "untreated" on the truck. After pretreatment, the proprietary finishing is applied, which is the predominant coating that provides incredible protection of the step ramp.The "country of origin" is defined in
