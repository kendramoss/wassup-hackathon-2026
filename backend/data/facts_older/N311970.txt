In your letter dated May 21, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Modafinil Tablets.Modafinil, imported in 100 mg and 200 mg tablets, is a wakefulness-promoting agent. It is indicated to improve wakefulness in adult patients with excessive sleepiness associated with narcolepsy, obstructive sleep apnea (OSA), or shift work disorder (SWD).You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Modafinil, is manufactured in Italy and that the finished Modafinil Tablets in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Modafinil will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final tablets in dosage form.
