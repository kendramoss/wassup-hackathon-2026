In your letter dated January 3, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on a home monitoring camera kit.The merchandise under consideration is a home monitoring kit that includes three cameras (1 model number CS-9022 and 2 model number CS-9032) and an access point (AP) (model number WAP-N33), which is imported bundled together as product number KX-HN7003W. The subject cameras can capture images and convert them into an electronic signal that is transmitted as a video image to a location outside the camera for remote viewing or remote recording. None of the subject cameras has any internal recording capability. The subject AP is a wireless network and has a subsidiary function to record video onto a micro SD card. Camera model CS-9022 has three types of PCBA’s. The Mainboard receives image data from the Sensor Board, transmits the image data by Wi-Fi; the Sensor Board that contains an image sensor and sends image data to mainboard; and the PIR board senses if something is close to the camera, and sends a signal to the mainboard.Camera model CS-9032 has five PCBA’s. The System Mainboard provides Wi-Fi, audio, video, power, control, and processing functions. The IR-LED board is used for human detection. The Image Sensor Board captures images and audio, detects light, etc., the Charge Board provides the battery charge control and system power on/off controls, and the Power Board inputs the DC power. The subject AP (WAP-N33) contains one PCBA that performs on Wi-Fi 2.4G band. When imported as a set packaged for retail sale, the subject home monitoring camera kit meets the definition of “goods put up in sets for retail sale.” As per
