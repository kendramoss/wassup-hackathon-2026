In your letter dated December 23, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Perphenazine Tablets. Perphenazine Tablets, imported in 2 mg, 4 mg, 8 mg and 16 mg, is an antipsychotic drug. It is indicated for the treatment of schizophrenia and for the control of severe nausea and vomiting in adults.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Perphenazine, is manufactured in Italy and that the finished Perphenazine Tablets in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Perphenazine will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final tablets in dosage form.
