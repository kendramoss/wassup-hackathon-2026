In your letter dated January 7, 2022, filed on behalf of Dr. Reddy’s Laboratories, Inc, you requested a tariff classification and country of origin ruling.Carmustine Injection, imported in 100 mg injectable vials, is an antineoplastic chemotherapy drug. It is an alkylating agent indicated for the treatment of certain types of brain tumors, such as glioblastoma, brainstem glioma, medulloblastoma, astrocytoma, ependymoma and metastatic brain tumors.
