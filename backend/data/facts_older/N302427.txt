This is in response to your letter dated December 12, 2018, on behalf of Komelon USA Corporation, requesting a ruling on the country of origin of tape measures assembled in China. Each tape measure consists of a steel spring, a steel printed tape measure and other various parts such as a plastic case, bobbin, hook and belt clip. You have stated that the raw steel used to produce the spring and printed tape measure is procured in Japan and shipped to South Korea. In South Korea, it is annealed and slit to achieve the specific thickness and width of the tape measure and spring. The tape measure is then painted and printed with the measuring graduations. The printed tape measure and spring are subsequently shipped from Korea to China. In China, the printed tape measure and spring are assembled together with the Chinese sourced plastic case, bobbin, hook and belt clip. The complete tape measure is then exported from South Korea to the United States. The "country of origin" is defined in
