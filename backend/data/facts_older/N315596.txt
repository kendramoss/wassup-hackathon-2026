In your letter dated October 30, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Mesalamine Rectal Suppositories.Mesalamine, imported in 1000 mg rectal suppositories, is an anti-inflammatory drug. It is indicated in adults for the treatment of mildly to moderately active ulcerative proctitis.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Mesalamine, is manufactured in Italy and that the finished suppositories in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Mesalamine will be produced into the final Mesalamine Rectal Suppositories in dosage form.
