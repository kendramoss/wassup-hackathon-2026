In your letter dated June 19, 2019, you requested a tariff classification ruling.The submitted sample, style number CS20-0059, toddler size Fire Truck Costume, also referred to as the Halloween Fire Truck Costume, consists of an upper body garment and a hat. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The 100% polyester non-woven fabric upper body garment imparts the essential character of the set. The garment is shaped like a fire truck with faux wheels held on by shoulder straps. There is an opening at the bottom of the fire truck so that a child’s body can slip through while attached shoulder straps hold the garment on the child’s body. This gives the appearance of the child as a fire truck.
