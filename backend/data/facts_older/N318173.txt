In your letter dated March 9, 2021, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Benzonatate Capsules.Benzonatate, imported in 100 mg and 200 mg capsules, is a non-narcotic antitussive agent that is indicated for the symptomatic relief of cough. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Benzonatate, is manufactured in Taiwan and that the finished capsules in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Benzonatate will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Benzonatate Capsules in dosage form.
