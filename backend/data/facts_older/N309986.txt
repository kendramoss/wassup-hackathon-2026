In your letter dated February 20, 2020, you requested a tariff classification ruling.You submitted a sample of item number CT18118, Angel of Death, teen size costume, which consists of a pullover top, leggings, robe, hood, waist cincher and wings. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The long, flimsy coat you refer to as a robe imparts the essential character and features raw, unfinished edges with hanging threads all around the neck, front opening, long sleeve ends and bottom hem. The interior seams are stitched with long loose straight stitches. The costume will also be imported under item number CG18118, girl’s size, CF18118, women’s size and CW18118, women’s plus size. All costumes are said to be identical except for size.
