In your letter dated August 4, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Amantadine Hydrochloride Capsules.Amantadine Hydrochloride, imported in 100 mg capsules, is an antiviral that is indicated for the prophylaxis and treatment of signs and symptoms of infection caused by various strains of influenza A virus. It is also indicated for the treatment of Parkinsonism and drug-induced extrapyramidal reactions.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Amantadine Hydrochloride, is manufactured in Spain and that the finished Amantadine Hydrochloride Capsules in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Amantadine Hydrochloride will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final capsules in dosage form.
