In your letter submitted April 28, 2021, you requested a country of origin ruling for item 54006, “Bestway Family Rectangular Inflatable Pool.” A description of the production process was submitted with your request. Each “Bestway Family Rectangular Inflatable Pool” is made of PVC sheeting, 2 valves/drain valves, a repair patch, and package glue, all produced in China. The PVC sheeting on a roller and the aforementioned spare parts are shipped from China to a Vietnam factory where the production of the pool takes place. In Vietnam, the manual as well as the automated PVC sheet cutting, stamping, marking and screen printing takes place. The high frequency welding of the spare parts to the PVC sheets and the welding of the side flanges and support beams is also required in order to give the pool its rectangular shape, as well as its dimensions which are 8’7” x 69” x 20”. This process occurs through a series of precise welds by trained worker teams. All this is followed by Quality Assurance (QA) testing, inflation testing and folding for packing and shipping to the U.S. Several welding machines, as well as 650 workers are required to complete the welding process, since machines are equipped with copper molds meant for specific welds.
