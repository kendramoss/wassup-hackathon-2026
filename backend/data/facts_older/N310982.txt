In your letter dated March 30, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Methotrexate Tablets.Methotrexate, imported in 2.5 mg, 10 mg and 20 mg tablets, is an antineoplastic and immunosuppressive drug. It is indicated for the treatment of gestational choriocarcinoma, chorioadenoma destruens and hydatidiform mole.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Methotrexate Disodium, is manufactured in Finland and that the finished Methotrexate Tablets in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Methotrexate Disodium will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final tablets in dosage form.
