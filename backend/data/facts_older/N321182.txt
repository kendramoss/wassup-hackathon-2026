In your letter dated August 24, 2021, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of your client, California Costume, Inc.You have submitted a costume identified as the child size, Kindhearted King, item number 3121-171, which consists of an upper body garment you refer to as a tunic, a coat, sash and a crown. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The flimsy coat imparts the essential character of the set and features a single ply faux fur collar with large visible loose overlock stitching, a raw unfinished full front opening with no means of closure, and faux fur hem bottom. The coat is constructed with long loose straight stitching. Although the coat exhibits some minor styling features, such as pieced sleeves, it is not well made.
