It is constructed of 100% nylon that is coated with polyurethane (PU) plastic sheeting on the outer surface of the article. The article is designed to be worn around the waist for hands-free storage of personal effects during sport activities and travel. The fanny pack provides protection, portability, and organization to its contents. It measures approximately 6.5 inches (H) by 11.5 inches (W) by 5 inches (D). The article has a zipper closure and one adjustable strap with a plastic and magnetic snap closure. The interior features an open wall pocket each side. The exterior features one open pocket.
