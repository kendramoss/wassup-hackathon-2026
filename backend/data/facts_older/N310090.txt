In your letter dated February 25, 2020, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of your client, Orchard Yarn. The ruling request includes four yarns. This ruling will address the classification of two of the yarns: Article# 766, Comfy Cotton Fetti, and Article# 251, Winter Nights, only. Currently, we are unable to issue a classification ruling for Article #508, Date Nights - HT2143 (Metallic), and Article#768, Sponge Cake, which were submitted under ruling request N308536 and forwarded to our Headquarters office for review. The classification review of these two articles is still pending by our Headquarters office. All samples will be returned, as requested.Article# 766, Comfy Cotton Fetti-JX-562, is a 4-ply yarn stated to be composed of 50 percent acrylic and 50 percent cotton fibers. The yarn is presented in a skein and weighs 100 grams. The yarn is for retail sale and is intended for hand knitting. The country of origin is China.Due to the fact that yarn Article# 766, Comfy Cotton Fetti-JX-562, is constructed of a 50/50 blend of fibers, it is classified using Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS) Section XI Note 2(A) and Subheading Note 2(A). The yarn will be classified as if it consisted wholly of that one textile material which is covered by the heading which occurs last in numerical order among those which equally merit consideration. Acrylic staple fibers are in Chapter 55 and cotton is classified in Chapter 52; therefore, this yarn will be classified as being of synthetic staple fibers. Even a slight change in the fiber content may result in a change of classification. The yarn, Article# 766, may be subject to Customs laboratory analysis at the time of importation, and if the yarn is other than a 50/50 blend it may be reclassified by Customs at that time.Article# 251, Winter Nights, STO766, is a gimped chainette yarn with small (? inch diameter) irridescent plastic sequins looped onto the chainette core every 2 inches, creating a decorative effect. The chainette yarn is loosely gimped with two spun yarns in opposite directions. The yarn is composed of 65 percent acrylic and 35 percent polyester fibers. The yarn is presented in a cake, weighs 100 grams and is intended hand knitting. The country of origin is China.In your letter, you suggest that Article# 251, Winter Nights, STO766, may be classified under subheading 5511.20.0000, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS), which provides for yarn (other than sewing thread) of man-made staple fibers, put up for retail sale; of synthetic staple fibers, containing less than 85 percent by weight of such fibers.
