In your letter dated January 8, 2021, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Alanod Westlake Metal Industries Inc.The product under consideration, identified as a bright aluminum clad sheet, is used in various applications such as lighting reflectors, solar thermal energy conversion, decoration for buildings, and decorative trims for furniture, automobile, and electronics. According to your submission, the aluminum sheet will be imported into the United States (U.S.) in 1250 mm wide coils and will range in thickness from 0.1 mm to 1.0 mm. The surface of the sheet will be anodized and may also have a lacquer finish, a physical vapor disposition (PVD) finish or both. The bright aluminum clad sheet consists of a core layer and a cladding layer. The slabs for the core layer will be sourced from various countries and imported into Austria, while the slabs for the cladding layer are manufactured in Austria. You have described the manufacturing process as follows: The first step is melting and casting. While the melting and casting of the core layer is performed in various countries, the melting and casting of the cladding layer is performed in Austria. The second step, plate rolling, is the roll bonding process during which the core layer and the cladding layer are passed through a pair of flat rollers and are metallurgically bonded together. Next, the sheet is etched for cleaning before the bright cold-rolling reduction takes place. The cold-rolling reduction is a thermo-mechanical process that alters the microstructure of the aluminum and also reduces the thickness by as much as 30 percent. If the sheet is to be annealed, it will happen after the bright cold-rolling reduction phase and before the sheet is exported to Germany.Once the sheet arrives in Germany, it is anodized. If the sheet is scheduled to have a PVD finish, it will happen after anodization. The last step, if applicable, is lacquering. The sheet is then imported into the U.S. as a bright aluminum clad sheet.You have suggested that the country of origin is Austria because a substantial transformation has occurred in Austria while the processing completed in Germany is considered minor processing and
