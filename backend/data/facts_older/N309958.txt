In your letter dated February 19, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Bromocriptine Mesylate Capsules.Bromocriptine Mesylate, imported in 5 mg capsules, is a semisynthetic ergot alkaloid derivative. It is indicated for the treatment of dysfunctions associated with hyperprolactinemia, including amenorrhea with or without galactorrhea, infertility or hypogonadism. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Bromocriptine Mesylate, is manufactured in the Czech Republic and that the finished capsules in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Bromocriptine Mesylate will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final capsules in dosage form.
