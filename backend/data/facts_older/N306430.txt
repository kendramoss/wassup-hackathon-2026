In your letter dated September 25, 2019, you requested a country of origin determination under Section 102.21(c)(3) Customs Regulations.You submitted a sample of style 100086528 oatmeal color, a women’s cardigan. In addition, samples of the component panels of the cardigan were included in the submission, which include front and back panels, and sleeve panels from which the sweater is to be assembled. Style 100086528 oatmeal color is a women’s cardigan constructed from 55 percent cotton, 30 percent polyester and 15 percent acrylic. The outer surface of the garment’s fabric measures more than nine stitches per two centimeters in the direction the stitches were formed. The cardigan features a V shaped front neckline and a round rear neckline, a full front opening without a means of closure, front and back yokes, long hemmed sleeves, two pockets (one on either side) and a finished hem that reaches to below the waist. All of the principal component panels of the cardigan (the “major parts”), i.e. the front and back panels, and the sleeve panels, are considered to be knit to shape for the purpose of determining the country of origin.The manufacturing operations for the women’s knit to shape cardigan are as follows:  Hong Kong All components are knit to shape China all panels of the cardigan are assembled
