In your letter dated October 24, 2019, on behalf of your client, Theory LLC, you requested a ruling on the country of origin of a men’s upper body garment. Your sample will be retained in our office for training purposes.Style I0188711 “Lie Vos Hood” is a men’s cardigan-styled garment constructed from 100% cashmere knit fabric. The fabric measures 18 stitches per two centimeters counted in the horizontal direction. Style I0188711 features a self-fabric hood, a full-front opening with a double-zippered closure, long sleeves with self-finished ends, vertical pockets below the waist, and a self-finished bottom. The manufacturing operations for Style I0188711 are as follows:All of the garment’s components (the front panels, the back panel, the hood panels, the sleeves, the front placket/hood placket trim, the front placket facing trim, the pocket bags, the pocket facing trim, the neck trim, and the hood trim) are knit to shape in Hong Kong and exported to China.In China, all components are looped and linked, or otherwise stitched, to form the finished garment, and the zipper is attached to the front plackets. Also in China, the garment is washed and ironed.
