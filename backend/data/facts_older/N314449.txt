In your letter dated September 7, 2020, you requested a tariff classification ruling.Budesonide Nebulizer Solution, imported in dosages of 0.25 mg/2 ml, 0.5 mg/2 ml and 1 mg/2 ml ampoules, is a non-halogenated corticosteroid derivative. It is indicated for the treatment of asthma and chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD). In your letter, you stated the country of origin of Budesonide Nebulizer Solution is Italy and it will be exported from India.
