In your letter dated March 21, 2019, on behalf of Gemmy Industries Corporation, you requested a tariff classification ruling.You submitted samples of two inflatable textile costumes. Item 24529, adult size Inflatable Bull Rider, consists of an inflatable bull suit, a hat, a belt clip battery pack and a battery-powered fan to inflate the costume. When the air-blown costume is inflated, it makes the wearer appear as if he is riding a bull. Item 70676, Inflatable Referee, consists of a textile jumpsuit, a hat, a belt clip battery pack and a battery-powered fan to inflate the costume. The zebra striped jumpsuit has a patch on the chest with the phrase, “I Callz ‘Em Like I Seez ‘Em.” The same phrase is also on the accompanying hat. When the air-blown costume is inflated, it makes the wearer look abnormally large and about to burst out of his clothes.
