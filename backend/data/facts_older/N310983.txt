In your letter dated March 30, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Tizanidine Hydrochloride Capsules.Tizanidine Hydrochloride, imported in 2 mg, 4 mg and 6 mg capsules, is a central alpha2-adrenergic agonist indicated for the management of spasticity. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Tizanidine Hydrochloride, is manufactured in the Czech Republic and that the finished Tizanidine Hydrochloride Capsules in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Tizanidine Hydrochloride will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final capsules in dosage form.
