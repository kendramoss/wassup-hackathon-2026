In your ruling request dated August 9, 2021, you requested a tariff classification and marking ruling on “Disperse Orange 47.” Your submission describes the subject product as coloring matter, which you identify as “Disperse Orange 47” CAS # 5718-26-3, also known as Solvent Orange 107. “Disperse Orange 47” is a disperse dye that will be imported in powder form and intended to be used in the plastics industry.
