In your letter dated June 10, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Walgreens Company.The merchandise under consideration is described as a 10-foot Micro Cable. The cable is constructed of a 10-foot length of insulated conductor with a micro-USB connector at one end and a standard USB connector at the other. The cables are designed to connect and sync a variety of mobile devices to host units or charging stations. In your request, you state that the manufacturing process is fully completed in Vietnam. The process begins with raw Vietnamese cable being annealed, drawn, twisted, and jacketed prior to being insulated. The ends are then prepared for the addition of the Chinese connectors. The connectors are soldered to the ends before the cables are tested and packaged for shipment to the United States. The “country of origin” is defined in
