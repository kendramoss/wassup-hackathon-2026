In your letter dated November 4, 2019, you requested a tariff classification and country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Mario Camacho Foods, FL.An ingredients breakdown and a narrative description of the manufacturing process were provided with your inquiry. The green olives are grown in Morocco, Greece, or Egypt where they are harvested and cleaned, cured in brine solution, packed in brine-filled drums and shipped to Spain where they are pitted and some are stuffed with a pimento paste. After repackaging the pitted stuffed and unstuffed green olives are shipped to their final destination, the United States.
