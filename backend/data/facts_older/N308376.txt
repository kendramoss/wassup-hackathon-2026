The bar mop, item MF 1418BLSTR, is of ribbed knit pile construction composed of 80 percent polyester and 20 percent polyamide microfiber. The white towel measures 14 x 18 inches and features a gold stripe knit into the fabric down the center. The edges are finished with overlock stitching.Item MF 1616YL is an 80 percent polyester and 20 percent polyamide microfiber general purpose cleaning towel of knit pile construction. The yellow towel measures 16 x 16 inches and features overlock stitching around the edges.The towels are bulk packed at time of importation and sold to institution and hospitality customers such as restaurants and hotels.The manufacturing operations for both towels are as follows: China -Fabric is knit.Fabric is exported to Canada.Canada -Fabric is cut and sewn.Finished towel is exported to the United States.
