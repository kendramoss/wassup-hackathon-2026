In your letter dated April 22, 2019, you requested a tariff classification ruling.You submitted a sample of item number CK76017, Monkey Business – Baby Costume, which consists of a jumpsuit and a hood. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The flimsy 100% polyester knit jumpsuit imparts the essential character, and has visible, loose overlock stitching and thin elastics at both the wrists and ankles. It also has a single straight stitched waist, a large back opening with a hook and loop tab closure, and visible, loose overlock stitching at the closure edge. The costume will be imported in the following sizes: 0-6 months, 6-12 months, 12-18 months, and 18-24 months. An attached caution label states that the costume is not to be used as sleepwear.
