In your letter dated October 31, 2019, you requested a tariff classification and country of origin determination under Section 102.21(c)(3) Customs Regulations, on behalf of your client Theory LTD.You submitted a sample of style K028702R. In addition, samples of the component panels of the cardigan were also included in the submission, which include front and back panels, and sleeve panels from which the cardigan is to be assembled. Style K028702R is a women’s cardigan constructed from 100% cashmere jersey knit fabric. The outer surface of the garment’s fabric measures more than nine stitches per two centimeters in the direction the stitches were formed. The cardigan features a self fabric capped V-shape neckline extending down to the bottom, a full front opening without a means of closure, long finished sleeves, 2 belt loops (one on either side) a same fabric sash which can be used as a tie belt and a finished bottom that reaches to below the waist.
