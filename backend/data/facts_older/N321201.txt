In your letter dated August 25, 2021, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of your client, California Costume, Inc.You have submitted a costume identified as the child size, Shadow of Death, item numbers 3121-190 and SP3121-190, which consists of a robe, hood, shoulder drape and a waist tie. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The flimsy robe imparts the essential character of the set. The robe feature large visible overlock stitching at the neck and has long sleeves and a hem bottom with raw, unfinished jagged cut edges.
