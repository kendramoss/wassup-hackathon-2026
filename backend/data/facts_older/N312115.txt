In your letter dated May 28, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Fluocinonide Cream, USP 0.1%.Fluocinonide Cream, USP 0.1% is a topical medicinal preparation containing Fluocinonide, a synthetic corticosteroid, as the active ingredient. It is indicated for the relief of inflammatory and pruritic manifestations of corticosteroid-responsive dermatoses. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Fluocinonide, is manufactured in Italy and that the finished Fluocinonide Cream in dosage form is made in India. In India, the Fluocinonide will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final cream in dosage form.
