In your letter dated February 18, 2021, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Voriconazole Tablets.Voriconazole Tablets, imported in 50 mg and 200 mg, is a triazole antifungal medication. It is indicated for the treatment of fungal infections. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Voriconazole, is manufactured in Spain and that the finished tablets in dosage form are made in India. In India, Voriconazole will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Voriconazole Tablets in dosage form.
