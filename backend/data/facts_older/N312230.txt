In your letter dated June 3, 2020, on behalf of Dr. Reddy’s Laboratories Incorporated, you requested a tariff classification and country of origin ruling.Naloxone Hydrochloride Injection, imported in 2 mg/2 mL (1 mg/mL), is a synthetic morphinane alkaloid belonging to a class of drugs known as opioid antagonists. It is used to block the effects of opioids and works by reversing the depression of the central nervous system and respiratory system caused by opioids.
