This is in response to your letter dated May 1, 2019, on behalf of Komelon USA Corporation, requesting a ruling on the country of origin of a measuring wheel assembled in China. A fully assembled measuring wheel is a hand-held linear measuring instrument comprised of a wheel and counter head. As the wheel is rolled over a surface, the attached counter head measures the distance between two points. You have stated that the counter head is produced in Japan by a Japanese supplier. The counter head is then shipped from Japan to China, where final assembly of the measuring wheel is undertaken.
