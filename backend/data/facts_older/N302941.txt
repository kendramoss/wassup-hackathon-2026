In your letter dated February 8, 2019, you requested a tariff classification ruling.The products to be imported are bathroom and kitchen pods. You indicate that these pods consist of steel stud framing covered with gypsum boards, electrical wiring/fixtures, plumbing fixtures, piping and tiles. The pods will be fully assembled and finished overseas and then imported as one unit into the U.S. ready for installation in a building under construction.You suggest classification of the bathroom and kitchen pods as prefabricated buildings in heading 9406, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS). Bathroom and kitchen pods are not classifiable in heading 9406 as they are not prefabricated buildings. The pods are meant to be built into an existing building and must be connected to that building's wiring, plumbing, and electricity. As such, they are not a free-standing, independent structure, as a building is required to be.
