In your letter dated December 9, 2019, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Formlabs, Inc. (Formlabs).The Formlabs additive manufacture device, or 3-D Printer, is a device that produces three-dimensional objects through stereolithography (SLA). SLA is a process in which a liquid material, i.e., such as plastic resin, is cured into a solid material by laser light.
