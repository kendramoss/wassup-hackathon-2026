In your letter dated August 24, 2021, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of your client, California Costume, Inc.You have submitted a costume identified as the adult size Eagle-Maniac, item number 5121-010, which consists of a jumpsuit, shoe covers and a mask. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The flimsy man-made textile jumpsuit imparts the essential character of the set. The neck and deep back opening of the jumpsuit feature large visible loose overlock stitching and is closed in back with two pairs of flimsy string ties. The long sleeves and leg openings have raw unfinished edges. The jumpsuit is constructed with long, loose, straight stitching.
