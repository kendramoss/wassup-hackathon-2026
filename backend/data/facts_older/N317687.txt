In your letter dated February 18, 2021, you requested a tariff classification ruling. In addition to photographs of all items, a pillowcase and unquilted pillow sham were provided as samples. You state the pillowcase is representative of the sheets and the pillow sham is representative of the duvet cover. The samples will be returned to you, as requested. You also state that all items are made from unprinted, 100 percent hemp woven fabric and will be separately packaged for retail sale. The pillowcase, style 10320, has an envelope side closure and will be imported in two sizes: 20” x 28” for standard bed pillows and 20” x 36” for king sized bed pillows. The pillowcases will be packaged and sold in sets of two.The flat sheet, style 10300, is hemmed around the edges with a four inch hem at the top. The fitted sheet, style 10310, is hemmed with elasticized edges. The sheets will be imported for queen and king sized beds. The duvet cover, style 10200, has an opening at the bottom end and an 11 (queen) or 12 (king) button closure at the bottom. There are internal ties in the upper corners to prevent the duvet from shifting inside the cover. The duvet cover will be imported for queen and king sized beds. The pillow sham, also stated to be style 10200, is plain, unquilted and has a slightly overlapped opening at the center of the back of the sham. The sham will be imported in three sizes: 20” x 28” for standard bed pillows; 20” x 36” for king sized bed pillows; and 26” x 26” for euro sized pillows.
