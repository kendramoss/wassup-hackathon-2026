This is in response to your letter dated March 19, 2019, requesting a ruling on whether the proposed marking on the packaging, instead of on the individual knives, is an acceptable country of origin marking for the imported knives described as Berserk, K9, Warden, Vipera, and Recoil. Samples were submitted with your letter for review and will be returned separately.From the samples provided, each knife comes with a zippered case, which has a paper sleeve that fits over the case. The paper sleeve is marked “Made in China” using an applied sticker. There are no other country of origin markings on this product.
