This is in response to your letter dated December 3, 2018 requesting a country-of-origin ruling on Salted Hog Casings. You outline a scenario where hog intestines are obtained from a slaughterhouse in Germany, sorted according to size then cleaned to eliminate manure, intestinal mucosa, fat, threads and animal fluids. The ensuing product is placed in barrels that contain a salt solution then shipped to China for further processing. In China, the casings are removed from the salt solution, rinsed and soaked in fresh water then hand massaged to separate the strands and prevent dry spots. The casings are subsequently placed in warmer water to generate natural fat making the casings suitable to be placed over a stuffing horn. The pre-tubed and pre-flushed casings which are sold in bundles and hanks are packed in 55-gallon containers comprising of a highly concentrated salt liquid solution. Salted Hog Casings are intended for the manufacturing of hot dogs or sausages in the food service industry.
