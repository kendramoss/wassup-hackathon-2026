In your electronic submission dated May 6, 2021, you requested a tariff classification ruling on five styles of footwear. You have submitted photographs, a ruling request letter, samples of five styles, and laboratory reports.Style 97126 Ladies Booties/Black is a woman’s, closed toe/closed heel, above-the-ankle, below-the-knee, fashion bootie. The bootie measures approximately 7 ½ inches tall. You have provided a laboratory report showing the external surface area of the upper (esau) and the outer sole are comprised of 100 percent rubber or plastics. A functional slide fastener is located on the medial side of the bootie. The bootie does not have a foxing or a foxing-like band. You provided an F.O.B. value of $7.98 per pair. Style 97127 Ladies Booties/Brown is a woman’s, closed toe/closed heel, above-the-ankle, below-the-knee, high-heel boot. The boot measures approximately 8 inches tall and features a decorative strap with a gold-tone, metal buckle that crisscrosses in front of the ankle, and a pull tab. The esau and outer sole are comprised of 100 percent rubber/plastics. You provided an F.O.B. value of $7.98 per pair. Style 97128 Ladies Booties/Teal Gore is a woman’s, closed toe/closed heel, above-the-ankle, below-the-knee, high-heel fashion boot. The boot measures approximately 7½ inches tall. There is a functional slide fastener on the medial side of the boot. The boot features strips of upper material sewn on top of the elastic gore. It does not have a foxing or a foxing-like band. The outer sole is composed of rubber or plastics and has an attached high heel resembling stacked leather. The F.O.B. value is $7.98 per pair.
