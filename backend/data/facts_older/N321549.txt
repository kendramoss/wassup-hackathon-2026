This replaces Ruling Number, N321215 dated September 8, 2021, which contained a typographical error relating to the item numbers. A complete corrected ruling follows.In your letter dated August 25, 2021, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of your client, California Costume, Inc.You have submitted a costume identified as the toddler size, Posh Vampire, item numbers 2021-138 and SP2021-138 which consists of a full-length cape, dress, brooch and a headband with hat. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The flimsy man-made textile cape imparts the essential character of the set and features a single ply collar that is closed with two small hook and loop tabs. The front opening has raw unfinished edges, and the hem bottom has jagged cut, raw edges. The cape is constructed with long, loose straight stitching.
