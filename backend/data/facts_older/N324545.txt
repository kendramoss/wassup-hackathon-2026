In your letter dated February 28, 2022, you requested a tariff classification and country of origin ruling.Doxepin Hydrochloride, imported in 10 mg, 25 mg, 50 mg, 75 mg, 100 mg, and 150 mg capsules, is a tricyclic antidepressant. It is indicated for the treatment of psychoneurotic patients with depression and/or anxiety, depression and/or anxiety associated with alcoholism, depression and/or anxiety associated with organic disease and psychotic depressive disorders with associated anxiety including involutional depression and manic-depressive disorders.
