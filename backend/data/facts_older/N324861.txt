In your letter dated March 16, 2022, on behalf of Dr. Reddy’s Laboratories, Incorporated, you requested a tariff classification ruling.Melphalan Hydrochloride for Injection, imported in 50 mg vials, is an alkylating antineoplastic agent. It is used to treat multiple myeloma (a type of cancer of the bone marrow) in people who are unable to take melphalan by mouth. Melphalan Hydrochloride is also used to destroy bone marrow and cancer cells in preparation for a bone marrow transplant in people with multiple myeloma.
