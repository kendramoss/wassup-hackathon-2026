In your letter dated March 25, 2020 you requested a country of origin ruling.The merchandise under consideration is referred to as the Thunderbolt 3 Docking Station (Docking Station) and is identified as Product Number DKRA100. The Docking Station is described as a an enclosure containing a main printed circuit board assembly (PCBA), a solid state drive (SSD), and a cooling fan. On the rear and side of the Docking Station there are a number of connection sockets, such as HDMI, Thunderbolt, Display Port, USB, audio, and RJ45. The Docking Station is used to connect portable automatic data processing machines to a network and various peripherals, such as a monitor, a keyboard, a mouse, etc.In your request, you state the individual electronic components are sourced from various origins, such as China, Taiwan, and Malaysia. The SSD is said to be produced in Taiwan, while the fan and the top/bottom enclosure subassemblies are produced in China. In Taiwan, you describe the PCBA as being manufactured by surface mounting approximately 1,100 individual electronic components such as integrated circuits, resistors, capacitors, diodes, MOSFETs, and more, to produce a PCBA main board. Once the PCBA is produced in Taiwan, it is shipped to China for assembly. In China, the PCBA is inserted into the enclosure base along with the cooling fan and the SSD, the assembly is secured with screws, loaded with firmware, and tested prior to packing with a Chinese power adapter and Thunderbolt 3 cable.
