In your letter dated July 8, 2020, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of you client, Walgreens Boots Alliance, Inc. Images were provided in lieu of samples. There are two products under consideration in your request. The first is described in your submission as “Two Tone Ceramic Greenery Purple 4.25[inches] x 4.25[inches] x 7.25[inches],” Nature’s Mark item number CS2475N-PP and Walgreens Item Code (WIC) number 597850 (“Two Tone Ceramic Greenery Purple”). The second is listed as “Two Tone Ceramic Greenery Yellow 4.25[inches] x 4.25[inches] x 7.25[inches],” Nature’s Mark item number CS2475N-YL and Walgreens Item Code (WIC) number 597854 (“Two Tone Ceramic Greenery Yellow”). These two arrangements are near identical, except for coloration, and both consist of multiple artificial succulents, made of plastic, which are inserted and glued into a polystyrene foam base. The arrangements along with small stones are each housed in a ceramic pot. “Two Tone Ceramic Greenery Purple” features purple flowers and a purple stripe at the base of the white pot. “Two Tone Ceramic Greenery Yellow” features yellow flowers and a yellow stripe at the base of the white pot. They are designed for indoor decoration. The Harmonized Commodity Description and Coding System Explanatory Notes (ENs) constitute the official interpretation of the Harmonized System at the international level. While not legally binding nor dispositive, the ENs provide a commentary on the scope of each heading of the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS) and are generally indicative of the proper interpretation of these headings. As per the ENs, heading 6702 covers “[a]rtificial flowers, foliage and fruit in forms resembling the natural products, made by assembling various parts (by binding, glueing, assembling by fitting into one another or similar methods).” Per your correspondence, you stated that these succulents are created by gluing or binding together. As these artificial arrangements would meet the requirements set forth by this heading, specifically it is assembled from various parts by binding or gluing, and is not subject to any of the listed exclusions, classification within heading 6702 would be considered appropriate.
