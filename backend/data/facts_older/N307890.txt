In your letter dated November 25, 2019, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Cholestyramine for Oral Suspension USP Powder.Cholestyramine Oral Suspension USP Powder is a medicinal preparation containing Cholestyramine Resin, an antihyperlipidemic, as the active ingredient. It is indicated as adjunctive therapy to diet for the reduction of elevated serum cholesterol in patients with primary hypercholesterolemia (elevated low density lipoprotein [LDL] cholesterol) who do not respond adequately to diet. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Cholestyramine Resin, is manufactured in the United Kingdom and that the finished oral suspension powder in dosage form is made in India. In India, the Cholestyramine Resin will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final oral suspension powder in dosage form.
