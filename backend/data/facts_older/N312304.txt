The garden stand will be imported with all hardware included for assembly.The Explanatory Notes (ENs) to the HTSUS constitute the official interpretation of the tariff at the international level. The ENs to Chapter 94, heading 9403, under the category of [Private dwellings, hotels, etc.,] identify plant stands as falling under the list of exemplars for other furniture classified in
