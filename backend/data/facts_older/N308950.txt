In your letter dated January 14, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Ursodiol Tablets.Ursodiol, imported in 250 mg and 500 mg tablets, is a naturally occurring bile acid. It is indicated for the treatment of patients with primary biliary cirrhosis (PBC).You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Ursodiol, is manufactured in Italy and that the finished tablets in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Ursodiol will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final tablets in dosage form.
