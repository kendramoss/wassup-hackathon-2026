In your letter dated March 11, 2021, you requested a country of origin ruling. The item concerned is referred to an 802.11 AX Ethernet Gateway (model # ASL-84440L) which is a high performance 4x4 Wi-Fi 6 router/gateway (802.11ax). The ASL-84440L provides wired and wireless Internet access. This device undergoes manufacturing processes in two different countries, Taiwan, and China. The ASL-84440L contains two printed circuit board assemblies (PCBA). Both PCBAs are produced within China from a variety of electrical elements/components made in various countries. Within China the printed circuit board assemblies are manufactured using surface mount technology (SMT) and pick and place machines. During this process electrical elements (i.e. diodes, transistors, capacitors, etc.) are placed on a printed circuit board and soldered into the circuitry. Next the PCBAs are tested, packaged, and shipped to Taiwan for the final assembly process.In Taiwan, the PCBAs are assembled within a housing and connected to antennas, brackets, etc. This process involves simple hand assembly. Finally, the finished item is tested.
