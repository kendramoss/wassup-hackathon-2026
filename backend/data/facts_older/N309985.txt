In your letter dated February 20, 2020, you requested a tariff classification ruling.The submitted sample is a men’s size costume identified as Dark Executioner, item # CM11135, which consists of an upper body pullover garment, a hood, a pair of fingerless gloves, a belt with a metal buckle, a pair of boot covers and a mask. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The flimsy upper body garment is made of man-made knit textile fabric and imparts the essential character of the set. The flimsy upper body pullover garment features large, visible, loose overlock stitching on the neck. The sleeve ends are raw and unfinished. A plastic chain and skull medallion are attached to the pullover by small hook and loop tabs in the front. The bottom of the garment features a raw unfinished edge.You state in your letter that the costume will also be offered in child size as item # CB11135 and in men’s plus size as item # CP11135. All three costumes are identical except for size.
