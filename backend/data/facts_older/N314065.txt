In your letter dated August 19, 2020, you requested a tariff classification ruling for a Wraptor Claw. Photographs of the subject articles in use and the retail packaging were submitted for our review.The article under consideration is identified as the Wraptor Claw. The Wraptor Claw is described as a lifting device to help the user with carrying heavy items which include drywall, buckets, plywood, propane tanks, gas cans, cement boards, construction material and farming material. You stated that “The product attaches to your wrist and has metal bars that protrude out to help the user pick up the objects. The part of the product that wraps around your wrist is made of nylon and the metal bars are made of carbon steel.” The Wraptor Claw consists of a nylon wrist band/strap and a steel bar/handle. The nylon and steel components are classified in different headings.
