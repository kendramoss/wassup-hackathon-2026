In your letter dated June 18, 2020, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of your client, California Costume, Inc. You submitted a sample identified as the child size Amelia Kit costume, item number PC3020-140, which consists of a textile jacket, scarf and googles. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The flimsy long-sleeved jacket imparts the essential character and features single straight stitched seams, large, visible, loose overlock stitches at the neck and raw, unfinished edges on the sleeves. The front opening is closed with small hook and loop patches.
