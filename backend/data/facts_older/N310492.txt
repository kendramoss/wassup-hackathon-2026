In your letter dated March 7, 2020, you requested a tariff classification ruling. An instructions manual was provided with your request.The item under consideration has been identified as a Kari-Tek High Lift Easy Load Roof Rack (ELRR), which you refer to as a kayak loader. The ELRR is imported disassembled in a box as a kit and is sold to the ultimate purchaser in the United States, who assembles the system on their private vehicle. The kit consists of handles, tubes, pins, rails, and etc. for easy installation on the vehicle.
