In your letter dated May 28, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Desonide Cream 0.05%.Desonide Cream 0.05% is a topical corticosteroid anti-inflammatory agent. It is indicated for the treatment of a variety of skin conditions. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Desonide, is manufactured in Italy and that the finished Desonide Cream in dosage is made in India. In India, the Desonide will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final cream in dosage form.
