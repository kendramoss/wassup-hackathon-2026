In your letter dated June 18, 2020, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of your client, California Costumes, Inc. You submitted a sample of child size costume identified as Colonial Village Girl Kit, item number PC3020-142, which consists of a long dress, a cap and shawl. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The flimsy pullover dress imparts the essential character and features single stitched seams and raw, unfinished edges at the neck, sleeves and hemline. The short sleeves and waist have narrow exposed elastic attached by one row of large loose single needle stitches.
