In your letter dated June 19, 2019, you requested a tariff classification ruling.The submitted sample, style number CS20-0093, child size Halloween Shower Poof Costume, also referred to as a bath pouf, consists of a dress and a hat. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The 100% polyester knitted fabric and knitted mesh dress imparts the essential character of the set, and features visible, loose overlock stitching at the neck and armholes, straight single stitching at the seams, a hook and loop tab closure with visible, loose overlock stitching at the closure edge.
