In your letter dated March 13, 2020, you requested a tariff classification ruling.You submitted an adult size sample of a costume identified as the Women’s Under Wraps Mummy Costume, item number FUN6180AD, which consists of a long-sleeved jumpsuit and head wrap. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The flimsy jumpsuit imparts the essential character and features large, loose, visible overlock stitching at the neck, sleeve ends, leg openings and large back opening. The large back opening is closed by four small hook and loop tabs. The costume is also offered in child sizes under item number FUN6180CH. The construction of both costumes are exactly the same except for size.
