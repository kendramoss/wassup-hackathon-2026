In your letter dated October 17, 2019, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Bumetanide Tablets.Bumetanide, imported in 0.5 mg, 1 mg and 2 mg tablets, is a diuretic. It is indicated for the treatment of edema associated with congestive heart failure, hepatic and renal diseases, including the nephrotic syndrome.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Bumetanide, is manufactured in Italy and that the finished tablets in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Bumetanide is mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final tablets in dosage form.
