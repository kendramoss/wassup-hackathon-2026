In your letter dated May 31, 2021, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Stanley Electric Sales of America, Inc.The merchandise under consideration is described as an LCD module, which is comprised of a glass sandwich injected with liquid crystals, polarizer, diffuser, and flexible printed circuit (FPC) connector. No size dimensions are defined for the finished module. The subject module is meant to be used as a display device after inclusion within a variety of end units. In your request, you state that the manufacturing process begins in China where strips of glass are cut to size and placed into fixtures. The liquid crystals are then injected between the two strips before the unit is sealed and cut into the final desired size. This glass segment is then sent to Thailand for final processing. In Thailand, the segment is cleaned and a polarizer film is added to the front and back. The FPC connector is then adhered to the back before the finished unit is tested for function and packaged to be shipped to the U.S. The “country of origin” is defined in
