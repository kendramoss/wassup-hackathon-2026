In your letter dated July 22, 2021, filed on behalf of Dr. Reddy’s Laboratories, Inc., you requested a tariff classification ruling.Chlordiazepoxide Hydrochloride and Clidinium Bromide Capsules is a medicinal preparation containing a fixed combination of Chlordiazepoxide hydrochloride (5 mg), a benzodiazepine, and Clidinium bromide (2.5 mg), an anticholinergic, as the active ingredients. Chlordiazepoxide Hydrochloride and Clidinium Bromide Capsules are indicated to control emotional and somatic factors in gastrointestinal disorders. It may also be used as adjunctive therapy in the treatment of peptic ulcer and in the treatment of the irritable bowel syndrome (irritable colon, spastic colon, mucous colitis) and acute enterocolitis.
