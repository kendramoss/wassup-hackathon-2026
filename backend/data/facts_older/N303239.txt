In your letter dated February 28, 2019, you requested a tariff classification, country of origin, marking, and application of a trade program or trade agreement ruling.A product catalog and eleven samples of various sealed wrapped food products accompanied your inquiry. Three samples were identified, examined and discarded. The three samples were cassava flour, dried fish, and dried mopani worms. Nine samples are being returned to you because there were no product descriptions on them, and they did not match any of the exhibits in the catalog.
