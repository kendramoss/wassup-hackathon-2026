In your letter dated July 26, 2020, you requested a tariff classification and country of origin ruling. You have provided a detailed description and photographs of all sides of a sandal. Additional manufacturing and country of origin information was provided via email.The photographs of the “Rhamani sport sandal” show a unisex, open-toe/open-heel, below the ankle, sandal. The upper consists of two separate straps made of woven polyester textile webbing material. The removable straps are laced through the holes and channels of the molded EVA rubber/plastic outer sole unit. They are secured to the foot via plastic connector buckles and a hook and loop closure located on the lateral side of the ankle. You provided an F.O.B. value of $15.71 per pair.
