In your letter, dated August 14, 2019, you requested a ruling on tariff classification, marking, country of origin, and trade program or trade agreement eligibility. A detailed description and photos of a journal were provided for our review.The product under consideration is a bound journal containing 100 lined paper pages. The journal measures approximately 4 inches by 8 inches. The covers have a 1 mm thick veneer of treated and varnished bamboo wood applied. A strip of what appears to be plastic sheeting (faux leather) forms the spine that connects the front and back covers. An attached elastic loop functions as a page marker or closure.You inquire whether the bamboo-veneered covers pose a conflict to classification of the journals as notebooks of heading 4820, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS). The Harmonized Commodity Description and Coding System Explanatory Notes (ENs) constitute the official interpretation of the Harmonized System. While not legally binding nor dispositive, the ENs provide a commentary on the scope of each heading of the and are generally indicative of the proper interpretation of these headings.
