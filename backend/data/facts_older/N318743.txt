You state that the subject item is intended to be used for electrical wiring within automobiles, boats, buses and other motor-driven equipment. In your request, you state that the copper conductor wire is manufactured in China and shipped to Vietnam for further processing. In Vietnam, the copper conductor is stranded and insulated through the extrusion of PVC particles.
