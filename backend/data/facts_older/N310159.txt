In your letter dated February 26, 2020, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of your client, Orchard Yarn. Your letter was accompanied by a sample. The sample will be returned, as requested.Article# 532, “I Wanna Make A Blankie,” is a 100 percent polyester yarn presented in a cake weighing 800 grams. The yarn is constructed from staple fibers inserted between a twisted core yarn to form a yarn with a pile surface, creating the chenille effect. The yarn is for retail sale and is intended for hand knitting. The country of origin is China.
