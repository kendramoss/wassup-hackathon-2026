In your letter dated December 12, 2019, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Vardenafil Hydrochloride Tablets.Vardenafil Hydrochloride, imported in 2.5 mg, 5 mg, 10 mg and 20 mg tablets, is indicated for the treatment of erectile dysfunction.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Vardenafil Hydrochloride, is manufactured in Poland and that the finished tablets in dosage form are made in India. In India, Vardenafil Hydrochloride will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final tablets in dosage form.
