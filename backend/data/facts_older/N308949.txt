In your letter dated January 14, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Pyridostigmine Bromide Tablets.Pyridostigmine Bromide, imported in 60 mg tablets, is a cholinesterase inhibitor. It is indicated for the treatment of muscle weakness in patients with myasthenia gravis.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Pyridostigmine Bromide, is manufactured in Italy and that the finished tablets in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Pyridostigmine Bromide will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final tablets in dosage form.
