In your letter dated January 9, 2022, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of your client, California Costume, Inc.You have submitted a sample of a child size costume identified as Dragon Master Ninja, item numbers 3122-032 and SP3122-032, which consists of a hooded pullover top, pull on pants, leg ties, arm ties, mask and a non-garment accessory which consists of a waist sash with attached shoulder straps and blue “tabard.” A medallion was mentioned in the request but was not included with the sample. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The flimsy man-made textile hooded top imparts the essential character of the set and features a deep back opening with small hook and loop tabs for closure, raw, unfinished edges on the sleeves and bottom hem. The front opening of the hood is finished with large, visible, loose, overlock stitching. The top is constructed with long, loose straight stitching.
