In your letter dated February 26, 2020, you requested a tariff classification ruling.The submitted sample is a child size costume identified as Mage Fade In/Out, item number 134902, which consists of a man-made textile long sleeved upper body garment you refer to as a tunic, a pair of pull-on pants, a hooded mask, a waist sash, a satchel, a pair of gloves and a battery powered fade eye mechanism worn behind the mask. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The flimsy pullover upper body garment imparts the essential character of the set and features large, visible, loose overlock stitching at the neck, and sleeve ends as well as raw, jagged cut edges at the bottom of the garment. The large back opening has a raw unfinished edge and is closed with small hook and loop tabs. You state in your letter that the costume will also be offered in men’s size as item number 134904 and in men’s plus size as item number 134905. All three costumes are identical except for size.
