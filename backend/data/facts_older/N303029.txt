In your letter dated February 4, 2019, on behalf of California Costumes, Inc., you requested a tariff classification ruling. You submitted a sample of item # WMT01002, Scarecrow, which is a textile costume that consists of a long-sleeve shirt with straw at the wrists, a waist tie, a hat, a mask and a collar. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The flimsy textile shirt imparts the essential character, and has visible, loose overlock stitching at the neck, jagged, raw edges at the sleeves and hem, a hook and loop tab closure, and visible loose overlock stitching at the closure.
