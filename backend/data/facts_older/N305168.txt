In your letter dated June 25, 2019, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of your client, Schultz Fur Mfg. Ltd., DBA Lambskin Specialties. Two samples were submitted with your letter. The samples will be returned under separate cover, as requested. Item MFC-AP256 is a microfiber knit pile all purpose cleaning cloth comprised of 80 percent polyester and 20 percent polyamide. The square cloth measures approximately 15 x 16 inches and has sewn edges.Item MFS-DP100 is a microfiber knit pile dusting sleeve comprised of 80 percent polyester and 20 percent polyamide. You state that the dusting sleeve is manufactured from item MFC-AP256. It appears, in manufacturing MFS-DP100, that MFC-AP256 is folded in half and the edges of the side opposite the fold are sewn together and the edges of one of the short sides are sewn together leaving one end open to create the sleeve. The dusting sleeve measures approximately 15 x 6 ¾ inches. You indicate in your request that the cloth, item MFC-AP256, is knit, cut and sewn in China and exported to Canada where the sleeves, MFS-DP100, are manufactured. In Canada, MFC-AP256 is folded, cut (trimmed) and sewn to create the sleeve. In your letter, you requested a ruling on the country of origin of the dusting sleeve, MFS-DP100. However, in order to conduct a country of origin analysis, the classification of both articles must be determined.
