In your letter dated February 18, 2020, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of your client, California Costume, Inc.You have submitted a sample of a costume identified as the Trash Panda costume, item numbers 3120-089 and SP3120-089, which consists of an upper body garment that simulates a barrel when worn, and a mask. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The flimsy upper body garment imparts the essential character, and features large visible, loose overlock stitching at the neck. The sleeve ends and large back opening have raw, unfinished edges with hanging threads. The large back opening closes with small hook and loop tabs. The upper body garment is constructed with long, loose, single needle straight stitched seams.
