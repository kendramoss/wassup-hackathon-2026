This letter replaces ruling letter N316410, dated December 23, 2020, which contained two typographical errors. Style 959655 Children’s Trekking Sandal/Navy/Blue/Lime sandal should have been correctly identified as 96165 Children’s Trekking Sandal/Navy/Blue/Lime. Style 96166 Children’s Trekking Sandal/Grey/Rainbow/Multi sandal was inadvertently associated with two Harmonized Tariff Schedule numbers. A complete and corrected ruling follows. In your electronic submission dated December 10, 2020, you requested a tariff classification ruling on three styles of footwear. You have submitted photographs, a ruling request letter, samples of three styles, and copies of laboratory reports.Style # 95962 Ladies Block Heel/Brown is a woman’s, open toe/closed heel, casual shoe. The upper consists of an approximately 1 ½ inch wide strap over the toes, and a separate piece that covers the back of the shoe. A strap sewn to the center and extending over the vamp, is attached to the piece covering the back of the shoe and secured on the lateral side with a buckle closure. The external surface area of the upper consists of 100 percent rubber/plastics excluding accessories or reinforcements. The shoe has a separately attached, two inch tall wedge shaped heel. The outer sole is comprised of 100 percent rubber/plastics. The shoe does not incorporate a metal toe cap, a foxing or foxing-like band, and is not considered protective. The F.O.B. value is $5.43 per pair.
