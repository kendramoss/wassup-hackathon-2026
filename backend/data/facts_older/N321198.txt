In your letter dated August 24, 2021, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of your client, California Costume, Inc.You have submitted a costume identified as the adult size, Funhouse Freak, item number SP5121-128 which consists of a jumpsuit, a separate collar and a mask. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The flimsy man-made textile jumpsuit imparts the essential character of the set. The neck of the jumpsuit has large, visible, loose overlock stitching and a deep back opening with raw, unfinished edges closed by a single tab of hook and loop fastener. The long sleeves and leg openings have raw, unfinished edges and thin exposed elastic. The jumpsuit is constructed entirely with long loose straight stitching.
