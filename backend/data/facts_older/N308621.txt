This replaces Ruling Number N308127 which inadvertently stated the incorrect country of origin. A complete corrected ruling follows.In your letter dated November 26, 2019, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of your client, Balenciaga America, Inc. Descriptive literature and samples identified as Balenciaga’s Speed Footwear Style numbers 530349 W05G0 1000 and 560235 W1HP0 1000, were submitted to our office for review. The samples will be returned at your request. Via email correspondence you provided additional information for clarification.Balenciaga’s Speed Footwear Style numbers 530349 W05G0 1000 and 560235 W1HP0 1000 are closed toe/closed heel, above-the-ankle, slip-on footwear with outer soles of rubber or plastic materials. The sock-type uppers are composed of knitted textile materials. Both styles feature foxing-like bands applied to and molded at the soles and overlapping the uppers ¼ of inch or more. In your letter you state the foxing-like bands cover 73 percent of the circumference of the shoes. Style #530349 W05G0 1000 has an F.O.B. value of $193 per pair. Style # 560235 W1HP0 1000 has non-functional decorative laces and an F.O.B. value of more than $15/pair.
