In your letter dated November 25, 2019, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Nadolol Tablets. Nadolol, imported in 20 mg, 40 mg and 80 mg tablets, is a non-selective beta blocker indicated for the long-term management of patients with angina pectoris. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Nadolol, is manufactured in Finland and that the finished tablets in dosage form are made in India. In India, Nadolol will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final tablets in dosage form.
