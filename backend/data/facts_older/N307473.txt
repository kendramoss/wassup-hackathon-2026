In your letter dated November 5, 2019, you requested a country of origin marking ruling on behalf of your client, Voyetra Turtle Beach Inc. The product that is the subject of this country of origin ruling request is a gaming headset identified as: Recon 70. Voyetra Turtle Beach Inc. is planning to manufacture the gaming headsets within Vietnam. The manufacturing process that occurs within Vietnam is as follows:Printed circuit boards (PCB) which are manufactured in Vietnam are populated with electrical components (i.e. capacitors, resistors, switches, etc.) from China. A process called Surface Mount Technology (SMT) is used to place and connect the components. The resulting printed circuit board assembly (PCBA) forms the electrical control module of the headset. Bulk rolls of electrical cable, plastic resin and connectors from China, are manufactured into cable assemblies which are then electrically connected to the headset. The cable assembly is used to connect the headset to an audio source.Plastic resin from China is used to create various components (housing, ear cups, microphone boom, etc.) using a hot resin injection press manufacturing process.Speakers, microphones, headbands, ear cushions, etc., from China and Vietnam are then assembled and/or electrically connected to the assembly.Finally the headset is tested and packaged.A detailed explanation of all manufacturing and assembly steps has been provided.
