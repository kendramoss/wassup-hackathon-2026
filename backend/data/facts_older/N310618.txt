In your letter dated February 28, 2020, on behalf of Medline Industries, Inc., you requested a ruling on the country of origin and application of Section 301 additional duties for digital stick thermometers.Under consideration are three digital stick thermometers, which are each reusable, battery-powered, dual scale (Fahrenheit and Celsius), and are imported with a one-piece snap case. Model MDS9655 is a sixty-second oral digital stick thermometer, model MDS9950 is a thirty-second oral digital stick thermometer, and model MDS9952 is a thirty-second rectal digital stick thermometer. Each thermometer consists of a sensor, battery, plastic housing, LCD display, and an integrated circuit assembly consisting of electronic (e.g., a printed circuit board) and electromechanical discrete components. In your request, you provided a description of the manufacturing process for the digital stick thermometers, which is the same for all three models. The design research and product development for the finished digital thermometers, including the temperature probe, the integrated circuit and printed circuit board, as well as the creation of the custom software for later incorporation into the integrated circuit assembly, is performed in Taiwan. The Taiwanese-produced sensors/probes and software are sent to China where they are assembled with additional Chinese components including the plastic housing, LCD display, integrated circuit assembly and battery. Products are then inspected, tested, and packaged for retail sale. With regard to your request for the appropriate country of origin of the digital thermometers,
