In your letter dated May 12, 2021, on behalf of your client, Crystal Apparel Ltd., you requested a ruling on the country of origin of four styles of men’s and women’s shorts. Your samples will be returned.Style CAL2100001 is a pair of men’s shorts constructed from 98% cotton and 2% spandex woven denim fabric. The shorts feature a flat waistband with five belt loops, a zippered fly front opening with a metal button closure on the waistband, two front inset pockets, an inner coin pocket within the right front pocket, two rear patch pockets, and a rear yoke. Style CAL2100002 is a pair of women’s shorts constructed from 98% cotton and 2% spandex woven denim fabric. The shorts feature a flat waistband with five belt loops, a zippered fly front opening with a metal button closure on the waistband, two front inset pockets, an inner coin pocket within the right front pocket, two rear patch pockets, and a rear yoke. Style CAL2100003 is a pair of men’s shorts constructed from 100% cotton woven denim fabric. The shorts feature a flat waistband with five belt loops, a fly front opening secured with five button closures, two front inset pockets, an inner coin pocket within the right front pocket, two rear patch pockets, and a rear yoke. Style CAL2100004 is a pair of women’s shorts constructed from 100% cotton woven denim fabric. The shorts feature a flat waistband with five belt loops, a fly front opening secured with five button closures, two front inset pockets, an inner coin pocket within the right front pocket, two rear patch pockets, and a rear yoke. The manufacturing operations for all garments are as follows:China:Fabric is cut into component partsWaistband is cutPockets are sewn to the panelsZipper is attached to the front panels of styles CAL2100001 and CAL2100002; button fly is attached to the front panels of styles CAL2100003 and CAL2100004Yoke is attached to the top of both back panelsBack panels are joined togetherBelt loops are madeMacau:Front rise is sewnSide seams are sewnInseam is sewnChina: Waistband is attachedBelt loops are cut to size and attachedButtonhole(s) are created and button(s) and rivets are attachedUpper portion of side seams are topstitchedLeg openings are hemmedLabels are attachedGarment is bartacked, washed, ironed, packed, and inspected
