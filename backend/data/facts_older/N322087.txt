RE: The tariff classification, marking and status under the African Growth and Opportunity Act (AGOA) of Distilled Spirits from TogoDear Mr. Klatt:This is in response to your letter dated September 20, 2021, requesting a ruling on the classification, marking and eligibility of the AGOA on Distilled Spirits. The subject merchandise is described as distilled spirits made from fermented palm tree sap. The palm tree sap is extracted from palm trees (grown in Togo), fermented with natural yeast, distilled and heated to produce the Distilled Spirits which has an alcohol by volume content of approximately 45 to 50 percent. The finished product will be packaged in bulk form in 1,000 liter plastic totes ready for export to the United States. You state that no other ingredients are added to the distilled palm spirit during distillation, and all manufacturing is done in Togo.Classification:
