In your letter dated August 8, 2019, on behalf of Illoom Balloon USA LLC, you requested a ruling on the country of origin of LED Latex Balloons. The three types of LED latex balloons at issue are identified as Supersize Alien Light Up Balloons, Marble Light Up Balloons and regular Light Up Balloons. Each variation consists of a latex balloon and an LED light component. As detailed in your submission, the LED balloons can be inflated and used as a regular balloons but also contain a small plastic pull tab which goes through the neck of each balloon. Prior to inflation of the balloon, the user pulls the insulating tab out, thereby activating a small LED light that will temporarily illuminate the balloon for a few hours. The first product under consideration is referred to as the Supersize Alien Light Up Balloon. The product consists of a 24 inch latex balloon depicting the shape of an alien, inserted with a small LED light that is designed to illuminate the balloon once the plastic tab attached to the LED light is pulled by the user. You indicate in your request that the latex balloon component of the product is sourced from Italy, and the LED light component of the product is sourced from China. The second product at issue is identified as Marble Light Up Balloons. The product consists of multicolored latex balloons with a marbled pattern, inserted with a small LED light that is designed to illuminate the balloon once the plastic tab attached to the LED light is pulled by the user. As noted in your request, the dipping process for the formation of the marbled latex balloons requires specialist knowledge that is limited to fewer manufacturers worldwide. You indicate in your request that the latex balloon component of the product is sourced from Taiwan, and the LED light component of the product is sourced from China.The third product under consideration is referred to as Light Up Balloons. The product consists of 12 inch latex balloons in various colors, also inserted with a small LED light that is designed to illuminate the balloon once the plastic tab attached to the LED light is pulled by the user. You indicate in your request that the latex balloon component of the product is sourced from Malaysia, and the LED light component of the product is sourced from China.As noted in your submission, prior to importing the subject LED balloons into the United States, one of the company’s affiliates located in China, performs certain simple assembly operations. Such operations include placing the LED light, pull tab and small battery into a light housing unit, sealing and inserting the same into the aforementioned latex balloons. As detailed in your request, these steps are performed and completed in a matter of seconds by low-skilled workers. It is further noted that the packaging operations are performed by the same Chinese affiliate prior to importation.Initially, we note that the classification of Illooms LED Balloons has been previously established, please see New York ruling N173614, dated July 28, 2011.With regard to your request for the appropriate country of origin of the three categories of Light Up Balloons,
