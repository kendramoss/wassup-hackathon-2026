In your letter dated November 1, 2019, on behalf of LF Centennial Pte. Ltd. of Hong Kong, you requested a tariff classification ruling.The submitted sample, item # 10309, is a toddler Peppa Pig Red Dress with Hood costume that consists of a dress and a detachable hood. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The flimsy polyester dress imparts the essential character, and has visible, loose overlock stitching at the neck and armholes, and raw net at the hem. The dress also has single straight stitch seams and a hook and loop tab closure.You also submitted a sample of item # 10310, which is a toddler Peppa Pig Skater Princess costume that consists of a dress, a hood, and a pair of wings. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The flimsy polyester dress imparts the essential character, and has visible, loose overlock stitching at the neck, armholes, hem and closure edge. The dress also has a single straight stitch waist and a hook and loop tab closure.
