In your letter dated March 23, 2020 you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Walgreens Co.The merchandise under consideration is identified as the USB-A and USB-C Car Charger, Model HKDP3342-18, which consists of a retail package containing a 12 V vehicle power adapter plug having dual USB output sockets. The function of the power adapter is to convert vehicle 12 V to the 5 V and supply the voltage to the USB-A and USB-C output sockets. Based on the information supplied, all of the component materials that make up the Car Charger are sourced from China and the assembly of the device occurs in Vietnam. In your request, you state that the subject power adapter is assembled in Vietnam by soldering 31 electrical components, such as resistors, capacitors, integrated circuits, fuses, diodes, and LEDs, onto a bare printed circuit board. The female USB Type-A and USB Type-C connectors are then soldered onto the assembly and encapsulated in a plastic case. You indicate that testing and quality control procedures are conducted on the parts as well as the finished product throughout the various stages of the assembly process. With regard to the origin of the Car Charger, the marking statute, section 304, Tariff Act of 1930, as amended (
