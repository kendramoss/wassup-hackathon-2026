In your letter dated October 31, 2019, on behalf of your client, Theory LLC, you requested a ruling on the country of origin of a men’s pullover. Your sample will be retained in our office for training purposes.Style K0286705 “Goris PL” is a men’s pullover constructed from knit fabric with a stated fiber content of 61% viscose (rayon), 37% polyester, and 2% other fibers. The fabric measures 22 stitches per two centimeters counted in the horizontal direction. Style K0286705 features a rib knit spread collar, a partial front opening with two left-over-right button closures, short sleeves with self-finished ends, and a rib knit bottom band that is formed by a change in knit pattern, and which provides tightening at the bottom of the garment.The manufacturing operations for Style K0286705 are as follows:All of the garment’s components (the front and back panels, the sleeves, the collar, and the neckline placket trim) are knit to shape on flat-knitting machines in Hong Kong and exported to China. In China, all components are assembled by looping and linking, buttonholes are formed on the placket, buttons are sewn to the placket, and the finished garment is washed and ironed.
