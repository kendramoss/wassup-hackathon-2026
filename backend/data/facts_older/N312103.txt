In your letter dated May 27, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Doxycycline Injection.Doxycycline Injection, imported in 100 mg vials, is a tetracycline antibiotic. It is used to treat or prevent infections that are proven or strongly suspected to be caused by bacteria.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Doxycycline Hyclate, is manufactured in Macau and that the finished Doxycycline Injection in dosage form is made in India. In India, the Doxycycline Hyclate will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final injection in dosage form.
