In your letter dated July 23, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling. The item under consideration is a 2500 mAh lithium-ion power bank, model 9W-258A. The power bank includes lithium polymer battery cells, a plastic housing, a printed circuit board assembly (PCBA), a USB-A female connector output, a micro-USB connector input, and a LED capacity indicator which displays the status during charging. The power bank is designed to charge mobile phones using the output port, while the input port is used to charge the power bank. You state the power bank is manufactured in the Philippines from components sourced from China and the Philippines.
