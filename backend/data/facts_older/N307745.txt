In your letter dated November 19, 2019, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of your client, Jungbunzlauer Inc.The first item is Magnesium Lactate Dihydrate (CAS-No 18917-93-6). You state that it is a new product that Jungbunzlauer Inc. has prepared for market sale. The Magnesium Lactate Dihydrate is produced by the neutralization of Lactic Acid and is mainly used as a mineral source in food, food supplements, foods for nutritional purposes, and pharmaceutical preparation. It comes in a powder form, has a neutral taste and is practically odourless. The second item is Zinc Lactate Dihydrate (CAS-No 16039-53-5). It is also stated to be a new product that Jungbunzlauer Inc. has prepared for market sale. The Zinc Lactate Dihydrate is produced by the neutralization of Lactic Acid with a high purity zinc source and subsequent crystallization and is mainly used in dental care products such as toothpaste or mouthwash. It comes in a granular or powder form and is soluble in water. Both products as indicated have Austria as their country of origin.You comment that based on your review of the ENs to Chapter 29 that it is your opinion that both products would be classified as salts of Lactic Acid under HTSUS subheading 2918.11.We agree.
