In your letter submitted April 20, 2021, you requested a country of origin ruling on Item# F4012, “Love is Dead” card game. The “Love Is Dead” card game consists of (65) Action Cards, (12) End of Game Cards, (5) Reference Cards, (22) Transparent Boo Cards (1) sheet of 6 Boo stickers and instructions for game play. The card game is designed for children 13 years of age and older.Your submission indicates that, except for the 22 transparent cards and the sticker sheet containing 6 stickers, the above-described game is manufactured in Vietnam and packaged there for retail sale.
