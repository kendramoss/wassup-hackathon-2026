In your letter dated March 6, 2020, on behalf of Encube Ethicals Private Ltd., you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Desonide Ointment.Desonide Ointment 0.05%, imported in 15 gram and 60 gram tubes, is a topical corticosteroid anti-inflammatory agent. It is indicated for the relief of the inflammatory and pruritic manifestations of corticosteroid responsive dermatoses. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Desonide, is manufactured in Italy and that the finished ointment in dosage form is made in India. In India, the Desonide will be mixed with white petrolatum to produce the final ointment in dosage form.
