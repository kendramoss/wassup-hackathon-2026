In your letter dated April 5, 2019, you requested a tariff classification ruling.You submitted a sample of item number 135544, Big Top – Tiny Terror Adult Costume, which consists of a top, pants with attached circus stand, a hat and a mask. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The flimsy 100% polyester woven top imparts the essential character. The single layer top is stained with fake blood, and has visible, loose overlock stitching at the collar, hem, wrists, seams and closure. It also has a thin elastic at the wrists and a hook and loop tab closure.
