In correspondence dated November 25, 2019, on behalf of your client, Theory LLC, you requested a ruling on the country of origin of a men’s pullover. Your sample will not be returned.Style K0284707 “Greeson HD” is a men’s pullover constructed from 100% cotton knit fabric that measures 18 stitches per two centimeters counted in the horizontal direction. Style K0284707 features a self-fabric hood with a knit drawstring, long sleeves with rib knit cuffs, and a rib knit bottom band.The manufacturing operations for Style K0284707 are as follows:The front and back panels, the sleeves, the cuffs, the bottom band, the hood panels, the hood trim, and the neckline trim are knit to shape on flat-knitting machines in Hong Kong and exported to China. The drawstring is also knit in Hong Kong and exported to China. In China, all components, with the exception of the drawstring, are assembled by looping and linking; the drawstring is threaded through the hood; and the finished garment is washed and ironed.
