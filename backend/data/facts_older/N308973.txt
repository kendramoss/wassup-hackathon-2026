In your letter dated November 26, 2019, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of your client, Kibun Foods Inc., WA.An ingredients breakdown, a manufacturing flowchart, a narrative description of the manufacturing process and a sample of the labeled product accompanied your inquiry. The Super Bean Mix is said to contain approximately 50 percent water, 18 percent chickpeas, 15 percent soybeans, 9 percent kidney beans, 9 percent black beans, and a trace amount of sea salt and rice vinegar. The beans are grown in the United States where they are harvested, shelled and shipped to Japan raw and separate. In Japan they are mixed, cleaned, soaked in water with salt and vinegar, boiled and steamed. After repackaging, the product is imported for retail sale in boxes containing six 5-ounce pouches, net packed, to their final destination, the United States.
