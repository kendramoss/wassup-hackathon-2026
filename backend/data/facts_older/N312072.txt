In your letter dated May 26, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Eletriptan Hydrobromide Tablets.Eletriptan Hydrobromide, imported in 20 mg and 40 mg tablets, is in a class of medications called selective serotonin receptor agonists. It is indicated for the acute treatment of migraine with or without aura in adults. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Eletriptan Hydrobromide, is manufactured in Italy and that the finished Eletriptan Hydrobromide Tablets in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Eletriptan Hydrobromide will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final tablets in dosage form.
