In your letter dated May 25, 2022, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Ofloxacin Otic Solution.Ofloxacin Otic solution 0.3%, imported in 5 mL and 10 mL, is an antibacterial drug. It is indicated for the treatment of infections caused by susceptible isolates of the designated microorganisms in the specific conditions listed below:Otitis Externa in adults and pediatric patients, 6 months and older, due to Escherichia coli, Pseudomonas aeruginosa and Staphylococcus aureus.Chronic Suppurative Otitis Media in patients 12 years and older with perforated tympanic membranes due to Proteus mirabilis, Pseudomonas aeruginosa and Staphylococcus aureus.Acute Otitis Media in pediatric patients one year and older with tympanostomy tubes due to Haemophilus influenzae, Moraxella catarrhalis, Pseudomonas aeruginosa, Staphylococcus aureus and Streptococcus pneumoniae.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Ofloxacin is manufactured in Spain and that the finished solution in dosage form is made in India. In India, the Ofloxacin will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final solution in dosage form.
