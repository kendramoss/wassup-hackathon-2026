In your letter dated February 19, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Benzonatate Capsules in dosage form.Benzonatate, imported in 100 mg and 200 mg capsules, is a non-narcotic antitussive agent. It is indicated for the symptomatic relief of cough. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Benzonatate, is manufactured in Switzerland and that the finished capsules in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Benzonatate will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final capsules in dosage form.
