In your letter dated December 15, 2020 you requested a tariff classification ruling.The first item under consideration is described as “Castor complete”. It is a luggage wheel assembly that allows 360-degree swivel movement. The “Castor complete” includes a corner (wheel housing) with connector (stem) and castor (wheel). It is available in two styles described in your request as “Wheel Complete PC 50 Black” (Part # 13493), and “065 Complete Essential Black” (Part # 13006). The “Castor complete” is comprised of a corner made of a polycarbonate + polybutylene terephthalate plastic with a stainless steel or aluminum stem that is molded into the corner. It also includes a castor comprised of tires made of polyurethane, an inner wheel made of Polyamide, a shielded metal bearing shaft and a rivet (castor pin) that connects the entire castor together. The “Castor compete” is available with wheels of 50 mm and 65 mm diameter widths. You state this item will be imported from Canada.The second item under consideration is described as “Castor (Wheel) only”. The “Castor (Wheel) only”, is available in two styles: “Castor 50 Black Noise Reduced” (Part # 12176), and “Castor 65 int. Black NVI” (Part # 12174). This item is an individual castor used for luggage repair. The castor is comprised of tires made of polyurethane, an inner wheel made of Polyamide, a shielded metal bearing shaft and a rivet (castor pin) that connects the entire castor together. The castor is available in 50 mm and 65 mm diameter sizes. You state this item will be imported from Canada.The third item under consideration is described as “Accessories (castors only packaged for retail)”, Part # 5150000400. This item is a set of four castor wheels packaged together for retail sale. You state the castors are available in multiple colors and are interchangeable on certain Rimowa suitcases. The castors are comprised of tires made of polyurethane, an inner wheel made of Polyamide, a shielded metal bearing shaft and a rivet (castor pin) that connects the entire castor together. The wheels are available in 50 mm and 65 mm diameter widths. You state in your request that the country of origin for the set of four castor wheels packaged together for retail sale is France.The fourth item under consideration is a wheel housing described as “Castor corner (Wheel housings) with stem, no Castor (Wheel)”, Part # 11363. The corner is made of a polycarbonate and polybutylene terephthalate plastic, and includes a stainless steel or aluminum stem that is molded into the corner. You state the country of origin for the “Castor corner (Wheel housings) with stem, no Castor (Wheel)” is the Czech Republic or Germany.
