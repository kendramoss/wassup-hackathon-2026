In your letter dated September 16, 2021, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of your client, California Costume, Inc.You have submitted a costume identified as the adult size, Funhouse Freak, item number 5121-128 which consists of a jumpsuit, a collar and a mask. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The flimsy man-made textile jumpsuit imparts the essential character of the set. The neck of the jumpsuit has large visible loose overlock stitching and a deep back opening with raw unfinished edges closed with small hook and loop tabs. The sleeves and leg openings feature thin exposed elastic and raw unfinished edges. The jumpsuit is constructed entirely with long loose straight stitching. This item is identical to item number SP5121-128, previously ruled on in ruling number N321198.
