In your electronic submission dated November 20, 2020, you requested a tariff classification ruling on five styles of footwear. You have submitted photographs, a ruling request letter, samples of five styles, and copies of laboratory reports.Style # 95960 Ladies Block Heel/Black is a woman’s, open toe/closed heel, casual shoe that has a separately attached, two inch tall heel. The upper consists of an approximately 1 ½ inch wide strap over the toes, and a piece that covers the heel and the back of the ankle. An approximately ½ inch wide strap wraps around the ankle and is secured on the lateral side with a buckle closure. The external surface area of the upper consists of rubber/plastics. The rubber/plastic outer sole has an overlay of textile material covering 70.95 percent of the external surface area in contact with the ground. The shoe does not incorporate a metal toe cap, a foxing or foxing-like band, and is not considered protective. The F.O.B. value is $5.43 per pair.
