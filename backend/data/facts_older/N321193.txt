In your letter dated August 24, 2021, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of your client, California Costume, Inc.You have submitted a costume identified as the child size, Fangs Out, item numbers 3021-157 and SP3021-157 which consists of a hooded pullover dress, a waist sash with a vampire fang design and pointed wrist cuffs which you refer to as fingerless gloves. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The flimsy man-made textile dress imparts the essential character of the set and features the front neck finished with large visible loose overlock stitching, the hood is attached at the back neck. The dress features long open cape sleeves with raw unfinished edges all around, the armholes are finished with large, visible, loose overlock stitches, the waist has narrow exposed elastic and the hem bottom is raw and unfinished. The dress is constructed with long, loose straight stitching.
