In your letter dated November 8, 2019, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Tamoxifen Citrate Tablets.The subject product, Tamoxifen Citrate, imported in 10 mg and 20 mg tablets, is an anti-estrogenic drug. It is indicated for the treatment of metastatic breast cancer. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Tamoxifen Citrate, is manufactured in Israel and that the finished tablets in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Tamoxifen Citrate will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final tablets in dosage form.
