In your letter dated July 19, 2019 you requested a country of origin ruling.The merchandise under consideration is identified as the AIO Touch Screen (AIO), PN E462193, which is described as a touch screen computing device for use in commercial environments for point of sale machines and similar applications. The AIO consists of a motherboard, a LCD display module, various printed circuit board assemblies (PCBAs) such as a touch controller, two USB controllers, a keypad controller, an antenna, speaker, power supply, camera module and various cables and hardware. The assembly of the AIO involves mounting the motherboard and control PCBAs into the enclosure, attaching the cabling, assembling the enclosure and wireless antenna module, and mounting the LCD module. The AIO then receives its firmware and operating system, is tested, and packaged for shipment. In your request, you indicate that all of the parts that make up the AIO are all sourced from China and the assembly of the AIO is conducted in Taiwan.
