In your letter dated January 9, 2022, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of your client, California Costume, Inc. You have submitted an adult size chicken costume identified as “What the Cluck,” item number 5222-089, which consists of a jumpsuit, shoe covers and a headpiece. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The flimsy man-made textile jumpsuit imparts the essential character of the set. The neck and deep back opening of the jumpsuit feature large, visible, loose overlock stitching and is closed in back with a pair of flimsy string ties. The long sleeves and leg openings have raw, unfinished edges. The jumpsuit is constructed with long, loose, straight stitching.
