On January 31, 2021, you submitted a ruling request on behalf of your client, Osmium-Institut zur Inverkehrbringung und Zertifizierung von Osmium GmbH. Images and descriptive information were provided.The items under consideration are made of 99.99995% osmium created through a crystallization of raw osmium to wafer or cylindrical shape with a thickness between approximately 0.2 to 2.5 mm. They include the following items:Articles R1-R30, “Ring,” are described as small, curved bars. They are comprised of osmium that is crystallized on cylindrical substrates, resulting in a cylindrical, or ring shape. They are 2 mm thick, 15 mm in diameter, and weigh between 1 to 31 grams.Articles W2-W9, “Star Row,” include between 2 to 9 stars. They are comprised of osmium that has been crystallized on flat substrates and cut to shape. They range between 9 mm to 36.5 mm wide, 4.2 to 4.5 mm in height, and weigh between .032 to .926 grams.Articles D1-D9, “Small Round Bars” are described as the geometrical inverse of smalledged bars. They are comprised of osmium that has been crystallized on flat substrates and cut to shape. They range between 1 mm to 10 mm wide and weigh between .002 to .940 grams.Articles S1-S9, “Small Edged Bars” are described as the shape of a square with concave fillets on each of the four sides. They are comprised of osmium that has been crystallized on flat substrates and cut to shape. They range between 1 mm to 10 mm wide and weigh between .004 to .662 grams.Articles T1-T9, “Triangle” are described as the shape of an equilateral triangle. They are comprised of osmium that has been crystallized on flat substrates and cut to shape. They range between 1 mm to 10 mm wide and weigh between .001 to .518 grams.In your ruling request, you suggested a classification of 7110.49.00.50, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS), which provides for “Platinum, unwrought or in semimanufactured forms, or in powder form: Other, Other.” However, these are articles of precious metals (of osmium), and not semi-manufactured per Chapter 71, Additional
