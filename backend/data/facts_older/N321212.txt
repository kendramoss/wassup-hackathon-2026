In your letter dated August 25, 2021, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of your client, California Costume, Inc.You have submitted a costume identified as the adult size, Gold Fever, item number SP5021-155 which depicts a 70’s era glittery disco dancer and consists of a pullover top, pull-on wide leg pants, a waist sash and a head tie. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The flimsy man-made textile pullover top imparts the essential character of the set. The sleeveless pullover top features a single ply high neck collar with a raw unfinished edge, a deep back opening with long ties and raw unfinished edges. The hem bottom has raw unfinished edges. The top is constructed with long loose straight stitches.
