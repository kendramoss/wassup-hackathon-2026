In your letter dated May 13, 2019, on behalf of Creative Apparel Concepts, you requested a tariff classification ruling.You submitted a sample of a “Clown Mask/Tee Costume Combo,” identified as item number F161231 on its packaging. The costume consists of a knitted polyester pullover and a clown mask. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The flimsy pullover imparts the essential character, and has raw edges with hanging threads at the neck, arms and hem. Your sample is being returned as you requested.
