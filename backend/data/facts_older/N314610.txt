In your letter dated September 17, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling. The item concerned is referred to as an Ethernet switch (model # EX78934E-0VB). It is a hardened DIN rail mounted, managed, PoE Ethernet switch equipped with up to 4 - 60 watt PoE ports, up to 8 - 30 watt PoE ports and 4 dual-rate fast & gigabit Ethernet SFP ports. The Ethernet switch is assembled in Taiwan. It consists of a main printed circuit board assembly (PCBA), a PoE PCBA, a Power PCBA and various housing/mounting/electrical components. A bill of materials listing all sub-assemblies and components with their countries of origin has been submitted.The manufacturing process that takes place within Taiwan involves the manufacturing of the printed circuit board assemblies (PCBA) using surface mount technology (SMT) and dual in-line processing (DIP). During this process electrical elements (i.e. diodes, transistors, capacitors, etc.) are placed on a printed circuit board and soldered into the circuitry. When complete each PCBA is tested. A manufacturing narrative and various flow charts, photographs and diagrams explaining the manufacturing process have been submitted. Next, the completed PCBAs and other housing/support/electrical components from various countries of origin, are assembled into the finished device. Finally, firmware is installed into the device, and the finished products are inspected.
