The subject merchandise, identified as a “Beautyrest® Platinum® Luxury Heated Throw,” is an electric blanket that measures 50 x 60 inches. The blanket is constructed from two layers of 100 percent polyester plush pile knit fabric with warming wires sewn in place between the two layers. The wires connect to an electric controller with a power cord that allows the user to adjust the temperature to three settings. All the edges are hemmed.The manufacturing operations for the electric blanket are as follows: Fabric is formed and finished in India.Electrical components are manufactured in China.Fabric is cut, sewn and assembled into a blanket in China.Completed blanket is packaged in China and exported to the United States.
