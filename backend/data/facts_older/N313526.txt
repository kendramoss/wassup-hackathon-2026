In your letter dated July 28, 2020, you requested a country of origin.There are two items at issue with this request. The first is identified by model number BAWG0682 and described as a USB-C to Lightning cable. The cable consists of a 3-foot length of insulated conductor affixed at one end with a USB-C connector and a lightning-style connector at the other. The subject cable is used to connect and charge a variety of mobile devices to host units and charging stations. The second item is identified by model number BAWG0701 and described as a USB-A to Lightning cable. This cable consists of a 3-foot length of insulated conductor affixed at one end with a USB-A connector and a lightning-style connector at the other. This cable is, likewise, used to connect and charge a variety of mobile devices to host units and charging stations. In your request, you state that the manufacturing process for both cables is virtually identical and fully completed in the Philippines. This process begins with bulk wire conductor being extruded, twisted, braided, jacketed, and insulated prior to being cut to the desired length. The ends are then prepared for the addition of the specific connectors, all originating from Taiwan. The connectors are soldered to each end before the cables are tested and packaged for shipment to the United States.The “country of origin” is defined in
