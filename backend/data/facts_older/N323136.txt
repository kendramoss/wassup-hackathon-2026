In your letter dated December 8, 2021, you requested a tariff classification ruling. Your sample will be returned.The submitted sample, Style 70243-012, is a men’s jumpsuit constructed from 65% polyester and 35% cotton woven twill fabric. The garment features a notched collar; a left-over-right front opening extending from the neckline to below the waist, which is secured by six snap closures; a patch pocket on the left chest; short, hemmed, raglan sleeves; an elastic strip stitched to the inner rear waist; and long, hemmed leg openings. You have suggested classification of Style 70243-012 under subheading 6211.39.8010, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS), which provides for men’s jumpsuits of other textile materials. However, the garment is more correctly classified elsewhere in the tariff schedule.
