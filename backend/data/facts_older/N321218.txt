In your letter dated August 25, 2021, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of your client, California Costume, Inc.You submitted a toddler size costume identified as Little Witch Coat, item numbers 2021-119 and SP 2021-119, which consists of a dress, pettiskirt and conical hat. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The flimsy man-made textile dress imparts the essential character of the set. Although the dress exhibits some styling features, it has a full front opening with only three small hook and loop tabs for closure. The front neck features two single ply, faux collar pieces while the back neck is finished with large, visible, loose overlock stitching. The unfinished long sleeves have unraveling edges and the waist features thin exposed elastic attached by a single line of long loose straight stitching.
