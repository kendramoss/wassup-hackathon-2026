In your letter dated January 13, 2020, you requested a tariff classification ruling.The submitted sample is an adult size men’s costume identified as Alexander Hamilton Uniform, item # 136884, which consists of a pullover upper body garment depicting a colonial coat with vest front, pull-on pants, jabot and pair of sleeve cuffs. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The flimsy upper body garment is made of man-made knit textile and imparts the essential character of the set. The flimsy upper body garment features large, visible, loose overlock stitching on the neck, attached vest front and faux lapels. The buttons are non-funtional and glued on, not stitched. The faux lapels and cuffs are folded back and glued, not stitched. The bottom of the garment features a raw unfinished edge.You state in your letter that the costume will also be offered in men’s plus size as item # 136885 and in child size as item # 136882. All three costumes are identical except for size.
