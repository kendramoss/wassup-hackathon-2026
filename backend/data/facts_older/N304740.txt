In your letter dated June 11, 2019, you requested a tariff classification ruling.The product under consideration is seamless alloy steel line pipe. According to your submission, Japanese origin line pipe made to API Specification 5L is exported to Indonesia where it will be internally clad with a 3-5 mm layer of Inconel 625 (nickel alloy material). The pipe has an outside diameter of 6.625 inches (168.28 mm) and a wall thickness of 1.119 inches (28.42 mm). You indicate that the purpose of the cladding (welding overlay) material is to create a corrosion and wear-resistant layer on the inside of the pipe.
