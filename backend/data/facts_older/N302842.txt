In your letter dated January 7, 2019, written on behalf of Recasens USA LLC, you requested a tariff classification ruling on five fabrics. Samples were provided for all fabrics and will be retained for filing and training purposes.Style Recaflex is fabric which has been sandwiched between two layers of material. According to the information provided it will be imported on rolls 60 meters in length and is intended for use in awnings. According to Customs and Border Protection (CBP) laboratory analysis, the sample is a plain woven fabric wholly composed of polyester fibers which has been completely coated on both sides with compact polyvinyl chloride (PVC). The weight of the material is 627.1 g/m2. Style Recacril Marine is a plain weave fabric. According to the information provided it will be imported on rolls 60 meters in length and is intended for use in the marine industry. According to CBP laboratory analysis, the sample is of plain woven construction and composed of acrylic staple yarns of different colors. The weight of the fabric is 292.5 g/m2. The fabric is coated with a plastic material, however, it is not visible to the naked eye. Style Recscreen is a yarn-coated woven fabric. According to the information provided it will be imported on rolls 25 meters in length and is intended for use on solar screens and awnings. According to CBP laboratory analysis, the sample is of other than plain, twill, or satin weave construction. The polyester yarns of the fabric have been coated with a polyvinyl chloride type plastic material. The yarns were coated before the fabric was constructed. Style Recwater is a coated plain woven fabric. According to the information provided it will be imported on rolls 60 meters in length and is intended for use in the marine industry. According to CBP laboratory analysis, the sample is a plain weave fabric of acrylic fibers, coated on one side with a polyvinyl chloride (PVC) type plastic material. The fabric was coated after the weaving process.Style Recsystem is a coated plain woven fabric. According to the information provided it will be imported on rolls 60 meters in length and is intended for use in the marine industry. According to CBP laboratory analysis, the sample is a dyed plain weave fabric composed of staple acrylic yarns and has been coated, covered, impregnated, or laminated with acrylate type plastic material. The weight of the fabric is 327.1 g/m2. The plastic coatings in regards to Recscreen, Recwater and Recsystem are visible to the naked eye.
