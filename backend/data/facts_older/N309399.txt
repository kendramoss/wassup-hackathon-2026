In your letter dated December 20, 2019, on behalf of your client, Theory LLC, you requested a ruling on the country of origin of a men’s pullover. Your sample will be returned.Style J044703R “Lie Vos Hood SL” is a men’s cardigan-style garment constructed from knit fabric with a stated fiber content of 99% cotton and 1% other fibers. The outer surface of the garment’s fabric measures more than nine stitches per two centimeters in the direction the stitches were formed. Style J044703R features a self-fabric hood, a full-front opening with a double-zippered closure, long sleeves with rib knit ends that are formed by a change in knit pattern, vertical pockets below the waist, and a rib knit bottom that is formed by a change in knit pattern. The manufacturing operations for Style J044703R are as follows:The front and back panels, the sleeves, the hood, the pocket bags, the hood trim, the pocket trim, the pocket facing trim, the neck trim, and the front placket trim are knit to shape on flat-knitting machines in Hong Kong and exported to China. The placket facing trim is also knit in Hong Kong and exported to China. In China, the majority of the components are looped and linked to form the finished garment, the zipper is attached, the placket facing trim is cut to the length used in the finished garment and stitched to the front panels and hood trim, and the garment is washed and ironed.
