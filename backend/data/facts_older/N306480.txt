In your letter dated September 23, 2019, on behalf of your client, VDA Jewellery Limited, you requested a tariff classification and country of origin ruling on a seahorse pendant. We received literature and a photo for review.You request classification in subheading 7113.19.5090, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS) and suggested that the country of origin is Hong Kong.Per the literature and photo provided, the subject merchandise is a jeweled, gold pendant in the shape of a seahorse. In Hong Kong, the seahorse is cast in gold, cleaned and buffed. The pendent is then sent to China with the diamonds (India) and colored stones (Thailand), where the casting is electroplated with rhodium, the diamonds and colored stones are mounted in the casting. The finished pendant is cleaned and packaged for shipping to the United States.CLASSIFICATION
