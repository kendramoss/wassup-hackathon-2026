In your letter dated May 13, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Haloperidol Decanoate Injection.Haloperidol Decanoate Injection, imported in dosages of 50 mg and 100 mg vials, is an antipsychotic drug. It is indicated for the treatment of schizophrenic patients who require prolonged parenteral antipsychotic therapy. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Haloperidol Decanoate, is manufactured in Italy and that the finished Haloperidol Decanoate Injection in dosage form is made in India. In India, the Haloperidol Decanoate will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Haloperidol Decanoate Injection in dosage form.
