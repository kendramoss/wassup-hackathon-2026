In your letter dated March 2, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling of NNC Closure Caps. NNC Closure Caps (screws) (SAP No. 048.324S Lot ZC742) are manufactured in Switzerland and shipped to the United States for final processing. In the United States, the screws undergo washing, blister packaging, primary box packing, final inspection of the product, packing for sterilization, sterilization of load, inspection after sterilization, and lot release. The finished product will be exported from the United States.The "country of origin" is defined in
