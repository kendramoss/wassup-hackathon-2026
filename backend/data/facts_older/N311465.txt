In your letter dated February 18, 2020, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of your client, California Costumes, Inc. You submitted a sample of a child size costume identified as Worm Wishes from the Grave costume, item numbers 3120-087 and SP3120-087, which consists of a shirt, undershirt, pull-on pants, strips of fabric and a mask. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The flimsy shirt imparts the essential character and is constructed of loose single thread stitching with raw, unfinished edges at collar, sleeve ends and the bottom hem area.
