In your letter, dated September 3, 2021, you requested a tariff classification and country of origin ruling on padfolios. On behalf of your client, Lowe’s Companies. A partial description of the manufacturing operations and a sample of the item were submitted for our review.The item under consideration is a padfolio, specifically the “Work Pad Folio,” item number 1059308.The padfolio measures approximately 13” (L) x 10” (W) by 1.5” (D), when zipped closed, and is constructed of what appears to be plastic sheeting and cardboard covered with textile. The interior includes a slot into which an 8.5” x 11” lined writing pad has been inserted. The opposing interior cover incorporates an elastic pen loop, a small calculator, three small pockets for holding business cards or the like, and two vertical pockets for holding slim items, such as papers. You state a belief that the padfolios are classified in subheading 4202.92.00, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS). We disagree.
