This replaces Ruling Number, N305268, dated July 12, 2019, which contained a clerical error. This office incorrectly identified several components with country code “CH” as being from China instead of Switzerland. A complete corrected ruling follows.The items under consideration have been identified as five (5) styles of bicycle wheel rims (models WXRC120BFIXCO05023, WXRC120TFDRCO05027, W0E1900TFDQSO07069, W0M1700BFIXSO05147, and WEX1700TFDRSO11455) that are assembled in Taiwan from parts manufactured in China, Switzerland, and Taiwan. You state that these bicycle wheel rims may potentially be sold under the “DT Swiss” name. You are requesting a ruling on the completed bicycle wheel rim when imported into the United States.The bicycle wheel rim consists of several components. The rims are manufactured in China, the nipples are manufactured in Switzerland, the spokes are manufactured in either Switzerland or Taiwan, and the wheel hubs, rim tape, valves, decals, washers, and manuals are manufactured in Taiwan.
