In your letter dated February 1, 2021, you requested a country of origin ruling.The merchandise under consideration, identified by part number BAWG0738, is described as a USB-C to USB-C cable. The cable consists of a 6-foot length of braided insulated conductor affixed at each end with a USB-C connector. The subject cable is used to connect and charge a variety of mobile devices to host units and charging stations.You state that the manufacturing process is fully completed in the Philippines. This process begins with bulk wire conductor being extruded, twisted, braided, jacketed, and insulated prior to being cut to the desired length. The ends are then prepared for the addition of the connectors, which originate in China. The connectors are soldered to each end before the cables are tested and packaged for shipment to the United States. The “country of origin” is defined in
