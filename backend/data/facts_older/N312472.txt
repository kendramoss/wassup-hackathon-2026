In your letter dated June 15, 2020, on behalf of Dr. Reddy’s Laboratories, Inc., you requested a tariff classification and country of origin ruling.Abiraterone Acetate, imported in 250 mg tablets, is a structural analogue of a steroid hormone with anti-androgen activity. It is indicated for use in combination with prednisone for the treatment of patients with metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer.
