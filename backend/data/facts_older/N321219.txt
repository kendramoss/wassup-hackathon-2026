In your letter dated August 25, 2021, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of your client, California Costume, Inc.You have submitted a costume identified as the child size, I Love The 90’s, item number SP3021-105 which consists of a denim vest, a pair of leggings, a fanny pack, a scrunchie and rhinestone stickers. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The flimsy denim vest imparts the essential character of the set. The vest has raw unraveling edges at the collar, armholes, hem bottom and has no means of closure. The vest is constructed entirely with long loose straight stitching.
