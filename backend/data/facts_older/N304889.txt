This is in response to your letter dated June 19, 2019 requesting a country of origin determination on behalf of Newport International of Tierra Verde Inc., (St. Petersburg, FL) for “Processed Crabmeat” You have presented a scenario whereby Red Swimming Crabs (Portunus haanii) caught on the coast of China, from which the carapace, claws and gills have been removed, will be exported frozen to Vietnam. In Vietnam, the crabs are thawed, washed, cooked, air cooled, chilled, and meat picked and packed in either cans or pouches. The finished product will be imported in 16-ounce pasteurized cans; and/or 1-pound and 4-pound vacuum-sealed pouches, respectively. You have stated that the 4-pound pouches will be individually quick frozen (IQF); however, it was not specified how the 1-pound pouches would be preserved for importation. The “Processed Crabmeat” will be sold to the food service industry (i.e., restaurants, wholesalers, manufacturers). With regard to country of origin, Section 304, Tariff Act of 1930, as amended (
