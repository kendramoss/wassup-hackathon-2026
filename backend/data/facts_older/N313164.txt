In your letter dated July 13, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Tractor Supply Company. The subject flex handle is a hand tool composed of a long handle/neck with a hinged-joint headthat is used to tighten or loosen fasteners at various angles. The tool’s head fits onto fasteners via flex handle socket attachments of various sizes that are not imported with this product or included with this product when it is sold (the sockets are bought separately and used in association with the flex handle). When the tool is grasped by the handle and its hinged-joint head (with socket) is attached to a fastener, it can be positioned at various angles in order to provide the torque necessary to tighten or loosen the fastener.
