In your letter dated January 31, 2022, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Acetaminophen Extended-Release Tablets.Acetaminophen, imported in 650 mg extended-release tablets, is a medicinal preparation containing Acetaminophen, an analgesic and antipyretic. It is indicated for the temporary relief of minor aches and pains and the temporary reduction of fever. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Acetaminophen is manufactured in the United States and that the finished tablets in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Acetaminophen will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final tablets in dosage form.
