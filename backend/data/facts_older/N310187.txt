In your letter dated February 26, 2020, you requested a tariff classification ruling.The submitted sample is a child size costume identified as Gaming Fighter, item number 135572, which consists of a vest, a jumpsuit, a holster belt, a hood, a pair of gloves, goggles and arm and shin guards. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The non-durable vest imparts the essential character of the set. The vest features large, visible, loose overlock stitching at the neck, armholes and hem, as well as small hook and loop tabs at both side seams. You state in your letter that the costume will also be offered in men’s size as item number 135574 and in men’s plus size as item number 135575. All three costumes are identical except for size.
