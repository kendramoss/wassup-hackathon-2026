In your letter dated August 13, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Magna Electronics Technology, Inc.The merchandise under consideration, identified by part number 9043954AA, is described as a lens assembly for use within an Automotive Driver Assistance Camera System. The subject assembly consists of an optical lens, filter, retainer, spacers, and a holder. We note that there are no electronics incorporated at the time of importation. After importation, the lens will be incorporated within the vehicle camera assembly and placed on either the rear, front, or side of a vehicle in order to allow the driver to view the surrounding areas to aid in safe operation. In your request, you state that the manufacturing process begins with the importation of glass rods into Vietnam from China. In Vietnam, the glass rods are cut down into lens blanks that are then ground and polished into finished lenses. This process imparts the items with the desired optical properties. The lenses are then centered and a further optical coating is applied. The retainer and spacer are then adhered to the lens before the item is tested and inspected for form and function. The “country of origin” is defined in
