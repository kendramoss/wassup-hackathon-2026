In your letter dated August 7, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Voyetra Turtle Beach Inc. The product that is the subject of this country of origin ruling request is a wireless gaming headset identified as: Stealth 700X. Voyetra Turtle Beach Inc. is planning to manufacture the gaming headsets within Thailand. The manufacturing process that occurs within Thailand is as follows:Printed circuit boards (PCBs) which are manufactured in Thailand are populated with over 200 electrical components (i.e. capacitors, resistors, switches, etc.) predominantly from China with additional components coming from Thailand, Singapore, Malaysia and Taiwan. A process called Surface Mount Technology (SMT) along with DIP automatic insertion is used to place and connect the components to the PCB forming a printed circuit board assembly (PCBA). Once the PCBAs are manufactured firmware is then loaded onto each one by trained operators.Each PCBA is then subjected to extensive testing by skilled operators Plastic resin from China is used to create over 30 various plastic components (outer shell, housing, ear cups, etc.) using a hot resin injection press manufacturing process.Audio components, headbands, ear cushions, and various miscellaneous parts and parts of general use made in China and Thailand are assembled into the product. Finally the headset is packaged in a retail box with user manual both of which are manufactured in Thailand.A detailed explanation of all manufacturing and assembly steps has been provided.
