In your letter dated October 24, 2019, on behalf of your client, Theory LLC, you requested a ruling on the country of origin of a men’s pullover. Your sample will be retained in our office for training purposes.Style K0284703 “Toledo HL” is a men’s pullover constructed from 66% cotton and 34% nylon knit fabric. The fabric measures 11 stitches per two centimeters counted in the horizontal direction. Style K0284703 features a left-over-right, partial front opening with three button closures, long sleeves with self-finished ends, neckline/placket trim, armhole trim, and a self-finished bottom. The manufacturing operations for Style K0284703 are as follows:All of the garment’s components (the front and back panels, the sleeves, the neckline/placket trim, and the armhole trim) are knit to shape on flat-knitting machines in Hong Kong and exported to China.In China, all components are looped and linked to form the finished garment, and the garment is washed and ironed.
