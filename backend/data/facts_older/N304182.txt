This is in response to your letter dated May 1, 2019, requesting a ruling on whether the proposed country of origin marking on styles 11016 Crockbands and 10001 Classic, rubber/plastics, clog-type footwear with riveted straps was acceptable. Marked samples were not submitted with your letter for review.You provided a photograph of the country of origin marking of an outer sole of rubber/plastics molded footwear. The molded imprint, “Made in Mexico,” is located between the heel and the forefoot on the external surface of the outer sole that faces the ground. It is placed in a central location over the brand name. It is in English and is of a sufficient size to be read by someone with normal vision and without strain.
