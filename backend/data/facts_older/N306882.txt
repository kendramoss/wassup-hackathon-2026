In your letter dated September 26, 2019, you requested a tariff classification ruling.The submitted sample, style number CS20-0102, is a child size Light Up Stick Figure Costume, which consists of a hooded jumpsuit and gloves. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The jumpsuit has single stitched seams and waist, and visible, loose overlock stitching at the arms and ankles. The jumpsuit also has a back opening from the neck to the waist with visible, loose overlock stitching and hook and loop tab closures. There are large gaps between the hook and loop tabs. A small pocket holds a battery pack that when activated illuminates the costume.
