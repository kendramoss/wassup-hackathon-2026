In your letter dated April 29, 2022, on behalf of Dr. Reddy’s Laboratories, Inc., you requested a tariff classification and country of origin ruling.Thiotepa for Injection USP, imported in 15 mg vials, is an anti-cancer (“antineoplastic” or “cytotoxic”) chemotherapy drug. It is indicated for the treatment of breast and ovarian cancer, Hodgkin's and non-Hodgkin's lymphomas, superficial tumors of the bladder, and to help control the accumulation of fluid in body cavities that may result from certain cancers.
