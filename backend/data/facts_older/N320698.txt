In your letter dated July 28, 2021, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Colchicine Tablets.Colchicine Tablets, imported in 0.3 mg and 0.6 mg, is an alkaloid obtained from the plant colchicum autumnale with anti-gout and anti-inflammatory activities. It is indicated for the treatment and prevention of acute gout flares. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API) Colchicine, is manufactured in Italy and that the finished tablets in dosage form are made in India. In India, Colchicine will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final tablets in dosage form.
