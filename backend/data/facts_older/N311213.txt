In your letter dated April 13, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Doxycycline Capsules.Doxycycline, imported in 50 mg and 100 mg capsules, is an antibiotic. It is indicated for use in the treatment of Rocky Mountain spotted fever, typhus fever and respiratory tract infections. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Doxycycline Monohydrate, is manufactured in Macau and that the finished Doxycycline Capsules in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Doxycycline Monohydrate will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final capsules in dosage form.
