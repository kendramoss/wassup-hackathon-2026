In your letter dated June 10, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Walgreens, Company.You state that there are five cables at issue with this request that are described as USB C to C Cables. The difference in the five cables is only in that they are manufactured in varying lengths of 3 feet, 4 feet, 6 feet, 9 feet, and 10 feet. The cables are constructed of the requisite length of insulated cable with a male USB type C connector affixed at each end. The cables are designed to connect and sync a variety of mobile devices to host units or charging stations. In your request, you state that the manufacturing process is fully completed in Vietnam and is the same for all cable lengths. The process begins with raw Vietnamese cable being annealed, drawn, twisted, and jacketed prior to being insulated. The ends are then prepared for the addition of the Chinese connectors. The connectors are soldered to the ends before the cables are tested and packaged for shipment to the United States. The “country of origin” is defined in
