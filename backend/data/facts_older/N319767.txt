This is in response to your letter dated May 30, 2021, you requested a classification and country of origin ruling determination on electric scooters on behalf of your client, Neutron Holdings, d/b/a Lime, located in Hayward, California.The merchandise under consideration has been identified as the Lime “T1” series of seated electric scooters.The T1 scooter is a seated scooter powered by a 1200- watt rear-wheel hub motor capable of a maximum speed of 20 mph with a range of 30 miles. The T1 weighs 106 lbs. and has a maximum weight of 330 lbs.You state that while the scooter will be ultimately assembled in China, the scooter frame will be made in Taiwan. You state that these electric scooters contain nearly identical type components to ones found in electric bicycles, and therefore should be classified under the same classification, 8711.60.0090, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS). We agree.
