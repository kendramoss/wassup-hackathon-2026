In your letter dated December 9, 2019, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Potassium Citrate Extended-Release Tablets.Potassium Citrate, imported in 5 mEq, 10 mEq and 15 mEq extended release tablets, is a medicinal preparation containing Potassium Citrate, as the active ingredient. It is indicated for the management of renal tubular acidosis. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Potassium Citrate, is manufactured in Germany and that the finished tablets in dosage form are made in India. In India, Potassium Citrate will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final extended-release tablets in dosage form.
