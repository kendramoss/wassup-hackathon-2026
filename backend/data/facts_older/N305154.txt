In your letter dated July 5, 2019, on behalf of your client iRobot, Corp., you requested a country of origin determination for purposes of Section 301 duties for the Roomba® “670 Series Robotic Vacuum Cleaner.”The subject merchandise is a Roomba® “670 Series Robotic Vacuum Cleaner.” The Roomba® is a Wi-Fi connected vacuum robot used to clean floors. It features a 3-stage cleaning system allowing it to loosen, lift and suction dirt, dust and hair from hard floors and carpet. The set includes the Roomba® vacuum robot, a rechargeable battery, a docking station for recharging and a power cord. The Roomba® may also include an optional accessory known as a “virtua Wall®” which contains the robotic within a certain space. The Roomba® will be imported from Malaysia, packaged for retail sale.
