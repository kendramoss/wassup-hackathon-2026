In your letter dated April 10, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Memantine Hydrochloride Tablets and Indomethacin Capsules.Memantine Hydrochloride, imported in 5 mg and 10 mg tablets, is an orally active NMDA receptor antagonist. It is indicated for the treatment of moderate to severe dementia of the Alzheimer's type. Indomethacin, imported in 25 mg and 50 mg capsules, is a nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drug (NSAID). It is indicated for the treatment of moderate to severe rheumatoid arthritis including acute flares of chronic disease. For the Memantine Hydrochloride Tablets, you stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Memantine Hydrochloride, is manufactured in Italy and that the finished tablets in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Memantine Hydrochloride will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final tablets in dosage form. For the Indomethacin Capsules, you stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Indomethacin, is manufactured in Italy and that the finished capsules in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Indomethacin will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final capsules in dosage form.
