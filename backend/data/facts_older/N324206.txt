In your letter dated February 7, 2022, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of your client, California Costume, Inc. You have submitted a child size costume identified as “Back from the Dead,” item numbers 3022-041 and SP3022-041, which consists of a long-sleeved pullover upper body garment you refer to as a tunic, an undershirt, pull-on pants and an “exposed bone” leg accessory. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The flimsy man-made textile upper body garment imparts the essential character of the set and features a neckline finished with large, visible, loose overlock stitching, a deep back opening with raw edges and small hook and loop tabs for closure. The long sleeves have raw, unfinished edges. The bottom hem features raw jagged cut edges. The garment also features two large holes with raw unfinished edges. The garment is constructed with long, loose straight stitching.
