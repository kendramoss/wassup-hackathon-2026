In your letter dated April 23, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Lens Link.The merchandise under consideration, identified by part number 6400-5, is described as an Eyepiece Assembly and is comprised of a lens, holder, and spacer. You state that the subject assembly is designed to be utilized in a variety of optical devices including telescopes, riflescopes, and microscopes. The function of the Eyepiece Assembly is to collect and focus the light rays that pass through the lens doublet to create images. In your submission, you state that the lens, referred to as a doublet, is manufactured and optically worked in China. This process includes polishing, coating, and cementing unworked lens blanks. Upon completion, the lenses are capable of creating optical effects including magnification, reduction, and filtering. The Chinese origin lens is then sent to Singapore where it is cleaned and inspected prior to being assembled with Singaporean originating parts into the finished article. The lens is affixed to the spacer, using adhesive, followed by this unit being screwed onto the holder to complete the assembly. The finished Eyepiece Assembly is cleaned, tested, and packaged for export to the United States.The “country of origin” is defined in
