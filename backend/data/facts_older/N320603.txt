In your letter dated July 20, 2021, you requested a country of origin determination for Yellowfin Tuna.You presented a scenario in which Yellowfin Tuna is caught by Chinese-flagged vessels. The whole tuna fish is gilled and gutted on board the vessel. The tuna is exported to Japan where the following processes are performed: the tail and head are removed, then the fish is split into two separate pieces followed by the removal of the fin bone and spine. The remaining loins are pruned to remove the dark flesh from the surface and then from under the skin after the fish is skinned. Lastly, the belly is removed and cleared of the remaining small bones and bloodlines.
