In your letter dated June 4, 2019, and your follow up letter dated July 10, 2019, you requested a country of origin determination for a pillow shell on behalf of your client, Brentwood Originals.  You submitted a sample of a pillow shell. The pillow shell is composed of 100 percent woven polyester fabric, cut into a square shape and sewn along all four sides, leaving a 6 inch unfinished seam to allow the pillow to be stuffed. The pillow shell features tassels on each of the four corners and measures approximately 19 inches square. After importation, the pillow shell will be stuffed and sewn closed.
