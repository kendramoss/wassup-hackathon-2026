In your letter dated October 24, 2019, on behalf of your client, Theory LLC, you requested a ruling on the country of origin of a men’s sweater. Your sample will be retained in our office for training purposes.Style K0184704 “Cadiz C” is a men’s sweater constructed from 100% cotton knit fabric that measures 5 stitches per two centimeters in the horizontal direction. Style K0184704 features a rib knit crew neckline, long sleeves with self-finished ends, and a self-finished bottom. The manufacturing operations for Style K0184704 are as follows:All of the garment’s components (the front and back panels, the sleeves, and the collar) are knit to shape in Hong Kong and exported to China.In China, all components are looped and linked to form the finished garment, and the garment is washed and ironed.
