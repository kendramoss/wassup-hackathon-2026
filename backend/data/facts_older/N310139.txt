In your letter dated February 25, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Doxazosin Tablets in dosage form.Doxazosin, imported in 1 mg, 2 mg, 4 mg and 8 mg tablets, belongs to a class of medications called alpha-blockers. It is indicated for the treatment of Benign Prostatic Hyperplasia (BPH) and hypertension. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Doxazosin Mesylate, is manufactured in Germany and that the finished tablets in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Doxazosin Mesylate will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final tablets in dosage form.
