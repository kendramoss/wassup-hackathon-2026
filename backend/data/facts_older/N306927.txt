In your letter, dated October 11, 2019, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of your client, Orchard Yarn & Thread Company, Inc. You submitted a sample of one article identified as “hatnothate” gel pen, item number PD-65. The article at issue consists of one plastic gel pen with a permanently-affixed, pink knitted hat pen topper. The sample will be retained in our office.You propose the classification in subheading 9608.20.0000, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS), which provides for felt tipped and other porous-tipped pens and markers. We disagree. The pen at issue is neither felt tipped nor porous tipped. It is a ballpoint gel pen.
