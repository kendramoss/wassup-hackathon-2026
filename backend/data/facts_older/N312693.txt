In your letter dated June 23, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Fluroxypyr-meptyl ester emulsion concentrate. The merchandise under consideration is called Fluroxypyr-meptyl ester (CAS No.: 81406-37-3) in the form of an emulsion concentrate. You provided the following description of the processing involved in its manufacture.Fluroxypyr ester herbicide is formulated in Malaysia. One of the ingredients (Fluroxypyr Acid (CAS No.: 69377-81-7) is imported from China. Kenso will import [4-amino-3,5-dichloro-6-fluoro-2-pyridyloxyacetic acid (CAS No.: 69377-81-7, in a short form as Fluroxypyr Acid) that is a stable dormant solid form molecule from China in bulk bags.Fluroxypyr Acid (CAS No.: 69377-81-7) will undergo an esterification process with 2-methyl-1-heptanol (CAS No.: 60435-70-3) in the Malaysia facility to form Fluroxypyr-meptyl ester (CAS No.: 81406-37-3). After the production of Fluroxypyr-meptyl ester (CAS No.: 81406-37-3), the mixture will then be formulated as emulsion concentrate herbicide “as desired.” The process of mixing and formulating is also performed in Malaysia. The "country of origin" is defined in
