In your letter dated March 23, 2022, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of your client, California Costume, Inc.You submitted a detailed description of the adult plus size costume identified as Golden Goddess, item number 8022-090. The costume is said to have the same construction as the adult size Golden Goddess costume, item numbers 5021-167 and SP5021-167, previously ruled on in New York Ruling Letter N321208. The Golden Goddess costume consists of a pullover dress, shoulder drape, sash with rope ties and a headpiece. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The flimsy sleeveless dress imparts the essential character of the set. The one shoulder dress is made of man-made knit fabric and features a single flimsy strap at the opposite shoulder, decorated with textile leaf shapes. The hem bottom is raw and unfinished. The entire dress is constructed with long, loose straight stitching.
