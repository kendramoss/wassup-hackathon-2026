In your letter dated August 25, 2021, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of your client California Costume.You submitted an adult size costume identified as Golden Goddess, item numbers 5021-167 and SP 5021-16, and consists of a pullover dress, shoulder drape, sash with rope ties and a headpiece. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The flimsy sleeveless dress imparts the essential character of the set. The one shoulder dress is made of man-made knit fabric and features a single flimsy strap at the opposite shoulder, decorated with textile leaf shapes. The hem bottom is raw and unfinished. The entire dress is constructed with long, loose straight stitching.
