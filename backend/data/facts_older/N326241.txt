In your letter dated May 20, 2022, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on behalf of your client, Laroot Products, LLC. The merchandise under consideration is referred to as L338 Velcro Discs. They are abrasive discs designed for use with orbital sanders. From the information you provided, natural aluminum oxide corundum, aluminum oxide corundum with zirconia abrasive grains, a polyester film base, and adhesive are sourced from South Korea. In South Korea, the abrasive grains are affixed to the polyester film base using the adhesive. This material is baked, coated with resin and a stearate coating before being rolled into 1380 millimeter-wide rolls and shipped to China. In China, a hook-and-loop backing, which is manufactured in China, is laminated onto the rolls. Next, the laminated rolls are die-cut into specific sizes and hole patterns.
