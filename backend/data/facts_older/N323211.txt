This is in response to your letter dated December 14, 2021, requesting a ruling on tariff classification, marking, and country of origin for Preserved Reindeer Moss.You present a scenario in which Preserved Reindeer Moss (Cladonia Stellaris) is grown in Ukraine, processed in Georgia, and then shipped from France to the United States. The product’s use is described as a plant and flower decoration exclusively for indoor purposes.
