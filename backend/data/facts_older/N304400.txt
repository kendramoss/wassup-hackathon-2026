In your letter dated May 16, 2019, you requested a ruling on the tariff classification, country of origin and status of Panela Sugar from Colombia under the CTPA. You provided samples and ingredients breakdown for our review. The samples will be retained in our office.The subject merchandise is described as Panela Sugar with a stated polarity of approximately 66 degrees. The sugar is obtained from the dehydration of water from the sugar cane juice. The Panela Sugar is said to contain 99.487 percent sugarcane juice (country of origin: Colombia), 0.5 percent coconut palm oil (country of origin: Vietnam), and 0.013 percent sodium bicarbonate (country of origin: Colombia). The ingredients are combined in Colombia to make the Panela Sugar. Shipments will be in bags of 0.5 kilograms, 2.5 kilograms and 6 gram packets to be put up for retail sale.
