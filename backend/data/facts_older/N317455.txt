In your letter dated February 9, 2021, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Sevelamer Carbonate Tablets.Sevelamer Carbonate, imported in 800 mg tablets, is a phosphate binder that is available by prescription only. It is indicated for the control of serum phosphorus in adults and children 6 years of age and older with chronic kidney disease (CKD) on dialysis.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Sevelamer Carbonate, is manufactured in Taiwan and that the finished tablets in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Sevelamer Carbonate will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final Sevelamer Carbonate Tablets in dosage form.
