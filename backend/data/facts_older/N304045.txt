In your letter dated April 22, 2019, you requested a tariff classification ruling.You submitted a sample of item number 119322, Carnevil Clown Girl’s Costume, which consists of a dress, gloves, boot cuffs and knee high socks. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The flimsy polyester dress imparts the essential character, and has visible, loose overlock stitching at the collar, visible, loose overlock stitching at the hem, a single straight stitched waist, thin elastics at the wrists, and a large back opening that extends from the neck to the back waist. The dress also has three large red pom-poms glued down its front, a hook and loop tab closure at the back opening, and visible, loose overlock stitching at the closure edge. Additionally, the dress collar, front and hem have a deliberately filthy appearance. You also submitted a sample of item number 119324, Carnevil Clown Women’s Costume, which is constructed exactly the same as the Carnevil Clown Girl’s Costume except it has raw, jagged (scissor cut) hem edges instead of visible, loose overlock stitched hem edges.
