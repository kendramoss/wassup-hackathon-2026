In your letter dated December 28, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Chlorthalidone Tablets.Chlorthalidone, imported in 25 mg and 50 mg tablets, is a diuretic medication. It is indicated as adjunctive therapy in edema associated with congestive heart failure, hepatic cirrhosis, and corticosteroid and estrogen therapy.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Chlorthalidone, is manufactured in Italy and that the finished Chlorthalidone Tablets in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Chlorthalidone will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final tablets in dosage form.
