In your letter dated August 26, 2019 you requested a country of origin ruling.The merchandise under consideration is described as a traditional style wooden or plastic photo frame that wirelessly charges a user’s smart mobile device. The backing of the frame, which houses the electronics for the wireless charger, can be removed to change the photo within the frame. The wireless charging pad is powered through a micro USB cable, which is provided with the frame. We would note that the subject photo frame does not contain any LCD/LED display or memory for the storage of pictures.
