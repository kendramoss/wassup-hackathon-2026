In your letter dated September 17, 2019, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Mesalamine Delayed-Release Tablets.Mesalamine Delayed-Release Tablets, imported in 1200 mg, is an anti-inflammatory drug. It is indicated for the induction of remission in patients with active, mild to moderate ulcerative colitis and for the maintenance of remission of ulcerative colitis.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Mesalamine, is manufactured in Italy, and that the finished tablets in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Mesalamine is mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final tablets in dosage form.
