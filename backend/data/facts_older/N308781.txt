In your letter dated December 27, 2019, you requested a ruling on behalf of your client, Luvata Ohio, Inc., concerning the tariff classification and country of origin of copper wire produced in Vietnam. According to your submission, Luvata Ohio, Inc. will import copper silicon C65600 braze wire that is produced in Vietnam. The manufacturing process begins when Japanese-origin copper cathodes are imported into China where they are cast and alloyed into wire rod measuring approximately 1.7 mm in diameter. The wire rod is then shipped to Vietnam where it undergoes drawing, annealing, testing, inspection, coiling and packing operations. The wire rod is drawn from 1.7 mm down to 1.143 mm. The tensile strength is increased from 500 – 600 MPa to 900 – 1000 MPa. Also, the cast and helix of the wire is set, and the wire is wound onto a designated spool.
