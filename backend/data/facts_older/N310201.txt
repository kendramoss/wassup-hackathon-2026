In your letter dated February 26, 2020, on behalf of your client, Theory LLC, you requested a ruling on the country of origin of a men’s upper body garment. Your sample will be retained in our office for training purposes. Style K0584701 “Lievos Hood SR” is a men’s cardigan-style garment constructed from knit fabric with a stated fiber content of 88% cotton and 12% nylon. The outer surface of the garment’s fabric measures more than nine stitches per two centimeters in the direction the stitches were formed. Style K0584701 features a self-fabric hood, a full-front opening with a zippered closure, long sleeves with rib knit ends that are formed by a change in knit pattern, vertical pockets below the waist, and a rib knit bottom that is formed by a change in knit pattern. The manufacturing operations for Style K0584701 are as follows:The front and back panels, the sleeves, the hood, the pocket bags, the front placket trim, the trim at the edge of the hood, the trim at the center back of the hood, the neck trim, the pocket trim, and the pocket facing trim are knit to shape on flat-knitting machines in Hong Kong and exported to China. The placket facing trim is also knit in Hong Kong and exported to China. In China, the majority of the components are looped and linked to form the finished garment, the zipper is attached, the placket facing trim is cut to the length used in the finished garment and stitched to the front panels and neck trim, and the garment is washed and ironed.
