In your letter dated August 11, 2020, you requested a tariff classification and country of origin ruling. Additional information was provided via email dated August 17, 2020. The documents provided were submitted to our laboratory for analysis.The ECL solution kit is comprised of three bottles (tubes), package together for retail sale. Bottle #1 contains a mixture of water and luminol (CAS# 521-31-3). Bottle #2 consists of a mixture of water and p-Coumaric acid (CAS# 501-98-4). Bottle #3 consisits of a mixture of water and hydrogen peroxide (CAS# 7722-84-1). The ECL solution kit will be used as a biology reagent for pharmaceutical and biomedical research use, but it is not for clinical diagnostic procedure or use.According to our laboratory, the enhanced chemiluminescence solution kit is used for the detection of an analyte. It engages in a chemical reaction in response to an analyte, is consumed during the reaction, and produces an observable or measurable response. The applicable classification for the ECL solution kit will be 3822.00.5095, HTSUS, which provides for “[d]iagnostic or laboratory reagents on a backing and prepared diagnostic or laboratory reagents, whether or not on a backing, other than those of heading 3002 or 3006; certified reference materials: [d]iagnostic or laboratory reagents on a backing, prepared diagnostic or laboratory reagents, whether or not on a backing, other than those of heading 3002 or 3006: [o]ther: [o]ther.” The general rate of duty will be free.This merchandise may be subject to the requirements of the Toxic Substances Control Act (TSCA), which are administered by the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA).
