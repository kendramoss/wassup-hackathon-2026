In your letter dated September 25, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Lowe’s Companies, Inc.The merchandise under consideration is described as a booster cable used to assist in starting a vehicle with a dead battery. The subject cables consist of a length of insulated conductor with two clamps at each end. The clamps are designed to attach to the positive and negative terminal of automotive batteries. In your request, you state that copper conductor wire is manufactured in China and sent to Vietnam. In Vietnam, the copper conductor is is insulated through the extrusion of red and black PVC particles. These insulated conductors are then cut to length and the Chinese originating metal clamps are affixed to the ends. The finished cables are then inspected for form and function prior to being packaged for shipment to the United States. The “country of origin” is defined in
