In your letter dated September 13, 2019, you requested a tariff classification ruling.The submitted sample, style number AC18-0038, Dragon Wings, are gray and black serrated edged dragon wings with “scales” on the back. The wings have elastic bands that slide over the wearer’s shoulders and hold the wings in place, so that they can be worn like a cape. There are small elastic bands on the front corners of the wings that attach to the wearer’s wrists for additional stability. The dragon wings are made of polyester non-woven fabric and sized for ages three and older.
