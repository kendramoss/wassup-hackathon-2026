The subject merchandise consists of a tufted bath rug. The pile surface is made from twisted, uncut loops of polyester chenille yarns that are tufted into a pre-existing polyester woven base. The tufts are approximately 1/2 inch in length from the surface. The tufted fabric is laminated to a 1/8 inch thick layer of polyurethane (PU) foam which is then backed with a polyvinyl chloride (PVC) mesh material to create a non-skid surface. The edges of the rug are capped with a narrow strip of knit binding fabric. The finished rug measures 27 x 45 inches. You submitted two manufacturing scenarios with variations within each scenario.
