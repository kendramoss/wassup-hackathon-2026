In your ruling request dated April 24, 2019, you requested a country of origin ruling on 4 Play-Doh playsets.You describe the 4 products at issue as playsets, designed for the amusement of children 3 years of age and older. Product # 1. Item # E4902 “Play-Doh Mini Classics Assortment” consists of 3 popular Play-Doh mini sets, including “Play-Doh Mini Fun Factory,” “Play-Doh Mini Crazy Cuts,” and “Play-Doh Mini Doctor Drill N’ Fill.” The components within each mini assortment are as follows:“Play-Doh Mini Fun Factory” consists of 2 cans of Play-Doh modelling compound, 1 plastic Play-Doh extruder tool, 1 plastic knife, and 3 plastic Play-Doh shape mold rails.“Play-Doh Mini Crazy Cuts” consists of 2 cans of Play-Doh modelling compound, 1 plastic Play-Doh character thimble, 1 plastic pair of Play-Doh scissors, and 1 plastic Play-Doh razor.“Play-Doh Mini Doctor Drill N’ Fill” consists of 2 cans of Play-Doh modelling compound, 1 plastic toothbrush, 1 plastic base, 1 plastic drill, and 1 plastic tooth mold.All components in the Play-Doh sets are designed to be used in conjunction with the modelling compound to contribute to the overall play experience of children. You state that the accessories directly contribute to the play value by allowing the children to immerse themselves in the three different creative Play-Doh experiences featured in each mini set. Product # 2. Item # E3342 “Play-Doh Role Play Tools Assortment” consists of 3 cans of Play-Doh modelling compound, 1 plastic hammer rolling stamper, 1 plastic saw tool, 1 plastic tape measure rolling stamper, 1 plastic screwdriver cutter, 1 plastic wood texture roller, and 1 plastic wrench extruder rail tool. All components in the Play-Doh set are designed to be used in conjunction with the modelling compound to create a role play experience for children as they use the Play-Doh construction tools to build their own unique creations. Product # 3. Item # E5400 “Play-Doh Wheels – Crane and Forklift” consists of 3 cans of Play-Doh modelling compound, 1 plastic crane and rail, and 1 plastic toy forklift truck. All components in the Play-Doh set are specifically designed to be used in conjunction with the modelling compound for children to role play as they build and maneuver around their own Play-Doh construction zone. Product # 4. Item # E4294 “Play-Doh Wheels – Excavator and Loader” consists of 3 cans of Play-Doh modelling compound, 1 plastic toy excavator truck, and 1 plastic toy front-end loader truck. All components in the Play-Doh set are designed to be used in conjunction with the modelling compound to create an immersive play experience for children. Your submission indicates that, except for the Play-Doh cans of modelling compound, the above described playsets are manufactured in Vietnam from raw materials and packaged for retail sale at time of export to the United States.
