In your ruling request dated April 24, 2019, you requested a country of origin ruling on 4 Play-Doh playsets.You describe the 4 products at issue as playsets, designed for the amusement of children 3 years of age and older. Product # 1. Item # B5520 “Play-Doh Doctor Drill ‘N Fill” consists of 5 cans of Play-Doh modelling compound, 1 plastic tooth-brush, 1 plastic base, 1 plastic tweezer, 1 plastic drill, 1 plastic mirror, 1 plastic roller, and 1 plastic tooth mold. All components in the Play-Doh set are designed to be used in conjunction with the modelling compound for children to enhance their creativity and imagination by role playing as a dentist.Product # 2. Item # E5109 “Play-Doh Kitchen Creations – Sprinkle Cookie Surprise” consists of 5 cans of Play-Doh modelling compound, 1 plastic cookie tower base, 5 plastic Play-Doh cookie cutters, 1 plastic rolling pin, 1 plastic cookie rolling stamper, 1 plastic rolling cutter, 1 plastic frosting tool, 1 plastic frosting knife, 2 plastic plates, and 2 Play-Doh creation recipe cards. All components in the Play-Doh set are designed to be used in conjunction with the modelling compound to inspire children to channel their inner baker and use their imagination to create and decorate Play-Doh cookies. Product # 3. Item # E1935 “Play-Doh Kitchen Creations – Ultimate Swirl Ice Cream Maker” consists of 6 cans of Play-Doh modelling compound, 2 cans of Play-Doh Plus modelling compound, 1 plastic ice cream maker base, 6 plastic ice cream cones, 5 plastic dishes, 4 plastic spoons, and 2 Play-Doh creation recipe cards. All components in the Play-Doh set are designed to be used in conjunction with the modelling compound for children to use their creativity to create Play-Doh ice cream treats. Product # 4. Item # E4293 “Play-Doh Wheels – Gravel Yard” consists of 4 cans of Play-Doh modelling compound, 1 plastic gravel grinder base, 1 plastic toy dump truck, and 1 plastic toy steam roller truck. All components in the Play-Doh set are designed to be used in conjunction with the modelling compound for children to create their own Play-Doh construction zone. Your submission indicates that, except for the Play-Doh cans of modelling compound, the above described playsets are manufactured in Vietnam from raw materials and packaged for retail sale at time of export to the United States.
