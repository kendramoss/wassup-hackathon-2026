In your letter dated December 18, 2021, you requested a country of origin ruling on behalf of your client, Amazon Fulfillment Service, Inc.The merchandise under consideration is identified as the 24 W Dual-Port USB Car Charger Adapter (Power Adapter), ASIN No. B06Y58WLX9, which is described as a 5 V, 4.8 Amp rectifier having two female USB socket connectors on one side and 12 V vehicle accessory electrical plug on the other. The subject power adapter is intended to be used in a vehicle for charging up to two personal electronic devices at a time and is comprised of a plastic molded housing containing a printed circuit board assembly (PCBA) and plug terminals. In your submission, you state the power adapter is made of components sourced from China and Vietnam and manufactured in Vietnam. The assembly process begins with the manufacturing of a printed circuit board assembly (PCBA) that occurs in Vietnam by surface mounting/through hole soldering of individual components, such as integrated circuits, resistors, a fuse, capacitors, etc. onto a bare printed circuit board. Also in Vietnam, the female USB connectors and the anode head are glued onto the case assembly along with the PCBA, which is then encapsulated in a plastic housing by means of ultrasonic welding. Throughout the assembly process, the parts and finished product are tested and visually inspected. Then the power adapter is packaged for importation into the United States.
