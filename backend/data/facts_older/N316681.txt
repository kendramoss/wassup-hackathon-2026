In your electronic submission dated December 30, 2020, you requested a tariff classification ruling on five styles of footwear. You have submitted photographs, a ruling request letter, samples of five styles, and copies of laboratory reports.Style 95966 Men’s Trekking Sandals/ Black-Grey and style 95967 Men’s Trekking Sandals/ Dk.brown-black are men’s closed toe/open heel, below-the-ankle sandals. The external surface area of the uppers is predominantly rubber/plastics (less than 90 percent.) The balance of the uppers is textile materials. Elastic lace is threaded through eyelets and cross over the foot. Style 95967 Men’s Trekking Sandals/ Dk.brown-black has an adjustable heel strap with a hook and loop closure and a separate tongue. According to laboratory reports, the thick rubber/plastics outer soles have textile material covering the majority of the external surface area in contact with the ground. The F.O.B. value is $8.28 per pair.
