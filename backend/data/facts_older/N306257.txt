In your letter dated September 9, 2019, you requested a tariff classification, country of origin and marking ruling.Ingredients breakdowns, manufacturing narrative descriptions, manufacturing flowcharts, and copies of the retail labels accompanied your inquiry. In Canada, all the vegetables will be cleaned, peeled, sliced or cut and repackaged into retail plastic containers. The Apples & Grapes are said to contain a fresh cut blend of approximately 50 percent whole grapes, and 50 percent sliced apples. The grapes will be grown in Chile, South Africa, the United States, Peru, and Mexico. The sliced apples will be grown in the United States, Chile, New Zealand, South Africa, and Canada.In your letter, you suggested tariff classifications for the apples in the Apples & Grapes product under 0808.10.0030, Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS), which provides for apples, pears and quinces, fresh: Apples . . . valued not over 22 cents per kg. Based on the product description, the product will be classified elsewhere. In your letter, you also suggested tariff classifications for the grapes in the Apples & Grapes product under 0806.10.2000, and 0806.10.4000, HTSUS, which provides for grapes, fresh or dried ... fresh. If entered during the period from April 1 to June 30, inclusive, in any year, they will be classified in subheading 0806.10.4000, HTSUS. Based on the product description, the product will be classified elsewhere. The Mango & Pineapple Spears are said to contain a fresh cut blend of approximately 50 percent mango spears, and 50 percent pineapple spears. The pineapples will be grown in Costa Rica. The mangoes will be grown in Peru, Brazil, and Mexico. In your letter, you suggested tariff classifications for the mangoes in the Mango & Pineapple Spears product under 0804.50.4055 and 0804.50.6055, HTSUS, which provides for fresh mangoes … other if entered during the period from September 1, in any year, to the following May 31, inclusive. If entered at any other time, the applicable subheading for the mangoes will be 0804.50.6055, which provides for mangoes… other. Based on the product description, the product will be classified elsewhere. In your letter, you also suggested tariff classifications for the pineapple spears in the Mango & Pineapple Spears product under 0804.30.6000, HTSUS, which provides for dates, figs, pineapples ... fresh or dried ... pineapples ... reduced in size. Based on the product description, the product will be classified elsewhere.
