The sensor gives a comprehensive picture of the machinery’s status for enhanced operations, reliability and maintenance practices. The 3XS Sensor contains a three-axis accelerometer, which is used to detect the vibrations and relay information to the user. The device also monitors the temperature and magnetic values of machinery. The collected data is sent, via Bluetooth, to a nearby hub, which will then be relayed via the internet to the customer’s server. The 3XS Sensor is used in large machinery such as power plants, water treatment, food and pharmacy industries.
