In your letter dated October 21, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling.The products under consideration are aluminum panels. According to your submission, cut-to-length aluminum panels manufactured in Norway ranging in thicknesses from 1.5 mm to 10 mm are shipped to Germany where they will be anodized and subsequently exported to the United States (U.S). You indicate that in the U.S., the panels are cut-to-size and either milled, engraved or printed upon the customer’s request. The panels will be used in the aerospace, computer and health industry, U.S. government institutions and research laboratories. You are of the opinion that anodizing is minor processing and that the country of origin of the panels should be Norway, the country in which they are manufactured.
