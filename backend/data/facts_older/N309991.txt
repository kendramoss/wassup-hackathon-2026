In your letter dated February 20, 2020, you requested a tariff classification ruling.The submitted sample is a teen size costume identified as Dead Dancer, item number CT18119, which consists of a leotard, a tutu, tights and a plastic crown with a simulated knife. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The flimsy pull-on tutu imparts the essential character of the set and features an exposed elastic waist with raw edges on the skirt with hanging threads.You state in your letter that the costume will also be offered in girl’s size as item number CG18119, in women’s size as item number CF18119, and in women’s plus size as item number CW18119. All four costumes are identical except for size.
