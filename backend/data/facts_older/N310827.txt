In your letter dated March 23, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling. The item concerned is a wireless doorbell speaker. This device undergoes manufacturing processes in two different countries, Taiwan and China. The wireless doorbell speaker contains a printed circuit board assembly, which is produced in Taiwan from electrical elements/components made in various countries. In Taiwan, the programming/flashing of integrated circuit chips (IC’s) takes place. The printed circuit board assembly is manufactured using surface mount technology (SMT) machines such as, circuit printing machines, reflow ovens, paster machines, X-ray machines, router machines, etc. During this process electrical elements (i.e. IC’s, diodes, transistors, capacitors, etc.) are placed on a printed circuit board and soldered into the circuitry. Next, the PCBAs are tested, packaged and shipped to China for the final assembly process.In China, the PCBAs are assembled within a housing and connected to antennas, batteries, speaker, etc. Finally, the finished item is tested.
