In your letter dated August 7, 2019, on behalf of Dr. Reddy’s Laboratories, Incorporated, you requested a tariff classification and country of origin ruling.Isotretinoin, imported in 10 mg, 20 mg, 30 mg and 40 mg capsules, is a synthetic Vitamin A acid derivative known as 13-cis-retinoic acid. It is avaible by prescription only for the treatment of severe nodular acne.
