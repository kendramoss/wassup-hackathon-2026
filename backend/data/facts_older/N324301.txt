In your letter dated February 11, 2022, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of your client, California Costume, Inc.You submitted an adult size costume, identified as the “Angel of Darkness,” item numbers 5022-064 and SP5022-064, which consists of a sleeveless pullover upper body garment which you refer to as a tunic, glovelettes, printed wings and a halo headpiece. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The flimsy man-made textile upper body garment imparts the essential character of the set and features large, visible, loose overlock stitching at the neckline and at the bottom of the armholes. The top of the armholes and bottom hem have raw, jagged cut edges. The arm ties which are attached at the front and back shoulders, the cross strapping at the neck and the attached waist tie are all just strips of fabric with raw unfinished edges. The upper body garment is constructed with long, loose straight stitching.
