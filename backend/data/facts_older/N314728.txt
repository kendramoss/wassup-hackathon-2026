In your letter dated September 22, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Fluphenazine Hydrochloride Tablets.Fluphenazine Hydrochloride, imported in 1 mg, 2.5 mg, 5 mg and 10 mg tablets, belongs to a class of medications called phenothiazines. It is indicated in the management of manifestations of psychotic disorders. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Fluphenazine Hydrochloride, is manufactured in Italy and that the finished tablets in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Fluphenazine Hydrochloride will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final tablets in dosage form.
