This is in response to your letter, dated April 26, 2019, requesting a ruling on whether the proposed country of origin marking on four samples of imported footwear is acceptable. The submitted samples are identified as Model 1, “Rainboot” (China), Model 2, “Sandal” (China), Model 3, “Fitness shoe” (China), and Model 4, “Gardenclog” (Italy). All submitted samples will be returned.Model 1, “Rainboot” is a child’s closed toe/closed heel, above-the ankle, below-the-knee boot with an upper and outer sole of rubber or plastic. The country of origin label stating “Made in China” is conspicuously and permanently stitched to the inner shaft towards the top of the boot.Model 2, “Sandal” is a woman’s open toe/open heel, below-the-ankle, thong sandal, with a rubber or plastic upper and outer sole. The country of origin sticker printed with “Made in China,” located on the outer sole, is removable and not permanent.Model 3, “Fitness shoe” is a closed toe/closed heel, below-the-ankle, lace-up athletic shoe, with a textile upper and outer sole of rubber or plastic. The country of origin label showing “Made in China” is located on top of the inner sole is removable and not permanent.Model 4, “Gardenclog” is a unisex, closed toe/open heel, below-the-ankle clog, with a molded rubber or plastic upper and outer sole. The country of origin marking, “Made in Italy,” is permanently and legibly molded into the outer sole.
