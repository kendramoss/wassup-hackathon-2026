In your letter dated February 22, 2022, on behalf of Encube Ethicals Private Limited, India, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Clobetasol Propionate Cream. Clobetasol Propionate Cream USP, 0.05% is a corticosteroid formulation indicated for the relief of the inflammatory and pruritic manifestations of corticosteroid-responsive dermatoses. The Clobetasol Propionate Cream will be imported in retail packs.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Clobetasol Propionate, is manufactured in Italy and that the finished cream in dosage form is made in India. In India, the Clobetasol Propionate will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final cream in dosage form.
