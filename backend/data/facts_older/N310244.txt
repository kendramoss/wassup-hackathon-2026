In your letter dated February 28, 2020, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Haloperidol Tablets.Haloperidol, imported in 5 mg, 10 mg and 20 mg tablets, is an antipsychotic drug. It is indicated for the management of manifestations of psychotic disorders. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Haloperidol, is manufactured in Germany and that the finished tablets in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Haloperidol will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final tablets in dosage form.
