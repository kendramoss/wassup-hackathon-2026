This is in response to your letter dated August 13, 2020, requesting a ruling on the country of origin for aircraft engine forgings and castings. Pictures, diagrams, and descriptive literature were provided with your request. The components under review are the High Pressure Turbine Compressor Stator Case, Housing Support - #4 Bearing, Nozzle Support, and Stationary Seal Support - #4 Bearing. You state that the aircraft engine forgings and castings are made of steel, nickel, or titanium, which are produced in the United States by various metal works companies to GE specifications on material and shape. The forgings and castings, the shape of which approximate the outline of the finished aircraft engine parts they will form, are exported from the United States to various machine shops in China for milling, drilling, and finishing processes.
