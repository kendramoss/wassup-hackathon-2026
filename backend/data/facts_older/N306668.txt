In your letter dated October 11, 2019, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Elianto Lumino White Intensive Essence and Elianto Lumino White Advanced Night Cream, on behalf of your client, MOIRA Cosmetics, Inc. Both products are indicated for use in skin care applications. In your request letter you provide the following pertinent information: The Elianto Lumino White Intensive Essence directions for use indicate “Two pumps of this is sufficient for the whole face. Pat gently onto the face.” It is indicated to soothe and protect the skin, and to increase and restore elasticity and suppleness to the skin. It is also stated to whiten the skin by lightening blemishes.You indicated that it contains: aqua (water), butylene glycol, niacinamide, glycerin, phenoxyethanol, ethylhexylglycerin, panthenol, ppg-26-buteth-26, sodium hyaluronate, carbomer, peg-40 hydrogenated castor oil, parfum (fragrance), sodium hydroxide, chondrus crispus powder (carrageenan), limonene, linalool, biosaccharide gum-1, pancratium maritimum extract.The Elianto Lumino White Advanced Night Cream directions for use indicate to “dot the night cream over face and neck, massage gently into the skin from the neck upwards. To be used after cleansing and toning details of the components.” It is stated to moisturize, nourish and brighten the skin while you sleep. You indicated that it contains (in part): carprylic/capric triglyceride, niacinamide, glycerin, butylene glycol, sweet almond oil, shea butter, peppermint extact, and various other natural extracts, emulsifiers and other ingredients. You provided the following description of the manufacturing process for both products:The bulk is entirely produced in Switzerland and then exported to Malaysia. The filling process is done in Malaysia without any substantial changes in the bulk. The bulk is filled into “a respective container/packaging without changing the bulk nature.” After filling is done, then the finished goods are assembled for packing before shipping to customers.You indicate that packaging materials are of Chinese origin. You also state that all filling and assembling occurrs in Malaysia. You state that no substantial changes, additions or alterations to the bulk occurs in Malaysia.
