In your letter dated January 31, 2022, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Potassium Chloride Extended-Release Tablets.Potassium Chloride, imported in 10 mEq and 20 mEq extended-release tablets, is a medicinal preparation containing Potassium Chloride, as the active ingredient. It is indicated for the treatment and prophylaxis of hypokalemia with or without metabolic alkalosis.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Potassium Chloride is manufactured in the United States and that the finished tablets in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Potassium Chloride will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final tablets in dosage form.
