In your letter dated March 6, 2020, on behalf of Encube Ethicals Private Ltd., you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Clobetasol Propionate Ointment.Clobetasol Propionate Ointment 0.05%, imported in 15 g, 30 g, 45 g and 60 g tubes, is a synthetic corticosteroid. It is indicated for the relief of the inflammatory and pruritic manifestations of corticosteroid responsive dermatoses.You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Clobetasol Propionate, is manufactured in Italy and that the finished ointment in dosage form is made in India. In India the Clobetasol Propionate will be mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final ointment in dosage form.
