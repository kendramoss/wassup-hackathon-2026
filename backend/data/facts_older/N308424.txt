In your letter dated December 5, 2019, on behalf of LaFrance Corp (“LaFrance”), you requested a ruling on the country of origin for RFID icons and badges.The merchandise under consideration is metal icons and plastic badges, each of which contains a radio frequency identification (“RFID”) chip. The subject RFID icons and badges are used for “asset protection.” Each of the subject icons and badges has an electronic IC (with a controller or microprocessor and ROM and/or RAM memory) in the form of an RFID chip and contains an embedded antenna, but do not contain any other active or passive elements. Moreover, each RFID icon and badge are imported recorded with a unique number that cannot be changed.
