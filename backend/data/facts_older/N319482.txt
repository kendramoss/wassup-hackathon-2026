In your letters dated April 13, 2021, and May 17, 2021, you requested a tariff classification ruling. Included with your submissions were photographs and descriptive literature for a footwear style identified as Savannah.Photographs of footwear style Savannah show a woman’s, closed/toe, closed heel, below-the-ankle, sneaker. You state the external surface area of the upper consists of polyurethane (PU) material. The toe box and heel patch are decorated with silver colored rubber/plastics glitter. The sneaker features laces as a means of closure. The rubber or plastics outer sole overlaps the upper by the requisite ¼ of an inch, thereby constituting a foxing-like band. The F.O.B. value provided is $10.00 per pair. The shoe is made in China.
