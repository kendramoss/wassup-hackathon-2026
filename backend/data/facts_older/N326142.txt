In your letter dated May 17, 2022, on behalf of Dr. Reddy’s Laboratories, Inc., you requested a tariff classification and country of origin ruling.Fesoterodine Fumarate, imported in 4 mg and 8 mg extended-release tablets, is an antimuscarinic drug. It is indicated for the treatment of overactive bladder (OAB) in adults with symptoms of urge urinary incontinence, urgency, and frequency.
