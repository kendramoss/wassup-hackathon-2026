In your letter dated August 20, 2019, you requested a country of origin ruling determination on Albendazole Tablets. Albendazole, imported in 200 mg tablets, is a synthetic anthelmintic of the benzimidazole class. It is indicated for the treatment of parenchymal neurocysticercosis due to active lesions caused by larval forms of the pork tapeworm, Taenia solium. You stated that the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API), Albendazole, is manufactured in Mexico and that the finished tablets in dosage form are made in India. In India, the Albendazole is mixed with various inactive ingredients to produce the final tablets in dosage form.
