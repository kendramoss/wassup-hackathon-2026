In your letter dated May 24, 2022, you requested a tariff classification ruling.The merchandise under consideration is referred to as KG00801 Commercial Dry Run-Mock Apheresis (Saline). The tariff classification and country of origin determination of the other product included in your ruling request, KG00802 Commercial Dry Run Final Product (Saline) will be addressed in ruling N326477. KG00801 is a sterile 0.9% solution of sodium chloride in water (saline), imported in 400 milliliter bags. From the information you provided, the saline is shipped from a hospital in Australia to your manufacturing facility in the United States. Once the saline is received, it is immediately discarded. You state that the saline mimics the requirements of T-cells collected from lymphoma patients. This process allows the hospital to practice sample collection and disposition, and to test shipping logistics and process readiness, before patients are enrolled to receive your time- and environment-sensitive commercial products. At no point is the saline used or administered to patients.
