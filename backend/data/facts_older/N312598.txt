In your letter dated June 18, 2020, you requested a tariff classification ruling on behalf of your client, California Costume, Inc. You submitted a sample of a child size costume identified as the Susan B. Anthony Kit, item number PC3020-139, which consists of a long dress, a lace collar and a ruffled collar. GRI 3(b) is applicable when goods are, prima facie, classifiable under two or more headings, and have been put up in sets for retail sale. GRI 3(b) states that the goods “shall be classified as if they consisted of the material or component which gives them their essential character.” The flimsy dress imparts the essential character and features loose, single stitched seams and raw, unfinished edges at the neckline, sleeves, back opening and hemline. The long sleeves and waist have narrow exposed elastic. The large back opening is closed with a small hook and loop tab.
